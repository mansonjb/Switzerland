# Sources: data/guides/laax.ts

Checked 16/09/2026. Every fact below comes from the research dossier `data/research/laax.md`, itself
sourced to LAAX / Weisse Arena (laax.com), Flims Laax Falera (flimslaax.com), the Swiss national

- Trutg dil Flem: 8.6 km, 3 h 30 downhill, 4 h 30 uphill, seven bridges by Jürg Conzett, Prix Rando 2014, snow- and ice-free season only | VERIFIED | https://www.flimslaax.com/aktiv-sein/wandern/trutg-dil-flem
