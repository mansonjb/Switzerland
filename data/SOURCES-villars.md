## Hotel website verification (21/09/2026)
Individual hotel domains were not fetched in the research pass (flagged UNVERIFIED in data/research/villars.md).
To avoid publishing a broken or guessed link, each hotel's own website was resolved from the tourist office's
own hotel detail sub-pages on alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/G4062 (the same approved source already used for the hotel
facts), then confirmed reachable (HTTP 200) on 21/09/2026. No new editorial facts were taken from these sites,
only the outbound URL.
- Villars Palace -> https://www.villarspalace.ch/ (via alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/P103856)
- Villars Lodge -> https://www.villarslodge.ch/ (via alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/P129898)
- Hotel Victoria and Residence -> https://www.villarsvictoria.ch/ (via alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/P129879)
- Viu Hotel Villars -> https://www.hotelviu.ch/en/ (via alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/P73766)
- Hotel Les Mazots du Clos -> https://fr.mazotsduclos.com/ (via alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/P88709)
- Hotel Alpe Fleurie -> https://www.alpe-fleurie.com/ (via alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/P129876)
- Le Chalet RoyAlp Hotel & Spa -> https://www.royalp.ch/ (via alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/P129888)

rejected; villarsvictoria.ch is the correct site confirmed via the tourist office's own listing page.

Note on French decimals: the Roc d'Orsay to Villars walk is 7.8 km (dossier), written "7,8 km" in French
typography; stripped of punctuation by the prose checker this reads as 78, same figure.
