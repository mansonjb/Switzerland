# Sources: Sils Maria guide

Checked 16/09/2026 (dossier), guide written 21/09/2026. One line per fact, then UNVERIFIED.

- Altitude 1802 m, population "rund 810 Einwohnern", two localities Sils Maria and Sils Baselgia plus Val Fex, name origin (Meierei for Sils Maria, San Lurench church 1356 / "Basilika" for Sils Baselgia), "verkehrsfrei" quote, Grevasalvas/Heidi backdrop | VERIFIED | https://www.sils.ch/de/guide/sils-im-engadin/dorfportrait
- Access: Zurich HB to Sils/Segl Maria Posta, total 3 h 27, 2 changes onto the Rhaetian Railway then the Engadin Bus; St. Moritz to Sils about 24 minutes by Engadin Bus; Julier/Albula passes from Chur (Albula open summer only); Vereina tunnel car shuttle Klosters/Selfranga to Sagliains in about 18 minutes, car and driver stay inside; Parkhaus Segl / Val Fex car park | VERIFIED | Swiss public transport timetable data (transport.opendata.ch), queried 16/09/2026, and https://www.sils.ch/de/guide/sils-im-engadin/anreise-mobilitaet-vor-ort
- Postcodes: Sils Maria 7514, Sils Baselgia 7515 | VERIFIED | address listings on sils.ch, e.g. https://www.sils.ch/en/guide/accommodation/hotels/hotel-waldhaus and https://www.hotel-edelweiss.ch
- Furtschellas-Corvatsch: 120 km of pistes ("120 Pistenkilometern"), highest mountain station in canton Graubünden at 3303 m ("3'303 Metern"), Engadin Bus serves Furtschellas valley station summer and winter, Fexer Alpenflora protected since 1954, named tours Senda Ils Lejins (6.05 km / 6,05 km, 2:32 h, 393 hm up and down, graded hard) and Via Gastronomica Furtschellas-Val Fex-Sils Maria (6.83 km / 6,83 km, 2:10 h, 27 hm up / 545 hm down, graded medium) | VERIFIED | https://www.sils.ch/de/guide/berge-bahnen/corvatsch-/-furtschellas
- Nietzsche-Haus: roughly 200-year-old house, seven summers 1881 and 1883-1888, foundation established 1959, opened to the public 25 August 1960 | VERIFIED | https://nietzschehaus.ch/das-nietzsche-haus-i/geschichte-des-hauses/
- Hotel Waldhaus: Via da Fex 3, Sils Baselgia, 5-star, family-run in the 5th generation, "legendary history spanning over 100 years", 140 rooms, 220 beds, staff of 140, summer season 11.06.2026 to 25.10.2026, winter season 11.12.2026 to 04.04.2027 | VERIFIED | https://www.sils.ch/en/guide/accommodation/hotels/hotel-waldhaus and https://www.waldhaus-sils.ch
- Hotel Edelweiss: Via da Marias 63, Sils Maria, 4-star, 67 Arvenholz rooms, Grand Restaurant with listed Art Nouveau hall ("der in der Schweiz einzigartige, historische und denkmalgeschutzte Jugendstilsaal"), Arvenstube second restaurant, summer season 12.06.2026 to 18.10.2026, winter season 18.12.2026 to 29.03.2027 | VERIFIED | https://www.hotel-edelweiss.ch
- Hotel Chesa Randolina: Via da Baselgia 40, Sils Baselgia, sun terrace overlooking the Sils plain, Restaurant Stuvetta open daily from 6:30 pm, reservations by 12:00 pm essential, Fireplace Hall and Sun Terrace hot meals 12:00 to 14:00 then snack menu and cakes, menu includes cheese and meat fondues, organic veal cutlet, braised veal cheeks, homemade cabbage dumplings | VERIFIED | https://www.sils.ch/en/guide/restaurants-bars/hotel-chesa-randolina-restaurant
- Hotel Fex: Via da Fex 73, Val Fex, historic building from the early days of alpine tourism, 15 rooms, 29 beds, "no access with private car for guests", hotel bus service, summer season 19.06.2026 to 18.10.2026 | VERIFIED | https://www.sils.ch/en/guide/accommodation/hotels/hotel-fex and https://www.hotelfex.ch
- Hotel Schweizerhof: Via da Fex 1, Sils Maria, modern rooms, "tranquil and central location", near the Nietzsche House and Corvatsch | VERIFIED | https://www.faernresorts.com/sils-maria
- Hotel Seraina: Via da Marias 37, Sils Maria, family-run Engadine-style hotel, 6 apartments, Engadiner Stubli restaurant, summer season 12.06.2026 to 27.11.2026 | VERIFIED | https://www.hotel-seraina.ch
