## UNVERIFIED (dropped or excluded from the guide)

- Population 1404 (31 December 2024) and territory 39 km2: sourced only to a bank commune-data page (raiffeisen.ch),
  not a municipal or Federal Statistical Office page read directly. Not used anywhere in the guide.
- Exact AOMC train journey time from Aigle to Champery in minutes: not published on the Region Dents du Midi
  transport page checked; the guide points readers to the TPC timetable instead of stating a time.
- Winter 2026/2027 opening and closing dates for Planachaux / Portes du Soleil at Champery: not published on the
  pages checked; the guide's snapshot.watch and seasons.winter.base flag this explicitly instead of stating dates.
- Whether the national Swiss Travel Pass (as opposed to the regional Portes du Soleil Multi Pass) is honoured
  on the Champery-Croix de Culet cable car: not confirmed on an official page; stated as unconfirmed in the
  practical block rather than assumed either way.
- Hotel Plein Ciel's own operator domain (swissalpshotels.net) was not treated as a primary source in the
  dossier; the guide uses the Region Dents du Midi tourist-office listing as the hotel's url instead, and
  hotelsNote flags this distinction.
- admin-champery.ch (the municipality's own site) could not be read in the research pass (TLS certificate
  error); no fact in the guide depends on it beyond what valais.ch and portesdusoleil.com already confirm.
