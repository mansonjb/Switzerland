# Sources: data/guides/adelboden.ts

Checked 16/09/2026. Every fact below comes from the research dossier `data/research/adelboden.md`,
itself sourced to the municipality of Adelboden (3715.ch), Tourismus Adelboden-Lenk-Kandersteg,
Bergbahnen Adelboden-Lenk, Bergbahnen Engstligenalp, Ski World Cup Adelboden or the hotels' own sites.
No ratings, no reviews, no invented numbers.
