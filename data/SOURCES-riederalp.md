# Sources: data/guides/riederalp.ts

Checked 16/09/2026. Every fact below comes from the research dossier `data/research/riederalp.md`,
itself sourced to Aletsch Arena AG and Aletsch Bahnen AG (aletscharena.ch), Pro Natura Centre Aletsch,
Golfclub Riederalp and the hotels' own sites. No ratings, no reviews, no invented numbers.

## Identity and access

- Altitude 1925 m, canton Valais, car-free, reached from Mörel by cable car, no road at all | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/riederalp
- Municipality also takes in Greich, Goppisberg and Ried-Mörel | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/riederalp
- Temperature difference of roughly 10 degrees between Riederalp and the valley | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/riederalp
- Ride times: Mörel-Greich-Riederalp Mitte 9 min, transfer 10 min; Mörel-Ried-Mörel 5 min; Ried-Mörel-Riederalp West 8 min, transfer 5 min to Hohfluh, 20 min to Moosfluh; Riederalp-Hohfluh 4 min; Riederalp-Moosfluh 7 min | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility/timetable-season-times
- Feeder railway Mörel to Riederalp runs all year, part of the SBB GA and half-fare network; car parks Mörel-Riederalp AG, Stone barn park, Parking Aletsch at Mörel-Filet, EV charging; baggage cart recommended, from CHF 10 per person | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility
- Aletsch Express shuttle links Riederalp and Bettmeralp, from CHF 6.00 per person | VERIFIED | aletscharena.ch webshop listing (research dossier)

## What to do

- Moosfluh Geology Trail: 706 m, 30 minutes, 30 m of climb, easy | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/summer-autumn/hiking
- Riederalp-Riederfurka-Riederhorn loop: 7.44 km, 2 h 45, 375 m climb and descent, medium, passes Villa Cassel | VERIFIED | same hiking page
- Four Aletsch Arena view points, 2227 to 2869 m, platforms over the Great Aletsch Glacier, 20 km long, longest in the Alps, UNESCO Swiss Alps Jungfrau-Aletsch, 40 four-thousand-metre peaks; Riederalp-Hohfluh 4 min, Riederalp-Moosfluh 7 min, Moosfluh runs 6 June to 25 October 2026, 08:30 to 16:30 | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/view-points and the timetable page
- Pro Natura Centre Aletsch, Villa Cassel: first nature conservation centre in Switzerland, opened 1976; built early 20th century by Sir Ernest Cassel; ran as a hotel for decades; open in summer only; carbon neutral since the 2019 refurbishment, air-water heat pump and photovoltaics | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/pro-natura-center-aletsch
- Golfclub Riederalp: 9 holes at 2000 m, highest golf course in Switzerland, reached by cable car, 4000 m peaks and the Aletsch Glacier in view, season 4 June to 18 October 2026 | VERIFIED | https://www.golfclub-riederalp.ch/sites/de/
- Restaurant Alpenrose, Bahnhofstrasse 24: Italian, vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free options, restaurant, pizzeria and umbrella bar | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/service-amenities/food-drink and https://www.alpenrose-riederalp.ch/
- Tea-Room Zurschmitten, Aletschpromenade 70: coffee house | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/service-amenities/food-drink

## UNVERIFIED (not used on the page)

- Hotels beyond Golfhotel Riederhof: the Aletsch Arena tourist office keeps accommodation behind a booking widget with a cookie banner that could not be dismissed without consent, so no plain hotel list could be read. Restaurant Matterhorn and Restaurant Riederfurka are possibly hotels but unconfirmed. artfurrer.ch is flagged by Cloudflare as a suspected phishing site and was not opened. Walliser Spycher (walliser-spycher.ch) is apartments for sale, not lodging, and is not included. Chalet Arolle (zurschmitten.ch) is a holiday rental, not a hotel, and is not included.
- Resident population and tourist beds of Riederalp: no official figure found.
- Individual altitudes of the Hohfluh and Moosfluh stations: only the shared range 2227 to 2869 m for the four view points is published.
- Number of lifts in the Aletsch Arena ski area: not published.
- Length and par of the golf course: not stated on the page read.
- Swiss Travel Pass coverage on the Riederalp lifts and the feeder railway: not stated on an official page beyond the GA/half-fare mention, so no `pass` block is used on this page.
- Exact days of the published maintenance closures (12 to 31 October, 2 to 22 November, 23 November to 11 December 2026): included in the calendar note by month only, since the dossier does not specify which line each closure affects.
