## UNVERIFIED (not published in the guide)

- Resident population and tourist beds: no official figure found for the municipality of Crans-Montana.
- Number of ski lifts: only given by aggregators (not official), not used.
- Precise altitudes of the Cry d'Er and Violettes lift stations: only Chetzeron's own 2112 m and the Plaine Morte restaurant's "3000 m" are published.
- Parking capacity in the resort: not published on the pages checked, so `practical` describes the shuttle network but gives no space count.
- Swiss Travel Pass coverage on the SMC funicular or the mountain lifts: the tourist office names the pass among the tickets to reach the resort but does not state a coverage percentage or fare for any specific lift, so no `pass` block is included in this guide.
- LeCrans Hôtel & Spa and Hôtel Splendide: URLs respond but no facts could be pulled from their own pages in this pass, so neither is in the hotel list.
- The exact 2026 summer opening date of the Crans to Cry d'Er cable car: a search result mentioned 7 June 2026 but it could not be confirmed on an operator page, not used.
