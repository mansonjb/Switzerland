# Sources: data/guides/kandersteg.ts

Checked 16/09/2026. Every fact below comes from the research dossier `data/research/kandersteg.md`,
itself sourced to Tourismus Adelboden-Lenk-Kandersteg, Bergbahnen Oeschinensee, the Sunnbüel cable car,
BLS, Blausee AG, Kandersteg International Scout Centre or the hotels' own sites. No ratings, no
reviews, no invented numbers.

## What to do

- Kandersteg to Oeschinensee: 4.4 km, 1 h 45, +423 m/-15 m, max 1594 m, moderate, dog-friendly | VERIFIED | https://adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/activities/tour/detail/kandersteg-oeschinen-lake
- From the Oeschinen top station the lake is about 30 minutes on foot | VERIFIED | https://adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/orte/poi/detail/oeschinensee-in-kandersteg
- Gasterntal: 8.9 km, 3 h, +432 m/-5 m, max 1631 m, min 1203 m, easy, station to Heimritz, optional bus back from Selden, UNESCO World Heritage area | VERIFIED | https://adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/activities/tour/detail/gasterntal-xi9vkxlc
- Oeschinen gondola: 8-seat cabins, 480 m of climb, about 10 minutes; 750-metre mountain coaster at the top station | VERIFIED | https://www.oeschinensee.ch/en/ and https://adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/orte/poi/detail/gondelbahn-oeschinensee-1
- Sunnbüel cable car: mountain station 1936 m, ride about eight minutes | VERIFIED | https://sunnbuel.ch/en/
- Restaurant Schweizerhof, Bahnhofstrasse 1: Friday-Tuesday 10:00-22:00, kitchen 11:30-16:00 and 18:00-20:30, closed Wed/Thu, "locality and seasonality" | VERIFIED | https://www.schweizerhofkandersteg.ch/
- Landgasthof Ruedihus: about 250 years old, annexe of the Doldenhorn, Swiss specialities | VERIFIED | https://doldenhorn-ruedihus.ch/en/ruedihus/
- Lötschberg car train Kandersteg to Goppenstein in 15 minutes | VERIFIED | https://www.bls.ch/en/fahren/autoverlad/kandersteg-goppenstein
- Blausee: 3717 Blausee-Mitholz, nature park, organic trout farm, hotel with spa, restaurant, shop, open 365 days a year | VERIFIED | https://www.blausee.ch/en/
