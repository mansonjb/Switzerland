## 2. Quick answer — who should sleep in Lauterbrunnen rather than Wengen/Mürren/Grindelwald

Framing built only from verified facts above: Lauterbrunnen sits at valley-floor level (795 m) and **is the only village in this cluster reachable by car**, with a large station-side car park; Wengen and Mürren are car-free and reached only via Wengernalpbahn or Grütschalp cable car + railway. Lauterbrunnen is also the operating hub of the Wengernalpbahn (12 min to Wengen, 42 min to Kleine Scheidegg) and the valley base for Trümmelbach and Staubbach falls. Suggested wording: **"Stay in Lauterbrunnen if you are driving, want a car park directly at the station, or want the valley-floor base with the shortest possible walk to Staubbach Falls and the fastest road/rail links onward to Stechelberg, Grütschalp and Mürren."** Do not claim it is "quieter" or "cheaper" than Wengen/Mürren — no verified source supports either comparison.

## 7. Low season

No official closing date for the village itself (Lauterbrunnen is inhabited year-round and the station operates year-round). The clearest dated low-season signal found: **Trümmelbach Falls closes for the season some time in November** (exact date weather-dependent, not fixed) and **reopens "beginning of April"** — VERIFIED via [truemmelbachfaelle.ch/e/](https://www.truemmelbachfaelle.ch/e/). ## 8. FAQ (5)

1. **Can I drive to Lauterbrunnen?** Yes. Lauterbrunnen is road-accessible and has a large station-side car park (Parkhaus Lauterbrunnen, 940 spaces per jungfrau.ch); Wengen and Mürren beyond it are not reachable by private car.
2. **How do I get to Mürren?** Either by cable car Lauterbrunnen→Grütschalp then rail to Mürren, or by bus to Stechelberg then the Schilthornbahn cable car — note the Grütschalp cable car has a scheduled closure 13 Apr–10 Jul 2026, in which case the Stechelberg route is the alternative (confirm current status before travel).
3. **How long does Trümmelbach Falls stay open?** Daily, start of April to November, 09:00–17:00 (08:30–18:00 in July/August) — exact season-edge dates depend on weather, per the operator.
4. **How do I reach Kleine Scheidegg from Lauterbrunnen?** By the Wengernalpbahn cog railway, 42 minutes, via Wengen (12 minutes to Wengen alone).
5. **Is there a discount to Jungfraujoch with the Swiss Travel Pass?** Sources conflict (20% vs 25%) on the exact percentage in this research pass — confirm at a Jungfrau Railways ticket counter or on jungfrau.ch before buying.

## 2. Quick answer — who should sleep in Interlaken rather than Lauterbrunnen/Grindelwald/Wengen

Built only from verified facts: Interlaken is the regional rail hub with two mainline stations (Ost and West) and is where paragliding lands, where Lake Thun and Lake Brienz boats depart, and where the Harder Kulm funicular starts. Suggested wording: **"Stay in Interlaken if you want the widest choice of direct trains to/from the rest of Switzerland, lake access on both sides of town, and a base that isn't a cable car or cog railway ride from the valley floor."** Do not claim it is more central to the high mountains than Lauterbrunnen/Grindelwald — Lauterbrunnen and Grindelwald are the actual valley-floor bases for the Jungfraujoch railways.

## 8. FAQ (5)

1. **Which station do I arrive at — Ost or West?** Trains from Lauterbrunnen/Grindelwald and the Jungfrau region use Interlaken Ost; Lake Thun boats and the Bern direction use Interlaken West.
2. **Can I see both lakes from Interlaken?** Yes — Lake Thun lies west of town (Interlaken West side) and Lake Brienz lies east (Interlaken Ost side); BLS Schifffahrt operates boats on both, though Lake Brienz cruises stop for winter from 7 December.
3. **How do I get up Harder Kulm?** By the Harderbahn funicular from Interlaken Ost, 8 minutes for 755 m of ascent; season 3 April–29 November 2026, evening rides 18 April–25 October 2026.
4. **How fast is the Eiger Express to Jungfraujoch?** It cuts the Grindelwald Terminal–Eigergletscher leg to 15 minutes; exact total door-to-door time from Interlaken was not confirmed on an official page in this research pass — re-check jungfrau.ch/v-bahn.jungfrau.ch before publishing an exact minute figure.
5. **Does the Swiss Travel Pass cover the trip to Jungfraujoch?** The valley legs to Grindelwald/Wengen are reportedly free, with a discount above that (cited inconsistently at 20% or 25% by different resellers) — confirm the exact figure with Jungfrau Railways before publishing.
