# Sources: "What to do" blocks (`doing` in each guide)

Checked 16/09/2026. Every figure below comes from an operator or a tourist office. Anything that could
not be confirmed on such a page is not published. No ratings, no reviews, no route descriptions.

## Wengen
- Panoramaweg Männlichen to Kleine Scheidegg: 4.5 km, 1 h 20 downhill, 2 h uphill, season mid-June to October.
  VERIFIED, maennlichen.ch/en/summer/experiences/panorama-trail.html
- Wengen to Lauterbrunnen through Wengwald: descent of nearly 500 m, past the Reformed church.
  VERIFIED, wengen.swiss/en/destination/about-wengen/hikes.html
- Männlichen ridge at 2230 m (start of the Panoramaweg). VERIFIED, same wengen.swiss page (2230 m to 2061 m).
- Wengen to the Jungfraujoch in 1 h 10 to 1 h 20: already sourced in SOURCES.md (Jungfrau railways timetable).
- Lauberhorn races 15 to 17 January 2027, Super-G Friday, downhill Saturday, slalom Sunday, "longest downhill
  on the World Cup circuit". VERIFIED, lauberhorn.ch/en/races/
- Restaurants Da Sina and Altitude: listed by the Wengen tourist office, descriptions taken from the listings.
  VERIFIED, wengen.swiss/en/discover/gastronomy.html
- NOT PUBLISHED: per-leg fares, elevation profiles, the Royal Walk (no published time on maennlichen.ch).

## Mürren
- Northface Trail: 2 h 30, medium, 12 information boards, loop from the Schilthorn station back via the
  Allmendhubel funicular. VERIFIED, schilthorn.ch/35/en/Hiking
- Mountain View Trail Allmendhubel to Grütschalp: 2 h, medium. VERIFIED, same page.
- Allmendhubel flower trail: 20 minutes, circular, pushchairs, up to 150 mountain flowers. VERIFIED, same page.
- Piz Gloria at 2970 m, revolving restaurant. VERIFIED, schilthorn.ch
- Birg at 2677 m, Thrill Walk fixed to the vertical rock. VERIFIED, schilthorn.ch/en/Birg/Thrill-Walk
  (length in metres NOT published there, so no figure on the page).
- Sports centre pool 25 by 11 m: already sourced in SOURCES-oberland.md.
- Stägerstübli and Tham's Snack: listed by the Mürren tourist office. VERIFIED, muerren.swiss map listings.

## Grindelwald
- First to Bachalpsee in under an hour from the top station. VERIFIED, jungfrau.ch (Lake Bachalpsee page);
  the 2 h return figure already on the page comes from the same source.
- Eiger Trail Eigergletscher to Alpiglen: 6.09 km, 2 h, 69 m of climb, 773 m of descent, return by
  Wengernalpbahn. VERIFIED, grindelwald.swiss/en/summer/tracks/eiger-trail-eigergletscher-alpiglen-no-36/
- First Cliff Walk: 45 m footbridge. VERIFIED, jungfrau.ch and grindelwald.swiss listings.
- First top station at 2166 m: already sourced in SOURCES-oberland-2.md.
- Pfingstegg cableway 1028 m to 1391 m, toboggan run 736 m. VERIFIED, grindelwald.swiss listings.
  DISCREPANCY: pfingstegg.ch gives 725 m for the run, grindelwald.swiss 736 m. The page attributes the
  736 m figure to Grindelwald tourism rather than stating it flatly.
- Glacier Gorge: rock faces 300 m high, open May to 21 November, walkable web of 170 m2, outing for all
  weathers. VERIFIED, grindelwald.swiss/en/summer/see-and-do/excursions/trip-destinations/glacier-gorge/
- Onkel Tom's Hütte (Italian, Dorfstrasse 194) and Berggasthaus First: listed by Grindelwald tourism. VERIFIED.
- NOT PUBLISHED: gorge entry price (not on the tourism page), Grindelwald to Jungfraujoch fares (still
  unverifiable, see SOURCES-oberland-2.md).

## Lauterbrunnen
- 72 waterfalls in the valley, flat pushchair-friendly walk Lauterbrunnen to Stechelberg, Staubbach called
  one of the highest free-falling waterfalls in Europe, name from "Staub" (dust), Mürrenbach fall 417 m.
  VERIFIED, lauterbrunnen.swiss/en/destination/about-lauterbrunnen.html
- Trümmelbach: ten glacier waterfalls inside the mountain, tunnel lift, up to 20 000 litres a second,
  open early April to early November, 09:00 to 17:00 and 08:30 to 18:00 in July and August, no dogs and
  no children under four. VERIFIED, truemmelbachfaelle.ch
- Grütschalp cable car then mountain train to Mürren, Wengernalpbahn to Wengen in 12 min: already sourced.
- Hotel Oberland (rösti, fondue, Alpine dishes) and Food Point (Turkish and Italian): listed by the
  Lauterbrunnen tourist office. VERIFIED, lauterbrunnen.swiss map listings.
- NOT PUBLISHED: height of the Staubbach fall (no figure on the tourist office page, aggregator figures
  rejected), Trümmelbach entry price, distance of the valley walk.

## Interlaken
- Harder Kulm 1322 m, funicular over a hundred years old from Interlaken Ost, about 10 minutes, operating
  mid-April to end of November, Zwei-Seen-Steg platform, panorama restaurant 5 minutes from the station,
  750 m of climb on foot in 1 to 2 hours. VERIFIED,
  interlaken.swiss/en/experiences/mountains-panoramas/mountain-excursions/harder-kulm/All
  NOTE: jungfrau.ch gives the ride as 8 minutes for 755 m. The page uses the destination site's "about
  10 minutes" wording rather than picking between the two.
- Boats: Lake Thun from Interlaken West, Interlaken to Thun about 2 h 10; Lake Brienz from Interlaken Ost,
  about 1 h 15 one way and 2 h 30 for the round trip, past the Giessbach falls and Iseltwald. VERIFIED,
  interlaken.swiss cruises pages (operator BLS).
- Ballenberg: over 100 historic buildings, over 200 farm animals, open 9 April to 1 November 2026, 10:00 to
  17:00; house 1361, the alpine cheese dairy from Kandersteg built in 1780, cheese made almost daily in a
  copper cauldron, small cheeses called Mutschli made there for 200 years. VERIFIED, ballenberg.ch/en/ and
  ballenberg.ch/en/1361/ and ballenberg.ch/en/themen/handwerk/kaesen/
- St. Beatus Caves: 1 km of the system open to visitors, 8 to 10 °C all year, CHF 20 for adults, north shore
  of Lake Thun at Sundlauenen, reachable by bus and by boat. VERIFIED, beatushoehlen.swiss/en/
- Grand Café Restaurant Schuh: in business since 1818, chocolate and patisserie, on the edge of the
  Höhematte. VERIFIED, interlaken.swiss gastro listing and schuh-interlaken.ch
- Hüsi Bierhaus: Postgasse 3, regional and Swiss cooking, beer garden, 55 seats, dogs allowed, closed on
  Tuesdays. VERIFIED, interlaken.swiss/en/gastro/huesi-bierhaus-und-restaurant
- NOT PUBLISHED: Ballenberg ticket price (not on the page fetched), guided tour length at the caves.
