## Suggested national-level Swiss "theme" hub pages (no scaled-content risk, max 6)

Editorial suggestion only, not researched beyond general knowledge — flagging as such per instructions.

1. **Car-free villages of Switzerland** (Wengen, Zermatt, Saas-Fee, Mürren, Rigi Kaltbad, Braunwald) — travellers actively filter for "no cars" when picking a base, esp. families and older travellers; also a strong differentiator vs generic alpine-village SEO.
2. **Best winter ski bases for non-skiers / mixed groups** — searched by families where one person skis and one doesn't; needs villages with train-accessible viewpoints and sledging, not just piste count.
3. **Best summer hiking bases (no car needed)** — distinct intent from "ski resorts," searched May–Sept; needs villages with a hub-and-spoke cable-car/train network (Wengen, Grindelwald, Zermatt, Mürren).
4. **Switzerland by train — scenic-base villages near panoramic rail routes** (Glacier Express, GoldenPass, Bernina Express stopover towns) — captures the "no rental car" traveller segment, high commercial intent for accommodation near stations.
5. **Lake vs mountain base — where to stay for a first trip to Switzerland** — very common "which region" decision-stage query; a comparison hub reduces need for many thin single-lake/single-mountain pages.
6. **Swiss villages for waterfalls & short easy walks (low-mobility / non-hiker friendly)** — e.g. Lauterbrunnen valley, Giessbach — underserved intent (most hiking content assumes fit hikers), naturally links out to day-trip pages like Trümmelbach without needing a dedicated page per waterfall.
