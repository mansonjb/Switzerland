## Access

- Appenzeller Bahnen connects Appenzell to St. Gallen (via Gossau or Teufen/Trogen) and to the
  Alpstein trailheads | VERIFIED | https://appenzellerbahnen.ch
- Free return travel: book 3 or more consecutive nights, register public-transport arrival in advance,
  Appenzellerland Tourismus sends free 2nd-class tickets plus a P+Rail promo code; accommodation supplies
  the Appenzeller Ferienkarte, needed for the final leg from Altstätten, Gossau, Herisau or St. Gallen,
  where it also serves as the train ticket; GA travelcard holders exempt | VERIFIED |
  https://www.appenzell.ch (page "Gratis Anreise")
- Rebooking or cancelling under 24 hours before travel costs a CHF 20 per-ticket fee | VERIFIED |
  https://www.appenzell.ch (page "Gratis Anreise")
- Appenzeller Ferienkarte: free with 3+ nights booked, over 20 free offers across the region | VERIFIED |
  https://www.appenzell.ch; https://appenzeller-hotels.ch

## What to do

- Landsgemeinde: last Sunday of April, on the Landsgemeindeplatz, first securely documented in 1403
  (probably 1378, undocumented until 1403); service at St. Mauritius church at 09:00, procession to the
  square at 12:00 led by the Musikgesellschaft Harmonie Appenzell; voters are Swiss citizens aged 18 or
  over living in the canton | VERIFIED | https://www.ai.ch (page "Landsgemeinde")
- Hoher Kasten Seilbahn: 8 minutes, Brülisau to 1794 m, views described as reaching six countries,
  barrier-free 360-degree Europa-Rundweg, Alpengarten, described as the only revolving restaurant in
  Eastern Switzerland | VERIFIED | https://hoherkasten.ch
- Hoher Kasten current hours: ascent 08:00-17:00, descent 08:20-17:20, checked 16 September 2026, not
  published as a fixed year-round schedule | VERIFIED (live reading, flagged as such) | https://hoherkasten.ch
- Appenzeller Alpenbitter AG: guided tour and tasting listed among the tourist office's regular public
  activities (e.g. 16 September 2026, 10:00) | VERIFIED | https://www.appenzell.ch (events calendar)
- Schaukäserei Stein: public guided tour listed by the tourist office (e.g. 16 September 2026, 14:00) |
  VERIFIED | https://www.appenzell.ch (events calendar)
- Restaurant Säntis (first floor, Hotel Säntis) and Landsgmendstobe (traditional dining room plus
  terrace), both at Hotel Säntis | VERIFIED | https://appenzeller-hotels.ch
