## 2. Quick-answer positioning

Mürren is the only one of the region's classic car-free balcony villages reached exclusively by cable car/mountain train (no road at all, not even for deliveries by normal vehicle), sits directly under the Schilthornbahn to Piz Gloria, and is described by its own tourism board as the highest permanently inhabited village in Canton Bern — VERIFIED, [muerren.swiss – About Mürren](https://muerren.swiss/en/destination/about-murren.html) / [muerren.swiss – Arrival](https://muerren.swiss/en/destination/arrival.html). Choose Mürren over Wengen/Gimmelwald for: the Schilthorn/Piz Gloria cable car leaving from the village itself, and the Allmendhubel funicular leaving from the village centre (VERIFIED, same sources plus [muerren.swiss – Allmendhubel](https://muerren.swiss/en/map/detail/allmendhubel-4afbeaa1-005f-4e3e-9df4-fae6d8b05bb9.html)). Any comparative ranking ("quieter than Wengen", "best views") is UNVERIFIABLE — no official source ranks the villages against each other; propose neutral wording such as "car-free village reached only by cable car or mountain train, directly below the Schilthornbahn."

## 8. FAQ (Mürren)

1. **Is Mürren really car-free?** Yes — it can only be reached by cable car, mountain train, on foot or by bike from Lauterbrunnen; there is no road access for private cars. VERIFIED, [muerren.swiss – Arrival](https://muerren.swiss/en/destination/arrival.html).
2. **How do I get from Interlaken to Mürren?** Train to Lauterbrunnen, then either the Grütschalp cable car + mountain train (about 20 minutes from Lauterbrunnen) or the Stechelberg bus + Schilthornbahn via Gimmelwald (about 30 minutes); total from Interlaken Ost is 53 minutes. VERIFIED, [muerren.swiss – Arrival](https://muerren.swiss/en/destination/arrival.html).
3. **How much is the Mürren–Schilthorn return trip with the Swiss Travel Pass?** CHF 45.70 versus CHF 91.40 full fare (fares valid to 12 December 2026). VERIFIED, [schilthorn.ch – Timetable & Tariff](https://schilthorn.ch/en/Infos/Timetable__and__Tariff).
4. **What's Mürren's altitude?** Sources differ slightly: schilthorn.ch states 1,638 m; muerren.swiss and jungfrau.ch state 1,650 m. Both VERIFIED on their respective official pages — [schilthorn.ch](https://schilthorn.ch/en/Info/Murren_1638_m_._5374_ft), [jungfrau.ch](https://www.jungfrau.ch/en-gb/muerren/).
5. **Is there a swimming pool in Mürren?** Yes, the Alpine Sports Centre at Höhematte has an indoor 25 x 11 m pool plus spa facilities. VERIFIED, [muerren.swiss – Alpine Sports Centre](https://muerren.swiss/en/destination/about-murren/alpine-sports-centre-murren.html).

## 2. Quick-answer positioning

Grindelwald is the only one of the three (Mürren, Wengen, Grindelwald) that allows cars and has full road access, with two dedicated car parks (Parkhaus Eiger+, 252 spaces; Grindelwald Terminal, 1,000 spaces) — VERIFIED, [jungfrauregion.swiss – Arrival](https://jungfrauregion.swiss/en/destination/about-the-region/arrival.html). It is also the operational hub of the region: Grindelwald Terminal (opened December 2019/2020) houses the Eiger Express gondola base, the Grindelwald–Männlichen gondola base, car parking and the BOB rail terminus, distinct from the older village-centre Grindelwald station — VERIFIED, [grindelwald.swiss – About the region](https://grindelwald.swiss/en/destination/about-the-region.html) and Terminal/Eiger Express sourcing in §1/§3/§4. Choose Grindelwald over Mürren/Wengen for direct car access and the fastest, most direct lift connection toward Jungfraujoch (Eiger Express, Grindelwald Terminal to Jungfraujoch in about 45 minutes total). Any "best for families / best for skiers" ranking is UNVERIFIABLE — no official ranking source found; use neutral wording only.

## 8. FAQ (Grindelwald)

1. **Can I drive to Grindelwald?** Yes — unlike Mürren and Wengen, Grindelwald has normal road access and dedicated parking (Parkhaus Eiger+, 252 spaces; Grindelwald Terminal car park, 1,000 spaces). VERIFIED, [jungfrauregion.swiss – Arrival](https://jungfrauregion.swiss/en/destination/about-the-region/arrival.html).
2. **How long is the train from Interlaken to Grindelwald?** About 29 minutes to Grindelwald Terminal, roughly 34 minutes to the main village station, on the direct Berner Oberland Railway. VERIFIED, [jungfrauregion.swiss – Arrival](https://jungfrauregion.swiss/en/destination/about-the-region/arrival.html).
3. **What's the fastest way from Grindelwald up to the Jungfraujoch, and how long does it take?** The Eiger Express gondola from Grindelwald Terminal to Eigergletscher (15 minutes), then the Jungfrau Railway on to Jungfraujoch; total journey about 45 minutes. VERIFIED, [jungfrau.ch – Grindelwald](https://www.jungfrau.ch/en-gb/grindelwald/).
4. **Is Grindelwald one village or several areas?** It spans Grindelwald Terminal (transport hub for the Eiger Express and the Grindelwald–Männlichen gondola), the village centre/Dorf (station, main street, most hotels) and Grund (lower valley, on the line to Kleine Scheidegg). VERIFIED for the three-part structure; exact inter-distances not all independently confirmed — see §3.
5. **What can I do at Grindelwald First without skiing?** The Firstbahn gondola (25 minutes) reaches First at 2,166 m, gateway to the First Cliff Walk by Tissot (a 45 m footbridge, about 20 minutes to walk, included in the gondola ticket), the First Flyer mountain coaster (800 m, ~10 minutes, up to 84 km/h, runs year-round), and the hike to Bachalpsee lake (about 2 hours return). VERIFIED, [jungfrau.ch – Grindelwald-First](https://www.jungfrau.ch/en-gb/grindelwaldfirst/).
