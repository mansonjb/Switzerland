Villars-sur-Ollon sits on a south-facing natural plateau with views of the Dents du Midi, Grand
Muveran and Mont Blanc. The tourist office describes it as a cosmopolitan village with several
international schools (over 1000 students) and more than 300 ski instructors.
Source: alpesvaudoises.ch/villars

## 2. ACCESS

**By train.** Change at Aigle or Bex. From Aigle, a TPC bus continues to Villars. From Bex, the BVB
(Bex-Villars-Bretaye) train serves Gryon and Villars-sur-Ollon; the Bex-Villars leg takes about
45 minutes. Published overall journey times to Villars: Montreux 1 h, Lausanne 1 h 20, Martigny
1 h 20, Geneva 2 h 05, Paris (Gare de Lyon) 5 h 20 via Lausanne on TGV Lyria. Note: from 16 March to
29 November the R74 mountain leg (Villars-Bretaye) is replaced by bus, and bicycles are not carried on
the substitute bus.
Source: alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/Z49 and alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/P11364

**By car.** Via the A9 motorway, Aigle or Bex/St-Triphon exit, then signs to Ollon-Villars. Driving
times: Geneva 1 h 15, Chamonix 1 h 30, Zermatt 2 h, Munich 5 h 30, Paris 6 h 15. Swiss motorway
vignette (CHF 40, annual) required.
Source: alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/Z49

**By air.** Nearest airports: Sion 60 km, Geneva 120 km, Lyon 240 km, Zurich 250 km, Milan 290 km.
Source: alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/Z49

## 4. SEASONS

**Winter.** Villars is one of three connected stations (with Gryon and Les Diablerets) in the
Villars-Gryon-Diablerets ski area: more than 130 km of pistes, 1200 m to 3000 m altitude range (3000 m
at Glacier 3000, the highest point in canton Vaud), accessed via the Bretaye cogwheel train and the
Roc d'Orsay cable car, with dedicated beginner areas and snow gardens for children.
Source: alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/Z144

**Summer.** Biking, hiking, mountain cart and golf; Les Bains de Villars and the sports centres run
year-round.
Source: alpesvaudoises.ch/villars

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do you reach Villars by train?**
   Change at Aigle (then TPC bus) or Bex (then the BVB train, about 45 minutes to Villars); published
   overall times are Montreux 1h, Lausanne 1h20, Geneva 2h05.
   Source: alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/Z49
2. **How big is the Villars ski area?**
   More than 130 km of pistes across three connected stations (Villars, Gryon, Les Diablerets), from
   1200 m to 3000 m at Glacier 3000, the highest point in canton Vaud.
   Source: alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/Z144
3. **Is the Bretaye mountain train running all year?**
   No: from 16 March to 29 November the R74 leg is replaced by a bus, which does not carry bicycles.
   Source: alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/P11364
4. **Is there a spa in Villars?**
   Yes, Les Bains de Villars at Route du Village 8: hammams, saunas, cold plunge, heated outdoor pool
   and a 25 m indoor lap pool, open 09:00-20:00 (seasonal), adult entry CHF 28.
   Source: alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/P9119
5. **What altitude is Villars at?**
   1300 m, on a south-facing natural plateau with views of the Dents du Midi, Grand Muveran and
   Mont Blanc.
   Source: alpesvaudoises.ch/villars
