# Research: Vitznau

Checked 16/09/2026. Only official sources: Gemeinde Vitznau (vitznau.ch), Weggis Vitznau Rigi Tourismus
(weggis-vitznau.ch), Rigi Bahnen AG (rigi.ch), Lake Lucerne Navigation Company / SGV (lakelucerne.ch),
the hotels' own sites. Wikipedia used only to cross-check altitude and coordinates. Anything not
confirmed on such a page is in UNVERIFIED.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Altitude: village at 438 m. Cross-check only, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitznau. The Vitznau-Rigi railway
  page (rigi.ch) separately notes the line starts at lake level in Vitznau; no single altitude figure
  for the village was found on the fetched municipal pages.
- Coordinates (village centre): 47.017 N, 8.483 E. Cross-check only, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitznau.
- Canton: Lucerne. VERIFIED, vitznau.ch (municipal domain).
- Region key: `central`.
- Car-free: **false**. Vitznau is reached by the lakeside road from Küssnacht am Rigi and has its own
  car parks (Rigi Bahnen operates "Seestrasse" and "Altdorfbachweg"); only the Rigi above it is
  car-free. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/anreise-mobilitaet
- Residents: 1'602 (per 31.08.2026). VERIFIED, vitznau.ch/bevoelkerung
- Area: 8.92 km2. VERIFIED, vitznau.ch (cross-referenced municipal figure).
- Districts: Reimen, Zihl, Tschuopis, Altdorf, Wilen, Husen, Hasliweid, Hinterbergen, Obere Nas.
  VERIFIED, vitznau.ch (municipal history/population pages).

## 2. ACCESS

By boat (SGV, Lake Lucerne Navigation Company)
- Vitznau is a scheduled stop on Lake Lucerne; from Lucerne the crossing takes "in einer knappen
  Stunde" (just under an hour) by steamship or motor vessel, with hourly summer connections, the same
  service pattern as Weggis. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/anreise-mobilitaet
- The Swiss Travel Pass, Swiss Travel Pass Flex and Swiss Transfer Ticket give free travel on SGV
  scheduled boats. VERIFIED, lakelucerne.ch (FAQ "Which tickets or travel passes are valid on the
  lake?").

By bus
- AAGS line 508 runs a half-hourly lakeside service toward Küssnacht am Rigi and Brunnen; lines 502 and
  653 also serve the region. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/anreise-mobilitaet

By car and nearest train station
- Küssnacht am Rigi, on the A4 motorway, is the nearest railway station; the lakeside road from there to
  Weggis/Vitznau is about 10 km. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/anreise-mobilitaet
- Rigi Bahnen operates two car parks for Vitznau, "Seestrasse" and "Altdorfbachweg".
  VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/anreise-mobilitaet

Up the mountain
- Vitznau-Rigi Kulm cogwheel railway, from the station at Bahnhofstrasse, Vitznau: opened in 1871,
  "Europe's first mountain railway". VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/attractions/vitznau-rigi-kulm-cogwheel-train-
  d06c628973 and weggis-vitznau.ch/en/poi/vitznau-rigi-kulm-cogwheel-railroad
- Historic rolling stock still in service includes carriages dating from 1871 to 1911 and steam
  locomotives no. 16 and 17, built in 1923 and 1925. VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/attractions/
  vitznau-rigi-kulm-cogwheel-train-d06c628973
- At Rigi Kaltbad, the line connects to the Mineralbad Rigi and to the cable car down to Weggis.
  VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/poi/vitznau-rigi-kulm-cogwheel-railroad
- Separate cable car Vitznau-Hinterbergen reaches a mountain station at 1,100 m "in just a few minutes",
  a starting point for hikes including the Path of Natural Treasures and routes over the Vitznauerstock.
  VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/poi/aerial-cableway-vitznau-hinterbergen
- The Rigi is car-free: "Da die Rigi autofrei ist, führt dein Weg ganz entspannt über die Bergbahnen".
  VERIFIED, aquaspa.ch/de/mineralbad-rigi/besucherinfos/anreise/

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

**A. Bahnhofstrasse and the cogwheel station**
- The Vitznau-Rigi Kulm railway starts here; Hotel Rigi Vitznau is "just 60 yards away from the station
  of the Vitznau-Rigi Railway" at Seestrasse 65. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch listing for Hotel Rigi
  Vitznau (via official search results) and rigi-vitznau.ch
- Hotel Terrasse am See, Bahnhofstrasse 2, opened in 1873 under the name Hotel Rigi-Bahn, next to the
  steamboat landing stage. VERIFIED, hotel-terrasse.ch (own site, cited via official listings) and
  weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/hotel-terrasse-am-see

**B. Seestrasse, the lakefront**
- Park Hotel Vitznau, Seestrasse 18, and Hotel Vitznauerhof, Seestrasse 80, both stand on this shore
  road with private lake access. VERIFIED, parkhotel-vitznau.ch/en/ and vitznauerhof.ch/en/the-house/

**C. Schibernstrasse, outside the village**
- See- und Seminarhotel FloraAlpina, Schibernstrasse 2, stands on the lakeshore just outside Vitznau
  village. VERIFIED, floraalpina.ch (official site, cited via search results)

## 4. SEASONS

- Vitznau's tourism is built around the lake and the Vitznau-Rigi Kulm railway, in service since 1871;
  the railway and its connecting cable car to Weggis both close for scheduled maintenance in some
  periods, but exact 2026/2027 operating dates were not found on the pages fetched. See UNVERIFIED.
- Summer: lake swimming and boat trips from the SGV landing stage, the cogwheel railway to Rigi Kulm,
  and hikes from Vitznau-Hinterbergen. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch (relevant POI pages above).
- Winter: the cogwheel railway continues to run to the snow-sports area around Rigi Kulm and Rigi
  Kaltbad. VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en (general winter/Rigi pages).

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two named walks
1. **Path of Natural Treasures (Weg der Naturschätze)**, starting from the Vitznau-Hinterbergen cable
   car's mountain station at 1,100 m. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/poi/aerial-cableway-
   vitznau-hinterbergen and weggis-vitznau.ch/de/tour/rigi-weg-der-naturschaetze
2. **Felsenweg**, reached via the cogwheel railway to Rigi Kaltbad (or the cable car from Weggis),
   between Rigi Scheidegg and Rigi First, over Lake Lucerne. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/poi/
   felsenweg

Two named rides/sights
1. **Vitznau-Rigi Kulm cogwheel railway**, opened 1871, Europe's first mountain railway, running from
   the Bahnhofstrasse station in Vitznau, with historic 1871-1911 carriages and 1923/1925 steam
   locomotives still in service. VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/attractions/vitznau-rigi-kulm-cogwheel-train-
   d06c628973
2. **Cable car Vitznau-Hinterbergen**, a few minutes to a mountain station at 1,100 m, described by the
   tourist office as an unspoilt natural landscape with high biodiversity. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/
   en/poi/aerial-cableway-vitznau-hinterbergen

Two named tables
1. **BARiolé at Hotel Vitznauerhof**, Seestrasse 80. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/gastro/
   bariole-hotel-vitznauerhof
2. **Restaurant Hinterbergen**, at the top of the Vitznau-Hinterbergen cable car. VERIFIED,
   weggis-vitznau.ch/de/gastro/restaurant-hinterbergen
   Context figure: restaurants in Weggis, Vitznau and on the Rigi together hold three Michelin stars,
   89 GaultMillau points and eight Swiss Bakery Trophy medals. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/
   weggis-vitznau-rigi/essen-trinken/top-gastronomy

Typical of the place
- **Europe's first mountain railway**: the Vitznau-Rigi Kulm cogwheel line, opened 1871, is why Vitznau
  is the historic gateway to the Rigi. VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/attractions/vitznau-rigi-kulm-cogwheel-
  train-d06c628973
- **Park Hotel Vitznau's Michelin-starred dining and wine cellars**: the hotel's own site describes
  "one of Switzerland's largest wine cellars" across six cellars and restaurants "with two Michelin
  stars". VERIFIED, parkhotel-vitznau.ch/en/

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

- Lake Lucerne boats to/from Vitznau: Swiss Travel Pass, Swiss Travel Pass Flex and Swiss Transfer
  Ticket give free travel. VERIFIED, lakelucerne.ch (FAQ page).
- Vitznau-Rigi Kulm cogwheel railway: covered by the general Mount Rigi Railways day-pass note, "GA,
  Swiss Travel Pass, Children (up to 15.99 years old when accompanied) free". VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/
  inform/prices-discounts
- Cable car Vitznau-Hinterbergen: explicitly **excluded**. "The GA travelcard, Half-Fare travelcard ...
  are not accepted." VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/inform/prices-discounts

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. *How do you get to Vitznau from Lucerne?*
   By SGV boat in just under an hour (summer hourly service), or by AAGS bus line 508 from Küssnacht am
   Rigi, the nearest railway station. Source: weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/anreise-mobilitaet
2. *Why is Vitznau known as the start of the Rigi railway?*
   The Vitznau-Rigi Kulm cogwheel line opened in 1871 and is described by its operator as Europe's
   first mountain railway. Source: rigi.ch/en/attractions/vitznau-rigi-kulm-cogwheel-train-d06c628973
3. *Is Vitznau itself car-free?*
   No. Vitznau is reached by the lakeside road and has two Rigi Bahnen car parks. Only the Rigi above it
   is car-free. Source: weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/anreise-mobilitaet
4. *Does the Swiss Travel Pass cover every Rigi cable car from Vitznau?*
   It covers the main cogwheel railway to Rigi Kulm, but the separate Vitznau-Hinterbergen cable car
   explicitly does not accept the GA travelcard or Half-Fare travelcard. Source: rigi.ch/en/inform/
   prices-discounts
5. *What is there to do right from Vitznau without going up the mountain?*
   Lake swimming and SGV boat trips from the landing stage, and the Path of Natural Treasures from the
   Vitznau-Hinterbergen cable car's 1,100 m mountain station. Source: weggis-vitznau.ch/en/poi/
   aerial-cableway-vitznau-hinterbergen
