# Research: Stoos

Checked 16/09/2026. Only official sources: Stoosbahnen AG (stoos.ch), Gemeinde Morschach (morschach.ch,
the municipality Stoos belongs to), Switzerland Tourism (myswitzerland.com), the hotels' own sites.
Anything not confirmed on an official page is in UNVERIFIED.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Altitude: 1,608 m. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/stoos/ (Switzerland Tourism's own
  weather/altitude data for the resort).
- Administrative status: Stoos lies within the municipality of **Morschach**, canton Schwyz, whose
  official figures are: permanent resident population **1'368** (as of 31 December 2025), municipal
  area **2'344 ha**, lowest point Urnersee at 434 m, highest point **Klingenstock at 1'935 m**.
  VERIFIED, morschach.ch/gemeindeinzahlen
- Canton: Schwyz. VERIFIED, morschach.ch and stoos.ch (contact address "6433 Stoos").
- Region key: `central`.
- Car-free: **true**. Switzerland Tourism describes Stoos as "this car-free mountain village" and
  states "Europe's steepest funicular will take you up to the car-free mountain village in Central
  Switzerland". VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/stoos/. Both hotels confirm the same
  status directly ("car-free mountain village location" / "car-free location accessible via the
  world's steepest funicular railway"). VERIFIED, wellnesshotel-stoos.ch and stoos-lodge.ch

## 2. ACCESS: HOW STOOS IS REACHED (no car)

- **Stoosbahn funicular**, operator Stoosbahnen AG, from Schwyz-Schlattli: opened **December 2017**;
  climbs **744 metres** of elevation over **1,740 metres** of track; ride time **4 to 7 minutes**;
  reaches a maximum gradient of **110% (47.7 degrees)**, which the operator states makes it "die
  steilste Standseilbahn der Welt" (the world's steepest funicular): "Keine andere Standseilbahn der
  Welt ist steiler." VERIFIED, stoos.ch/de/stoosbahn
- **Alternative: Morschach-Stoos cable car**, from Morschach. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/
  destinations/stoos/
- No public road reaches the village; parking is at the valley station in Schwyz-Schlattli (implied by
  the funicular being the described means of arrival). See UNVERIFIED for an explicit parking statement.
- One-night hotel stays give access to the Schwyz Guest Card, with discounts on mountain railways;
  children travel free in summer with the Junior Card. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/
  stoos/

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

**A. By the Stoosbahn mountain station**
- The funicular's mountain station is the arrival point for the whole resort; Wellness Hotel Stoos and
  Stoos Lodge both describe arrival "via the world's steepest funicular railway" directly at the
  village. VERIFIED, wellnesshotel-stoos.ch and stoos-lodge.ch

**B. Toward Fronalpstock**
- Panoramazelt Fronalpstock, a tented/glamping-style accommodation named by Switzerland Tourism among
  Stoos's own lodgings, sits on the Fronalpstock side of the resort.
  VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/stoos/

**C. Stoosseeli, the village lake**
- The Stoosseeli (Stoossee) is reached from the village by "a more leisurely stroll", according to
  Switzerland Tourism. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/stoos/

## 4. SEASONS

- Winter: "35 kilometres of slopes are available for skiing or snowboarding, and there are also
  toboggan runs and winter hiking trails." VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/stoos/
- Summer: hiking, mountain biking and paragliding. VERIFIED, same page.
- Exact 2026/2027 opening/closing dates for the Stoosbahn or the Fronalpstock/Klingenstock lifts are not
  published on the pages fetched. See UNVERIFIED.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two named walks
1. **Klingenstock-Fronalpstock ridge hike**, described by Switzerland Tourism as offering "fantastic
   360-degree panoramic views" over Lake Lucerne, the Mythen, the Rigi and Pilatus.
   VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/stoos/
2. **Walk to the Stoosseeli**, the village lake, described as "a more leisurely stroll" from the
   centre of Stoos. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/stoos/

Two named rides/sights
1. **Stoosbahn funicular**, the world's steepest at a 110% (47.7-degree) maximum gradient, opened
   December 2017, 744 m of ascent over 1,740 m of track in 4-7 minutes.
   VERIFIED, stoos.ch/de/stoosbahn
2. **Fronalpstock**, reached from the village, promoted by the operator for its "360° Panorama" and
   as a destination for families with school-age children. VERIFIED, stoos.ch/de/fronalpstock

Typical of the place
- **A village with no road at all**: every guest, resident and delivery reaches Stoos by cable car or
  the record-breaking funicular. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/stoos/ and
  stoos.ch/de/stoosbahn
- **Klingenstock, at 1,935 m, is the highest point of the municipality of Morschach**, which Stoos
  belongs to. VERIFIED, morschach.ch/gemeindeinzahlen

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. *How do you get to Stoos, since there is no road?*
   By the Stoosbahn funicular from Schwyz-Schlattli (744 m of ascent in 4-7 minutes, opened December
   2017) or by the Morschach-Stoos cable car from Morschach. Source: stoos.ch/de/stoosbahn and
   myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/stoos/
2. *Why is the Stoosbahn famous?*
   It reaches a maximum gradient of 110% (47.7 degrees), which its operator states makes it the
   steepest funicular in the world. Source: stoos.ch/de/stoosbahn
3. *Is Stoos really car-free?*
   Yes, described directly as a car-free mountain village by Switzerland Tourism and by both hotels
   confirmed in this dossier. Source: myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/stoos/
4. *What is there to do at Stoos in winter?*
   35 km of ski and snowboard slopes, plus toboggan runs and winter hiking trails. Source:
   myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/stoos/
5. *Which municipality is Stoos part of?*
   Morschach, canton Schwyz, whose highest point, Klingenstock, reaches 1,935 m. Source:
   morschach.ch/gemeindeinzahlen
