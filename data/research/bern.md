Cloudflare challenge and returns HTTP 403 to every automated request, both WebFetch and curl.** Nothing in

## 2. ACCESS

- Bern is on the main Swiss rail spine (Zurich, Basel, Geneva, Lausanne, Interlaken). **No official journey

  UNVERIFIED, publish nothing.
- City transport: trams and buses are run by **Städtische Verkehrsbetriebe Bern (BERNMOBIL)**; fares are
  set in the **Libero** network and sold via libero-webshop.ch. No zone or price figure published on the
  page fetched. UNVERIFIED.

  bern.com, which is blocked. **Do not publish it without checking by hand.**
- No parking arrangement to describe: Bern is not car-free.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. The old town between the station and the Zytglogge (Bahnhofplatz, Zeughausgasse, Hirschengraben)**
- Hotel Schweizerhof Bern is at **Bahnhofplatz 11**, on the station square. Hotel National is at
  **Hirschengraben 24, two minutes from the main railway station** by its own count. Hotel Kreuz is at
  **Zeughausgasse 41**, in the old town centre. Hotel Bern is **five minutes on foot from the station**.
- This is the arcaded half: the covered arcades run **about six kilometres** through the old town.

**B. The lower old town towards the Nydeggbrücke and the bear park (Gerechtigkeitsgasse, Muristalden)**
- Boutique Hotel Belle Époque is at **Gerechtigkeitsgasse 18**, and states the **Bear Park is 3 minutes
  away on foot**. The sandstone arcades run along the same street.
- Across the **Nydeggbrücke** are the BearPark, the Altes Tramdepot brewery restaurant at
  **Grosser Muristalden 6** and, above them, the Rosengarten.

**C. Below the old town on the Aare, and out at the Engehalbinsel**
- The **Marzili quarter** sits on the Aare directly beneath the old town: the youth hostel is at
  **Weihergasse 4**, with a view of the Federal Palace.
- Further out, **Innere Enge at Engestrasse 54** is in its own grounds, with **Marians Jazzroom** in the
  basement.
- The **Bellevue Palace at Kochergasse 3-5** sits on the edge of the terrace above the Aare, next to the
  Federal Palace.

## 4. SEASONS

**All year**
- **BearPark: open 365 days a year, around the clock**, with staff on site 08:00 to 17:00. It covers about
  **5000 m2**, from the former Bärengraben (bear pit) down to the bank of the Aare.
- **Zentrum Paul Klee: Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 17:00, closed Monday.**
- **Guided tours of the Parliament Building** run Tuesday to Saturday outside sessions:
  **Tuesday to Friday 11:30 and 15:00, Saturday 11:30, 14:00, 15:00 and 16:00**, about **60 minutes**,
  **free of charge**, in German, French, Italian and **English on Saturdays only**. No tours while
  Parliament is sitting. Anyone aged 16 or over needs an ID document at security.
  Note: tours may be restricted during **ceiling lighting refurbishment, January to February 2027**.
  Next **open day: 24 October** (year not stated on the page; confirm before publishing).

**Summer**
- The Aare is the summer of Bern: swimming down the river from the Marzili. Official season dates, prices
  and safety rules are on bern.ch and bern.com, neither of which yielded them here. UNVERIFIED.
- Marians Jazzroom at the Innere Enge runs **September to May, Tuesday to Saturday, two concerts an
  evening, 130 seats**. Founded December 1992.

**Winter**
- Restaurant Rosengarten serves **fondue on the veranda from mid-October to the end of February**.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2, with published figures)**
1. **The arcades of the old town** — **about six kilometres** of covered arcades run through the UNESCO old
   town, with sandstone houses, historic fountains and vaulted cellars holding shops, studios and bars.
   This is the walk; there is no need to invent a route.
2. **Across the Nydeggbrücke to the BearPark and the Rosengarten** — the BearPark covers about **5000 m2**
   and runs from the former bear pit down to the Aare; it is **3 minutes on foot from Gerechtigkeitsgasse**
   and open **round the clock, 365 days a year**. Restaurant Rosengarten, a few steps further up at
   **Alter Aargauerstalden 31b**, has what it calls the finest view over the old town.
   (The walking time from the bridge up to the Rosengarten is not published anywhere I could read, so give
   no figure for that leg.)

**Rides and sights (2, plus the arcades and the bear park the brief asks for)**
1. **The Parliament Building (Bundeshaus)** — free guided tours of about **60 minutes**, Tuesday to
   Saturday outside sessions, in four languages, English on Saturdays. Booking opens at the earliest three
   days ahead for individuals and up to six months ahead for groups of 10 to 30.
2. **Zentrum Paul Klee**, Monument im Fruchtland 3 — holds **the world's largest collection of works by
   Paul Klee**. Founded by **Maurice E. and Martha Müller and the heirs of Paul Klee**. Open Tuesday to
   Sunday 10:00 to 17:00.
3. **Bern Minster** — the **cornerstone was laid in 1421**. Tower height, the number of steps and the entry
   price were not obtainable from the parish site in this check. UNVERIFIED.
4. **The Aare** — Switzerland Tourism calls it turquoise-blue and names swimming and rafting as what people
   do in it. No figures.

**Restaurants (2, from their own sites)**
1. **Altes Tramdepot**, Grosser Muristalden 6, by the bear park — a **brewery restaurant** that brews its
   own **Tram beers** on site, with an ice cream workshop (Eiswerkstatt) and the Zollhaus hotel under the
   same roof. **Restaurant daily 11:00 to 00:30, kitchen to 23:00; ice cream shop daily 10:00 to 21:00.**
2. **Restaurant Rosengarten**, Alter Aargauerstalden 31b — in the Rosengarten park a few steps from the
   bear park, with a terrace and veranda over the old town. Traditional dishes and Mediterranean cooking,
   breakfast, set menus. **Daily 09:00 to 23:30**; fondue on the veranda mid-October to end of February;
   brews its own beer, WIRTBIER, with other local houses.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Why is Bern's old town a World Heritage site?** It has been on the UNESCO list since 1983, for its
   sandstone houses, its fountains and its roughly six kilometres of covered arcades.
   (myswitzerland.com)
2. **Can you visit the Swiss parliament?** Yes, and the guided tour is free. It lasts about an hour and
   runs Tuesday to Saturday when Parliament is not sitting, in English on Saturdays. Anyone 16 or over
   needs an ID document. (parlament.ch)
3. **Where are the bears?** In the BearPark across the Nydeggbrücke, about 5000 m2 running from the old
   bear pit down to the bank of the Aare. It is open 365 days a year, around the clock, with staff on site
   from 08:00 to 17:00. (tierpark-bern.ch)
4. **What is at the Zentrum Paul Klee?** The world's largest collection of works by Paul Klee, at Monument
   im Fruchtland 3, open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 17:00 and closed on Mondays. (zpk.org)
5. **How old is the cathedral?** The cornerstone of Bern Minster was laid in 1421. (bernermuenster.ch)
