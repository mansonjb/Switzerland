# Research dossier: Wildhaus

Checked 16/09/2026. Official sources: Gemeinde Wildhaus-Alt St. Johann (wildhaus-altstjohann.ch),
Ferienregion Toggenburg Tourismus (toggenburg.swiss), Bergbahnen Wildhaus AG (wildhaus.ch), Toggenburg
Bergbahnen AG / Chäserrugg (chaeserrugg.ch), Klangwelt Toggenburg (klangwelt.ch), Switzerland Tourism
(myswitzerland.com), PostAuto, and the hotels' own sites. No ratings, no reviews.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Slug on the sheet: `wildhaus`. Region key `eastern`. Canton: **St. Gallen**, district Toggenburg.
- Wildhaus is a village inside the merged municipality **Wildhaus-Alt St. Johann** (merger 2010), which
  also includes Unterwasser and Alt St. Johann.
- Altitude: **around 1090-1095 m** for the village itself, at the Wildhaus mountain pass. The
  Hirschen hotel's own site states "at the mountain pass at 1090 m"; Wikipedia (cross-check only) gives
  1095 m for the village point. The municipality's own published range runs from 860 m (Starchenbach,
  lowest) to 2502 m (Säntis, highest) (wildhaus-altstjohann.ch, "Zahlen und Fakten").
- Coordinates (Wikipedia, cross-check only): 47.200 / 9.350.
- carFree: **false**.
- Resident population of the whole merged municipality: **2,664** as of 1 January 2026
  (wildhaus-altstjohann.ch). Area of the municipality: **87.53 km2**.
- What defines the place practically: **Wildhaus sits at the pass itself**, with the seven **Churfirsten**
  peaks to the south and the **Alpstein/Säntis** massif to the north; its own gondola goes up to
  **Oberdorf/Gamsalp**, while the region's showcase cable car, **Chäserrugg**, leaves from neighbouring
  Unterwasser, a short Postauto ride away in the same municipality.

## 2. ACCESS

- No railway reaches Wildhaus directly. Reach it by train to **Nesslau-Neu St.Johann or Buchs SG**, then
  **PostAuto line 80.790 (the "Churfirsten-Linie"), Wattwil-Nesslau-Wildhaus-Buchs**, to the stop
  "Wildhaus, Dorf" or "Wildhaus, Lisighaus" (oev-info.ch published PostAuto timetable for line 80.790;
  PostAuto's own Churfirsten-route page). Exact end-to-end minutes from Zurich were not read on an
  official page this session; UNVERIFIED.
- Postauto services from Wildhaus towards Buchs SG were retimed **3 minutes earlier from 27 February 2023**
  specifically to connect with the IR13 and S4 trains towards Sargans
  (wildhaus-altstjohann.ch, municipal news item).
- The valley-station bus stop is about **5 minutes' walk** from the Gamplüt gondola's valley station
  (search-extracted from a gamplüt.ch source; treat the exact minute figure as UNVERIFIED since the page
  itself could not be re-fetched this session).
- No parking arrangement to describe: Wildhaus is not car-free.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. The village centre / Dorf (around the pass)**
- Hotel Sonne (26 rooms) and Hotel Toggenburg (Hauptstrasse 88, 28 rooms) are both in the village centre.
  Hotel Toggenburg is described by its own site as between the Postauto stop "Lisighaus", the Wildhaus
  cable cars (Oberdorf/Gamsalp) and the curling centre.

**B. Munzenrietstrasse / around the Zwingli house**
- Gasthaus Friedegg, a 300-year-old house at **Munzenrietstrasse 2**, is close to the main street; the
  Zwingli birthplace museum is on the same street, at **Munzenrietstrasse 1**.

**C. Lisighaus, towards the Gamplüt gondola**
- Hotel Alpenblick is at **Lisighaus, 9658 Wildhaus** (16 rooms). Reka-Feriendorf Wildhaus, 57 apartments
  across 7 chalet-style buildings, is at **Moosstrasse 28**, at around 1100 m, between the Säntis and the
  Churfirsten.

## 4. SEASONS

**All year**
- Zwingli birthplace museum: open **Christmas to Easter and Whitsun (Pentecost) to mid-October, Tuesday to
  Sunday 14:00-16:00** (huldrych-zwingli-wildhaus.ch, search-extracted; the house itself dates from the
  **mid-15th century**, built by the reformer's grandfather).
- Klangweg Toggenburg (sound trail): open in the snow-free months; no exact opening/closing dates were read
  on an official page this session. UNVERIFIED for exact dates.

**Summer**
- Chäserrugg cable car (from Iltios, reached from Unterwasser) and the Klangweg run in the summer season;
  the current Toggenburg Bergbahnen summer tariff booklet is dated **valid from 1 May 2026**
  (chaeserrugg.ch tariff handbook, search-extracted; exact closing date for the season was not read
  directly).
- Reka-Feriendorf's Circus World programme for children runs **April to October**.

**Winter**
- Wildhaus-Gamsalp and the Chäserrugg/Toggenburg lift network together form what the region's own tourism
  site calls **"Eastern Switzerland's largest winter sports area"** (toggenburg.swiss). Exact season
  opening dates change every year and were not read on an official page for 2026/2027; UNVERIFIED.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2, with published length or time)**
1. **Village square to the Chämmerlitobel gorge** — **about 10 minutes on foot**, the short approach to the
   Thur waterfalls area (myswitzerland.com, "Thur Waterfalls").
2. **Klangweg Toggenburg (sound trail)** — **6 km in total**, in two signed sections: **Alp Sellamatt to
   Iltios, 2.5 km, about 1.5 hours, stroller- and wheelchair-accessible**, and **Iltios to Oberdorf (above
   Wildhaus), 3.5 km, about 3 hours**, past **27 sound installations** by Swiss and international sound
   artists (klangwelt.ch, "Klangweg"). Bergbahnen Wildhaus's own site gives a slightly different count of
   **28 installations**; both figures are the operators' own, so both are recorded here rather than
   silently picking one.

**Rides and sights**
1. **Chäserrugg cable car (Luftseilbahn Iltios-Chäserrugg)** — runs from **Iltios (1339 m)** up to
   **Chäserrugg (2262.4 m)**, the eastern summit of the seven Churfirsten, operated by **Toggenburg
   Bergbahnen AG**, reached from Unterwasser rather than from Wildhaus itself. **Adult return (from
   Unterwasser or Alt St.Johann): CHF 62**; **CHF 13 each way with a GA travelcard or Half Fare Card**
   (search-extracted from chaeserrugg.ch's own pricing pages; treat the exact CHF figures as needing a
   direct re-check before publishing, since the live price page itself returned no extractable table this
   session). UNVERIFIED for the precise current CHF figure, though the order of magnitude is confirmed.
2. **Gamplüt gondola** — Wildhaus's own lift, **wind- and solar-powered**, two pairs of coupled 4-seater
   cabins, from the village up to **Gamplüt**, with views to the Churfirsten, the Schafberg, the Rhine
   Valley and Liechtenstein; daily **09:00-16:30** (gamplüt.ch, search-extracted).
3. **Zwingli birthplace (Geburtshaus Huldrych Zwingli)**, Munzenrietstrasse 1 — the reformer **Huldrych
   Zwingli was born in Wildhaus in 1484**; the house itself was built in the **mid-15th century** by his
   grandfather. Opening dates and hours above.
4. **Thur waterfalls (Thurwasserfälle)** — a two-stage cascade in a gorge close to the village; the
   operator/tourist office describes them as especially striking during snowmelt or after heavy rain
   (myswitzerland.com). A height of 23 m is widely quoted on secondary hiking sites but was not read on an
   official page this session; UNVERIFIED for the exact metre figure.

**Restaurants (2, from the toggenburg.swiss directory)**
1. **Berggasthaus Oberdorf** — at **1230 m**, reachable by chairlift or on foot from Wildhaus
   (toggenburg.swiss).
2. **Gasthaus zum Schäfli** — in the village, listed by the tourist office (toggenburg.swiss).

## 6. HOTELS (6 read and loaded on 16/09/2026)

1. **Hotel Sonne** — https://www.sonne-wildhaus.ch/en/ — village centre
   - 26 rooms, some newly renovated; superior rooms with panoramic balcony
   - Indoor pool; also rents self-catering apartments (Brisi, Selun, Schafberg)
2. **Hotel Toggenburg** — https://hoteltoggenburg.com/ — Hauptstrasse 88
   - 3-star, family-run, 28 non-smoking rooms, on-site pizzeria (Trattoria Paolo)
   - Self check-in from 12:00; about 5 minutes' walk from the Oberdorf/Gamsalp lifts and the curling hall
   - South rooms face the Churfirsten, north rooms face the Schafberg
3. **Hotel Hirschen Wildhaus** — https://www.hirschen-wildhaus.ch/en/ — at the village square/pass
   - 4-star, family-run since 1871, 57 rooms
   - Three dining rooms: Chalet Max (fondue by the fireplace) and Hirschen Stube Gourmet & Beiz
   - At the Wildhaus pass, 1090 m
4. **Hotel Alpenblick** — Lisighaus, 9658 Wildhaus (booking-managed site; no independent first-party
   domain found this session)
   - 16 rooms, double and quadruple configurations
   - Free Wi-Fi throughout
5. **Gasthaus Friedegg (B&B)** — Munzenrietstrasse 2 (listed via toggenburg.swiss / ostschweiz.ch, the
   regional tourist office; no independent first-party domain found this session)
   - 300-year-old house, gently renovated, 15 rooms
   - Every room looks onto the Churfirsten or Alpstein; recreation room, mini gym, library
6. **Reka-Feriendorf Wildhaus** — https://reka.ch/en/rekaholidays/reka-holiday-village-wildhaus —
   Moosstrasse 28
   - 57 self-catering apartments across 7 chalet-style buildings, around 1100 m
   - Indoor pool, Circus World children's programme April-October, free guest card with bus travel and
     30% off the mountain railways

GAPS: **Best Western Hotel Acker** (Lisighaus) is not included: a February 2024 fire destroyed the
six-storey wooden building according to press coverage (Tages-Anzeiger), so it is not treated as a live,
bookable hotel here.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do I get to Wildhaus without a car?** Train to Nesslau-Neu St.Johann or Buchs SG, then PostAuto
   line 80.790 to "Wildhaus, Dorf". (oev-info.ch / PostAuto)
2. **Is Chäserrugg actually in Wildhaus?** No: the Iltios-Chäserrugg cable car leaves from neighbouring
   Unterwasser, in the same merged municipality, a short PostAuto ride from Wildhaus. Wildhaus has its own
   gondola to Gamplüt.
3. **Where was Zwingli born?** In Wildhaus in 1484; the house that stands today, at Munzenrietstrasse 1,
   was built in the mid-15th century by his grandfather and is open as a small museum in the warmer months.
4. **What is the Klangweg?** A 6 km, two-section sound trail with 27 (Klangwelt's figure) or 28
   (Bergbahnen Wildhaus's figure) sound installations, running from Alp Sellamatt via Iltios to Oberdorf
   above Wildhaus.
5. **How high is Wildhaus?** About 1090-1095 m, at the Wildhaus pass itself, between the Churfirsten and
   the Alpstein/Säntis massif.
