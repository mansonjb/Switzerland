# Research: Zug

Checked 16/09/2026. Only official sources: Stadt Zug (stadtzug.ch), Zug Tourismus (zug-tourismus.ch),
the hotels' own sites. Wikipedia used only to cross-check altitude and coordinates. Anything not
confirmed on such a page is in UNVERIFIED.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Altitude: 425 m ü. M. (city centre). VERIFIED, stadtzug.ch/de/ueber-die-stadt-zug/zahlen-fakten/
  in-zahlen. Cross-check: Wikipedia gives the central square at 425 m too, the municipality's lowest
  point at 415 m and its highest, the Zugerberg, at 1,039 m.
- Coordinates (city centre): 47.168 N, 8.517 E. Cross-check only, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zug,_Switzerland.
- Canton: Zug (Zug is the cantonal capital). VERIFIED, stadtzug.ch (municipal domain and pages).
- Region key: `central`.
- Car-free: **false**. Zug is a mainline railway city with a car-accessible centre and parking
  guidance system. VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en/arrival
- Residents: 32'122 permanent residents (2024), of whom 37.5% foreign nationals; 14'341 households
  (2023). VERIFIED, stadtzug.ch/de/ueber-die-stadt-zug/zahlen-fakten/in-zahlen
- Area: 2'165 ha excluding the lake. VERIFIED, same page.
- Economy: 7'826 businesses and 47'525 employees (2023); municipal tax rate 52 (2026).
  VERIFIED, same page.
- Zugerberg, Zug's local mountain, is stated by the tourist office at "1,093 metres", with "a diverse
  array of plant and animal species". VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en/attractions-sightseeing/
  Note the discrepancy with the Wikipedia cross-check of 1,039 m for the municipality's highest point;
  see UNVERIFIED.

## 2. ACCESS

- Zug railway station is "right in the center" of the city, with "international, national, and
  regional train services" stopping daily at frequent intervals. VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en/arrival
- Local public transport is run by Zugerland Verkehrsbetriebe (ZVB); airport connections are by train.
  VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en/arrival
- By car: the city has a parking guidance system directing drivers to available car parks.
  VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en/arrival
- Exact train travel times from Zurich or Lucerne are not published on the tourism pages fetched. See
  UNVERIFIED.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

**A. Bahnhofplatz and the Altstadt**
- Zug Tourism's own office and the railway station are here; the old town is described as having
  "narrow streets" and a historic atmosphere, "especially atmospheric when it's foggy".
  VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en/zug-old-town/
- Hotel Löwen am See, Landsgemeindeplatz 1, sits between the old town and the lakefront.

**B. Lakefront (Zugersee)**
- Hotel Löwen am See stands "am Ufer des wunderschönen Zugersees" (on the shore of Lake Zug), in the
  low-traffic old-town district. VERIFIED, official hotel site (loewen-zug.ch).
- Restaurant Schiff, on the lake with a terrace, is in this sector. VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en/
  restaurants/

**C. Industriestrasse / Metallstrasse, the design-hotel side**
- Park Hotel Zug, Industriestrasse 14, and its sister City Garden Hotel Zug, Metallstrasse 20, stand
  about 70 m apart in this sector, outside the old town. VERIFIED, parkhotel.ch and citygarden.ch
  (official hotel sites).

## 4. SEASONS

- Zug functions as a year-round city break; the tourist office promotes the Zugerberg and other
  viewpoints for "hikes, excursions or relaxing moments" and lists Lake Zug sunset spots as a specific
  draw. VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en/most-beautiful-viewpoints-canton-zug/ and zug-tourismus.ch/en/
  sunset/ (page existence, via navigation).
- Zug Castle hosts a Medieval Festival, dated 19-20 September 2026 on the tourist office's events
  listing. VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en (homepage events reference).

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two named walks
1. **Zugerberg**, Zug's local mountain at 1,093 m according to the tourist office, with a stated
   diversity of plant and animal life. VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en/attractions-sightseeing/
2. **Guggiwiese**, in the city of Zug, "an idyllic picnic spot with a magnificent view of Lake Zug and
   the city". VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en/most-beautiful-viewpoints-canton-zug/

Two named rides/sights
1. **Zytturm**, the clock tower named by the tourist office as one of Zug's "quintessential landmarks",
   alongside the "Chriesi" (dialect for cherry, the local culinary symbol). VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/
   en/attractions-sightseeing/
2. **Theater Casino Zug**, which the tourist office states "will kick off its new season in September
   2026". VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en/attractions-sightseeing/

Two named tables
1. **Restaurant Schiff**: on the lake, with a terrace, bar and seminar room. VERIFIED,
   zug-tourismus.ch/en/restaurants/
2. **Restaurant zur Taube**: fine dining, in the city. VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en/restaurants/

Typical of the place
- **The Zytturm and the Chriesi (cherry)**: the tourist office names the clock tower and the cherry,
  the raw material of Zuger Kirschtorte, together as Zug's defining symbols. VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/
  en/attractions-sightseeing/
- **64 playgrounds across the canton**, a figure the tourist office gives as a sign of the region's
  family orientation. VERIFIED, zug-tourismus.ch/en (homepage).

## 6. HOTELS

1. **Park Hotel Zug**, https://www.parkhotel.ch/ , sector: Industriestrasse.
   - Industriestrasse 14, 4-star, 79 rooms (67 Superior, 6 Junior Suites, 6 Suites with terraces).
   - aigu Restaurant & Bar; indoor pool and sauna; ibex fairstay GOLD-Label and Swisstainable "Leading"
     certification (2025). VERIFIED, parkhotel.ch
2. **City Garden Hotel Zug**, https://www.citygarden.ch/ , sector: Metallstrasse.
   - Metallstrasse 20, 4-star design hotel, 64 Superior rooms (27 m2), 11 Junior Suites (36 m2), 3
     Suites (53 m2).
   - Built entirely from wood, heated and air-conditioned with water from Lake Zug; breakfast in the
     Secret Garden; shares the aigu Restaurant & Bar with Park Hotel Zug, 70 m away.
     VERIFIED, citygarden.ch
3. **Hotel Löwen am See**, sector: Landsgemeindeplatz/lakefront.
   - Landsgemeindeplatz 1, 4-star, on the shore of Lake Zug in the low-traffic old town.
   - Attached brasserie with a French-inspired interior; reception 06:30-23:00.
   - VERIFIED, official hotel site (loewen-zug.ch).

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. *How central is Zug railway station?*
   The tourist office states arrival is "right in the center" of the city. Source: zug-tourismus.ch/
   en/arrival
2. *What is the Zytturm?*
   The clock tower the tourist office names as one of Zug's quintessential landmarks, alongside the
   "Chriesi" (cherry), Zug's culinary symbol. Source: zug-tourismus.ch/en/attractions-sightseeing/
3. *How high is the Zugerberg?*
   The tourist office states 1,093 m; note a Wikipedia cross-check gives 1,039 m for the municipality's
   highest point, so treat the exact figure with care. Source: zug-tourismus.ch/en/
   attractions-sightseeing/
4. *How many people live in Zug?*
   32'122 permanent residents as of 2024. Source: stadtzug.ch/de/ueber-die-stadt-zug/zahlen-fakten/
   in-zahlen
5. *Is Zug on a lake?*
   Yes, Lake Zug (Zugersee); the tourist office lists dedicated sunset viewing spots on the shore.
   Source: zug-tourismus.ch/en (navigation to /en/sunset/).
