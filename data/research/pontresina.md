## 2. ACCESS

Times below are live Swiss public transport timetable connections (SBB / Rhaetian Railway data via the Swiss open transport data service, transport.opendata.ch), queried 16/09/2026 for a Wednesday in January 2027 (20/01/2027, departure around 09:00).

**By train from Zurich**
- Zurich HB 09:07 to Pontresina, Bahnhof 12:29. Total **3 h 22**, 2 changes (IC 3 to Chur/Landquart axis, then RE13 Rhaetian Railway, then the Pontresina branch train).

**By train from Chur**
- Chur 09:01 to Pontresina 11:29. Total **2 h 28**, 2 changes (S12, then RE13, then the local connecting train).

**By train from St. Moritz**
- St. Moritz 09:15 to Pontresina 09:44. Total **29 minutes**, 1 change (regional bus/train connection within the Upper Engadin network).

**By train from Samedan**
- Samedan 09:05 to Pontresina 09:29. Total **24 minutes**, direct, Rhaetian Railway Bernina line (route 607).

**By car**
- "The Upper Engadine is accessible from all directions by the Rhaetian Railway and, via well-maintained Alpine passes, also by private car", with the caveat "it's always advisable to keep an eye on the weather." Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/unser-pontresina/travel-mobility
- Pontresina publishes the Mulin and Rondo car parks and Mobility car-sharing stations; through-traffic and parking exist, so the resort is not car-free. Same source.
- The Rhaetian Railway car shuttle for vehicles operates through the Vereina Tunnel (not at Pontresina itself, but on the same regional network). Same source.

**International connections**
- The tourist office states the Bernina line gives "connections to Livigno (Italy)" and onward access "to Lake Como (Italy), Ticino, and Lugano." Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/unser-pontresina/travel-mobility

**Local guest benefit**
- From the Engadin St. Moritz destination (covers Pontresina, St. Moritz and Sils): "Free use of the cable car from the second night" for guests booked at a participating hotel or apartment, with "up to 10 mountain railways" usable for free, without limit, in summer and autumn. Source: https://www.engadin.ch/en/guide/mountains-railways/mountain-railway-tariffs/summer-tariffs-mountain-railways/mountain-railways-included

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. Village centre, Via Maistra
- Pontresina's main street, Via Maistra, runs through the historic centre and is lined with the resort's oldest hotels and restaurants.
- Hotel Steinbock, Via Maistra 219, "Bereits 1651 eroffnet, gilt der Steinbock als altestes Gasthaus von Pontresina" (opened in 1651, the oldest guesthouse in Pontresina). Source: https://hotelsteinbock.ch
- Grand Hotel Kronenhof, Via Maistra 130, "The Grande Dame since 1848". Source: https://www.kronenhof.com/en/
- Hotel Bernina, Via Maistra 207, "has been welcoming guests since 1900". Source: https://www.hotelbernina.ch/en/

### B. Near the railway station (Fulluns / Sportzentrum area)
- The area around Pontresina, Bahnhof (opened 1908) and the Sunstar hotel at Fulluns 6.
- Sunstar Hotel Pontresina, Fulluns 6, 7504 Pontresina, near the train station and the Sports Centre (Sportzentrum) side of the resort. Source: https://pontresina.sunstar.ch/en/
- Hotel Saratz, Via da la Staziun 2 ("station road"), CH-7504 Pontresina. Source: https://www.saratz.ch/en/

### C. Val Roseg approach (car-free side valley)
- Val Roseg opens south of the village and is closed to public cars; Hotel Restaurant Roseg Gletscher inside the valley is "accessible from Pontresina on foot or by horse-drawn carriage" only. Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/accommodation/hotels/hotel-restaurant-roseg-gletscher
- The valley is the departure point for the walk/ride to the Roseg glacier and is one of the named winter cross-country and summer walking corridors out of the village (see WHAT TO DO).

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **Morteratsch Glacier Trail**, starting at Morteratsch railway station (reached directly by Rhaetian Railway train, on the Bernina line toward Pontresina/Alp Grum). The trail runs along the floor of Val Morteratsch to the tongue of the Morteratsch Glacier, "the third longest glacier in the eastern Alps", with "16 stations all providing information on the Morteratsch Glacier" along the way; the tourist office states the glacier "has retreated around 2.5 kilometres due to man-made climate change" since operations began at the station. Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/activities/outdoor-mountainsports/hiking/hiking-tours/detail/morteratsch-glacier-trail/ (exact km/duration not published on this page, see UNVERIFIED).
2. **Panorama Trail, Muottas Muragl to Alp Languard**, a "7-kilometer trail" described as "almost level", linking the two viewpoints around the Schafberg with the Bernina range in view throughout; Alp Languard itself sits at "2,327 m" and is home to what the tourist office calls "the largest Ibex (Capricorn) Colony in Switzerland". Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/mountains-and-valleys/alp-languard-muottas-muragl

**Two named mountain rides / sights**
1. **Diavolezza cableway**, summit at "2,978 metres above sea level" (the Diavolezza Chalet), with views of "high alpine streams of ice and majestic peaks such as the Piz Palu and the Piz Bernina (at 4049 metres above sea level)"; the Diavolezza Chalet has a "heated outdoor Jacuzzi" at the top. Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/mountains-and-valleys/the-diavolezza-and-the-lagalb
2. **Muottas Muragl funicular**, summit "at the top, at 2,453 metres above sea level"; "the Muottas Muragl funicular railway" runs "with a gradient of up to 55 per cent" and operates "up until 23:00h". Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/mountains-and-valleys/alp-languard-muottas-muragl

**Two named restaurants (listed by the tourist office, no opinion)**
1. **Berghaus Diavolezza**, Rest. Bellavista, at the top of the Diavolezza cableway, 7504 Pontresina. "Located at 3,000 meters elevation"; hot meals "according to the Diavolezza timetable, hot meals 11:00am - 3:30pm"; signature dish listed as "Spaghetti Diavolezza with beef cubes, tomato sauce, cream and brandy"; has a roof terrace with a telescope for watching climbers on Piz Palu. Website https://www.diavolezza.ch. Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/restaurants-bars/eatdrink-berghaus-diavolezza
2. **Hotel Restaurant Roseg Gletscher**, Val Roseg, 7504 Pontresina, reachable "on foot or by horse-drawn carriage" only. Open "12.06.2026 to 18.10.2026" (summer) and "05.12.2026 to 04.04.2027" (winter); the tourist office calls its dessert buffet "renowned throughout the valley". Website https://www.roseg-gletscher.ch. Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/accommodation/hotels/hotel-restaurant-roseg-gletscher

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **Mountain-guide tradition**: "The Mountain Guide Association was founded in 1871" in Pontresina. Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/unser-pontresina/town-profile
- **Bernina Express / Bernina line**: the Rhaetian Railway's regional trains and the Bernina Express "run past the gigantic Morteratsch Glacier" on the line that also serves Pontresina, on the way toward Alp Grum and Tirano (Italy). Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/unser-pontresina/travel-mobility
- **Ibex colony**: Alp Languard is home to "the largest Ibex (Capricorn) Colony in Switzerland". Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/mountains-and-valleys/alp-languard-muottas-muragl
- **Lagalb ski run**: "the steepest serviced downhill run in the Grisons with a gradient of 86%". Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/mountains-and-valleys/the-diavolezza-and-the-lagalb

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

**Not accepted** at the two Pontresina-area mountain railways checked. The Engadin St. Moritz tourist office states explicitly: for Muottas Muragl, "No GA (SBB general abonnement), half-fare cards, Reka checks or Reka cards are accepted at the Engadin St. Moritz Mountains AG points of sale (Corviglia, Muottas Muragl)"; for the Alp Languard chairlift, "Not valid are: Half-fare subscription, GA, Junior Card, Senior Card, Swiss Card, SwissPass". Source: https://www.engadin.ch/en/guide/mountains-railways/mountain-railway-tariffs/summer-tariffs-mountain-railways/summer-tariffs-muottas-muragl-alp-languard-chairlift

What **is** published for Diavolezza: a separate discount, not the Swiss Travel Pass, on the trip itself (see UNVERIFIED for exact wording, not directly confirmed on a Pontresina/RhB page this session).

Rhaetian Railway trains themselves (the line Pontresina sits on): Swiss Travel Pass coverage of the Rhaetian Railway network in general was not confirmed on rhb.ch pages checked this session. **Not published** on the pages checked; do not state a blanket "included" on the page.

What Pontresina does publish instead is its own regional benefit: from the Engadin St. Moritz destination, guests booked at a participating hotel get "free use of the cable car from the second night", with "up to 10 mountain railways" usable without limit in summer and autumn. Source: https://www.engadin.ch/en/guide/mountains-railways/mountain-railway-tariffs/summer-tariffs-mountain-railways/mountain-railways-included

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Is Pontresina car-free?**
   No. The municipality publishes car parks (Mulin, Rondo) and Mobility car-sharing stations; only the side valley Val Roseg is closed to public cars. Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/unser-pontresina/travel-mobility
2. **How far is Pontresina from St. Moritz?**
   About 29 minutes by public transport with one change (St. Moritz 09:15 to Pontresina 09:44 on the timetable checked); Samedan to Pontresina is 24 minutes direct on the Bernina line. Source: Swiss public transport timetable data, queried 16/09/2026.
3. **Can I reach the Morteratsch Glacier without a car?**
   Yes. Morteratsch has its own Rhaetian Railway station on the Bernina line, and the glacier trail starts right at the station. Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/activities/outdoor-mountainsports/hiking/hiking-tours/detail/morteratsch-glacier-trail/
4. **Does the Swiss Travel Pass work on the local mountain railways?**
   No, not at Muottas Muragl or the Alp Languard chairlift; both explicitly exclude the GA travelcard, half-fare card and SwissPass. Source: https://www.engadin.ch/en/guide/mountains-railways/mountain-railway-tariffs/summer-tariffs-mountain-railways/summer-tariffs-muottas-muragl-alp-languard-chairlift
5. **What altitude is Pontresina at?**
   The valley floor sits at 1,800 metres above sea level according to the tourist office; Wikipedia's cross-check figure is 1805 m. Source: https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/unser-pontresina/town-profile
