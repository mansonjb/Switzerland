## 2. ACCESS

**By train/bus from Zurich**
- Zurich HB 09:07 to Sils/Segl Maria, Posta 12:34. Total **3 h 27**, 2 changes (IC 3, then RE13 Rhaetian Railway, then Engadin Bus). Source: Swiss public transport timetable data (transport.opendata.ch), queried 16/09/2026 for 20/01/2027.
- The tourist office states: "Infolge neuer Verbindungen gelangen Sie ab Zurich HB bereits in gut 3 Stunden ins Oberengadin" (thanks to new connections, Zurich HB to the Upper Engadin now takes just over 3 hours). Source: https://www.sils.ch/de/guide/sils-im-engadin/anreise-mobilitaet-vor-ort
- Two rail routes reach St. Moritz (the nearest station to Sils): "von Chur aus via Albulastrecke oder von Landquart via Klosters, Vereinatunnel und Unterengadin"; the Albula and Bernina lines of the Rhaetian Railway have been "seit 2008 zum UNESCO Weltkulturerbe" (UNESCO World Heritage since 2008). From St. Moritz, "der Engadin Bus" continues to Sils. Same source.

**By train/bus from Milan (south)**
- "Von Milano ist Sils und das Oberengadin ebenfalls gut zu erreichen": train along Lake Como to Chiavenna, then PostBus "durch das Bergell und den Malojapass hoch nach Sils". A Friday-to-Sunday PostBus also runs from Lugano via Chiavenna, "reservationspflichtig" (reservation required). Same source.

**By car**
- Via Chur over the Julier or Albula pass ("Der Albulapass ist nur im Sommer offen" - open only in summer); via Davos over the Flueela pass in summer or through the Vereina tunnel in winter; from Italy via the Maloja, Bernina or Ofen passes. Parking at "Parkhaus Segl / Val Fex". Same source.
- Distances published by the tourist office: "Zurich - Sils: 200 km, 3h", "Milano - Sils: 170 km, 3h", "Munchen - Sils: 280 km, 4h 30min", "Basel - Sils: 290 km, 3h 45min". Same source.
- Rhaetian Railway car shuttle through the Vereina tunnel: "In nur 18 Minuten von Klosters/Selfranga nach Sagliains im Unterengadin", with the car and driver able to "bequem im Auto sitzen bleiben" (stay comfortably in the car). Same source.

**By air**
- "Vom Engadin Airport (Samedan) aus konnen Charterfluge zu den internationalen Flughafen von Zurich, Genf, Basel, Milano und Munchen gebucht werden" (charter flights from Engadin Airport, Samedan, to Zurich, Geneva, Basel, Milan and Munich). Same source.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. Sils Maria (the larger, central village)
- The bigger of the two localities, around Via da Marias, where the old Meierei (manor farm) once stood, giving the settlement its name.
- Hotel Edelweiss, Via da Marias 63, CH-7514 Sils-Maria, with "der in der Schweiz einzigartige, historische und denkmalgeschutzte Jugendstilsaal des Grand Restaurants" (Switzerland's unique, historic, listed Art Nouveau hall). Source: https://www.hotel-edelweiss.ch
- Hotel Seraina, Via da Marias 37, 7514 Sils. Family-run "Engadine style" hotel with 6 apartments and the Engadiner Stubli restaurant. Source: https://www.sils.ch/en/guide/accommodation/hotels/hotel-seraina
- The Nietzsche-Haus museum, the roughly 200-year-old house where Friedrich Nietzsche spent seven summers (1881 and 1883-1888), is in the centre of Sils Maria. Source: https://nietzschehaus.ch/das-nietzsche-haus-i/geschichte-des-hauses/

### B. Sils Baselgia (the lakeside hamlet)
- Grew up around the church of San Lurench, first documented in 1356; "Baselgia" derives from "Basilika" (church).
- Hotel Chesa Randolina, Via da Baselgia 40, 7515 Sils, with a "sun terrace overlooking the Sils plain" and the Stuvetta restaurant, "open daily from 6:30 pm". Source: https://www.sils.ch/en/guide/restaurants-bars/hotel-chesa-randolina-restaurant
- Hotel Waldhaus, Via da Fex 3, 7514 Sils, described as a "Majestic grand hotel with a legendary history spanning over 100 years", 5-star, "140 rooms, 220 beds and a staff of 140". Source: https://www.sils.ch/en/guide/accommodation/hotels/hotel-waldhaus

### C. Val Fex (car-free side valley)
- The Fex valley, closed to private cars past a certain point; described by the tourist office as "traffic-calmed" (verkehrsberuhigt).
- Hotel Fex, Via da Fex 73, 7514 Sils, a "historic building from the early days of alpine tourism", 15 rooms, 29 beds, with "no access with private car for guests" and a hotel bus service instead. Source: https://www.sils.ch/en/guide/accommodation/hotels/hotel-fex
- The Furtschellas-Val Fex-Sils and Via Gastronomica walks (see WHAT TO DO) both route through this valley.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks (published tours, Furtschellas-Corvatsch area)**
1. **Senda Ils Lejins, Sils/Furtschellas.** A circular route to six small mountain lakes: "Entdecke auf dieser Rundwanderung zu den 6 Lejins (Romanisch - kleine Seen) die einzigartige hochalpine Seen- und Pflanzenwelt des Oberengadins." Distance "6.05 km", duration "2:32 h", ascent/descent "393 hm / 393 hm", graded "schwer" (hard). Source: https://www.sils.ch/de/guide/berge-bahnen/corvatsch-/-furtschellas
2. **Via Gastronomica: Furtschellas - Val Fex - Sils Maria.** "Genusswanderung mit traumhafter Aussicht" (a scenic walk with a dreamlike view). Distance "6.83 km", duration "2:10 h", ascent/descent "27 hm / 545 hm", graded "mittel" (medium). Same source.

**Two named mountain rides / sights**
1. **Furtschellas-Corvatsch cableway.** The tourist office states the area has "120 Pistenkilometern" and "der hochsten Bergstation des Kantons Graubunden auf 3'303 Metern uber Meer" (the highest mountain station in the canton of Graubunden, at 3,303 m). The Engadin Bus stops at the Furtschellas valley station in both summer and winter; a free village bus (Ortsbus) also runs in winter. Source: https://www.sils.ch/de/guide/berge-bahnen/corvatsch-/-furtschellas
2. **Nietzsche-Haus museum**, in the centre of Sils Maria. The roughly 200-year-old house where the philosopher Friedrich Nietzsche "sieben Sommer verbrachte (1881 und 1883-1888)"; the Stiftung Nietzsche-Haus was founded in 1959 and the museum "offnete" to the public on "25. August 1960", the sixtieth anniversary of Nietzsche's death. Source: https://nietzschehaus.ch/das-nietzsche-haus-i/geschichte-des-hauses/

**Two named restaurants (listed by the tourist office / the establishment's own site, no opinion)**
1. **Restaurant Stuvetta, Hotel Chesa Randolina**, Via da Baselgia 40, 7515 Sils. "Open daily from 6:30 pm. Reservations by 12:00 pm are essential." The Fireplace Hall and Sun Terrace serve "hot meals... from 12:00 to 2:00 pm, followed by a snack menu and homemade cakes". Menu includes "cheese and meat fondues... organic veal cutlet, braised veal cheeks, homemade cabbage dumplings". Source: https://www.sils.ch/en/guide/restaurants-bars/hotel-chesa-randolina-restaurant
2. **Grand Restaurant, Hotel Edelweiss**, Via da Marias 63, 7514 Sils-Maria. Seating in "der in der Schweiz einzigartige, historische und denkmalgeschutzte Jugendstilsaal" (Switzerland's unique, historic, listed Art Nouveau hall); the hotel also runs the Arvenstube as a second restaurant. Source: https://www.hotel-edelweiss.ch

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **Nietzsche's "loveliest corner of the earth"**: the tourist office quotes Friedrich Nietzsche calling Sils the "lieblichsten Winkel der Erde". Source: https://www.sils.ch/de/guide/sils-im-engadin/dorfportrait
- **Grevasalvas**, one of the three Maiensass hamlets belonging to the municipality, high above Lake Sils, was "malerische Kulisse fur die erste Heidi-Verfilmung" (the picturesque backdrop for the first Heidi film). Same source.
- **Fexer Alpenflora**: the alpine flora of the Fex valley has been protected "seit 1954 unter Naturschutz" (under nature protection since 1954). Source: https://www.sils.ch/de/guide/berge-bahnen/corvatsch-/-furtschellas
- **Engadin houses**: many of the "stattlichen Engadiner Hauser" in Sils date from "meist im 16. und 17. Jahrhundert" (mostly the 16th and 17th centuries). Source: https://www.sils.ch/de/guide/sils-im-engadin/dorfportrait

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

**Not published with an exact statement** on sils.ch or the Furtschellas-Corvatsch operator pages checked this session.

What **is** published is a regional guest benefit similar to Pontresina's: hotel listings on the Sils/Engadin tourism site carry the tags "Mountain railways included summer" and "Public transport included summer/winter" for participating hotels (seen on the Hotel Waldhaus and Hotel Schweizerhof listings), and a comparable Engadin-wide hotel listing states: "With a stay of 2 nights or more in summer, you will receive the Engadin card free of charge. Not only does it grant you access to buses and trains, but also to all mountain lifts in the area. ## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Is Sils Maria car-free?**
   Most of the village is described as "praktisch verkehrsfrei" (practically traffic-free), and the Fex valley bars private cars for hotel guests beyond the valley car park, but Sils is not a fully car-free destination overall. Source: https://www.sils.ch/de/guide/sils-im-engadin/dorfportrait
2. **How do I get from St. Moritz to Sils without a car?**
   The Engadin Bus connects St. Moritz, the nearest railway station, to Sils; the timetable checked shows about 24 minutes direct. Source: Swiss public transport timetable data, queried 16/09/2026.
3. **Why is Sils associated with Nietzsche?**
   Friedrich Nietzsche spent seven summers in Sils Maria (1881 and 1883-1888) in the house that is now the Nietzsche-Haus museum, founded as a foundation in 1959 and opened to the public on 25 August 1960. Source: https://nietzschehaus.ch/das-nietzsche-haus-i/geschichte-des-hauses/
4. **What is the difference between Sils Maria and Sils Baselgia?**
   They are the two localities that make up the municipality of Sils: Sils Baselgia grew up around the church of San Lurench (first mentioned in 1356), while Sils Maria, the larger part, took its name from the old Meierei (manor farm) that once stood there. Source: https://www.sils.ch/de/guide/sils-im-engadin/dorfportrait
5. **Can I drive into the Fex valley?**
   Not freely: Hotel Fex, at the end of the valley, states there is "no access with private car for guests", who are moved by a hotel bus service instead; parking is at the Parkhaus Segl / Val Fex at the valley entrance. Source: https://www.sils.ch/en/guide/accommodation/hotels/hotel-fex and https://www.sils.ch/de/guide/sils-im-engadin/anreise-mobilitaet-vor-ort
