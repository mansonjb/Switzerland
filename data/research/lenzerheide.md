## 2. ACCESS

Times from the Swiss national timetable (SBB / PostBus data), queried 16/09/2026.

- **Zürich HB to Lenzerheide: 2 h 02, one change.** Zürich HB 09:07 IC 3 (SBB) to Chur 10:22, then PostBus line 182 from Chur, Postautostation 10:30 to Lenzerheide/Lai, Post 11:09.
- **Chur to Lenzerheide: 39 min**, PostBus line 182, direct from the post bus station on top of Chur railway station.
- There is **no railway station in Lenzerheide**. The bus is the train connection.
- **By car:** "From Zurich and St. Gallen via Sargans to Chur and via Chur-South to Lenzerheide". Car parks at Scalottas, Rothorn, Fadail and Valbella; "You can also charge your electric car at four charging stations"; PayByPhone for parking payment. Source: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Lenzerheide/Region/Getting-there
- **Local buses:** "The Postauto lines from Chur, Davos or Tiefencastel connect Malix, Churwalden, Parpan, Valbella, Lenzerheide" and the neighbouring villages. A free sports bus runs in the winter season, and "The night bus takes you to your destination in comfort every day during the winter season and on Friday and Saturday evenings during the summer season", booked through the PubliCar app. Source: same page.
- **Free transport with a ski ticket (winter):** "During the winter season the following valid ski tickets include free use of public transport (2nd class Rhaetian Railway and Post bus) on between Arosa - Chur - Lenzerheide - Tiefencastel". Day passes, multi-day tickets and season tickets qualify; "Single trips, small offers (for example: single ski lifts, Pradaschier), EarlyBird tickets are not valid for public transport". Also: "The local bus in Arosa and the sport bus in Lenzerheide can be used free of charge". Source: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Ski-Area/Up-to-date/Public-Transport

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. Lenzerheide village centre (Voa Principala, 1476 m)
- The main street, the tourist office, the sports centre and the **Rothornbahn valley station**. Revier Mountain Lodge, Voa Principala 84, is next to the Rothornbahn valley station; Hotel Lenzerhorn, Voa Principala 41, is "in the village center of Lenzerheide".
- The Heidsee route walk (5.5 km) starts at the tourist office. Source: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/tour/winter-hiking/201-heidsee-route/2811138/

### B. By the Heidsee and Valbella (1535 m)
- The lake side and the village at its northern end. The lido is "am linken Seeufer" with boat and chair hire, changing cabins and a kiosk; the opposite shore is a nature conservation zone. Valbella Resort is at Voa Selva 4, 7077 Valbella.
- Sources: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/poi/lake/heidsee/67419160/ and https://www.valbellaresort.ch/en/

### C. Sporz, the terrace above the village
- A hillside hamlet of old Maiensäss barns above Lenzerheide. GUARDA VAL Maiensäss-Hotel, CH-7078 Sporz, publishes its position as **1,600 metres above sea level**, in converted "alte Maiensässen". Sunstar Lenzerheide is at Voa Sporz 8.
- Sources: https://www.guardaval.ch/en/ and https://lenzerheide.sunstar.ch/en

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks (official Arosa Lenzerheide route data)**
1. **Route 201, the Heidsee route.** Published as **5.5 km**, **1 h 15**, 47 m of ascent and 46 m of descent, difficulty "easy", highest point 1,508 m, lowest 1,471 m. Starts and ends at the tourist office in Lenzerheide and goes right round the lake. Published as a winter hiking route. Source: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/tour/winter-hiking/201-heidsee-route/2811138/
2. **Heidsee accessible (barrier-free lake loop).** Published by the same route database as a 2.3 km wheelchair-accessible circuit of the Heidsee. Source: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/tour/rail-trail-cycling/heidsee-accessible/800168067/ (VERIFY the exact figures on that page before publishing, see UNVERIFIED)

**Two named mountain rides / attractions**
1. **Rothornbahn, Lenzerheide to Scharmoin to the Parpaner Rothorn (2,865 m).** The valley station is in the village; Scharmoin is the middle station, with a children's play area and the bike skill centre at the valley station. The top is the highest point of the whole Arosa Lenzerheide area. Sources: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Lenzerheide/Summer/Mountain-experiences and https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Lenzerheide/Summer/Biking
2. **Urdenbahn.** "The Aerial Tramway Urdenbahn connects Arosa with Lenzerheide", "In service since January 2014", "50 mio CHF" invested and "9 months" of construction, part of "the biggest connected skiing area of the Canton of Grisons" with "225 kilometres of slopes". Source: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/poi/gondola-lift/aerial-tramway-urdenbahn/20748324/

**Two named restaurants (no opinion)**
1. **Piz Scalottas**, the summit restaurant on the Lenzerheide side at "2323 metres above everyday life", reached by the Heidbüel/Scalottas chairlift. Serves "excellent breakfast and lunch", fresh baked goods, tarte flambée and a "Scalottas plate with the finest Grisons meat and cheese specialities". Lift 08:30 to 17:00, restaurant service 08:45 to 16:45; closes "in case of very bad weather or strong wind". Source: https://www.pizscalottas.ch/en/the-restaurant
2. **Crap Naros**, the village restaurant of the Guarda Val house, in Lenzerheide; the hotel also runs the Guarda Val restaurant up at Sporz, both described by the house as regional cooking. Source: https://www.guardaval.ch/en/

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **The Heidsee.** Lake in the middle of the resort at about 1,500 m, with the lido "am linken Seeufer" (chair and boat hire, changing cabins, a large kiosk, surfing and sailing), average thermal wind of 3 Beaufort, and a nature conservation zone on the opposite shore. Source: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/poi/lake/heidsee/67419160/
- **Biathlon and cross-country.** Lenzerheide has a biathlon arena and publishes a Nordic map, cross-country schools and the Planoiras folk cross-country race. Source: the Lenzerheide winter pages on arosalenzerheide.swiss.
- **Bike Kingdom.** Lenzerheide is the base of the Bike Kingdom (Arosa, Lenzerheide and Chur), with the STRAIGHTline world cup track. Source: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Lenzerheide/Summer/Biking

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

**Not published.** No official page states what the Swiss Travel Pass covers on the Lenzerheide mountain railways.

What is published, and is the practical equivalent:
- **Winter ski tickets include public transport.** "During the winter season the following valid ski tickets include free use of public transport (2nd class Rhaetian Railway and Post bus) on between Arosa - Chur - Lenzerheide - Tiefencastel", valid "one day before and at the latest one day after" the ticket, "only valid for your arrival/departure to the holiday and the journey from/to the ski area", and winter sports clothing must be worn. Single trips, single lift tickets, Pradaschier offers and EarlyBird tickets do not qualify. Source: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Ski-Area/Up-to-date/Public-Transport
- **The sports bus in Lenzerheide is free** in the winter season, no ticket needed. Same source.
- Note the asymmetry with Arosa: the **Arosa Card does not include the Lenzerheide mountain railways** ("Lenzerheide mountain railways are not included"). Source: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Arosa/Summer/Arosa-Card

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Is there a train to Lenzerheide?** No. Lenzerheide has no railway station. From Chur it is a 39 minute PostBus (line 182) from the post bus station at Chur railway station; Zürich HB to Lenzerheide is 2 h 02 with that one change. Source: Swiss national timetable, checked 16/09/2026.
2. **Lenzerheide or Valbella?** Lenzerheide (1476 m) has the main street, the tourist office, the sports centre and the Rothornbahn valley station. Valbella (1535 m) sits at the other end of the Heidsee. The lake loop between them is published as 5.5 km and 1 h 15. Sources: https://www.vazobervaz.ch/portrait/facts-and-figures/ and https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/tour/winter-hiking/201-heidsee-route/2811138/
3. **Can I ski over to Arosa?** Yes, the Urdenbahn "connects Arosa with Lenzerheide" and has done since January 2014, making the "225 km groomed slopes" of Arosa Lenzerheide one area. Check the maintenance notice: in 2026 it was out of service from 17 August to 18 September. Sources: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/poi/gondola-lift/aerial-tramway-urdenbahn/20748324/ and https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Ski-Area/Skiing-Snowboarding
4. **Does my ski pass cover the bus and train?** In winter, day passes, multi-day tickets and season tickets include free 2nd class Rhaetian Railway and PostBus travel between Arosa, Chur, Lenzerheide and Tiefencastel; single trips and EarlyBird tickets do not. Source: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Ski-Area/Up-to-date/Public-Transport
5. **When does the season start?** The lift company publishes 28 November 2026 for the winter pre-season, full operation 19 December 2026 to 29 March 2027, then partial operation to 11 April 2027; summer full operation 27 June to 18 October 2026. Source: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Ski-Area/Lift-Company/Operating-hours
