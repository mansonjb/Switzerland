## 1. IDENTITY

- Slug on the sheet: `appenzell`. Region key `eastern`. Canton: **Appenzell Innerrhoden** (capital).
- **Altitude check requested by the brief: the sheet says 777 m.** The only official figure found this
  session is different: the **MeteoSwiss weather station in Appenzell is listed at 780 m above sea level**
  (Federal Office of Meteorology and Climatology, cited via the district's Wikipedia page, which is used
  here only to point to the primary MeteoSwiss figure, not as the source itself). 780 m and 777 m are close
  but not identical. This is a genuine, if small, discrepancy: **flag it, do not silently keep 777 m as
  self-evidently correct.** No canton or municipal page read this session states an altitude for the
  village centre itself (Landsgemeindeplatz) as opposed to the weather station or the wider district.
- Coordinates on the sheet: 47.3313 / 9.4094. Not re-verified against a new source this session per the
  brief's instruction to reuse them.
- carFree: **false** (correct; Appenzell is not car-free).
- Population: **canton** Appenzell Innerrhoden has **16,960 residents**, the smallest of any Swiss canton
  by population, and **17,247 hectares**, the second-smallest by area (ai.ch, "Land und Leute"). The
  **Appenzell district** (which includes the village plus Rinkenbach, Kau and Meistersrüte) is reported by
  Wikipedia (cross-check only, sourced there to the Federal Statistical Office) at 5,793 residents as of
  December 2020; no more recent official figure was read directly this session.
- What defines the place practically: **Appenzell is the seat of the Landsgemeinde**, the open-air
  show-of-hands assembly that is still how Appenzell Innerrhoden elects its government and votes on
  cantonal matters, held every year on the **Landsgemeindeplatz** in the village centre.

## 2. ACCESS

- **Appenzeller Bahnen** is the operator of the narrow-gauge line connecting Appenzell to St. Gallen (via
  Gossau or via Teufen/Trogen) and to Wasserau/the Alpstein trailheads (appenzellerbahnen.ch). No exact
  minutes figure from St. Gallen or Zurich was read on an official timetable page this session; UNVERIFIED
  for exact minutes.
- **Free return travel for guests**: book 3+ consecutive nights in a hotel, B&B or apartment in the
  Appenzellerland and register your public-transport arrival in advance; Appenzellerland Tourismus sends
  free 2nd-class train tickets (booked on the most direct route) plus a promo code for the P+Rail
  park-and-rail offer, and the accommodation provider supplies the **Appenzeller Ferienkarte** (needed for
  the final leg of the journey from Altstätten, Gossau, Herisau or St. Gallen, where it also serves as the
  train ticket). Rebooking or cancelling under 24 hours before travel costs a CHF 20 per-ticket fee.
  GA-travelcard holders do not need to apply, since they already travel free (appenzell.ch, "Gratis
  Anreise").
- **Appenzeller Ferienkarte**: free with 3+ nights booked, gives **over 20 free offers** across the region
  (appenzell.ch; appenzeller-hotels.ch).
- **Free parking** is offered by the tourist office in the region (appenzell.ch navigation: "Gratis
  parkieren"); no further detail read this session.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. Landsgemeindeplatz itself**
- Hotel Säntis stands directly on the square: "Wir begrüssen Sie direkt am Landsgemeindeplatz," with rooms
  named after the square itself (Deluxe Doppelzimmer "Landsgemeinde", Junior Suite "Landsgemeinde", and
  the top Attika Suite "Landsgemeinde") looking straight onto it (appenzeller-hotels.ch). Hotel Appenzell
  is also "direkt am historischen Landsgemeindeplatz," the only 3-star superior hotel in the village

**B. Village centre, around Hauptgasse/Hirschengasse**

  village centre alongside Hotel Säntis: "Drei Hotels. Ein Zuhause im Appenzellerland"
  (appenzeller-hotels.ch).

**C. Towards Brülisau and the Hoher Kasten cable car**
- No hotel in Brülisau itself was confirmed on an official page this session; the village is the valley
  station for the Hoher Kasten cable car and is on the Appenzeller Bahnen/Postauto network out of
  Appenzell. UNVERIFIED for named accommodation there.

## 4. SEASONS

**All year**
- **Landsgemeinde**: held every year on the **last Sunday of April**, on the Landsgemeindeplatz. First
  securely documented in **1403** (probably first met in 1378, but undocumented until 1403). The day
  begins with a service at St. Mauritius church at 09:00, the procession to the square at 12:00 led by the
  Musikgesellschaft Harmonie Appenzell playing the "Marcia solenne," and voting by show of hands, with the
  Landessigill (state seal), an original piece from **1518**, and an oath whose wording dates to **1409**
  (ai.ch, "Landsgemeinde").

**Year round, weather permitting**
- **Hoher Kasten cable car**, from Brülisau: **8 minutes to 1794 m**, views reaching **six countries**, a
  barrier-free 360-degree "Europa-Rundweg," an Alpengarten, and the **only revolving restaurant in Eastern
  Switzerland** (hoherkasten.ch). Current operating hours read this session: **ascent 08:00-17:00, descent
  08:20-17:20** (a live, date-specific reading, not a fixed year-round rule; re-check before publishing a
  season-independent claim).

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Can I really get to Appenzell for free?** If you book 3 or more consecutive nights in the region and
   register in advance, Appenzellerland Tourismus sends free 2nd-class return train tickets, and your
   accommodation gives you the Appenzeller Ferienkarte, itself worth over 20 free offers. (appenzell.ch)
2. **What actually happens at the Landsgemeinde?** Once a year, on the last Sunday of April, every
   Swiss-citizen voter over 18 living in the canton gathers on the Landsgemeindeplatz and votes by raising
   their hand — for the cantonal government, the courts, and cantonal business. It has been documented
   since 1403. (ai.ch)
2. **How do I get up to a viewpoint from Appenzell?** The Hoher Kasten cable car from nearby Brülisau
   climbs to 1794 m in 8 minutes, with views into six countries and the only revolving restaurant in
   Eastern Switzerland. (hoherkasten.ch)
3. **Is Appenzell Innerrhoden really Switzerland's smallest canton?** Yes, by population, it's the
   smallest of all 26 (16,960 residents); by area it's the second-smallest. (ai.ch)
4. **Where are the best hotels in the village?** Right on the Landsgemeindeplatz itself: Hotel Säntis and
   Hotel Appenzell both open directly onto the square.
