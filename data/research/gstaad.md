## 2. ACCESS

Gstaad sits on the metre-gauge MOB line (Montreux Oberland Bernois), which "links up the villages of
Château-d'Oex, Rougemont, Saanen, Gstaad, Schönried, Saanenmöser and Zweisimmen" and runs "mainly every
half hour on the main transport axis" (gstaad.ch).

| Leg | Published / timetabled time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bern to Gstaad | 1 h 53, one change | IC/ICE Bern to Spiez, then the GoldenPass Express Spiez to Gstaad |
| Bern to Gstaad (alternative) | 1 h 56, one change | BLS RE1 Bern to Zweisimmen, then MOB Panoramic Zweisimmen to Gstaad |
| Spiez to Gstaad | 1 h 19 direct on the GoldenPass Express; 1 h 23 with a change at Zweisimmen | |
| Interlaken Ost to Gstaad | 1 h 50 direct on the GoldenPass Express | |
| Montreux to Gstaad | 1 h 25 direct on the GoldenPass Express; 1 h 38 direct on the MOB Panoramic | |
| Zweisimmen to Gstaad | about 20 min on the MOB | half-hourly |
| Montreux to Interlaken Ost end to end | "around 3 hours and 15 minutes", 115.34 km (62.43 km metre gauge Montreux to Zweisimmen, 52.91 km standard gauge Zweisimmen to Interlaken), three cantons | VERIFIED, BLS excursion page |

**GoldenPass Express.** Four trains a day run Montreux to Gstaad to Interlaken with no change of train, using
"unique technology with variable-gauge bogies". Three classes: 2nd, 1st and Prestige; reservation is compulsory
in Prestige. VERIFIED, gpx.swiss and gstaad.ch.

**Car and parking.** Gstaad is reached by road. The Col du Pillon road links it to Les Diablerets and the
Rhone valley. Parking capacity and tariffs in the village are not published on gstaad.ch in a fetchable page:
UNVERIFIED, do not put a figure on the page.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

### A. The Promenade, Gstaad village centre
- The shopping street of Gstaad; Hotel Olden is "Directly on the Promenade in Gstaad" and Posthotel Rössli is at
  Promenade 10. VERIFIED, hotelolden.com and posthotelroessli.ch.
- Gstaad railway station (MOB) is at the foot of the Promenade; the GoldenPass Express and the MOB Panoramic both
  call here.
- Hotel Gstaaderhof, Lauenenstrasse 19, states the cable cars are "a mere 10-minute walk away". VERIFIED,
  gstaaderhof.ch.

### B. The slope above the village (Palacestrasse / Oberbort)
- Gstaad Palace stands above the village at Palacestrasse 28. VERIFIED, palace.ch.
- Le Grand Bellevue is in the same upper part of the village, with a 3,000 square metre spa. VERIFIED,
  bellevue-gstaad.ch.
- Distances from these hotels down to the station are not published by the hotels: UNVERIFIED.

### C. The MOB villages: Saanen, Schönried, Saanenmöser
- All three are stops on the same MOB line as Gstaad, half-hourly. VERIFIED, gstaad.ch.
- HUUS Gstaad is at Schönriedstrasse 74, 3792 Saanen-Gstaad. VERIFIED, huusgstaad.com.
- Golfhotel Les Hauts de Gstaad & SPA is at Bahnhofstrasse 7, 3777 Saanenmöser, beside the Saanenmöser station.
  VERIFIED, golfhotel.ch.
- Horneggli (Schönried) and Rinderberg (Zweisimmen) cable cars start from this side of the valley. VERIFIED,
  bergbahnen-gstaad.ch.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

### Two named walks (published figures)
1. **W56 Gstaader Runde**, the village loop: 1 h 20, 4.9 km, rated easy. VERIFIED, gstaad.ch hikes listing.
2. **307 Wispile - Chrine - Lauenensee - Lauenen**: 3 h 50, 13.1 km, 115 m of climb and 786 m of descent,
   starting at the Wispile mountain station and ending at Lake Lauenen, with a post bus back to Gstaad.
   Highest point 1917 m, lowest 1236 m. VERIFIED,
   gstaad.ch/en/plan-book/tour/detail/wispile-chrine-lauenensee

Also published, if a third is wanted: 448 Gstaad to Saanen along the river, 50 min, 3.5 km, easy;
Eggli Panorama Trail 1 h 30, 3.7 km; Cheese Trail Wispile 45 min, 2.0 km; Flower Path Horneggli 1 h, 2.7 km.
VERIFIED, gstaad.ch and bergbahnen-gstaad.ch hiking listings.

### Two named mountain rides
1. **Wispile gondola**, from Gstaad village to the Wispile mountain restaurant at **1907 m**; summer operation
   from 8 June 2026. VERIFIED, gstaad.ch tour page (1907 m) and bergbahnen-gstaad.ch (start date).
   Ride time in minutes: NOT PUBLISHED.
2. **Rinderberg gondola**, from Zweisimmen, daily from 13 June 2026; part of the
   Rinderberg / Saanersloch / Horneggli winter sector. VERIFIED, bergbahnen-gstaad.ch and gstaad.ch.
   Top-station altitude: NOT PUBLISHED on the pages fetched.
Glacier 3000 (from Col du Pillon) is named by Gstaad Saanenland Tourismus as the only glacier ski area in the
Bernese Oberland, but its own operator page was not checked in this pass: use only the "only glacier ski area
in the Bernese Oberland" wording, with gstaad.ch as source.

### Two named restaurants (tourist office listing, no opinion, no points)
1. **Restaurant Rialto / Rialto's Bar**, Gstaad: the listing describes daily specials written on a slate,
   seasonal dishes with an Asian accent, and an all-day terrace. VERIFIED, gstaad.ch POI listing.
2. **Restaurant Sonnenhof**: off the main road between Saanen and Schönried, cooking described as inspired by
   Italian and French cuisine, terrace facing Gstaad. VERIFIED, gstaad.ch POI listing.
NOTE: both listings also carry GaultMillau points. House rule 2 forbids ratings; do not print the points.

### Typical of the place: the MOB panoramic line
The MOB is the reason Gstaad exists as a resort on the map: a metre-gauge line linking Château-d'Oex,
Rougemont, Saanen, Gstaad, Schönried, Saanenmöser and Zweisimmen, half-hourly on the main axis, with the
GoldenPass Express running four times a day straight through to Montreux and Interlaken on variable-gauge
bogies, 115.34 km end to end in about 3 h 15. VERIFIED, gstaad.ch, gpx.swiss, bls.ch.

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

**Not published locally.** gstaad.ch does not state Swiss Travel Pass coverage for the MOB or for the Gstaad
cable cars on the pages checked. What it does publish is the **GstaadCard**, given with an overnight stay:
"public transport in 2nd class is included / free of charge throughout the year during your stay in the Gstaad
Card experience area between Saanenland and Simmental", covering Lenk to Zweisimmen to Gstaad to Rougemont on
the MOB plus the PostBus routes to Turbach, Lauenen and Gsteig. Important exception published by the tourist
office: "The GstaadCard is NOT valid" on the four daily GoldenPass Express trains, and no transport guarantee is
given on them. VERIFIED, gstaad.ch/en/summer/plan-book/getting-there-around/getting-around-the-region

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do I get to Gstaad by train?**
   On the MOB from Zweisimmen or Montreux, half-hourly on the main axis. From Bern it is about 1 h 53 with one
   change, from Spiez 1 h 19 direct on the GoldenPass Express, from Interlaken Ost 1 h 50 direct.
   Sources: gstaad.ch (MOB, half-hourly), official Swiss timetable checked 16/09/2026.

2. **Is Gstaad car-free?**
   No. Gstaad is reached by road and cars come into the village; the Promenade is the pedestrian shopping street.
   Source: gstaad.ch arrival and parking information. (The exact status of the Promenade is not stated in words
   by the tourist office; see UNVERIFIED.)

3. **How big is the Gstaad ski area?**
   Gstaad Saanenland Tourismus publishes "200 kilometres of ski slopes nestled between 1,000 and 3,000 metres
   above sea level", spread over nine winter sports areas, including Glacier 3000, "the only glacier ski area in
   the Bernese Oberland". Source: gstaad.ch/en/winter/experience-enjoy/skiing-snowboarding

4. **What is the GoldenPass Express and does my guest card cover it?**
   It is the through train Montreux to Gstaad to Interlaken, four times a day, running on variable-gauge bogies
   so that no change of train is needed; the whole run is about 3 h 15 for 115.34 km. The GstaadCard is not valid
   on it. Sources: gpx.swiss, bls.ch, gstaad.ch.

5. **When do the Gstaad cable cars open in summer?**
   In 2026 the Wispile gondola from Gstaad ran from 8 June, Rinderberg from Zweisimmen and Horneggli from
   Schönried daily from 13 June, and La Videmanette from Rougemont from 27 June.
   Source: bergbahnen-gstaad.ch/en/summer
