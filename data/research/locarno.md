# Locarno research dossier

Checked 16/09/2026. Official sources only (city, tourist office Ascona-Locarno / Organizzazione turistica
Lago Maggiore e Valli, cableway and funicular operators, SBB timetable, Federal Statistical Office, hotels'
own sites). No ratings, no reviews, no invented numbers. Anything not confirmed sits in UNVERIFIED.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Altitude: 200 m. Cross-checked on Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locarno, infobox elevation 200 m).
  No altitude figure is published by the tourist office or the municipality, so this is a cross-check only.
- Coordinates of the main station (Locarno FFS): 46.172415, 8.801359 (Swiss public transport stop register
  via transport.opendata.ch/v1/locations?query=Locarno).
- Lake landing stage (Locarno, Debarcadero): 46.170293, 8.800503.
- Canton: Ticino.
- Region key: `ticino`.
- Car-free: false. Locarno is a normal Swiss town with roads, public car parks and a motorway feed
  (A2/A13 via Bellinzona). See ACCESS.
- Residents: 16 374 permanent resident population, 31.12.2024, commune 5113 Locarno
  (Federal Statistical Office, STAT-TAB table px-x-0102010000_101).
- Hotel beds: NOT PUBLISHED by the tourist office in a form I could verify. See UNVERIFIED.

## 2. ACCESS

From Zurich (SBB timetable, weekday sample 22/09/2026, queried through the Swiss public timetable API
transport.opendata.ch which serves the SBB timetable):
- Zurich HB 09:05 -> Bellinzona 10:42 (IC 2), Bellinzona 10:49 -> Locarno 11:15 (S 20). 2 h 10, one change.
- Zurich HB 09:33 -> Bellinzona 11:12 (EC 15), Bellinzona 11:19 -> Locarno 11:45 (S 20). 2 h 12, one change.
- Typical pattern: hourly IC/EC to Bellinzona, then the S 20 regional train down the Magadino plain.

From Milan:
- Milano Centrale 09:43 -> Locarno 11:38, direct, no change (RE 80, the Ticino cross-border line). 1 h 55.
  Hourly in the sample (09:43, 10:43, 11:43).

Driving and parking:
- You can drive. The Rhine of the south this is not: Locarno sits on the A13 spur off the A2 Gotthard axis,
  reached through Bellinzona. Swiss motorway vignette CHF 40 is compulsory on national roads
  (rheinfall.ch/en/inform, Rhine Falls FAQ, which states the national rate).
- Public car parks exist in town; the Hotel dell'Angelo's own site names the nearby Parking Castello for
  covered spaces (dellangelo.ch/en).
- Lido Locarno publishes 40 parking spaces at the lido plus 200 in Via Respini
  (ascona-locarno.com, Lido Locarno listing).

Local transport:
- Funicular Locarno - Madonna del Sasso - Orselina from Viale Balli 2, next to the station.
- Bus 1 links Locarno station with Ascona in about 7 to 11 minutes (SBB timetable sample).
- Bus 3 and bus 4 serve the Lido; bus 3 also reaches the Cardada cable car valley station in Orselina
  (cardada.ch/en/cardada-cimetta/cable-car).

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP

Three areas, all inside walking distance of each other.

1. Piazza Grande and the Città Vecchia
   The arcaded square that the tourist office calls "Locarno's pulsating heart", with the old town lanes and
   the Castello Visconteo on its edge (ascona-locarno.com, Locarno page). The open-air screen of the film
   festival stands here in August.
   - Hotel dell'Angelo is at Piazza Grande 1 (ascona-locarno.com restaurant listing, same building).
   - The Castello Visconteo is at Piazza Castello 2, on the edge of the old town.

2. Muralto, around the station and the landing stage
   The lakefront strip east of the old town where the FFS station, the boat pier and most of the lake hotels
   sit. Hotel Geranio au Lac describes itself as on the Locarno-Muralto lakefront "a few steps from the FFS
   station, the landing stage and the centre" (hotelgeranio.ch). Hotel Muralto publishes "5 min from the
   station" (hotelmuralto.ch).
   - Station and landing stage are about 200 m apart (46.1724/8.8014 against 46.1703/8.8005).

3. Orselina and the hillside above town
   The terrace at 395 m reached by the funicular, with the Madonna del Sasso sanctuary and the valley
   station of the Cardada cable car (cardada.ch, funicolarelocarno.ch). Hotel Belvedere Locarno sits on the
   same hill and calls itself "located on the hill which overlooks the city of Locarno"
   (belvedere-locarno.com).
   - Orselina cable car station: 395 m above sea level (cardada.ch).
   - The funicular runs every 15 minutes through the summer season (funicolarelocarno.ch).

## 4. SEASONS

- Locarno Film Festival: 4 to 14 August 2027, screenings on Piazza Grande plus twelve other theatres in
  the city (ascona-locarno.com event listing). The tourist office calls the Piazza Grande screen
  "the most beautiful open-air cinema".
- Locarno Camellia Exhibition: 27th edition, 18 to 22 March 2026, at the Parco delle Camelie, more than
  200 varieties on show, paid entry, 09:30 to 17:30 during the show
  (ascona-locarno.com, Parco delle Camelie listing).
- Parco delle Camelie normal season: 1 March to 30 September 09:00 to 18:00, 1 October to 28 February
  09:00 to 16:45, free entry (same listing).
- Castello Visconteo / archaeological museum: closed for the winter season from November to March; in
  season Tuesday to Sunday and holidays 10:00 to 16:30, Monday closed (ascona-locarno.com listing).
- Funicular: summer timetable April to October, first cars 08:05 then every 15 minutes; July and August
  last departures run past midnight (00:10, and 00:25 only during the Film Festival and Moon and Stars).
  Winter timetable November to March, every 30 minutes; in November the line closes for roughly three
  weeks for the annual inspection (funicolarelocarno.ch, Orari e tariffe).
- Cardada cable car and Cimetta chairlift: each closes about 6 to 8 weeks a year for the mandatory
  inspections (cardada.ch). The published fare table runs 20 December 2025 to 8 November 2026.
- Winterland: the tourist office lists a winter ice rink with igloos in Locarno
  (ascona-locarno.com, Locarno page). Exact dates NOT PUBLISHED there, see UNVERIFIED.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two walks
1. Locarno old town route, Piazza Grande to the Castello Visconteo. The tourist office describes the
   Città Vecchia as boutiques, cafés, palaces and churches with narrow lanes leading to Piazza Grande, and
   the Castello Visconteo standing on the edge of it (ascona-locarno.com, Locarno page; castle listing).
   No published length or time.
2. Cardada - Cima della Trosa - Mergoscia, the ridge walk from the top of the cable car, listed by the
   tourist office in its hiking register (ascona-locarno.com/en/hike/details/Cardada-Cima-della-Trosa-
   Mergoscia/62906659). Published length and time NOT read on the page fetched, see UNVERIFIED.

Two rides
1. Funicolare Locarno - Madonna del Sasso - Orselina. Vintage carriages from 1958, a steel truss bridge over
   the Ramogna and an eleven-arch viaduct up to the sanctuary; intermediate stops Belvedere and Santuario
   Madonna del Sasso on request. Return CHF 8.00 adults, CHF 4.00 children 6 to 16; single CHF 5.40 and
   CHF 2.40; bicycles CHF 2.40; dogs free. Swiss Travel Pass 25 per cent off; Ticino Ticket 20 per cent off
   on returns only; Half-Fare card and GA travelcard not accepted (funicolarelocarno.ch).
2. Cardada cable car and Cimetta chairlift. Cable car inaugurated June 2000, designed by the Ticino
   architect Mario Botta, two cabins for up to 40 people, 5 minutes from Orselina at 395 m to Cardada at
   1340 m. Chairlift inaugurated 1954, 32 two-seater side-facing chairs, 7 minutes from Cardada to Cimetta
   at 1670 m, described by the operator as the only side-seat chairlift in Switzerland. Fares valid
   20 December 2025 to 8 November 2026: Cardada return CHF 32.00, Cardada and Cimetta return CHF 39.00,
   Cimetta return alone CHF 12.00 (cardada.ch/en/timetable-fares/fares and the cable car and chairlift pages).

Two tables
1. Ristorante Dell'Angelo, Piazza Grande 1, 6600 Locarno. Listed by the tourist office; open 11:30 to 14:30
   and 17:00 to 23:00 daily; animals allowed, free Wi-Fi, private parking
   (ascona-locarno.com/en/restaurants/details/Ristorante-Dell-Angelo/75150). The hotel's own site describes
   a restaurant-pizzeria with wood-fired pizzas facing Piazza Grande (dellangelo.ch/en).
2. Grand Café Al Porto is in Bellinzona, not Locarno. For the second Locarno table the tourist office lists
   Ristorante La Palma au Lac inside the lakefront hotel of that name
   (ascona-locarno.com/en/restaurants/details/Ristorante-La-Palma-au-Lac/75663). Opening hours NOT read,
   see UNVERIFIED.

Typical, with a published fact
- Parco delle Camelie: opened 2005 for the International Camellia Society World Congress, 500 camellias
  planted at the start, today 1100 catalogued varieties including about forty species and ten perfumed
  hybrids, plus 70 unidentified camellias and 130 double camellias forming a hedge. Holder of the
  International Camellia Society "Garden of Excellence" designation and part of the Gardens of Switzerland
  network. Free entry (ascona-locarno.com, Parco delle Camelie listing).
- Lido Locarno: outdoor 50 m pool at 27 to 28 degrees with 24 degrees guaranteed, outdoor leisure pool and
  a diving tank at 27 to 28 degrees, Via Respini 11, buses 3 and 4 (ascona-locarno.com, Lido Locarno listing).
- Castello Visconteo: built in the 12th century, taken by the Visconti of Milan in 1342 from both the land
  and the lake side, first in Confederate hands in 1503; only a fifth of the original structure survives,
  dating from the 15th and 16th centuries (ascona-locarno.com castle listing).

## 6. HOTELS

1. Hotel Belvedere Locarno - https://www.belvedere-locarno.com/en - Orselina hillside above the old town.
   Own site: on the hill overlooking the city with a view of the mountains and Lake Maggiore; describes
   itself as the only 4-star-superior hotel in Locarno; Oasi Belvedere spa, wellness and beauty.
2. Hotel dell'Angelo - https://www.dellangelo.ch/en - Piazza Grande.
   Own site: on Piazza Grande, a few minutes from the train station and the lake; three sets of rooms
   (the historic hotel, the EasyRooms next door, the Residenze across the street); two rooftop terraces and
   covered parking in the nearby Parking Castello; open all year.
3. Hotel Millennium - https://millennium-hotel.ch/ - Muralto lakefront, opposite the Debarcadero.
   Own site: family-run, directly on the shore of Lake Maggiore opposite the landing stage, a few steps from
   the old town and Piazza Grande; public transport, the Madonna del Sasso funicular and the railway station
   close by.
4. Hotel Geranio au Lac - https://www.hotelgeranio.ch/ - Locarno-Muralto lakefront.
   Own site: on the Locarno-Muralto lakefront a few steps from the FFS station, the landing stage and
   Piazza Grande; all rooms air-conditioned with a balcony; family rooms available.
5. Hotel Muralto - https://www.hotelmuralto.ch/ - Muralto, by the station.
   Own site: 5 minutes from the station; air conditioning, Wi-Fi, parking, Ticino Ticket; Queen rooms and
   Comfort rooms with private balcony.
6. La Palma au Lac - https://www.lapalmaaulac.com/ - Muralto lakefront (Viale Verbano).
   Own site: hotel directly on the lake at Locarno with lake-view rooms and suites, a wellness and spa area,
   its own restaurant and pizzeria, and seminar rooms.
7. Boutique-Hotel Remorino - https://www.remorino.ch/en - Minusio, east of Muralto.
   Own site: four-star garni boutique hotel, member of Private Selection Hotels, 24 comfort rooms, run by
   the Kirchlechner family.

No prices here; prices come from data/prices/*.json only.

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

Officially stated for Locarno:
- Funicolare Locarno - Madonna del Sasso: Swiss Travel Pass gives 25 per cent off. Half-Fare card and GA
  travelcard are explicitly not accepted (funicolarelocarno.ch, Orari e tariffe).
- Cardada / Cimetta: the operator's fare table has a dedicated column "SBB Half Fare, GA, Day Pass, Swiss
  Travel Pass" at half the standard fare. Cardada return CHF 16.00 instead of CHF 32.00; Cardada and Cimetta
  return CHF 19.50 instead of CHF 39.00; Cimetta return CHF 6.00 instead of CHF 12.00 (cardada.ch).
- Trains and the Ticino buses are ordinary public transport, included in the pass under the general rule
  ("unlimited travel by train, bus and boat", Switzerland Tourism, myswitzerland.com Swiss Travel Pass page).
- Castello Visconteo museum admission with the pass: not published on the tourist office listing.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. How long does it take to get from Zurich to Locarno by train?
   About 2 h 10 with one change at Bellinzona: IC 2 Zurich HB 09:05 to Bellinzona 10:42, then S 20 to
   Locarno 11:15. Source: SBB timetable via transport.opendata.ch, sample 22/09/2026.

2. Is there a direct train from Milan?
   Yes. The RE 80 runs Milano Centrale to Locarno without a change in 1 h 55 (09:43 to 11:38 in the sample).
   Source: SBB timetable via transport.opendata.ch.

3. When is the Locarno Film Festival?
   4 to 14 August 2027, on Piazza Grande and in twelve other theatres in the city.
   Source: ascona-locarno.com/en/events/details/locarno-film-festival-2026/10056.

4. How do I get up to Cardada, and how high is it?
   Funicular from Locarno to Orselina, then the cable car: 5 minutes from Orselina at 395 m to Cardada at
   1340 m, and a further 7 minutes by chairlift to Cimetta at 1670 m.
   Source: cardada.ch/en/cardada-cimetta/cable-car and /chairlift.

5. Does the Swiss Travel Pass cover the Madonna del Sasso funicular?
   Not fully. The operator publishes a 25 per cent reduction for the Swiss Travel Pass, and states that the
   Half-Fare card and the GA travelcard are not accepted.
   Source: funicolarelocarno.ch, Orari e tariffe.
