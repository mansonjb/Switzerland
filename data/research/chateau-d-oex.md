Chateau-d'Oex markets itself as the "Capitale suisse des montgolfieres" (Swiss capital of hot air
balloons), built around the annual Festival International de Ballons, and as one of the birthplaces
of Swiss paper-cutting (papier decoupe) art, alongside L'Etivaz AOP cheese, made in wood-fired alpine
chalets in the surrounding hills.
Source: pays-denhaut.ch/chateau-doex

## 2. ACCESS

**By train (MOB).** The MOB (Montreux-Oberland Bernois) line runs Montreux to Zweisimmen through the
Pays-d'Enhaut, called by the tourist office "one of Switzerland's most beautiful railway journeys."
Published journey times to Chateau-d'Oex:
- From Montreux: 1 h 10
- From Lausanne: 1 h 40
- From Geneva: 2 h 40
Alternative approaches: TPC buses from Aigle, or TPF trains from Gruyere connecting to the MOB.
Source: pays-denhaut.ch/venir-au-pays-denhaut/

**By car.** From Lausanne, about 2 hours via the A9/A12 motorway, exiting at Bulle toward
Chateau-d'Oex. From Sion, via the A9 to the Aigle exit, then over the Col des Mosses. A Swiss
motorway vignette (CHF 40, annual) is required.
Source: pays-denhaut.ch/venir-au-pays-denhaut/

**Car sharing.** A Mobility electric car-share vehicle is stationed at Chateau-d'Oex railway station.
Source: pays-denhaut.ch/venir-au-pays-denhaut/

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP

Only two hotels were independently confirmed on official pages in this pass, both in the village
centre, a few minutes' walk from the station:

**Hotel de Ville** sits in the centre of the village, a few minutes' walk from the station; 3-star,
with its own restaurant Le Bistrot and the Caribou Bar Lounge with a terrace
(pays-denhaut.ch/points-d-interet/hotel-de-ville-chateau-doex/). **Hotel Roc et Neige**, at Route des
Monnaires 46, is a 3-star, 60-room mountain-chalet-style hotel within walking distance of the village
centre and station, with a garden beside a mountain stream
(pays-denhaut.ch/points-d-interet/roc-et-neige/).

## 4. SEASONS

**Winter.** Chateau-d'Oex is a winter sports base with its own local slopes plus access to the wider
Pays-d'Enhaut/Glacier 3000 area; the Festival International de Ballons is a winter fixture (see
section 5). Exact ski-area figures for Chateau-d'Oex itself were not found on an official page in
this pass.

**Summer.** Hiking, the Sarine river (rafting, kayaking, canyoning in spring snowmelt conditions), and
the L'Etivaz cheese caves (open by reservation, see section 5) are the main summer draws.
Source: pays-denhaut.ch/sports-et-aventures/ and pays-denhaut.ch/points-d-interet/caves-a-fromage-de-letivaz/

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks.**
The tourist office describes "a vast network of marked trails, suited to all levels" between alpine
pastures, forests and panoramas, navigable via its own Trail Connect app (maps, profiles, GPX,
opening information), but no specific named route with a distance or duration was found on an
official page reached in this pass. See UNVERIFIED.
Source: pays-denhaut.ch/sports-et-aventures/

**Sights (2).**
1. **Musee du Pays-d'Enhaut, Centre suisse du papier decoupe.** Shows everyday mountain life through
   furniture and tools, and holds an exceptional collection of paper-cuttings (decoupage), a local art
   form. Open through the year, closed in November. Accessible by lift; on-site shop.
   Source: pays-denhaut.ch/points-d-interet/musee-du-pays-denhaut/
2. **Espace Ballon.** Museum on the history of ballooning from the Montgolfier brothers to Bertrand
   Piccard, including the Breitling Orbiter capsule from the first round-the-world balloon flight
   attempt and the 1999 successful circumnavigation. Includes a 4D "balloon flight" film; a VR flight
   simulator costs extra. Open all year; visit takes about an hour; guided tours by reservation.
   Source: pays-denhaut.ch/points-d-interet/espace-ballon/

**Beyond the village (day trip).**
**Glacier 3000**, at Col du Pillon (Route du Pillon 253, Les Diablerets), reaches 3000 m and features
the Peak Walk by Tissot suspension bridge (described by the operator as the only one in the world
linking two mountain peaks), an Alpine Coaster, a glacier walk and the Lake Blue Ice. Combined public
transport tickets are available via the Mobilis network.
Source: glacier3000.ch/en (full treatment in the Les Diablerets dossier)

**L'Etivaz cheese caves.** L'Etivaz AOP is made in more than 130 alpine chalets between 1000 and
2000 m. The caves at L'Etivaz village show the maturing process (film plus guided tour) of about
20,000 wheels, matured at least 135 days, with a tasting of 5 regional cheeses. Open daily 10:00-14:00
by reservation, minimum visit 1 hour; free parking; reachable by PostBus (stop "Maison de l'Etivaz").
Source: pays-denhaut.ch/points-d-interet/caves-a-fromage-de-letivaz/

**Restaurants.**
**Le Bistrot**, at Hotel de Ville in the village centre, is the confirmed restaurant reached on an
official page in this pass. A second named restaurant was not confirmed on an official page (the
Buffet de la Gare page guessed did not resolve); see UNVERIFIED.
Source: pays-denhaut.ch/points-d-interet/hotel-de-ville-chateau-doex/

**Typical to the place.**
**Festival International de Ballons.** Held annually in Chateau-d'Oex for nearly fifty years. The next
edition runs **23-31 January 2027**, nine days of balloon flights, tethered rides, demonstrations,
food, music and meeting the pilots.
Source: festivaldeballons.ch/fr

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

**Not published for the national pass; a separate regional guest card exists.** The Pass d'Enhaut,
free and automatic from the first night in a regional accommodation, gives free public transport
(the MOB between La Tine and Zweisimmen, TPC buses between Chateau-d'Oex and Col des Mosses, and
seasonal PostBus routes, excluding special routes/bikes/animals), plus discounted or free activities:
discounted outdoor pools (summer), ice skating (CHF 5, winter), snowshoe rental (buy one get one free,
winter), 10% off hot air balloon flights year-round, CHF 10 cinema tickets, and discounted entry to
Espace Ballon and the Musee du Papier decoupe. Issued as a digital PDF/wallet pass or printable paper
card. Whether the national Swiss Travel Pass is separately honoured on the MOB or TPC lines was not
confirmed on an official page in this pass.
Source: pays-denhaut.ch/pass-denhaut/

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do you reach Chateau-d'Oex by train?**
   By the MOB line from Montreux (1h10), Lausanne (1h40) or Geneva (2h40), through the Pays-d'Enhaut.
   Source: pays-denhaut.ch/venir-au-pays-denhaut/
2. **What do overnight guests get for free in the Pays-d'Enhaut?**
   The Pass d'Enhaut: free public transport on the MOB (La Tine-Zweisimmen), TPC buses and seasonal
   PostBus routes, plus discounted activities from ice skating to hot air balloon flights.
   Source: pays-denhaut.ch/pass-denhaut/
3. **When is the balloon festival?**
   The Festival International de Ballons runs 23-31 January 2027, its nearly 50th consecutive year in
   Chateau-d'Oex.
   Source: festivaldeballons.ch/fr
4. **Can you visit where L'Etivaz cheese is made?**
   Yes, at the cheese caves in L'Etivaz village: a film, a guided tour of the maturing galleries
   (about 20,000 wheels, matured at least 135 days) and a tasting of 5 cheeses, open daily 10:00-14:00
   by reservation.
   Source: pays-denhaut.ch/points-d-interet/caves-a-fromage-de-letivaz/
5. **Is Glacier 3000 reachable from Chateau-d'Oex?**
   It sits at Col du Pillon near Les Diablerets, a separate day-trip destination from Chateau-d'Oex,
   reachable via public transport with Mobilis combined tickets.
   Source: glacier3000.ch/en
