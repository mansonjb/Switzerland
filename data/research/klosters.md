Klosters is in two parts, **Klosters Platz** (the main village, 1179 m) and **Klosters Dorf** (1124 m, 2 km down the valley), each with its own Rhaetian Railway station.

## 2. ACCESS

Times from the Swiss national timetable (SBB / Rhaetian Railway / PostBus data), queried 16/09/2026 for a mid-January 2027 weekday.

- **Zürich HB to Klosters Platz: 1 h 51, one change.** Zürich HB 09:07 IC 3 (SBB) to Landquart 10:11, then Rhaetian Railway RE 13 Landquart 10:20 to Klosters Platz 10:58.
- **Chur to Klosters Platz: 57 min, one change** at Landquart.
- **Landquart to Klosters Platz: 38 min, direct**, Rhaetian Railway RE 13, hourly. The same train goes on to Davos.
- **Klosters Dorf to Klosters Platz:** about 7 min by PostBus line 235 (Klosters, Rufinis 10:11 to Klosters Platz, Bahnhof 10:18); both are also stops on the same railway line.
- **By car:** yes, and this is the one Graubünden village where the car itself takes the train. The **Vereina car transporter** runs from Klosters Selfranga to Sagliains in the Engadin: "The train will take you through the 19 km long Vereina Tunnel in just 18 minutes", departures every 30 minutes from both stations. Source: https://www.rhb.ch/en/vereina-car-transporter/
- Hotel garage charges are published by the hotels, for example Hotel Sport Klosters "CHF 5 per day (summer) / CHF 10 per day (winter)", Chesa Grischuna "CHF 20 per day".
- **Local transport:** every overnight guest gets the Davos Klosters Premium Card, which for Klosters guests includes free 2nd class travel by train and bus "within zone 200/210 of the Klosters tariff association". Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/guest-information/guest-card/premium-card

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. Klosters Platz (1179 m)
- The main village, around Klosters Platz station on the Landstrasse and Bahnhofstrasse. The **Gotschnabahn** cable car up to Gotschnagrat (2285 m on the official hiking map) starts here and feeds the Parsenn ski area shared with Davos.
- Hotel Piz Buin, Alte Bahnhofstrasse 1, is "located in the centre of Klosters, 150 meters from cable car". Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/accommodation/hotels/piz-buin
- Chesa Grischuna is at Bahnhofstrasse 12, "in the heart of Klosters", by Bahnhof Platz. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/accommodation/hotels/accommodation-chesa-grischuna-swiss-historic-hotel

### B. Klosters Dorf (1124 m)
- The lower half, 2 km down the valley, around Klosters Dorf station and the **Madrisa** gondola (base station Madrisastrasse 7, 7252 Klosters Dorf), which has "23 kilometers of slopes" and the Madrisa-Land family park.
- Hotel Silvapina, Silvapina-Weg 6, is "only 4 minutes away from the Madrisa cable car and from the train station". Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/accommodation/hotels/silvapina-hotel
- Quieter than Klosters Platz; the Sunstar Hotel Klosters is at Boscaweg 7, Klosters Dorf.
- Walk K22, Klosters Platz station to Klosters Dorf by the Bildweg and Gäuggeli, is published as **¾ h**. Source: official panorama hiking map.

### C. Monbiel and the valley head
- The hamlet at the end of the road above Klosters Platz, where the Vereina and Silvretta valleys begin. The tourist office map marks a Klosters Monbiel taxi stop and the start of the long alp walks (Alp Garfiun, Fergenhütte SAC, Silvrettahütte SAC).
- Walk K11, Klosters to Aeuja to Monbiel and back to Klosters, is published as **1 h**. Source: official panorama hiking map.
- Note for the page: this is the sector for people who want a base at the end of the road rather than by the lift.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks (official Davos Klosters panorama hiking map, times as printed)**
1. **K11, Klosters to Aeuja to Monbiel and back to Klosters: 1 h.** Valley walk from the village up towards the Monbiel road head.
2. **K19, Klosters Platz to Gotschnaboden to Laret and back to Klosters Platz: 3½ h**, listed in the "up to 4 hours" group. Climbs the Gotschna side and returns through Laret.
   (Shorter alternatives on the same map: K22 Klosters Platz station to Klosters Dorf by the Bildweg, ¾ h; K17 Gotschna car park to Bosca to Klosters Dorf, ¾ h; K5 Klosters Platz to the Diethelmpromenade to Aeuja to Schafrongg, 1 h.)
   Source for all: https://resources.davos.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/dokumente/broschueren_karten/Panorama-Wanderkarte_web.pdf

**Two named mountain rides / attractions**
1. **Gotschnabahn, Klosters Platz to Gotschnagrat (2285 m on the official map).** The Klosters gate into the Parsenn ski area of "around 97 kilometers of slopes". Winter 27 November 2026 to 4 April 2027; summer 27 June to 11 October 2026. At Gotschnaboden the tourist office publishes a downhill bike track "six kilometers long" with "200 banked turns, jumps, and waves". Sources: https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/mountains-in-davos-klosters/parsenn , https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/mountains-in-davos-klosters/operating-times , https://www.davos.ch/en/klosters
2. **Madrisa gondola from Klosters Dorf** (base at Madrisastrasse 7, 7252 Klosters Dorf). "23 kilometers of slopes" in winter; in summer Madrisa-Land, with "a marble run, bouncy castle, slides, water features, and swings", described by the tourist office as "the first adventure park in the Swiss Alps to offer play opportunities for children with physical and visual impairments". Summer 13 June to 18 October 2026, winter 18 December 2026 to 29 March 2027. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/mountains-in-davos-klosters/madrisa

**Two named restaurants (listed by the tourist office, no opinion)**
1. **Chesa Grischuna**, Bahnhofstrasse 12, 7250 Klosters. Serves "the best of international cuisine", "à la carte menu, vegetarian specialities", "over 150 regional, national and international wines". Open daily 07:00 to 21:30, lunch 11:30 to 13:45, dinner 18:00 to 21:00; closed Monday and Tuesday in summer, open every day in winter. Website www.chesagrischuna.ch. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/experience/culinary-nightlife/restaurants/chesa-grischuna-swiss-historic-hotel
2. **Steinbock**, Landstrasse 146, 7250 Klosters. Dishes "primarily sourced from the region" on "a constantly changing menu, adapted to the seasons". Open daily 07:30 to 22:00, lunch 11:30 to 14:00, dinner 17:30 to 21:00, no seasonal closure published. Website www.steinbock-klosters.ch. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/experience/culinary-nightlife/restaurants/steinbock-klosters

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **Nutli Hüschi**, Monbielerstrasse / Talstrasse, 7250 Klosters: "a typical Walser house from the 16th century" that "has been a museum since 1925", holding "old utensils, furniture and tools from Klosters and the surrounding area". Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/art-culture/museums/museum-nutli-hueschi
- **Vereina car transporter**, loading at Klosters Selfranga: "19 km long Vereina Tunnel in just 18 minutes", every 30 minutes. Source: https://www.rhb.ch/en/vereina-car-transporter/
- **Gotschnaboden bike track**: "six kilometers long" with "200 banked turns, jumps, and waves". Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/klosters

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

**Not published** for Klosters. No official page checked states what the Swiss Travel Pass covers on the Gotschnabahn or Madrisa.

What is published is the **Davos Klosters Premium Card**: every guest booking at least one night in a hotel, holiday apartment or group accommodation gets it from their host; for Klosters guests it gives free 2nd class train and bus travel "within zone 200/210 of the Klosters tariff association", plus reduced mountain railway fares ("CHF 13" low season, "CHF 20" high season for one adult ascent or descent). Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/guest-information/guest-card/premium-card

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Klosters Platz or Klosters Dorf?** Klosters Platz (1179 m) is the main village with the shops, the station and the Gotschnabahn (Hotel Piz Buin is "150 meters from cable car"). Klosters Dorf (1124 m) is 2 km down the valley at the Madrisa gondola, quieter and better for families because of Madrisa-Land. The published walk between them, K22 by the Bildweg, takes ¾ h. Sources: davos.ch hotel listings and the official panorama hiking map.
2. **Is Klosters a good base for the Davos ski area?** The Parsenn area of "around 97 kilometers of slopes" is reached "from Davos with the Parsennbahn and from Klosters with the Gotschnabahn", and the Klosters side runs 27 November 2026 to 4 April 2027. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/mountains-in-davos-klosters/parsenn and the operating times page.
3. **How do I get there without a car?** Zürich HB to Klosters Platz is 1 h 51, one change at Landquart (IC 3 then Rhaetian Railway RE 13, hourly). From Chur it is 57 min. Source: Swiss national timetable, checked 16/09/2026.
4. **Can I take my car through to the Engadin from Klosters?** Yes, on the Vereina car transporter from Klosters Selfranga: "The train will take you through the 19 km long Vereina Tunnel in just 18 minutes", every 30 minutes. Source: https://www.rhb.ch/en/vereina-car-transporter/
5. **Do I get anything for staying overnight?** Yes, the Davos Klosters Premium Card from your host, which includes free train and bus travel in the Klosters tariff zone 200/210 and reduced mountain railway fares. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/guest-information/guest-card/premium-card
