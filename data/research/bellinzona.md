# Bellinzona research dossier

Checked 16/09/2026. Official sources only (Fortezza di Bellinzona, Bellinzona e Valli Tourism, SBB
timetable, Federal Statistical Office, hotels' own sites). No ratings, no reviews, no invented numbers.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Altitude: 241 m. Cross-checked on Wikipedia (de/en Bellinzona infobox, 241 m at the Stazione di
  Bellinzona). No altitude figure is published by the city or the tourist office.
- Coordinates of the main station (Bellinzona FFS): 46.195425, 9.029509. Bus stop "Bellinzona, Stazione":
  46.194814, 9.028208 (Swiss public transport stop register via transport.opendata.ch).
- Canton: Ticino. Bellinzona is the cantonal capital.
- Region key: `ticino`.
- Car-free: false. The castles themselves are: "Direct access to the castle by motor vehicles is not
  permitted" at Castel Grande (fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/), but the town is a normal road town with
  the Autosilo Piazza del Sole car park and two motorway exits.
- Residents: 45 305 permanent resident population, 31.12.2024, commune 5002 Bellinzona
  (Federal Statistical Office, STAT-TAB table px-x-0102010000_101). This is the merged municipality, which
  since 2017 also takes in Giubiasco and Monte Carasso.
- Hotel beds: NOT PUBLISHED by the tourist office. Room counts for individual hotels are published and are
  used below instead.

## 2. ACCESS

From Zurich (SBB timetable, weekday sample 22/09/2026, via transport.opendata.ch):
- Zurich HB 09:05 -> Bellinzona 10:42 (IC 2), direct, 1 h 37.
- Zurich HB 09:33 -> Bellinzona 11:12 (EC 15), direct, 1 h 39.
- Bellinzona is the junction where the Gotthard line splits for Locarno and Lugano, so most Ticino trains
  stop here.

From Milan:
- Milano Centrale 09:43 -> Lugano 10:58 (RE 80), Lugano 11:02 -> Bellinzona 11:16 (IC 2). 1 h 33,
  one change. Hourly in the sample.

Driving and parking:
- You can drive. Motorway exits "Bellinzona Nord" and "Bellinzona centro"; named car parks are the
  Autosilo Piazza del Sole, the Montebello car parks and the Sasso Corbaro car parks
  (fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/).
- Swiss motorway vignette CHF 40 (rheinfall.ch/en/inform).

Getting to the castles:
- Castel Grande: on foot from near the station by the Salita Castelgrande panoramic route, or by the lift
  at Piazzetta della Valle; the route on the bulwark gives direct access to the outer courtyard.
- Montebello and Sasso Corbaro: bus line 4 from Bellinzona station towards Sasso Corbaro serves both.
  (all fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/)

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP

1. Viale Stazione, between the station and the old town
   The avenue that carries the Rabadan carnival parades and most of the town's hotels. Hotel & SPA
   Internazionale is at Viale Stazione 35, Hotel Gamper at Viale Stazione 29, Hotel Croce Federale at
   Viale Stazione 12A (bellinzonaevalli.ch hotel listings).
   - Hotel Gamper publishes "100 m from the railway station (FFS), 5 minutes from the city centre".
   - Hotel Croce Federale publishes "a seven minute walk to the Hotel from the train station" and
     "just a couple of metres away from Castelgrande".
   - Hotel & SPA Internazionale publishes that it is "conveniently opposite the main railway station".

2. The historic centre, under Castelgrande
   Piazza Collegiata, Piazza Nosetto and Piazza del Governo, at the foot of the Castelgrande rock. Hotel
   Unione describes itself as "located at the foot of Castelgrande, a few steps from the pedestrian area
   and the historic centre" (hotelunione.org).
   - Piazza Collegiata is the start of the 10-minute climb to Montebello (80 m of ascent).
   - The Trenino Artù castle train leaves from Piazza Collegiata (and from Piazza Governo on Saturday
     mornings).

3. The Montebello slope, Via Nocca
   The hillside directly under the second castle. The Ostello Montebello youth hostel is in an 1800s
   building at Via Nocca 4, published as "10-15 min. from the train station and a couple of minutes from
   Bellinzona's historic center" (bellinzonaevalli.ch listing).

## 4. SEASONS

- The three castles, main season: 28 March to 8 November 2026, 10:00 to 18:00, last entry 17:30.
- Winter: 9 November 2026 to 18 March 2027, 10:30 to 16:00, last entry 15:30. Only Castel Grande stays
  fully open; Montebello and Sasso Corbaro can be visited in their outer and inner courtyards only.
  (both fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/)
- Castel Grande is the one castle "available all year round" for ticketed entry (same page).
- Trenino Artù, the castle train: runs until 8 November 2026 (same page).
- Ristorante Castelgrande: Tuesday to Saturday 19:00 to 00:00, closed Sunday and Monday (same page).
- Rabadan carnival: named on the Bellinzona e Valli hotel listing for Hotel Gamper, which describes the
  Viale Stazione parades. Dates NOT PUBLISHED on any official page reached. See UNVERIFIED.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two walks
1. Piazza Collegiata to the Castello di Montebello. Published as a 10-minute walk with 80 m of elevation
   gain, on the path that connects the castle with the historic town centre
   (fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/).
2. Montebello to Sasso Corbaro. Published as about a 30-minute walk with 150 m of elevation gain, on the
   path connecting the two castles (same page).

Two sights or rides
1. The three castles of the Fortress of Bellinzona, UNESCO World Heritage since 2000. Fortezza Pass
   (all three castles), valid 28.03.2026 to 08.11.2026: adults CHF 28, family of 2 adults and up to
   3 children CHF 70, single-parent family CHF 54, reduced CHF 18. Single tickets: Castel Grande adults
   CHF 15 (reduced CHF 8, school classes CHF 5 a pupil, free for AG Culture holders and under-6s),
   Montebello adults CHF 10 (reduced CHF 5), Sasso Corbaro adults CHF 15 (reduced CHF 8). Expo Pass for the
   temporary exhibitions at Castel Grande, Sasso Corbaro and Museo Villa dei Cedri CHF 30
   (fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/).
2. Trenino Artù, the castle train. Starts in the historic centre and runs up to Montebello and Sasso
   Corbaro; a full round takes about an hour with brief stops, or you can break the trip and rejoin a later
   service. Sunday to Friday 10:00, 11:20, 13:30, 15:00, 16:30 from Piazza Collegiata; Saturday 11:20 and
   13:30 from Piazza Governo, 15:00 and 16:30 from Piazza Collegiata. Adults CHF 13, children CHF 5, family
   of 2 adults and 2 children CHF 31, students and seniors CHF 10, adult groups CHF 11, Ticino Ticket
   CHF 9. The ticket does not include entry to Montebello or Sasso Corbaro
   (fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/).

Two tables
1. Ristorante Castelgrande, inside Castel Grande. Listed by the Fortress under Food and Drink, open
   Tuesday to Saturday 19:00 to 00:00, closed Sunday and Monday (fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/).
2. Locanda Orico, 6500 Bellinzona, in the historic centre at the foot of the Castelgrande hill a short
   distance from Piazza Governo. The tourist office listing describes a kitchen run by Lorenzo Albrici,
   a former trainee of Fredy Girardet, joining Italian and French cooking, with two small dining rooms
   (bellinzonaevalli.ch/en/restaurants/details/Locanda-Orico/74427.html; own site locandaorico.ch).
   Also listed: Confiserie & Café Al Porto, Via Ludovico il Moro 1 / Piazza Stazione, 6500 Bellinzona.

Typical, with a published fact
- The three UNESCO castles. Inscribed in 2000 as, in the Fortress's own words, "the only visible example in
  the Alpine region of medieval military architecture composed of several castles united by fortified walls
  to protect the population". The site covers Castel Grande, Montebello, Sasso Corbaro, the bulwark that
  blocked the whole Ticino valley and the town walls. The first UNESCO Management Plan for the site was
  drawn up in 2022 by the City of Bellinzona and the Republic and Canton of Ticino, covering 2022 to 2025
  (fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/discover/).
- The bulwark can be entered from the outer courtyard of Castel Grande, from Via Carlo Salvioni 12A, or
  from Viale Portone between numbers 39 and 41 (fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/).

## 6. HOTELS

1. Hotel & SPA Internazionale - https://hotel-internazionale.ch/en/ - Viale Stazione 35, opposite the station.
   Own site and tourist office listing: opposite the main railway station; two buildings, one from the early
   20th century and one opened in July 2014, 68 rooms and 3 suites (71 rooms in total), all renovated;
   soundproofed windows, anti-allergic linen, 24-hour reception; four modular conference rooms.
2. Hotel Croce Federale - https://hotelcrocefederale.ch/ - Viale Stazione 12 A, historic centre.
   Tourist office listing from the hotel's own text: in the middle of the historic centre, a couple of
   metres from Castelgrande, a seven-minute walk from the station; 13 rooms; free Wi-Fi in rooms and
   common spaces.
3. Hotel Unione - https://hotelunione.org/ - foot of Castelgrande, historic centre.
   Own site: family-run, at the foot of Castelgrande a few steps from the pedestrian area and the historic
   centre; Comfort and Family rooms with a view of the three castles or the surrounding mountains; run by
   the Berini family.
4. Hotel Gamper - https://www.bellinzonaevalli.ch/en/hotels/details/Hotel-Gamper/14534.html - Viale
   Stazione 29. NOTE: no separate hotel website was found; the tourist office listing is the source.
   28 rooms with bathroom and shower, television and telephone; 100 m from the railway station and
   5 minutes from the city centre; lift on all floors; its own pizzeria and restaurant; car park next to
   the hotel.
5. Ostello Montebello - Bellinzona Youth Hostel - https://www.youthhostel.ch/ - Via Nocca 4, under
   Montebello castle.
   Tourist office listing from the hostel's own text: a historic 1800s building at the foot of Montebello
   Castle, 10 to 15 minutes from the train station; 45 rooms in total, single and double rooms plus 3, 4, 6
   and 10-bed rooms and separate male and female dormitories; all rooms have a sink, showers and toilets on
   each floor, with some singles and doubles having private shower and WC.
6. Hotel Bellinzona Sud - https://www.hotel-bellinzona-sud.ch/ - 6513 Monte Carasso, inside the merged
   municipality of Bellinzona. Tourist office listing: 55 rooms.
7. La Tureta Boutique Hotel & Restaurant - https://latureta.ch/ - Piazza Grande 43, 6512 Giubiasco, inside
   the merged municipality of Bellinzona. Tourist office listing: 31 rooms; its own fine dining restaurant,
   Ristorante La Tureta.

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

- Not published. The Fortress of Bellinzona's own ticket page lists reductions for groups, students,
  children 6 to 16 and seniors, and free entry for AG Culture holders and children up to 5, but says
  nothing about the Swiss Travel Pass (fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/). The Trenino Artù fare table names
  the Ticino Ticket (CHF 9) but not the Swiss Travel Pass.
- Trains and the town buses are ordinary public transport, included under the general rule
  ("unlimited travel by train, bus and boat", myswitzerland.com Swiss Travel Pass page).

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. How long is the train from Zurich to Bellinzona?
   1 h 37 direct on the IC 2 (Zurich HB 09:05 to Bellinzona 10:42).
   Source: SBB timetable via transport.opendata.ch, sample 22/09/2026.

2. Can I visit all three castles in winter?
   Not fully. From 9 November 2026 to 18 March 2027 only Castel Grande is fully open; Montebello and Sasso
   Corbaro can be visited in their outer and inner courtyards only, 10:30 to 16:00.
   Source: fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/.

3. How much does it cost to see the three castles?
   The Fortezza Pass covers all three: adults CHF 28, reduced CHF 18, family of 2 adults and up to
   3 children CHF 70, valid 28.03.2026 to 08.11.2026.
   Source: fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/.

4. How do I get up to Montebello and Sasso Corbaro without a car?
   On foot, 10 minutes from Piazza Collegiata to Montebello (80 m of climb) and about 30 minutes on from
   Montebello to Sasso Corbaro (150 m of climb); or by bus line 4 from the station towards Sasso Corbaro.
   Source: fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/visit/.

5. Why is Bellinzona a UNESCO site?
   It was listed in 2000 as the only visible example in the Alpine region of medieval military architecture
   made of several castles joined by fortified walls, with a bulwark that blocked the whole Ticino valley.
   Source: fortezzabellinzona.ch/en/discover/.
