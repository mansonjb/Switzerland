## 2. ACCESS

Times below are live Swiss public transport timetable connections (SBB / Rhaetian Railway data via the Swiss open transport data service, transport.opendata.ch), queried 16/09/2026 for a Wednesday in January 2027 (20/01/2027, departure around 09:00).

**By train from Zurich**
- Zurich HB 09:07 to Chur 10:22. Total **1 h 15**, direct (IC 3).

**By train from Basel**
- Basel SBB 09:07 to Chur 11:22. Total **2 h 15**, 1 change (ICE, then IC 3).

**Onward to Arosa**
- Chur 09:08 to Arosa, Bahnhof 10:09. Total **1 h 01**, direct (Rhaetian Railway Arosa Line).

**By train, general**
- "The modern train station is centrally located and well connected to the national and international rail network." The Bernina Express (toward Poschiavo/Tirano), the Glacier Express (toward Zermatt) and the Arosa Line all start from Chur. Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/service/information/getting-chur (tourist office summary)

**Local transport to Brambrüesch**
- The valley station of the Chur-Brambruesch cableway is reached "within 3 minutes by city bus (lines 10, 11 or 12)" from Chur's train station, or a similar route (line 1, stop Stadthalle) for the Chur-Dreibundenstein mountain railways, also about 15 minutes on foot from the station. Source: https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-us/experiences/brambrueesch-churs-local-mountain/ and https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/tour-planner/brambruesch-pradaschier-via-dreibundenstein-big-route

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. Old Town (Altstadt)
- The traffic-free historic centre around Reichsgasse, Kupfergasse and the cathedral hill (Hof).
- Romantik Hotel Stern, Reichsgasse 11, 7000 Chur, a "4-star swiss historic hotel" that has "offered superb food and wines from Chur and Graubunden for over 300 years". Source: https://www.stern-chur.ch/home-en.html
- Ambiente Hotel Freieck, Reichsgasse 44, 7000 Chur, in a building dating to 1575, "quiet and traffic free area". Source: https://www.freieck.ch/en/Welcome
- Franziskaner Hotel + Restaurant am Ochsenplatz, Kupfergasse 18, 7000 Chur, inside the old town. Source: https://www.franziskanerchur.ch/contact.html
- Restaurant Hofkellerei, Hof 1, 7000 Chur, occupies "an old-gothic drinking room from 1522" right by the episcopal court and cathedral. Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/overview-restaurants/restaurant-hofkellerei-chur

### B. Near the railway station
- The station side of town, a short walk from the old town gate (Obertor).
- Hotel ABC, Ottostrasse 8, 7000 Chur, "the only 4-star hotel right by the station", "directly opposite the Chur train station". Source: https://www.hotelabc.ch/en/
- Hotel Chur, Welschdorfli 2, 7000 Chur, "at the entrance to Chur's old town and only 8 minutes from the train station". Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/ubersicht-accommodation/hotel-chur

### C. City-West / business district
- Comercialstrasse area near the Chur-Sud motorway exit, a business hotel district about five minutes' drive from the centre.
- Mercure Chur City West, Comercialstrasse 32, 7000 Chur, "located near the Chur-Sud highway exit" with "Chur city center only five minutes away"; 4 meeting rooms, largest "320 sqm" for up to "350" people. Source: https://all.accor.com/hotel/B7Z9/index.en.shtml

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **Chur Old Town walk.** The tourist office describes it as: "The historic centre is incredibly beautiful and completely free from traffic. Numerous boutiques, restaurants, bars, museums, and galleries, as well as regular markets and events." Above the old town, "the episcopal court with the more than 800-year-old cathedral and its Gothic high altar shining in golden splendour." Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch (old town / cathedral pages)
2. **Dreibundenstein circular hike from Brambruesch.** Published tour: "12.5 km", "5:10 h", elevation gain "588 mhd", elevation loss "590 mhd", starting and finishing at Brambruesch (reached by the Chur-Brambruesch cableway). Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/tour-planner/dreibundenstein-circular-hike-brambruesch

**Two named mountain rides / sights**
1. **Chur-Brambruesch cableway.** Mountain station "at 1,600 metres above sea level"; "In just 12 minutes, the mountain railway takes you from the Graubunden city of Chur to 1600 metres up to the local mountain Brambruesch." Valley station 3 minutes by city bus from the train station. Source: https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-us/experiences/brambrueesch-churs-local-mountain/
2. **Arosa Line (Rhaetian Railway).** "19 tunnels, dizzying passages over 41 bridges and the world-famous Langwieser viaduct crown the journey with the Rhaetian Railway through the picturesque Schanfigg. On only 26 kilometres the Arosa Line climbs 1000 metres. As a tram she strolls through Chur - past the city wall, the Malteser Tower and the Obertor." Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/activities/all-year-round/rail-travel/arosa-line

**Two named restaurants (listed by the tourist office, no opinion)**
1. **Restaurant Hofkellerei Chur**, Hof 1, 7000 Chur. Occupies "an old-gothic drinking room from 1522"; reopened after a three-year renovation in late November 2023; hours "Wednesday 18:00-22:00, Thursday 11:30-14:30 / 18:00-22:00, Friday and Saturday 11:30-14:30 / 18:00-23:00, Sunday 11:30-15:00". Website www.hofkellerei.ch. Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/overview-restaurants/restaurant-hofkellerei-chur
2. **GILDE Restaurant Zunfthaus zur Rebleuten**, Pfisterplatz 1, 7000 Chur. "In the middle of Chur's Old Town" within the pedestrian zone; seating "in the listed guild hall or in the cosy lounge with fireplace", plus "two hidden terraces between the roofs of the old town"; closed Tuesday and Wednesday. Website www.rebleutenchur.ch. Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/overview-restaurants/gilde-restaurant-zunfthaus-zur-rebleuten

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **Cathedral of the Assumption**: "the current cathedral was built between 1150 and 1272"; "the late Gothic high altar by Jakob Russ (1492)"; "completely restored between 2001 and 2007". Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/attractions/cathedral-of-the-assumption
- **Switzerland's oldest city**: "evidence of settlement dating back some 13,000 years", continuously inhabited "since the Pfyn culture (c. 3,900-3,500 BC)". Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/service/information/facts-figures
- **Curia Rhaetorum**: the Roman town "founded in 15 BC, later became an important episcopal see". Same source.
- **Oldest restaurant claim**: Restaurant Hofkellerei is presented by the tourist office as occupying the oldest restaurant room ("an old-gothic drinking room from 1522") in "Switzerland's oldest city". Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/overview-restaurants/restaurant-hofkellerei-chur

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Is Chur's old town car-free?**
   The historic centre is pedestrian: "The historic centre is incredibly beautiful and completely free from traffic." The city as a whole is a transport hub with a station, buses and roads, so Chur overall is not car-free. Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch
2. **How far is Chur from Zurich?**
   1 h 15 direct by train (IC 3), Zurich HB to Chur. Source: Swiss public transport timetable data, queried 16/09/2026.
3. **Can I ride a historic railway straight from the town centre?**
   Yes, the Arosa Line starts "as a tram" through the streets of Chur itself, past the city wall, the Malteser Tower and the Obertor, before climbing into the mountains; the round trip to Arosa takes about an hour each way. Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/activities/all-year-round/rail-travel/arosa-line
4. **How old is Chur really?**
   The tourist office states "evidence of settlement dating back some 13,000 years" and continuous habitation "since the Pfyn culture (c. 3,900-3,500 BC)"; the Roman town "Curia Rhaetorum" was founded in 15 BC. Source: https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/service/information/facts-figures
5. **How do I get up to Brambruesch, the local mountain?**
   The Chur-Brambruesch cableway leaves from a valley station 3 minutes by city bus from the train station and reaches 1,600 m in 12 minutes. Source: https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-us/experiences/brambrueesch-churs-local-mountain/
