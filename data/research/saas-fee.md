# Research dossier: Saas-Fee

Checked 16/09/2026. Official sources only (Saastal Tourismus / saas-fee.ch, Saastal Bergbahnen, PostBus,
the hotels' own sites). No ratings, no reviews, no opinions.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Slug on the sheet: `saas-fee`. Region key `valais`. Canton: Valais (Wallis), Visp district.
- Altitude on the sheet: **1800 m**. CONFIRMED by the tourist office: "the car-free village of Saas-Fee
  lies at 1800m above sea level at the foot of the highest mountain in Switzerland, the Dom (4545m)".
  No change.
- Coordinates on the sheet: 46.1081 / 7.9280. Consistent with the village. No change.
- carFree: **true**. saas-fee.ch describes Saas-Fee as a car-free village.
- How cars are handled: you drive up from Saas-Grund and stop at the village entrance. **Unload luggage at
  Terminal A or Terminal B**, then park either in the **multi-storey car park (Parkhaus Saas-Fee)** or in
  the open car park at the entrance to the village. Published tariff: summer free up to 30 minutes, up to
  **CHF 15 a day** and **CHF 105 a week**; winter up to **CHF 20 a day** and **CHF 140 a week**.
  SaastalCard holders get a reduction on multi-day parking.
- Holiday-apartment guests are collected by the landlord with an **electro-taxi**, or a handcart is provided.
- **No buses run inside the village in summer and autumn**; taxis and hotel transfers do the local work.
- Residents: no official figure found. UNVERIFIED.
- Tourist beds: no official figure found. UNVERIFIED.
- Number of parking spaces: NOT published on the official car park page. UNVERIFIED.

## 2. ACCESS

- Rail gateway is **Visp**. From Visp, **PostBus line 511** (direction Saas-Fee) runs **every half hour**
  straight to Saas-Fee.
- From **Zurich, Geneva, Basel and Milan airports the journey is about three hours** by public transport
  via Visp (saas-fee.ch arrival page).
- PostBus Visp to Saas-Fee takes about 50 minutes according to postauto.ch summaries; I could not confirm
  the figure on an official timetable page during this check. UNVERIFIED.
- By car from Visp: south towards Saas-Fee, second exit at the Killerhof roundabout, then right in
  Saas-Grund. Passes used: Lötschberg car train from the north (up to 6 trains an hour), Simplon from the
  south, Furka from the east.
- Parking: **Parkhaus Saas-Fee** at the village entrance, terminals A and B for luggage.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. Dorfplatz and the village centre**
- The main square and the shopping street (Obere Dorfstrasse, Dorfstrasse). The Dom Hotel is at
  **Dorfplatz 2**.
- The village is compact and flat by Alpine standards; there are no buses inside it in summer.

**B. Panoramastrasse and the lift side**
- The Saastal Bergbahnen head office is at **Panoramastrasse 5**, and the wellnessHostel4000 at
  **Panoramastrasse 1**, next to the Aqua Allalin wellness centre.
- This is the end of the village for the Alpin Express and the Felskinn cable car, and the ski-in/ski-out
  addresses (Saaserhof states it is directly on the piste, two minutes from the ski school meeting point;
  Hotel Bristol states one minute to the ski lifts and two to the cable cars).

**C. Wildi and the upper edge, towards the larch forest**
- Quieter addresses above the village on **Wildistrasse** and **Lomattenstrasse** (Waldhotel Fletschhorn
  at Wildistrasse 101, The Capra at Lomattenstrasse 6).
- The Feehof is instead on the **northern edge** of the village, on Dorfstrasse 28, near the entrance and
  the car park.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2, published times from saas-fee.ch)**
1. **Village to the Gletschergrotte mountain restaurant** — **50 minutes on foot from the village in
   summer**, restaurant at **2000 m**, off the piste and the Spielboden to Saas-Fee trail.
2. **Saas-Fee to Saas-Almagell on the woodland path** — **40 minutes**, listed by the tourist office among
   the pushchair-friendly walks. (Same listing: Saas-Fee to the Fletschhorn restaurant 20 minutes,
   Saas-Fee - Hohnegg - Mälchbodu - Bärufalla 1 hour, Dick Waldji 40 minutes, Hiner de Zy 30 minutes.)

**Rides and sights (2)**
1. **Metro Alpin to Mittelallalin, 3500 m** — the tourist office calls it the highest underground railway
   in the world, running from Felskinn up inside the mountain. About **40 minutes from Saas-Fee** including
   the transfer. At the top: the **Revolving Restaurant Allalin at 3500 m**, the highest revolving
   restaurant in the world, turning **360 degrees in one hour**, 200 seats indoors, open daily 09:00 to
   15:30 with hot meals 11:00 to 15:00. Also the **Ice Pavilion**, a cave in the Fee glacier, volume
   **5500 m3**. Beneath it, the **Allalinhorn at 4027 m**. Adult fare Saas-Fee to Mittelallalin:
   **CHF 62 one way / CHF 83 return in summer 2026**, **CHF 59 / CHF 79 in winter 2026/27**.
2. **Hannig, 2336 m** — reached in **about 5 minutes by cable car** from the village. Newly renovated
   mountain restaurant with a large terrace. Adult fare **CHF 30 one way / CHF 46 return**.
   Other published adult fares: Plattjen CHF 27 / 42, Spielboden CHF 33 / 50, Längfluh CHF 40 / 61,
   Felskinn CHF 35 / 55.

**Restaurants (2, from the saas-fee.ch mountain restaurant listings)**
1. **Revolving Restaurant Allalin**, Mittelallalin, **3500 m**: regional and international dishes, 200
   seats, daily 09:00 to 15:30, hot meals 11:00 to 15:00, reached by Metro Alpin.
2. **Mountain restaurant Spielboden**, **2448 m**: open in line with the Spielboden gondola timetable,
   large sun terrace, winter barbecues. The tourist office notes that **in spring and summer the marmots
   below Spielboden are a highlight**, and that bags of marmot feed are sold at the restaurant (deposit).
   (A third listed option, **Gletschergrotte at 2000 m**, serves Swiss cooking and a cake buffet.)

**Typical local fact**: the village sits at the foot of the **Dom, 4545 m, the highest mountain entirely in
Switzerland**, and the tourist office magazine is named 4545 after it.

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

- No official Swiss Travel System page consulted during this check, so the pass position for Saas-Fee is
  **NOT PUBLISHED** as far as this dossier is concerned. Do not state a percentage.
- What IS official: the Saastal Bergbahnen price page says only "information at the desk of the cable cars
  for discounts", listing Swiss Travel Pass, Half Fare Card and GA, **without publishing a percentage**.
- The relevant local card is the **SaastalCard**, and it is official and precise:
  - Free from your **first overnight stay** at a partner business.
  - **Free cable cars in the valley (9 mountain railways), except the Metro Alpin**, counted night for
    night: 1 night = 1 day, 3 nights = 3 days, with an option to add the arrival or departure day.
  - **Free PostBus from Niedergut to Saas-Fee and from Saas-Grund to Mattmark**, but **not on the arrival
    and departure days**.
  - Metro Alpin needs a separate **Metro Plus Ticket, CHF 50 for adults**.
  - Reduction on parking in Saas-Fee.
  - Valid for pedestrian use of the mountain railways to 30 November.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Can I drive into Saas-Fee?** No. Saas-Fee is car-free. You unload luggage at Terminal A or B at the
   village entrance and park in the multi-storey or the open car park. Summer parking is free up to 30
   minutes and up to CHF 15 a day; winter up to CHF 20 a day.
   (saas-fee.ch/en/arrival/car-park-saas-fee)
2. **How do I get there without a car?** Train to Visp, then PostBus line 511 to Saas-Fee, every half hour.
   About three hours in total from Zurich, Geneva, Basel or Milan airports.
   (saas-fee.ch/en/arrival/arrival-in-the-saas-valley)
3. **Do I pay for the cable cars?** Not for most of them if you stay the night: the SaastalCard covers nine
   mountain railways in the valley for as many days as you have nights, the Metro Alpin excepted, which
   needs a Metro Plus Ticket at CHF 50 for adults.
   (saas-fee.ch/en/saastalcard/saastalcard-in-summer-and-autumn)
4. **What is at the top of the Metro Alpin?** Mittelallalin at 3500 m: the highest revolving restaurant in
   the world, which turns 360 degrees in an hour, and the Ice Pavilion, a 5500 m3 cave inside the Fee
   glacier. About 40 minutes from the village. (saas-fee.ch)
5. **When do the lifts run in winter?** The Alpin Express, Metro Alpin and the Spielboden-Längfluh line
   open on 31 October 2026 and close on 18 April 2027. Plattjen and Hannig open later, on 19 December 2026.
   (saas-fee.ch winter timetable)
