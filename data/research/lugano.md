## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. Historic centre / lakefront (Via Nassa, Piazza Luini)
- The pedestrianised old town and lakeside promenade.
- International au Lac Historic Lakeside Hotel, Via Nassa 68 - Piazza Luini, 6901 Lugano, "di fronte al lago, nelle immediate vicinanze del centro storico"; "L'UNESCO gli ha conferito la distinzione onorifica di 'Hotel storico con menzione speciale'"; 75 rooms, 120 beds. Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/hotels/details/International-au-Lac-Historic-Lakeside-Hotel/13647.html
- Hotel Walter au Lac, Piazza Rezzonico 7, 6900 Lugano, "centenario... dal 1888, 4. generazione Famiglia Cereda", "posizione privilegiata in centro citta e direttamente sul lungolago"; 38 rooms, all lake-view. Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/hotels/details/Hotel-Walter-au-Lac/13634.html
- The church of Santa Maria degli Angeli, Piazza Luini 3, sits directly in this sector (see WHAT TO DO).

### B. Riva Caccia / west lakefront (near Monte San Salvatore)
- The lakefront stretch toward Paradiso and the San Salvatore funicular valley station.
- Hotel Splendide Royal, Riva Caccia 7, 6900 Lugano, a "storico palazzo della Belle Epoque affacciato direttamente sul lago", 93 rooms, 176 beds, restaurant "I Due Sud" with "16 punti GaultMillau e una stella Michelin". Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/hotels/details/Hotel-Splendide-Royal/13711.html

### C. Station / hillside (near the funicular to the centre, and Villa Montalbano)
- The area around Lugano station (served by the city funicular down to the lake) and the residential hillside above.
- Hotel Federale Lugano, Via Paolo Regazzoni 8, 6900 Lugano, family-run "dal 1923... Situato a pochi passi dalla stazione ferroviaria di Lugano (3 min a piedi)"; 47 rooms, 88 beds. Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/hotels/details/Hotel-Federale-Lugano/13636.html
- Villa Principe Leopoldo, Via Montalbano 5A, 6900 Lugano, a hillside luxury hotel with "2 Chiavi MICHELIN" and the restaurant "La Luce"; 37 suites. Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/hotels/details/Villa-Principe-Leopoldo/13761.html
- Hotel Delfino Lugano, Via Casserinetta 6, 6900 Lugano, "posizione centrale vicino alla stazione e con vista lago". Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/hotels/details/Hotel-Delfino-Lugano/13677.html

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **Lungolago: Giardino Belvedere to Parco Ciani.** The Belvedere Garden covers "11'000 mq" with "un doppio filare di alberi" (a double row of trees) along the promenade and "quello che e l'albero piu maestoso ed imponente della citta: una gigantesca sequoia" (the city's most imposing tree, a giant sequoia), ending near the Eden Hotel with flowerbeds and a round water basin. It leads to Parco Ciani, "63'000 metri quadrati di superficie", divided between an Italian/English-style garden near Villa Ciani and a more natural area with native "querce, tigli, platani e aceri" (oaks, lindens, plane trees, maples) stretching to the Cassarate river. Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/commons/details/Giardino-Belvedere-e-lungolago-di-Lugano/138077.html and https://www.luganoregion.com/it/commons/details/Parco-Ciani/85874.html (no total promenade length published, see UNVERIFIED)
2. **Monte Bre to Gandria.** Starting at "la vetta del Monte Bre" (933 m), a downhill walk of "1.30 h" (1 hour 30 minutes), "16 m" ascent and "652 m" descent, ending at the lakeside village of Gandria. Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/hike/details/The-mountain-lake-and-funicular-boat-itinerary-Monte-Br%C3%A8-Gandria-trail/801047846.html (no km distance published, see UNVERIFIED)

**Two named mountain rides / sights**
1. **Funicolare Monte San Salvatore.** Summit "912 m.s.l.m."; "in funzione dal 1890" (construction started July 1888); line "1660m di lunghezza" with gradients "37%" then "61%"; "raggiunge la vetta con le vetture panoramiche in 12 minuti"; cars hold "60 +1 persone". Source: https://www.montesansalvatore.ch/monte-san-salvatore/ and https://www.montesansalvatore.ch/monte-san-salvatore/funicolare/
2. **Funicolare Monte Bre.** Summit "a 933 m s.l.m."; "inaugurata nel 1908", marketed as "DA 115 ANNI, PARTE DELLA STORIA DI LUGANO"; departs from "Lugano (Cassarate)" with an alternative stop at "Suvigliana". Source: https://www.montebre.ch

**Two named restaurants (listed by the tourist office, no opinion)**
1. **Ristorante Galleria Arte al Lago**, Rivetta Alfonsina Storni 1, 6900 Lugano (inside Villa Castagnola). Open "da martedi a sabato"; "16 punti GaultMillau"; set inside an art gallery whose exhibitions change "due volte l'anno"; dress code "abbigliamento serale con pantaloni lunghi"; view described as "una vista mozzafiato sulle acque del Lago di Lugano". Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/restaurants/details/Ristorante-Galleria-Art%C3%A9-al-Lago-/75352.html
2. **Ristorante Luini6**, Via Bernardino Luini 6, 6900 Lugano. Central, "vista panoramica", terrace seating, wheelchair accessible; bistro-style menu of "poke, pinsa, bagel e tante altre prelibatezze", near the LAC cultural centre. Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/restaurants/details/Ristorante-Luini6/154116.html

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **Chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angeli**, Piazza Luini 3: former Franciscan convent "fondato nel 1490"; the church itself "costruita nel 1499"; contains what is described as "l'ultima opera di Bernardino Luini (1529-32)", a large Crucifixion fresco, plus his "Madonna con Bambino e S. Giovannino" (1530) and a "S. Francesco" by G. A. Petrini (1728). Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/commons/details/Chiesa-di-S-Maria-degli-Angeli-Lugano/2682.html and https://www.luganoregion.com/it/commons/details/Giardino-Belvedere-e-lungolago-di-Lugano/138077.html
- **Cattedrale di San Lorenzo**: a parish church already by "818"; became collegiate in "1078"; designated cathedral in "1888"; the Renaissance facade was completed "1500-1517" and is called "uno dei capolavori del rinascimento lombardo"; the octagonal baptismal font is dated "1430". Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/commons/details/Cattedrale-di-S-Lorenzo/2681.html
- **The Gotthard railway effect**: after the line opened in 1882, Lugano's population "grew from 6,949 to 14,998" over the following thirty years. Source: https://www.lugano.ch/la-mia-citta/identita-e-storia/storia.html

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How far is Lugano from Milan?**
   1 h 19 direct by train (EC), Milano Centrale to Lugano. Source: Swiss/Italian public transport timetable data, queried 16/09/2026.
2. **Which is higher, Monte Bre or Monte San Salvatore?**
   Monte Bre's summit is at 933 m; Monte San Salvatore's is at 912 m, so Monte Bre is slightly higher, though San Salvatore's funicular line is steeper (gradients of 37% then 61% over 1,660 m). Sources: https://www.montebre.ch and https://www.montesansalvatore.ch/monte-san-salvatore/
3. **Are the Luini frescoes really in Lugano?**
   Yes, in the church of Santa Maria degli Angeli, Piazza Luini 3: the Crucifixion fresco is described as "l'ultima opera di Bernardino Luini (1529-32)", his last work, alongside his 1530 Madonna con Bambino e S. Giovannino. Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/commons/details/Chiesa-di-S-Maria-degli-Angeli-Lugano/2682.html
4. **How old is Lugano's cathedral?**
   The site was already a parish church in 818, became collegiate in 1078, and was only designated a cathedral in 1888; its Renaissance facade dates from 1500-1517. Source: https://www.luganoregion.com/it/commons/details/Cattedrale-di-S-Lorenzo/2681.html
5. **Is Monte San Salvatore open year-round?**
   No: it closes for renovation works from 9 November 2026 to 12 March 2027. Source: https://www.montesansalvatore.ch/orari-e-tariffe/
