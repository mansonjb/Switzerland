# Swiss Travel Pass — Research

Research date: 21 September 2026. Prices are 2026 calendar-year fares as published by official sources at time of check.

## 1. What the pass covers

- Unlimited travel by **train, bus and boat** throughout the Swiss public transport network (national coverage).
- **Panorama trains** included, but **seat reservations are extra / not covered** — pass gives access to the base fare only, reservation is a separate mandatory charge (see Glacier Express / Bernina Express below).
- **Urban public transport**: unlimited use of public transport (trams, buses) in **more than 90 towns and cities**.
- **Museums**: free admission to **more than 500 museums** (the "Swiss Museum Pass" is automatically bundled in). Includes attractions like chocolate factories, show dairies, castles/fortresses, sports exhibitions. Verification list at museumspass.ch.
- **Mountain excursions fully included (free, no supplement)**: **Rigi, Stanserhorn, and Stoos**. (Both SBB and Switzerland Tourism state exactly these three; a non-official reseller — swiss-pass.ch — additionally lists Brunni and Klewenalp as free, but this could not be confirmed on an official domain, so treat the 2 extra names as UNVERIFIED.)
- **Mountain railways/cableways with a discount**: "up to 50% discount" on other mountain excursions and RailAway leisure offers, per SBB and Switzerland Tourism wording exactly. Per-destination percentages verified below (Pilatus, Gornergrat = 50%; Titlis, Jungfraujoch = see UNVERIFIED, official % not directly retrievable).

### Swiss Family Card

Children aged **6–15** travel **free** when accompanied by at least one parent holding a valid Swiss Travel Pass / Flex / Half Fare Card. The Family Card is issued automatically/free alongside the parent's pass (no separate purchase).

### Children under 6

**Always travel free and do not need a ticket** at all (no pass or Family Card needed) — exact SBB wording.

## 3. Half Fare Card for visitors (Swiss Half Fare Card)

- Price: **CHF 150**.
- Validity: **one whole month** from the start date (e.g. 15 July – 14 August).
- Gives: up to **50% discount** on train/bus/boat travel Switzerland-wide, panorama trains, mountain railways and gondola lifts, plus public transport in the same 90+ towns/cities covered by the full pass.
- Eligibility: only for visitors whose **permanent residence is outside Switzerland and Liechtenstein**; passport/ID must be carried for inspection.
- Buying it also grants a complimentary Swiss Family Card for children 6–15.

### Saver Day Pass (officially described, but NOT a Swiss Travel Pass product — separate SBB domestic fare product)

- 2nd class: from CHF 29 with a Half Fare Travelcard, from CHF 52 without.
- 1st class: from CHF 49 with a Half Fare Travelcard, from CHF 88 without.
- Covers one day of travel (tram/train/S-Bahn/bus/Postbus/boat) within the GA Travelcard validity area, valid until 5am the next day; up to 50% off some leisure offers.
- Must be booked in advance (1 day to 6 months ahead), quota-limited, price rises as quota sells down.
- Note for the guide: this is a discount day-ticket product for people who already have or buy a Half Fare Travelcard — it is not the same product as the Swiss Travel Pass and is not usually the right recommendation for a short-stay foreign visitor; include only as a "did you know" aside if relevant.

## 4. Where to buy / mobile ticket / activation

- Official channels: online at **SBB.ch**, or at a **staffed public transport point of sale** in Switzerland. Purchasable up to **11 months in advance**.
- **Mobile/e-ticket**: the standard (consecutive) Swiss Travel Pass is issued as an e-ticket and can be shown in the **SBB Mobile app**, but only if it was bought directly on SBB.ch while **signed in**, using that same login in the app. Passes bought without a login, via a reseller, as a guest, or Flex passes **cannot** be imported into SBB Mobile after purchase.
- **Activation**: the standard consecutive-day pass does not require separate activation — it is valid for the fixed date range chosen at purchase. The **Flex** version requires each individual travel day to be activated at **activateyourpass.com** before the first journey of that day; already-activated dates can be changed until 23:59 the day before that date starts.

## UNVERIFIED (could not confirm on an official page — flag before using in copy)

- **Rigi/Stanserhorn/Stoos "free list" possibly also includes Brunni and Klewenalp** — seen only on swiss-pass.ch (a reseller, Qmarketing.ch AG, not one of the approved official domains; self-describes as "an official reseller of the Swiss Travel System" but is not SBB/Switzerland Tourism/swisstravelsystem.com itself). SBB.ch and myswitzerland.com both state only Rigi, Stanserhorn, Stoos. Do not add Brunni/Klewenalp to guide copy without separate official confirmation.
- **swisstravelsystem.com** (the domain named in the brief as an official source) currently 302-redirects to `travelswitzerland.com`, a different-looking commercial site — could not use it as a source; flagging in case the brand has moved domains or the redirect is unintended/hijacked. Recommend double-checking this domain before citing it anywhere else.
- **Titlis mountain discount %** — widely reported as 50% (free train to Engelberg + 50% off cable cars Trübsee/Stand/Titlis, Ice Flyer excluded) across multiple secondary sources (switzerlanding.com, swissasap.com, myswissalps.com forum), but titlis.ch returned HTTP 429 (rate limiting) on every fetch attempt this session and could not be directly confirmed. freizeit.sbb.ch (an SBB subdomain) separately advertises "Titlis: up to 10% discount" — this appears to be a different bundled "Mountain'n'Rail" rail+mountain combo ticket product, not the standard Swiss Travel Pass mountain-excursion discount; the two should not be conflated in copy.
- **Jungfraujoch discount %** — conflicting secondary-source claims of 25% (free to Wengen/Grindelwald, then 25% off the final Jungfraubahn leg to the summit) versus 50%. jungfrau.ch's live ticket/price calculator lists "Swiss Travel Pass" as a selectable discount card but the fetched static page text never states the numeric percentage; jungfrau.ch/en-gb/faq/ also didn't surface it. Needs a follow-up check directly against the jungfrau.ch price calculator (JS-rendered, not fetchable as static text) or a phone/email confirmation before publishing a specific number.
- **Matterhorn Glacier Paradise discount** — third-party sources (switzerlanding-style summaries) claim 50% with Swiss Travel Pass, but the official matterhornparadise.ch ticket/FAQ pages fetched this session named only Peak2Peak and the Gornergrat round trip as explicit 50%-off items; Matterhorn Glacier Paradise itself was listed separately with a price (from CHF 120) but no stated discount tied to the Swiss Travel Pass specifically.
- **Bernina Express reservation fee** — reported as CHF 28 (St. Moritz–Tirano) and CHF 32 (low season) / CHF 36 (peak season) for Chur–Tirano, sourced from a WebSearch summary describing rhb.ch content; direct WebFetch of the relevant rhb.ch page (https://www.rhb.ch/en/panoramic-trains/bernina-express) returned HTTP 404 this session, so the exact current figures are unconfirmed against the live official page and should be re-checked before publishing.
