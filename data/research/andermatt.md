# Research: Andermatt

Checked 16/09/2026. Official sources only: Andermatt-Urserntal Tourismus / Andermatt+Sedrun+Disentis
(andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch, which andermatt.ch and andermatt.swiss both redirect to), the lift company
Andermatt-Sedrun Sport AG through the same site, and the hotels' own sites. Wikipedia used only to
cross-check coordinates.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Altitude: "Situated at 1,444 m above sea level, Andermatt has always attracted guests".
  VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/andemratt
  A second official page words it as "a place for skiing (1,500 m NN)" in a photo caption, so the page
  should use 1,444 m, the figure in the destination text.
- Coordinates (village centre): 46.6340 N, 8.5940 E. Cross-check only, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andermatt.
- Canton: Uri. The village lies in the Ursern valley, from which three routes lead over the Oberalp,
  Gotthard and Furka passes to Graubünden, Ticino and Valais.
  VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/andemratt
- Region key: `central`.
- Car-free: **false**. You drive in over the pass roads or through the Gotthard axis and park in the
  village; the Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn also runs a car shuttle train.
  VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/arrival ("The timetable for the car shuttle train is
  available on the Matterhorn Gotthard Railway (MGB) website").
- Residents and tourist beds: **not published** on the official pages fetched. See UNVERIFIED.

## 2. ACCESS

What the destination publishes
- "Thanks to its central location in the Swiss Alps, our destination can be reached from various
  directions. Whether by train, bus or car". Public transport timetables are on the SBB website, the car
  shuttle train timetable on the Matterhorn Gotthard Railway website, and in summer several Alpine passes
  lead into the region. VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/arrival
- **No journey time from Lucerne or Zurich is published** on that page; it refers the reader to sbb.ch.
  See UNVERIFIED.

Rail inside the region
- **Six lift stations are connected to the Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn.** The MGB connects Disentis and
  Andermatt on the route of the Glacier Express, over the **Oberalp Pass, the highest station between
  St. Moritz and Zermatt**. VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/winter-experiences

Driving and parking
- The three pass roads (Oberalp, Gotthard, Furka) are the summer approaches.
  VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/andemratt
- Hotel pages confirm village parking, for example Hotel St. Gotthard's "spacious car park" and free
  parking 50 metres from Zum Schwarzen Bären.
  VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch service-provider listings.

## 4. SEASONS

Winter
- **Andermatt+Sedrun+Disentis is the largest connected ski area in Central Switzerland, with over 180
  kilometres of slopes.** VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/andermatt/en/pages/skiing
- Over 1,600 vertical metres; some of the longest runs in Switzerland with a difference in altitude of
  over 1,500 m; Gemsstock in Andermatt has a vertical of **1,521 m**, Péz Ault in Disentis **1,606 m**.
  VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/winter-experiences
- "Andermatt is one of the snowiest places in the Alps with an average maximum snow depth of almost
  2 metres in the village." VERIFIED, same page.
- **The ski season lasts from mid-November to mid/end April.** VERIFIED, same page.
- Three children's areas: Matti in Andermatt, Valtgeva in Sedrun, Sax in Disentis. VERIFIED, same page.
- The Realp ski lift has a slope "approximately one kilometre long", for families and beginners.
  VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/andermatt/en/pages/skiing
- The Gemsstock is described as a freeride area with deep powder, steep gullies, and an Avalanche
  Training Centre on the Gurschen. VERIFIED, winter-experiences and the Gemsstockbahn listing.
- **The Gemsstock cable car runs only in winter.**
  VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/service-providers/bergbahn-andermattsedrun-sport-ag-gemsstock

Summer
- Hikes, mountain tours, fishing, via ferrata; racing cyclists ride laps with up to five pass crossings
  and almost 7,000 metres of altitude; the 18-hole Andermatt Swiss Alps Golf Course.
  VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/andemratt
- 13 mountain huts, long-distance routes including the Gotthard Crystal Trek and the Four Headwaters
  Trail. VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/andermatt/en/pages/hiking
- Via Ferrata Diavolo is accessible in summer only (see below).

2026/2027 dates
- The operator publishes pages titled "Summer season times 2026" and "Season times winter 2026/27", but
  the dates themselves are loaded dynamically and were not readable in the fetched HTML.
  Only the general statement "mid-November to mid/end April" is published as text. See UNVERIFIED.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two named routes with published figures
1. **Via Ferrata Diavolo, Schöllenen Gorge.** Difficulty K2-2, secured with **700 metres of wire rope,
   265 iron pins and hooks**, artificial steps and two ladders. From Andermatt it is a **20-minute walk**
   to the entrance at the Suworow monument at about **1,400 m**; the exit is at Teufelstalboden at about
   **1,900 m**; the ascent is about **500 metres in about 2.5 hours**. Summer only. Return to Andermatt
   on the Chilchenberg or Nätschen hiking trails, by Matterhorn Gotthard Railway or by cable car.
   VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/klettersteig-diavolo
2. **Ridge hike La Muota** (Schneehüenerstock to Nätschen and Gütsch side of the Oberalp), published by
   the same destination as **8.6 km, 4 h 00, 443 m of ascent, 790 m of descent, difficulty moderate**.
   VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/tour/ridge-hike-la-muota
   NOTE: the tour sheet files this route under the Disentis Sedrun part of the destination, so a page
   should say it starts from the Oberalp Pass rather than from Andermatt village.

Two named mountain rides
1. **Gemsstock cable car**: reversible aerial tramway cabins over **two sections to the 2,962-metre-high
   Gemsstock**, "the mountain with the greatest panoramic view in Central Switzerland", with a view over
   600 mountain peaks. Winter only. VERIFIED, Gemsstockbahn listing (link in SOURCES).
2. **Schneehüenerstock-Express** from the Oberalp Pass to the Schneehüenerstock at **2,600 m**.
   VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch (Schneehüenerstock pages). The Gütsch-Express runs in two
   sections from the village side. VERIFIED, same source.

Two named tables
1. **Restaurant Sonne** in the Hotel Sonne: fondue specialities, home-style cooking and trout from local
   waters, in a room with rustic ceilings and walls.
   VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/andermatt/en/service-providers/hotel-sonne-24
2. **Restaurant Zum Schwarzen Bären**, 100 metres from the Gemsstock cable car: Ticino cooking with
   French and Italian influences, open fireplace.
   VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/andermatt/en/service-providers/hotel-restaurant-zum-schwarzen-baeren-23
   (The listing also carries a guide score; it is deliberately not reproduced, house rule on ratings.)

Typical of the place
- The Ursern valley is "one of the most impressive high valleys in Switzerland", with the Oberalp,
  Gotthard and Furka roads leaving from it. VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/andemratt
- The Schöllenen Gorge with the Devil's Bridge, below the village, is crossed by the via ferrata route
  above. VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/klettersteig-diavolo
- The Glacier Express runs over the Oberalp Pass, the highest station between St. Moritz and Zermatt.
  VERIFIED, andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/winter-experiences

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. *How high is Andermatt?*
   1,444 m above sea level, in the Ursern valley.
   Source: andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/andemratt
2. *How big is the ski area?*
   Andermatt+Sedrun+Disentis is the largest connected ski area in Central Switzerland with over 180 km
   of slopes and over 1,600 vertical metres; the Gemsstock alone has a vertical of 1,521 m.
   Source: andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/andermatt/en/pages/skiing and /en/stories/winter-experiences
3. *When is the ski season?*
   Mid-November to mid or end April; the village has an average maximum snow depth of almost 2 metres.
   Source: andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/winter-experiences
4. *Can you go up the Gemsstock in summer?*
   No. The Gemsstock cable car runs only in winter. It reaches 2,962 m over two sections.
   Source: andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/service-providers/bergbahn-andermattsedrun-sport-ag-gemsstock
5. *Is the Schöllenen gorge walkable?*
   The Via Ferrata Diavolo climbs the gorge wall in summer: 20 minutes on foot from Andermatt to the
   entrance at about 1,400 m, about 500 m of ascent in about 2.5 hours to Teufelstalboden at about
   1,900 m, secured with 700 m of wire rope and 265 pins and hooks.
   Source: andermatt-sedrun-disentis.ch/en/stories/klettersteig-diavolo
