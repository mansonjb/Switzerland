## 2. ACCESS

**From Geneva airport (Geneve-Aeroport station, under the terminal), by train**
- IC 1, direct, **49 min** (10:25 to 11:14 on the sample date)
- IR 95, direct, **56 min**; IR 15, direct, **52 min**
- All three are direct: no change between the airport and Lausanne.
- Source: Swiss public transport timetable API (SBB/CFF timetable data), query 15/10/2026 10:00.

**From Lausanne**
- Lausanne is itself one of the two reference origins for this batch. Useful onward legs from
  Lausanne main station, same source and date:
  Montreux 20 min (RE 33, direct) · Vevey 14 min (RE 33, direct) · Neuchatel 47 min (IC 51, direct)
  · Fribourg 45 min (IC 1, direct) · Lutry 6 min (R 3) · Gruyeres 1 h 23 (change at Palezieux)
  · Les Diablerets 1 h 31 (change at Aigle) · Villars-sur-Ollon 1 h 09 (change at Aigle, then bus 144)
  · Chateau-d'Oex 1 h 40 (change at Montreux, MOB).

**By car**
- Yes. Lausanne is on the A1 and A9 motorways, there is no car ban and no access permit.
- Hotel Continental publishes the current station-area constraint: because of the redesign of
  Lausanne main station, vehicle access has changed, two 15-minute drop-off areas exist
  (Place de la Gare, marked "Hotel", and 44 Avenue de la Gare, marked "Depose rapide"), and the
  hotel directs guests to the Montbenon car park. Source: hotelcontinental.ch.
- Public transport inside town: the m2 metro links the lake at Ouchy to the upper districts,
  serving the main station, the Flon interchange and the historic centre. Elevation climbed by
  the line: 336 m. Source: mapa-metro.com (NOT official, see UNVERIFIED; the operator page
  t-l.ch was unreachable during this session).

**Boats**
- CGN serves Lausanne-Ouchy. The Swiss Travel Pass is valid on all CGN cruises except events,
  and so are half-fare and GA travelcards. Source: cgn.ch/en/faq.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. Ouchy, on the lake**
- The lakeside terminus of the m2 metro, at 372 m, the lowest point of the commune.
- The Olympic Museum is here, at 1 quai d'Ouchy, on the lakefront.
- CGN boats leave from Ouchy for the French shore and along the lake.
- Named by the tourist office as one of the "unmissable neighbourhoods", with three gardens,
  the Thai pavilion in the Denantou park and the Eole wind vane in the harbour.
- Hotels here: Beau-Rivage Palace, Chateau d'Ouchy, Angleterre & Residence, Hotel du Port.

**B. La Cite and the centre (cathedral, Place de la Palud, Saint-Francois)**
- La Cite is named by Lausanne Tourisme as "Lausanne's historical quarter"; the cathedral stands
  at the top of it at 526 m, the city-centre altitude.
- Place de la Palud, Rue de Bourg and Saint-Francois are the named shopping quarters.
- The night watchman calls the hours from the cathedral tower every night between 22:00 and 02:00.
- Hotels here: Lausanne Palace (Grand-Chene), Royal Savoy.

**C. Around the main station and the Flon**
- Lausanne main station is at 452 m, roughly midway between the lake and the cathedral on the m2.
- The Flon is the district between the station and the centre, named by the tourist office among
  the shopping quarters, and is the m2/m1 interchange.
- Sous-Gare / Grancy, immediately below the station, is also named by the tourist office as a
  shopping quarter.
- Hotels here: Hotel Continental (opposite the station), Hotel Victoria.

## 4. SEASONS

- The cathedral is open daily 09:00 to 19:00 from 1 April to 30 September, and 09:00 to 17:30
  from 1 October to 31 March. Source: lausanne-tourisme.ch.
- Because of work on the vaults of the south transept, **the rose window and its stained glass are
  inaccessible until spring 2027**. Source: lausanne-tourisme.ch. This is the single most useful
  "watch out" for a 2026/27 page.
- The night watchman calls the hours from the tower every night of the year, 22:00 to 02:00,
  a tradition the tourist office dates back "over 600 years".
- CGN: the Belle Epoque boats "Rhone" and "Savoie" and the Belle Epoque diesel boats "Vevey" and
  "Italie" sail all year; "La Suisse" and "Montreux" sail from mid-April to mid-October.
  Source: cgn.ch/en/faq.
- The Sauvabelin Tower is closed in strong wind, snow or ice. Source: lausanne-tourisme.ch.
- Fixed events with a published date: **no date found for a Lausanne event in this session.**
  Montreux Jazz (2 to 17 July 2027) and the Chateau-d'Oex balloon festival (23 to 31 January 2027)
  belong to their own pages. See UNVERIFIED.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **Lavaux vineyard terraces, Lutry to Saint-Saphorin.** 11.1 km, published walking time
   **03:15**, +369 m / -393 m, graded "easy", from Lutry station to Saint-Saphorin station.
   Source: montreuxriviera.com/en/V1433. Lutry is 6 minutes from Lausanne by train (R 3).
   Lavaux has been on the UNESCO World Heritage list since 2007 and counts more than 10 000
   terraces and 450 km of heritage walls, with about 180 winegrowing families.
   Source: lavaux-unesco.ch.
2. **Sentier nature Grand Lausanne (Grand Lausanne nature trail).** Crosses the city along the
   Vuachere, the shore of Lake Geneva, the rose garden in the Vallee de la Jeunesse park and the
   three city forests; the route is designed to be split into **two sections of ten kilometres**,
   one east and one west. Source: myswitzerland.com (Switzerland Tourism) route page.

**Two named sights**
1. **The Olympic Museum**, 1 quai d'Ouchy, on the lakefront. Open Tuesday to Sunday 09:00 to 18:00,
   last admission 17:00, closed Mondays except public holidays. Adults **CHF 18**, students and
   seniors CHF 10, free under 16. The museum suggests allowing 2 hours for the exhibitions.
   Source: olympics.com/museum/visit/practical-information.
2. **Lausanne Cathedral (Notre-Dame).** Built between the 12th and 13th centuries, consecrated in
   1275 in the presence of Pope Gregory X and Emperor Rudolf of Habsburg. Entry to the cathedral is
   free; the tower costs **CHF 6** for adults from 26, free under 26. Open 09:00 to 19:00 from
   1 April to 30 September and 09:00 to 17:30 from 1 October to 31 March.
   Source: lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/lausanne-cathedral/.

**Also typical, with a published fact**
- **Sauvabelin Tower**: 35 m high, erected in 2003 from local wood, mainly Douglas fir from the
  City of Lausanne's own forests, with a 360-degree platform; a maximum of 30 people at a time,
  access through an automatic gate, closed in bad weather. Source: lausanne-tourisme.ch.

**Two named tables**
1. **Cafe du Commerce**, Lausanne. Describes itself on its own site as a brasserie "a deux pas du
   centre" serving a seasonal menu from midday to evening, with a weekday lunch formula.
   Source: cafeducommerce.ch. (No opinion published here.)
2. **Cafe Beau-Rivage**, Place du Port 17-19, 1006 Lausanne, one of the four tables of the
   Beau-Rivage Palace at Ouchy, alongside Pic au Beau-Rivage Palace, Kaigan and La Terrasse.
   Source: brp.ch.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How long is the train from Geneva airport to Lausanne?**
   49 minutes on the fastest direct IC 1; IR 15 and IR 95 are also direct, at 52 and 56 minutes.
   No change is needed. (Swiss timetable, sample 15/10/2026.)
2. **Should I stay at Ouchy or in the centre?**
   Ouchy is the lake shore at 372 m, with the Olympic Museum and the CGN landing stage; the
   historical quarter La Cite is 526 m up, around the cathedral. The m2 metro joins the two and
   climbs 336 m across the line.
3. **Can I visit the cathedral rose window?**
   Not at the moment. Because of work on the vaults of the south transept, the rose and its
   stained glass are inaccessible until spring 2027. The cathedral itself stays open and free.
4. **Is there really a night watchman?**
   Yes. Every night between 22:00 and 02:00 the watchman announces the hours from the cathedral
   tower, a tradition the tourist office dates back over 600 years.
5. **Is the Lavaux vineyard walk doable from Lausanne?**
   Yes. Lutry, the start of the published 11.1 km / 3 h 15 route to Saint-Saphorin, is 6 minutes
   from Lausanne by regional train.
