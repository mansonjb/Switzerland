# Research: Rigi Kaltbad

Checked 16/09/2026. Only official sources: Rigi Bahnen AG (rigi.ch), Mineralbad & Spa Rigi Kaltbad
(aquaspa.ch), Weggis Vitznau Rigi Tourismus (weggis-vitznau.ch), Gemeinde Weggis (weggis.ch, the
municipality Rigi Kaltbad administratively belongs to), the hotels' own sites. Anything not confirmed
on an official page is in UNVERIFIED.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Altitude: the Mineralbad & Spa states its own altitude as "1433 m ü. M. auf der Rigi".
  VERIFIED, aquaspa.ch/de/mineralbad-rigi/. The Rigi Kaltbad Swiss Quality Hotel is listed at 1,438 m
  and the Kräuter Hotel Edelweiss Rigi at 1,550 m, both on weggis-vitznau.ch hotel pages, showing the
  settlement spreads across a range of altitudes rather than sitting at one figure.
  VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/rigi-kaltbad-swiss-quality-hotel and weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/
  kraeuter-hotel-edelweiss-rigi
- Administrative status: Rigi Kaltbad is one of the districts of the municipality of **Weggis**, canton
  Lucerne, alongside Hertenstein, Unterdorf, Oberdorf, Hinterdorf and Riedsort; the municipality's
  altitude range is given as reaching "1659 m ü. M. (Rotstock Rigi Kaltbad)".
  VERIFIED, weggis.ch/gemeinde
- Canton: Lucerne (via the municipality of Weggis). VERIFIED, weggis.ch/gemeinde
- Region key: `central`.
- Car-free: **true**. "Da die Rigi autofrei ist, führt dein Weg ganz entspannt über die Bergbahnen"
  (since the Rigi is car-free, you travel comfortably by mountain railway); only vehicles with a permit
  for the mountain's own operators are allowed. VERIFIED, aquaspa.ch/de/mineralbad-rigi/besucherinfos/
  anreise/. Both hotels confirm the same status directly: "car-free mountain location".
  VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/rigi-kaltbad-swiss-quality-hotel and weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/
  hotel-bergsonne-rigi
- Bathing tradition: the Mineralbad "knüpft an die 600-jährige Badetradition der Rigi an" (builds on
  600 years of bathing tradition on the Rigi). VERIFIED, aquaspa.ch/de/mineralbad-rigi/

## 2. ACCESS: HOW RIGI KALTBAD IS REACHED (no car)

- **Cable car from Weggis** (Luftseilbahn Weggis-Rigi Kaltbad, operator Rigi Bahnen AG): "in nur 10
  Minuten das Dorf Rigi Kaltbad" (reaches Rigi Kaltbad in just 10 minutes).
  VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/poi/luftseilbahn-weggis-rigi-kaltbad
- **Cogwheel railway from Vitznau** (Vitznau-Rigi Kulm line, operator Rigi Bahnen AG, opened 1871,
  "Europe's first mountain railway"), which calls at Rigi Kaltbad on the way to Rigi Kulm; at Rigi
  Kaltbad the line connects to the Mineralbad and to the cable car down to Weggis.
  VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/attractions/vitznau-rigi-kulm-cogwheel-train-d06c628973 and weggis-vitznau.ch/
  en/poi/vitznau-rigi-kulm-cogwheel-railroad
- Both hotels at Rigi Kaltbad state guests arrive "by cable car from Weggis or cogwheel/rack railway
  from Vitznau". VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/rigi-kaltbad-swiss-quality-hotel and
  weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/hotel-bergsonne-rigi
- No public road serves the resort; parking is at the valley stations in Weggis and Vitznau.
  VERIFIED, aquaspa.ch/de/mineralbad-rigi/besucherinfos/anreise/ ("Parkplätze sind an allen
  Talstationen vorhanden").
- A Rigi Taxi service is available for guests with reduced mobility.
  VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/hotel-bergsonne-rigi

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

**A. Zentrum, by the railway station and the Mineralbad**
- Rigi Kaltbad Swiss Quality Hotel, Zentrum 4, with direct access to the Mario Botta-designed
  Mineral Bath & Spa. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/rigi-kaltbad-swiss-quality-hotel

**B. Fyrabigweg**
- Hotel Bergsonne Rigi, Fyrabigweg 1, renovated in 2022 in an alpine-chic style.
  VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/hotel-bergsonne-rigi

**C. Staffelhöhenweg, toward Rigi Staffel**
- Kräuter Hotel Edelweiss Rigi, Staffelhöhenweg 61, at 1,550 m, on the higher path toward Rigi
  Staffelhöhe. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/kraeuter-hotel-edelweiss-rigi

## 4. SEASONS

- The Rigi as a whole offers "120 kilometers of hiking trails in summer and 35 kilometers of winter
  hiking trails". VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/experience/hiking
- The Mineralbad & Spa is open "Montag bis Sonntag 10.00-20.00 Uhr" (daily, 10am-8pm), bathing ending
  30 minutes before closing; the outdoor pool runs at about 35°C.
  VERIFIED, aquaspa.ch/de/mineralbad-rigi/
- Exact 2026/2027 opening/closing dates for the cable car and cogwheel line are not published on the
  pages fetched. See UNVERIFIED.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two named walks
1. **Felsenweg**, between Rigi Scheidegg and Rigi First, starting at Rigi Kaltbad station; described by
   the tourist office as one of the most scenic walks on the Rigi, over Lake Lucerne.
   VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/poi/felsenweg
2. **Känzeli**, a named viewpoint the operator's own viewpoint finder filters under the "Rigi Kaltbad /
   First" area. VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/experience/hiking/viewpoints

Two named rides/sights
1. **Mineralbad & Spa Rigi Kaltbad**, designed by architect Mario Botta, at 1,433 m, with an indoor
   bathing hall, an outdoor pool at about 35°C, a herbal steam bath, and a crystal bath reserved for
   guests 16 and over. VERIFIED, aquaspa.ch/de/mineralbad-rigi/
2. **Vitznau-Rigi Kulm cogwheel railway**, calling at Rigi Kaltbad, opened 1871, Europe's first mountain
   railway. VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/attractions/vitznau-rigi-kulm-cogwheel-train-d06c628973

Typical of the place
- **A resort with no road**: Rigi Kaltbad is reached only by the Weggis cable car or the Vitznau
  cogwheel railway; both hotels describe their own setting as a "car-free mountain location".
  VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/rigi-kaltbad-swiss-quality-hotel and weggis-vitznau.ch/en/hotel/
  hotel-bergsonne-rigi
- **600 years of bathing tradition**, continued today by the Mario Botta-designed Mineralbad & Spa.
  VERIFIED, aquaspa.ch/de/mineralbad-rigi/

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

- The general Mount Rigi Railways day-pass note states "GA, Swiss Travel Pass, Children (up to 15.99
  years old when accompanied) free", which covers the core network including the Weggis-Rigi Kaltbad
  cable car and the Vitznau-Rigi Kulm cogwheel line that both serve Rigi Kaltbad.
  VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/inform/prices-discounts
- Smaller feeder cable cars elsewhere on the mountain (Vitznau-Hinterbergen, Brunnen-Urmiberg) are
  explicitly excluded from GA/Swiss Travel Pass coverage, but neither of those serves Rigi Kaltbad
  itself. VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/inform/prices-discounts
- Treat the exact Weggis-Rigi Kaltbad fare-row wording as not line-item confirmed; see UNVERIFIED in
  the Weggis dossier.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. *How do you get to Rigi Kaltbad without a car?*
   By cable car from Weggis in 10 minutes, or by the Vitznau-Rigi Kulm cogwheel railway from Vitznau;
   there is no public road. Source: weggis-vitznau.ch/de/poi/luftseilbahn-weggis-rigi-kaltbad and
   aquaspa.ch/de/mineralbad-rigi/besucherinfos/anreise/
2. *Is Rigi Kaltbad really car-free?*
   Yes. Only vehicles with a permit for the mountain's own operators are allowed; both local hotels
   describe their setting as "car-free mountain location". Source: aquaspa.ch and weggis-vitznau.ch
   hotel pages.
3. *What is the Mineralbad & Spa?*
   A thermal bath designed by architect Mario Botta at 1,433 m, continuing 600 years of bathing
   tradition on the Rigi, open daily 10am-8pm. Source: aquaspa.ch/de/mineralbad-rigi/
4. *Which municipality does Rigi Kaltbad belong to?*
   Weggis, canton Lucerne; it is the municipality's highest district. Source: weggis.ch/gemeinde
5. *Is the Swiss Travel Pass valid on the way up to Rigi Kaltbad?*
   The core Mount Rigi Railways network, including the Weggis cable car and the Vitznau cogwheel line,
   is covered under the general GA/Swiss Travel Pass free-travel note; smaller feeder lifts elsewhere
   on the mountain are excluded. Source: rigi.ch/en/inform/prices-discounts
