## 2. ACCESS

**From Geneva airport, by train** (Swiss timetable, sample 15/10/2026 10:00)
- IR 90, **direct, 1 h 07** (10:55 to 12:02)
- IR 95, **direct, 1 h 14** (10:14 to 11:28)
- IC 1 to Lausanne then RE 33, 1 h 20 with one change.

**From Lausanne, by train**
- RE 33, **direct, 14 min** (10:01 to 10:15)
- IR 95, direct, 14 min; R 3, direct, 18 min.

**Onward from Vevey**
- Montreux: RE 33 direct, **6 min**.
- Chexbres-Village, in the middle of the Lavaux terraces: R 7 direct, **7 min**.

**By car and parking**
- Yes. No car ban and no permit is published for Vevey.
- Chaplin's World, at Route de Fenil 2, 1804 Corsier-sur-Vevey, charges **CHF 5 a day** for parking
  with admission and has two EV charging points. Source: chaplinsworld.com.
- No municipal parking tariff was fetched for Vevey itself. See UNVERIFIED.

**Buses**
- Bus 212 from Vevey SBB station to the "Chaplin" stop, **about 15 minutes**.
  Source: chaplinsworld.com.

**Boats**
- CGN calls at Vevey and one of its Belle Epoque boats is named "Vevey"; that boat and "Italie",
  "Rhone" and "Savoie" sail all year, while "La Suisse" and "Montreux" sail mid-April to
  mid-October. The Swiss Travel Pass is valid on all CGN cruises except events.
  Source: cgn.ch/en/faq.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. The Grande Place and the lakefront (Place du Marche)**
- The market square opens straight onto the lake. The market is held **every Tuesday and Saturday**
  with local produce. Source: montreuxriviera.com/en/P68726.
- The Alimentarium and the Fork sculpture standing in the water are on this front.
- Hotels here: Grand Hotel du Lac, Hotel des Trois Couronnes.

**B. Around Vevey railway station**
- 14 minutes from Lausanne and 6 minutes from Montreux by direct train, and the departure point of
  bus 212 for Chaplin's World.
- The Astra Hotel Vevey stands on the site of the 19th-century Hotel de la Gare, which became the
  Pavillon des Voyageurs in 1912 and then the Hotel Pavillon; Josef Ming bought the Buffet de la
  Gare in 1950. Source: astra-hotel.ch.
- Hotels here: Astra Hotel Vevey.

**C. The slopes above town, towards Blonay and Corsier**
- Residential ground climbing from the lake towards Mont-Pelerin, Blonay and Corsier-sur-Vevey.
- Chaplin's World is at Corsier-sur-Vevey, on a **four-hectare** estate. Source: chaplinsworld.com.
- Hotels here: Hotel Le Leman (Route de Blonay 20, 1800 Vevey). The Modern Times Hotel is further
  out, at Chemin du Genevrier 20, 1806 Blonay-Saint-Legier, so it is not a Vevey address.

## 4. SEASONS

- **Vevey market: every Tuesday and Saturday**, all year, on the Grande Place by the lake.
  Source: montreuxriviera.com/en/P68726.
- **Vevey-La Tour folk markets**: listed by the tourist office as a summer Saturday event on the
  Place du Marche and the Place de l'Hotel de Ville. The page did not load in this session, so
  **no hours and no dates are recorded here.** See UNVERIFIED.
- **Images Vevey biennial**: the Astra Hotel publishes "From 5 to 27 September 2026, Vevey will host
  the 10th edition of the Images Vevey Biennial, under the theme 'We are family!'".
  Source: astra-hotel.ch. This is a hotel quoting the festival, not the festival's own site;
  confirm on images.ch before printing.
- **Fete des Vignerons**: held in Vevey. The 2019 edition ran **18 July to 11 August 2019**, and the
  festival has been on the UNESCO Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of
  Humanity since **1 December 2016**. **No date is published for the next edition**, so the page
  must say so rather than guess a year.
- **CGN boats**: seasonal split as above.
- **Lavaux**: the vineyard terraces begin at the western edge of Vevey. The inscribed landscape
  covers ten communes including Corseaux, Corsier-sur-Vevey, Chardonne and Jongny, all on the
  Vevey side. Source: lavaux-unesco.ch.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **Lavaux vineyard terraces, Lutry to Saint-Saphorin.** 11.1 km, published walking time **03:15**,
   +369 m / -393 m, "easy", station to station. Saint-Saphorin is the Vevey end.
   Source: montreuxriviera.com/en/V1433.
2. **La Route des Grands Crus de Lavaux.** Published by Lavaux UNESCO as a **3-kilometre** walk
   through the terraces. Source: lavaux-unesco.ch. (No walking time is published; do not add one.)

**Two named sights**
1. **Chaplin's World**, Route de Fenil 2, 1804 Corsier-sur-Vevey. A **four-hectare** estate.
   Reached by **bus 212** from Vevey SBB station in **about 15 minutes**. Parking CHF 5 a day with
   admission, two EV charging points. The museum publishes hours of roughly 10:00 to 18:00 or
   17:00 with shorter winter hours and some winter closure dates, and **does not publish the adult
   price on the page fetched**. Source: chaplinsworld.com.
2. **The Alimentarium**, on the Vevey lakefront in the former head office of the Nestle group.
   Describes itself as the world's first food museum, open "for over 35 years". Opening hours
   **October to March 10:00 to 17:00, April to September 10:00 to 18:00**; adults **CHF 15**.
   Source: alimentarium.org/en/basic-page/opening-hours-entrance-fees. **Caveat: the site returned
   403 to this session's fetcher; these figures come from the indexed text of that official page
   and should be re-read in a browser before publishing.**

**The typical Vevey fact**
- **The Fork (La Fourchette)**, the giant fork planted in the lake off the Alimentarium, was put
  there for the museum's **10th anniversary**. Source: montreuxriviera.com (Alimentarium listing).

**Two named tables**
1. **La Coupole 1912**, the brasserie of the Astra Hotel Vevey by the station. The hotel publishes
   that it preserves the **1927 Fete des Vignerons frescoes** and stained glass by Michel Delanoe,
   and that the complex took the Astra name in 2005. Source: astra-hotel.ch.
2. **The Veranda**, the "brasserie chic" of the Grand Hotel du Lac, open every day, alongside the
   house's gourmet restaurant **Emotions by Guy Ravet** (Wednesday to Sunday, dinner only).
   Source: ghdl.ch.

## 6. HOTELS (5 firm, all URLs loaded on 16/09/2026)

1. **Grand Hotel du Lac** - https://www.ghdl.ch/ - lakefront
   - Gourmet restaurant Emotions by Guy Ravet, Wednesday to Sunday, dinner only.
   - The Veranda, "brasserie chic", every day.
   - Breakfast overlooking Lake Geneva; a Buddha Bar pop-up by the pool in summer.
2. **Hotel des Trois Couronnes** - https://www.hoteltroiscouronnes.ch/ - lakefront
   - **34** rooms facing Lake Geneva.
   - The house describes them as blending classical charm with modern comfort.
3. **Astra Hotel Vevey** - https://astra-hotel.ch/ - by the station
   - The site's history runs from the 19th-century Hotel de la Gare to the Pavillon des Voyageurs
     (1912), then the Hotel Pavillon; the Ming family took over the Buffet de la Gare in **1950**,
     starting with an 11-room house, and the complex was renamed Astra Hotel Vevey in **2005**.
   - Brasserie **La Coupole 1912**; 1927 Fete des Vignerons frescoes and Michel Delanoe stained glass.
   - Wellness centre and seminar rooms.
4. **Hotel Le Leman** - https://www.hotel-leleman.ch/ - Route de Blonay 20
   - Route de Blonay 20, 1800 Vevey, on the Vaud Riviera close to the centre of Vevey.
   - Hotel and restaurant in the same house.
5. **Modern Times Hotel** - https://www.moderntimeshotel.ch/ - Blonay-Saint-Legier (outside Vevey)
   - Chemin du Genevrier 20, 1806 Blonay-Saint-Legier.
   - Part of Marriott's Tribute Portfolio.
   - Its own site files its activity pages under "Activities in Vevey".
   - **Address is not in Vevey**: either present it as "above Vevey" or leave it out.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How far is Vevey from Lausanne?**
   14 minutes by direct RE 33 train. From Geneva airport it is 1 h 07 direct on the IR 90.
2. **When is the Vevey market?**
   Every Tuesday and Saturday on the Grande Place, the square that opens onto the lake.
3. **How do I get to Chaplin's World?**
   Bus 212 from Vevey SBB station to the "Chaplin" stop, about 15 minutes. The estate is at
   Route de Fenil 2, Corsier-sur-Vevey, and covers four hectares; parking is CHF 5 a day with
   admission.
4. **When is the next Fete des Vignerons?**
   No date is published. The last edition ran from 18 July to 11 August 2019, and the festival has
   been on the UNESCO Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity since
   1 December 2016.
5. **Is Vevey in the Lavaux World Heritage area?**
   The inscribed landscape covers ten communes, among them Corseaux, Corsier-sur-Vevey, Chardonne
   and Jongny, on the Vevey side; the terraces have been on the UNESCO list since 2007.
