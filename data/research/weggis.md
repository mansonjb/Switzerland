# Research: Weggis

Checked 16/09/2026. Only official sources: Gemeinde Weggis (weggis.ch), Weggis Vitznau Rigi Tourismus
(weggis-vitznau.ch), Rigi Bahnen AG (rigi.ch), Lake Lucerne Navigation Company / SGV (lakelucerne.ch),
the hotels' own sites. Wikipedia used only to cross-check altitude and coordinates. Anything not
confirmed on such a page is in UNVERIFIED.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Altitude: the municipality spans "zwischen 434 m ü. M. (Seespiegel) und 1659 m ü. M. (Rotstock Rigi
  Kaltbad)", from lake level to the Rotstock summit above Rigi Kaltbad. VERIFIED, weggis.ch/gemeinde
  Cross-check: Wikipedia gives the village itself at 435 m.
- Coordinates (village centre): 47.033 N, 8.433 E. Cross-check only, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weggis.
- Canton: Lucerne. VERIFIED, weggis.ch (municipal domain and administrative pages).
- Region key: `central`.
- Car-free: **false**. Weggis is reached by road from Küssnacht am Rigi and has its own car parks;
  only the Rigi above it is car-free. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/anreise-mobilitaet
- Residents: 4'607 (municipal page, no date given in the fetched extract). VERIFIED, weggis.ch/gemeinde
  The municipality comprises the districts "Hertenstein, Unterdorf, Oberdorf, Hinterdorf, Riedsort und
  Rigi Kaltbad": Rigi Kaltbad is administratively part of Weggis. VERIFIED, weggis.ch/gemeinde
- Character: Gemeinde Weggis calls the village "der mildeste Ort der Schweiz" in spirit, quoting Mark
  Twain, who in 1897 called Weggis the "lieblichster Ort" of the "Luzerner Riviera". VERIFIED,
  weggis.ch/mostcharmingplace
- Climate: "Weggis hat ... ein ausgesprochen mildes, fast mediterranes Klima, wo Palmen, Kastanien und
  Feigen wachsen" (a markedly mild, almost Mediterranean climate where palms, chestnuts and figs grow).
  VERIFIED, weggis.ch/mostcharmingplace

## 2. ACCESS

By boat (SGV, Lake Lucerne Navigation Company)
- Weggis is a scheduled stop on Lake Lucerne; from Lucerne the crossing takes "in einer knappen Stunde"
  (just under an hour) by steamship or motor vessel, with hourly summer connections.
  VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/anreise-mobilitaet
- The Swiss Travel Pass, Swiss Travel Pass Flex and Swiss Transfer Ticket give free travel on SGV
  scheduled boats. VERIFIED, lakelucerne.ch (FAQ "Which tickets or travel passes are valid on the
  lake?", via lakelucerne.ch/en)

By bus
- AAGS line 508 runs a half-hourly lakeside service toward Küssnacht am Rigi and Brunnen; lines 502 and
  653 also serve the region. Cash fares in CHF are accepted on board.
  VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/anreise-mobilitaet

By car and nearest train station
- Küssnacht am Rigi, on the A4 motorway, is the nearest railway station; the lakeside road from there to
  Weggis/Vitznau is about 10 km. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/anreise-mobilitaet
- Weggis has two car parks. VERIFIED, same page.

Up the mountain
- Cable car Weggis-Rigi Kaltbad (Luftseilbahn Weggis-Rigi Kaltbad, operated by Rigi Bahnen AG) reaches
  Rigi Kaltbad "in nur 10 Minuten" (in just 10 minutes). VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/poi/
  luftseilbahn-weggis-rigi-kaltbad
- The Rigi is car-free: "Da die Rigi autofrei ist, führt dein Weg ganz entspannt über die Bergbahnen"
  (since the Rigi is car-free, you travel comfortably by mountain railway). VERIFIED, aquaspa.ch/de/
  mineralbad-rigi/besucherinfos/anreise/ (Mineralbad Rigi, at Rigi Kaltbad)

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

**A. Seestrasse and the boat landing**
- The SGV landing stage and the tourist information office are here, Seestrasse 5.
  VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en (contact block).
- Hotel Beau Rivage and Hotel Alexander stand directly on this shore road.

**B. Dorfplatz and the old village**
- The historic centre, with the parish church and Café Vienna near the Hertenstein landing stage.
- Hotel Restaurant Alpenblick, Luzernerstrasse 31, is just back from the lake in this sector.

**C. Hertenstein and the cable-car side**
- Hertenstein, with its own boat stop, is where Villa Senar (Rachmaninoff's house, 1931-1933) stands,
  a 15-minute walk from the Hertenstein jetty. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/poi/rachmaninoff
- The Rigi Kaltbad cable-car valley station (Weggis Seilbahn bus stop) is on this side of the village.
  VERIFIED, zvb.ch stop list (Weggis Seilbahn among the municipality's ZVB stops).

## 4. SEASONS

- Weggis markets itself as a year-round lakeside base at the "mild" southwest foot of the Rigi, with a
  climate that supports palms, chestnuts and figs. VERIFIED, weggis.ch/mostcharmingplace
- Summer: lake swimming, boat trips on the SGV network, hiking and the Rigi cable car; winter: access to
  Rigi's snow sports via the same cable car. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en (regional activity pages).
- Exact opening dates for the Weggis-Rigi Kaltbad cable car season are not published on the pages
  fetched. See UNVERIFIED.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two named walks
1. **Seepromenade**, the lakeshore promenade linking the boat landing, the village centre and
   Hertenstein, past Café Vienna and the Hertenstein jetty. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch (Café Vienna
   listing, "located directly at the Hertenstein boat station and on the waterfront").
2. **Felsenweg**, on the Rigi between Rigi Scheidegg and Rigi First, reached from Rigi Kaltbad station
   (itself reached by cable car from Weggis or cogwheel train from Vitznau); described by the tourist
   office as one of the most scenic walks on the Rigi, over Lake Lucerne. VERIFIED,
   weggis-vitznau.ch/de/poi/felsenweg

Two named rides/sights
1. **Cable car Weggis-Rigi Kaltbad**, 10 minutes, operated by Rigi Bahnen AG, landing at the car-free
   resort of Rigi Kaltbad. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/poi/luftseilbahn-weggis-rigi-kaltbad
2. **Villa Senar**, Sergei Rachmaninoff's lakeside estate at Hertenstein, built 1931-1933 by architects
   Alfred Möri and Karl Friedrich Krebs; Rachmaninoff bought the property in 1930 and composed the
   Paganini Variations and his Third Symphony there. The park is open 15 May to 31 October, Thursday to
   Sunday, noon to 5pm; the villa itself only for public events or by arrangement; run since April 2023
   by the Serge Rachmaninoff Foundation as a cultural venue. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/de/poi/
   rachmaninoff

Two named tables
1. **Restaurant Central**, Weggis: regional and seasonal, Mediterranean-leaning menu, with a terrace.
   VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/gastro/restaurant-central
2. **Café Vienna**, at the Hertenstein boat station: Swiss and Austrian specialities on the waterfront.
   VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/gastro/cafe-vienna
   Context figure: restaurants in Weggis, Vitznau and on the Rigi together hold three Michelin stars,
   89 GaultMillau points and eight Swiss Bakery Trophy medals. VERIFIED, weggis-vitznau.ch/en/
   weggis-vitznau-rigi/essen-trinken/top-gastronomy

Typical of the place
- **Mild, near-Mediterranean climate**: palms, chestnuts and figs grow in the village, the reason Mark
  Twain called it the loveliest spot on the "Luzerner Riviera" in 1897. VERIFIED, weggis.ch/
  mostcharmingplace
- **Rigi Kaltbad as part of Weggis**: the municipality's highest district, reached only by the Rigi
  Bahnen cable car or, via Vitznau, the cogwheel train; no public road. VERIFIED, weggis.ch/gemeinde
  and aquaspa.ch/de/mineralbad-rigi/besucherinfos/anreise/

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

- Lake Lucerne boats to/from Weggis: Swiss Travel Pass, Swiss Travel Pass Flex and Swiss Transfer Ticket
  give free travel. VERIFIED, lakelucerne.ch (FAQ, "Which tickets or travel passes are valid on the
  lake?").
- Rigi Bahnen network (which includes the Weggis-Rigi Kaltbad cable car): the general Mount Rigi

  accompanied) free"; the individual Weggis-Rigi Kaltbad-Rigi Kulm fare row lists adult and half-fare
  prices without repeating the GA/STP note. VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/inform/prices-discounts and
  rigi.ch/en/inform/prices. Treat the exact Weggis-cable-car coverage as **not fully confirmed
  line-by-line**; see UNVERIFIED.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. *How do you get to Weggis from Lucerne?*
   By SGV boat in just under an hour (summer hourly service), or by AAGS bus line 508 along the lake
   from Küssnacht am Rigi, the nearest railway station. Source: weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/
   anreise-mobilitaet
2. *Is Weggis car-free?*
   No, Weggis itself is reached by road and has its own car parks. Only the Rigi above it, including
   Rigi Kaltbad, is car-free. Source: weggis-vitznau.ch/de/planen/anreise-mobilitaet and aquaspa.ch/de/
   mineralbad-rigi/besucherinfos/anreise/
3. *How do you reach Rigi Kaltbad from Weggis?*
   By the Rigi Bahnen cable car in 10 minutes. Source: weggis-vitznau.ch/de/poi/
   luftseilbahn-weggis-rigi-kaltbad
4. *Why is Weggis compared to the Riviera?*
   Its mild, near-Mediterranean climate lets palms, chestnuts and figs grow; Mark Twain called it the
   loveliest spot on the "Luzerner Riviera" in 1897. Source: weggis.ch/mostcharmingplace
5. *Is the Swiss Travel Pass valid on the Lake Lucerne boats to Weggis?*
   Yes, it gives free travel, along with the Swiss Travel Pass Flex and Swiss Transfer Ticket. Source:
   lakelucerne.ch (FAQ page).
