Note for the page: Davos is a **town in two halves**, Davos Platz and Davos Dorf, each with its own railway station, about 2 km apart along the Promenade. Both are listed by the tourist office as divisions of the municipality.

## 2. ACCESS

All times below are from the Swiss national timetable (SBB / Rhaetian Railway / PostBus data, queried through the Swiss open transport data service on 16/09/2026 for a mid-January 2027 weekday). They are timetable facts, not operator marketing claims.

**By train from Zurich**
- Zürich HB 09:07, IC 3 (SBB) to Landquart 10:11, then RE 13 (Rhaetian Railway) Landquart 10:20 to **Davos Platz 11:27**. Total **2 h 20**, one change.
- Same train to **Davos Dorf 11:22**. Total **2 h 15**, one change.
- Zürich Flughafen (airport) to Chur: 1 h 33 with a change at Zürich HB.

**By train from Chur**
- Chur to Davos Platz via Landquart: **1 h 26**, one change (S 12 Chur to Landquart 8 min, then RE 13).

**By train from Landquart (the junction)**
- Landquart to Davos Platz: **1 h 07**, direct, Rhaetian Railway RE 13, hourly.
- The same line serves Klosters Platz on the way (Landquart to Klosters Platz 38 min).

**By car**
- Yes, you can drive to Davos and park. The through road is the A28 / main road from Landquart via Klosters, or the Albula road from Filisur; the Flüela and Wolfgang passes continue east and north.
- Hotels publish their own garage charges, for example Grischa DAS Hotel "Garage available for CHF 20 per day" and Turmhotel Victoria "Garage CHF 30/day". Public parking tariffs: see UNVERIFIED.

**Local transport**
- The Davos Klosters Premium Card, given to every overnight guest, includes "Free travel (2nd class) by train and bus within zone 400, 410, 420" for guests staying in Davos. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/guest-information/guest-card/premium-card

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. Davos Platz
- The southern half of the town, around Davos Platz station and the Promenade. The Congress Centre (site of the World Economic Forum), the Kirchner Museum (Promenade 82) and the Jakobshorn base station are all here.
- Grischa DAS Hotel, Talstrasse 3, is "directly across from the Jakobshorn station" with "a direct underpass", and "just 10 minutes walk from the convention center". Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/accommodation/hotels/grischa-das-hotel-davos
- The Schatzalp funicular valley station is at Promenade 63, Davos Platz. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/accommodation/hotels/schatzalp-snow-mountain-resort-swiss-historic-hotel

### B. Davos Dorf
- The northern half, around Davos Dorf station, the Parsennbahn funicular and Lake Davos.
- Turmhotel Victoria, Alte Flüelastrasse 2, is "approximately 200 meters from Lake Davos, the Parsenn funicular, and Davos Dorf train station". Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/accommodation/hotels/turmhotel-victoria
- Hotel Seehof Davos, Promenade 159, is described by the tourist office as "located on Davos's promenade near the Parsenn cable car". Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/accommodation/hotels/hotel-seehof-davos

### C. Schatzalp (the terrace above Davos Platz)
- Reached only by the Schatzalp funicular, "four minutes from Davos Platz", 300 m above the town. No through traffic.
- Overnight guests get "Free transport for overnight guests with the Schatzalp funicular railway, daily from 8 am" to midnight. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/accommodation/hotels/schatzalp-snow-mountain-resort-swiss-historic-hotel
- The Alpinum botanical garden and the winter toboggan run (2.8 km) start here. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/mountains-in-davos-klosters/schatzalp-strela

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **Pischa panorama winter walk to Hüreli.** Published by the tourist office as a "6.3 km" winter walking route on Pischa, reaching Hüreli "at 2,444 meters elevation"; a shorter "1.3 km" circular route also starts at the Pischa top station. Winter only (Pischa runs 26 December 2026 to 29 March 2027). Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/activities/snowsports/winter-walks
2. **Thomas Mann Trail, Davos Platz up to the Schatzalp.** Named themed path of Davos Klosters, running from the town up to the Schatzalp, the sanatorium terrace that gave Thomas Mann "The Magic Mountain". Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/activities/hiking/themed-paths and https://www.davos.ch/en/information/portrait-image/storybook/thomas-mann-the-magic-mountain . No published length or walking time (see UNVERIFIED).

**Two named mountain rides / attractions**
1. **Schatzalpbahn funicular**, Promenade 63, Davos Platz to Schatzalp: "four minutes from Davos Platz", 300 m above the town. Season 1 May to 25 October 2026 and 28 November 2026 to 21 March 2027. At the top: the Alpinum alpine garden with "over 3,500 plant species and varieties" that "bloom from June to September", a 500 m summer toboggan run open "from May to October, weather permitting", and a 2.8 km winter toboggan run "Open from mid-December to the end of March" with "bends illuminated until 11 p.m." The Strelapass mountain restaurant is at "2353 m above sea level". Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/mountains-in-davos-klosters/schatzalp-strela
2. **Parsennbahn funicular from Davos Dorf to the Weissfluhjoch.** Serves the ski area with "around 97 kilometers of slopes"; the Weissfluhjoch station area is quoted at "2,620 meters above sea level" at the Schifer / Totalp junction; the run down to Küblis is "a full twelve kilometers". Season 13 November 2026 to 4 April 2027 in winter, 27 June to 6 September 2026 in summer. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/mountains-in-davos-klosters/parsenn and the operating-times page.

**Two named restaurants (listed by the tourist office, no opinion)**
1. **Schatzalp Belle Epoque Restaurant**, Schatzalp, 7270 Davos Platz. "Contemporary cuisine" with vegan options; service 18:00 to 22:00, hot food 18:00 to 20:00; open all year (no closing day published for summer or winter); reached by the Schatzalp funicular. Website www.schatzalp.ch. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/experience/culinary-nightlife/restaurants/schatzalp-belle-epoque-restaurant
2. **Gasthaus zum Tschuggen**, Flüelapassstrasse 33, 7260 Davos Dorf. Seating "in an original Walserstube with 50 seats or in the Arvenstube with 20 seats"; lunch 11:30 to 14:30, small menu 14:30 to 17:00, dinner 18:00 to 20:30; closed Wednesdays all year. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/experience/culinary-nightlife/restaurants/gasthaus-zum-tschuggen

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **Kirchner Museum Davos**, Promenade 82, 7270 Davos Platz, opened in 1992, holds "the largest collection of his works in the world" (Ernst Ludwig Kirchner, who lived in Davos from 1918 until his death). Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/art-culture/museums/kirchner-museum-davos
- **The world's first T-bar ski lift** was built on the Jakobshorn in 1934. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/mountains-in-davos-klosters/jakobshorn
- **Lake Davos**: shoreline "3,770 m"; the lido has a temporary water ski lift of "190 meters" and 4-seater pedalos at "CHF 27 per hour". Sources: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/portrait-image/davos and https://www.davos.ch/en/activities/water-sports/lake-davos
- **The World Economic Forum** meets at the Congress Centre in Davos Platz in January; the lift operator warns its published dates "may be affected by ... World Economic Forum closures in January". Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/mountains-in-davos-klosters/operating-times

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

**Not published** for Davos by any official page checked. The tourist office pricing page for the mountain railways carries no Swiss Travel Pass statement.

What **is** published is the local card, which is the thing worth explaining on the page:
- **Davos Klosters Premium Card.** "Anyone who books at least one night in a hotel, holiday apartment or group accommodation in Davos Klosters receives the Premium Card", issued by Destination Davos Klosters. It gives "Free travel (2nd class) by train and bus within zone 400, 410, 420" for Davos guests (zone 200/210 of the Klosters tariff association for Klosters guests), and reduced mountain railway fares: "CHF 13" for one ascent or descent in low season and "CHF 20" in high season for an adult, half price for children 6 to 17, free below. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/guest-information/guest-card/premium-card
- The mountain railway pricing page adds a "20% discount" on single journeys, return journeys, day tickets and 4-day optional subscriptions for holders of the Davos Klosters Card Private. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/mountains-in-davos-klosters/prices-tickets

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Should I stay in Davos Platz or Davos Dorf?**
   Both have their own station on the same Rhaetian Railway line and are about 5 minutes apart by train. Davos Platz has the Congress Centre, the Kirchner Museum (Promenade 82) and the Jakobshorn base station; Davos Dorf has the Parsennbahn funicular and Lake Davos, and Turmhotel Victoria puts the station, the funicular and the lake all "approximately 200 meters" away. Sources: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/accommodation/hotels/turmhotel-victoria , https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/art-culture/museums/kirchner-museum-davos , timetable (Davos Dorf 11:22, Davos Platz 11:27 on the same RE 13).
2. **Can I get to Davos without a car?**
   Yes. Zürich HB to Davos Platz is 2 h 20 with one change at Landquart (IC 3 to Landquart, then Rhaetian Railway RE 13, hourly). Davos Dorf is 5 minutes earlier on the same train. Source: Swiss national timetable, SBB and Rhaetian Railway data, checked 16/09/2026.
3. **Do I need to buy local bus tickets?**
   No. Every guest who books at least one night in a hotel, holiday apartment or group accommodation gets the Davos Klosters Premium Card from their host, which includes "Free travel (2nd class) by train and bus within zone 400, 410, 420". Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/information/guest-information/guest-card/premium-card
4. **Which hotel gets me closest to the slopes?**
   Grischa DAS Hotel is "directly across from the Jakobshorn station" with "a direct underpass" (Talstrasse 3, Davos Platz); Turmhotel Victoria is about 200 m from the Parsenn funicular (Davos Dorf). Sources: the two tourist office listings above.
5. **When do the lifts actually run?**
   Winter 2026/27: Parsenn Davos 13 November 2026 to 4 April 2027, Jakobshorn 27 November 2026 to 11 April 2027, Pischa only 26 December 2026 to 29 March 2027. Summer 2026: Schatzalpbahn 1 May to 25 October, Parsenn Davos only 27 June to 6 September. The operator warns dates can change and that the World Economic Forum can close things in January. Source: https://www.davos.ch/en/discover/mountains-in-davos-klosters/operating-times
