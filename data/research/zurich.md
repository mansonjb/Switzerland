## 2. ACCESS

- **Zurich Airport to Zurich main station: 10 to 15 minutes by train**, departures **every 5 to 10 minutes
  between 05:00 and midnight**, on the **S2, S16 and regional trains**. Single fare **CHF 7** (children
  CHF 3.50), day pass **CHF 14** (children CHF 7). **Free with the Zürich Card.**
- Alternatives from the airport: **tram line 10, about 35 minutes**, same fare; **taxi 10 to 20 minutes,
  CHF 45 to 70**, base fare CHF 6 plus CHF 3.80 a kilometre, ranks at Arrivals 1 and 2.
- City transport is run under the **Zurich Transport Association (ZVV)**, one ticket for trams, buses,
  trains, boats, funiculars and cable cars.
- **Zürich Card**: unlimited second-class travel on the whole city network for **24 or 72 hours**, including
  the airport transfer, plus free entry to a list of museums. Price not published on the page fetched.
- No parking arrangement to describe: Zurich is not car-free.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. The Old Town: Niederdorf, Lindenhof, Rennweg (Kreis 1)**
- The **Niederdorf runs parallel to the Limmatquai, is a pedestrian zone, and is 597 m, about 3 minutes on
  foot, from Zurich main station**. Shopping by day, bars and street performers in the evening.
- Hotel addresses here: Widder at **Rennweg 7** (nine historic buildings), Storchen at **Weinplatz 2** on
  the Limmat, Marktgasse at **Marktgasse 17**, Adler at **Rosengasse 10**.
- The **Grossmünster** and the **Swiss National Museum (Museumstrasse 2, 137 m from the main station)** are
  both in or beside this quarter.

**B. The lake basin: Bürkliplatz, General-Guisan-Quai, Utoquai, Seefeld**
- The lakeside park encloses the lower basin, along **Mythenquai, General-Guisan-Quai, Utoquai and
  Seefeldquai**, with **Bürkliplatz** as the centre and the boat departure point.
- Baur au Lac is at **Talstrasse 1**, in its own park on the shore.
- The lake baths are along these quays: **Seebad Enge at Mythenquai 9**, Seebad Utoquai, Strandbad
  Mythenquai.

**C. Zurich West and the Hürlimann area (Kreis 5 and Kreis 2)**
- **Zurich West**: former industrial quarter along Pfingstweidstrasse; the 25hours Hotel Zurich West is at
  **Pfingstweidstrasse 102**.
- **Hürlimann Areal**, the old brewery site: the B2 Boutique Hotel at **Brandschenkestrasse 152**, with
  direct access to the Hürlimannbad & Spa and its rooftop pool.
- The youth hostel sits south of this, at **Mutschellenstrasse 114**.

## 4. SEASONS

**Summer**
- The **lake and river baths** are the season. **Flussbad Oberer Letten** has a **400 m swimming channel in
  the Limmat**, **free entry**, 919 m from the main station, built in 1952 to a design by Elsa
  Burckhardt-Blum. **Seebad Enge** (built 1959/60) has **44 m lanes** enclosed in wooden planks and a
  **30 m wooden walkway**, entry **CHF 8**. The **Frauenbad at Stadthausquai** is women only, built in 1837
  and rebuilt in 1888 in art nouveau style, and becomes the **Barfussbar** three evenings a week, when men
  are admitted.
- **Street Parade: 8 August 2026**, around the lake basin.
- Exact bath opening dates are set by the city's sports department, not by the tourist office. UNVERIFIED.

**Spring**
- **Sechseläuten**: the Monday after the vernal equinox; **20 April 2026**, with the children's parade on
  19 April. The guild parade starts at **15:00** with about **3500 guild members** going down
  Bahnhofstrasse, turning at Bürkliplatz, crossing the Rudolf-Brun-Brücke and following the Limmatquai to
  Sechseläutenplatz. The **Böögg is 3.40 m tall** and is lit at **18:00**; its head is packed with
  fireworks, and a fast explosion is said to promise a fine summer.

**Winter**
- **Grossmünster tower hours: November to February, Monday to Saturday 10:00 to 17:00** (March to October
  10:00 to 18:00), Sundays after the service.
- **Swiss National Museum**: closed Mondays; Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday to Sunday 10:00 to 17:00, Thursday
  10:00 to 19:00.

**Boats, all year**
- ZSG published trip lengths from **Bürkliplatz**: **mini cruise 1 h, short cruise 1.5 h, standard cruise
  2 h, upper-lake cruise 3 h (weekends and holidays), long cruise 4.5 h**. The **Limmat river cruise** runs
  from the **Landesmuseum** and takes about **1 hour**. First class only on the long cruises; catering only
  on short and long cruises.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2, with published figures)**
1. **The Planet Trail on the Uetliberg** — an easy family walk that **takes about two hours**, running from
   the sun to Pluto past the eight planets, at a scale of **one metre to one million kilometres**.
2. **Niederdorf, the old town promenade** — a pedestrian route parallel to the Limmatquai, **597 m, about
   3 minutes, from the main station**, running through Niederdorf and Oberdorf as far as Bellevue.

**Rides and sights (2, plus the lake baths the brief asks for)**
1. **Uetliberg observation tower** — the tower rises **70 m** and you climb **178 steps** to a 360-degree
   view of the city, the lake and the Alps.
2. **Grossmünster, Karlsturm** — the church stands on the graves of the city's patron saints Felix and
   Regula. The **Karlsturm** has **187 steps** to a platform with **six large panoramic windows**.
   **Adults CHF 5**, children 6 to 16 and students CHF 2, **free with the Zürich Card**. Open March to
   October 10:00 to 18:00 and November to February 10:00 to 17:00, Monday to Saturday, Sundays after the
   service. Inside: **stained glass by Sigmar Polke**, **choir windows by Augusto Giacometti** and
   **bronze doors by Otto Münch**.
3. **The lake baths** — see SEASONS. Three named and sourced: **Flussbad Oberer Letten** (400 m channel in
   the Limmat, free), **Seebad Enge** (Mythenquai 9, 44 m lanes, CHF 8, sauna out of season),
   **Frauenbad Stadthausquai** (women only, 1837 and 1888, Barfussbar three evenings a week).
4. **Swiss National Museum**, Museumstrasse 2 — the history of Switzerland from the beginning to the
   present, in a historicist building by **Gustav Gull** with a modern extension. A free permanent
   exhibition, **Simply Zurich**, tells the city and canton through **60 objects**. **Adults CHF 13**,
   under 16 free, **free with the Zürich Card**.

**Restaurants and cafés (2)**
1. **Restaurant Bar Seebad Enge** — listed by Zürich Tourismus among the city's bars and lounges, at the
   Seebad Enge on Mythenquai 9: a lake bath that serves food and drink, with a sauna outside the swimming
   season. (Listing URL: zuerich.com/en/visit/bars-lounges/restaurant-bar-seebad-enge; I read the Seebad
   Enge nature listing, not the bar listing, so refetch the bar page before publishing a description.)
2. **Swiss Chuchi, at Hotel Adler** — Rosengasse 10 in the old town. The hotel states it went from being
   the first fondue parlour in Zurich's old town to a house for Swiss cheese and meat specialities.
   Open Monday to Friday 11:30 to 23:15, Saturday and Sunday 12:00 to 23:15.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do I get from the airport into town?** By train in 10 to 15 minutes, every 5 to 10 minutes from
   05:00 to midnight, CHF 7 single or CHF 14 for a day pass, and free with the Zürich Card. Tram 10 takes
   about 35 minutes; a taxi is CHF 45 to 70. (zuerich.com)
2. **Can you really swim in the city?** Yes. The Flussbad Oberer Letten has a 400 m swimming channel in the
   Limmat with free entry, less than a kilometre from the main station; Seebad Enge on Mythenquai has 44 m
   lanes and charges CHF 8. (zuerich.com)
3. **What is the Frauenbad?** An art nouveau open-air bath for women at Stadthausquai 12, first built in
   1837 and rebuilt in 1888, which turns into the Barfussbar three evenings a week, when men are also
   admitted. (zuerich.com)
4. **Is the Zürich Card worth it?** It gives unlimited travel on the city network for 24 or 72 hours plus
   the airport transfer, and free entry to the Grossmünster tower and the Swiss National Museum, which
   otherwise cost CHF 5 and CHF 13. (zuerich.com)
5. **What happens at Sechseläuten?** On the Monday after the vernal equinox, around 3500 guild members
   parade from 15:00 and the Böögg, a 3.40 m snowman with fireworks in its head, is burned at 18:00. In
   2026 it falls on 20 April. (zuerich.com)
