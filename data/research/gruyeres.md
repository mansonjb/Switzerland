**Car-free is the defining fact of this page.** The castle states: "The town of Gruyeres is car-free.
Allow 10 to 20 minutes to walk through it to reach the castle from the various car parks."
La Gruyere Tourisme states access to the town is by three paid car parks.

## 2. ACCESS

**From Geneva airport, by train** (Swiss timetable, sample 15/10/2026 10:00)
- **2 h 12** with two changes: IR 15 to Romont FR, RE 3 to Bulle, S 50 to Gruyeres.
- **2 h 26** with two changes via Lausanne and Palezieux.
- **2 h 43** with one change: IR 15 to Palezieux, S 51 to Gruyeres.
- There is no direct train from the airport.

**From Lausanne, by train**
- **1 h 23 to 1 h 31**, always one change at **Palezieux** (R 8 or R 9 to Palezieux, then S 50 or
  S 51 all the way to Gruyeres).

**Other useful legs**
- Bulle to Gruyeres: **7 to 8 min** (S 50 / S 51).
- Fribourg to Gruyeres: **53 min**, one change at Bulle.
- The S 50 and S 51 are Transports publics fribourgeois (TPF) services.

**By car and parking**
- You can drive to Gruyeres but **not into it**. Three paid car parks serve the medieval town, and
  the castle asks visitors to allow **10 to 20 minutes on foot** from them.
  Sources: chateau-gruyeres.ch/en/information/, fribourg.ch.

**Station note**
- The railway station called "Gruyeres" is at **Pringy**, at the foot of the hill, next to
  La Maison du Gruyere (Place de la Gare 3, 1663 Pringy-Gruyeres). The Hotel de Gruyeres publishes
  that from the station it is about a **15-minute walk**, or a bus to the "Gruyeres Ville" stop
  and then **5 minutes on foot**. Source: gruyereshotels.ch.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. Inside the medieval town (Rue du Bourg, the single street)**
- Car-free, one cobbled street climbing from the gate to the castle.
- The castle, the HR Giger Museum and the Tibet Museum are all on it.
- Walking time from any car park: 10 to 20 minutes.
- Hotels here: Hotel La Fleur de Lys.

**B. The entrance of the town, by the gate and the car parks**
- The top of the hill just outside the walls, where cars stop.
- Hotel de Gruyeres is at Ruelle des Chevaliers 7, at the entrance of the medieval city, with a
  view over Mount Moleson and the town.

**C. Pringy, by the station and the cheese dairy**
- The valley floor, at the railway station, with La Maison du Gruyere and its shop and restaurant
  (Place de la Gare 3, 1663 Pringy-Gruyeres).
- About 15 minutes on foot below the town, or a short bus ride.
- Practical if you arrive by train and want the cheese dairy first.

## 4. SEASONS

- **Chateau de Gruyeres**: April to October **09:00 to 18:00**, November to March
  **10:00 to 17:00**, last entry 45 minutes before closing. Source: chateau-gruyeres.ch.
- **La Maison du Gruyere**, exhibition: June to September **09:00 to 18:30**; October to May
  **09:00 to 18:00** (09:00 to 17:00 on weekdays from 5 to 30 January, and closed 12:30 to 13:15).
  Last admission 30 minutes before closing. On 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January the exhibition
  closes at 16:00. Source: lamaisondugruyere.ch.
- **Cheese making** is visible **2 to 4 times a day in the morning, between 09:00 and 12:30**,
  depending on the season; one production takes **two and a half hours**.
  Source: lamaisondugruyere.ch.
- The Marche Gruerien shop opens 07:30 to 19:00 (June to September) and 07:30 to 18:30
  (October to May); the restaurant 07:30 to 19:00 Monday to Saturday in summer, from 08:00 on
  Sundays. Source: lamaisondugruyere.ch.
- **Fixed events with a published date: none found for Gruyeres in this session.** Do not print one.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **The walk up from the car parks into the town.** The castle publishes the only official figure
   there is: **10 to 20 minutes on foot** through the car-free town from the car parks to the castle.
   Source: chateau-gruyeres.ch/en/information/.
2. **The Cheese dairy path (Sentier des fromageries).** Published by La Maison du Gruyere as one of
   its "visits and discoveries". Source: lamaisondugruyere.ch/visits-discoveries/cheese-dairy-path/.
   **No distance or walking time is published there**; do not invent one. If the page needs a second
   walk with a number, use the 10 to 20 minutes above and say plainly that the trail length is not
   published.

**Two named sights**
1. **Chateau de Gruyeres**, Rue du Chateau 8, 1663 Gruyeres. **13th century**, former residence of
   the Counts of Gruyeres, "eight centuries of art and history". Adults **CHF 13**, seniors and
   students CHF 9, children 6 to 15 CHF 5, free under 6, family ticket (2 adults and up to
   3 children) **CHF 29**. Combined ticket with La Maison du Gruyere **CHF 17**.
   Sources: chateau-gruyeres.ch, lamaisondugruyere.ch.
2. **HR Giger Museum**, in the medieval town, devoted to the Oscar-winning artist who designed the
   creature in Alien. Listed by La Gruyere Tourisme alongside the **Tibet Museum**.
   Source: fribourg.ch/en/la-gruyere/architecture-and-monuments/medieval-town-of-gruyeres/.
   **No opening hours or price were fetched from the museum's own site**; do not print any.

**The typical Gruyeres facts, from the cheese dairy itself**
- La Maison du Gruyere has **four vats of 4 800 litres**.
- It makes **up to 48 wheels of Gruyere a day**.
- Its cellar matures **7 000 wheels**.
- **22 farmers** deliver their milk **twice a day**.
- One production run lasts **two and a half hours**; you can watch 2 to 4 a day between 09:00 and 12:30.
- Admission: adults **CHF 8**, students from 12 and seniors CHF 6, family (2 adults plus children
  under 12) CHF 12, combined with the castle CHF 17. Tickets are sold **on site only**, there is no
  online museum ticket. Free for holders of the Passeport Musees Suisses or an equivalent museum pass.
- Source for all of the above: lamaisondugruyere.ch.

**Town recognition**
- Gruyeres was named a UNWTO **"Best Tourism Village" in 2021** and "most beautiful village in
  western Switzerland" in 2014. Source: fribourg.ch.

**Two named tables**
1. **Chalet de Gruyeres**, in the medieval town, part of the same house as the Hotel de Gruyeres.
   Its own site lists fondue, raclette and Gruyere double cream among the regional specialities.
   Source: gruyereshotels.ch/en/chalet-de-gruyeres.
2. **Table des Chevaliers**, in the medieval town, the restaurant of the Hotel de Gruyeres, with
   regional specialities including fondue and raclette. Source: gruyereshotels.ch/en/table-des-chevaliers.
3. (Spare, same house) the **Fondue Academy**, a fondue workshop run at the same address.
   Source: gruyereshotels.ch/en/fondue-academy.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Can I drive into Gruyeres?**
   No. The town is car-free. Three paid car parks serve it and the castle asks visitors to allow
   10 to 20 minutes on foot from them.
2. **Where is the Gruyeres railway station?**
   At Pringy, at the foot of the hill, next to La Maison du Gruyere at Place de la Gare 3. From
   there it is about a 15-minute walk up to the town, or a bus to the "Gruyeres Ville" stop and
   5 minutes on foot.
3. **When can I watch the cheese being made?**
   Between 09:00 and 12:30, two to four times a day depending on the season. One production run
   takes two and a half hours.
4. **How much is the castle?**
   CHF 13 for adults, CHF 9 for seniors and students, CHF 5 for children aged 6 to 15, free under 6,
   CHF 29 for a family of 2 adults and up to 3 children. A combined ticket with La Maison du Gruyere
   costs CHF 17.
5. **How long does it take to get there from Geneva airport?**
   2 h 12 at best, with two changes (Romont FR and Bulle). From Lausanne it is about 1 h 25 with
   one change at Palezieux. There is no direct train.
