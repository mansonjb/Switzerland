# Research dossier: St. Gallen

Checked 16/09/2026. Official sources: City of St. Gallen (stadt.sg.ch), St.Gallen-Bodensee Tourismus
(st.gallen-bodensee.ch), Switzerland Tourism (myswitzerland.com), Textile Museum St.Gallen

## 1. IDENTITY

- Slug on the sheet: `st-gallen`. Region key `eastern`. Canton: **St. Gallen**.
- Altitude: **669 m** at the train station / Bahnhofplatz (stadt.sg.ch, "Die Stadt in Zahlen"), cross-checked
  against Wikipedia which gives the same figure. The municipality's full range runs from 496 m
  (Goldachtobel, lowest point) to 1074 m (Birt, highest point).
- Coordinates: 47.4242 / 9.3708 (city centre, cross-checked on Wikipedia).
- carFree: **false**.
- Resident population: **83,403** (stadt.sg.ch, "Die Stadt in Zahlen", as of August 2026).
- What defines the place practically: the **Abbey District (Stiftsbezirk), with the Baroque cathedral and
  the Abbey Library, sits inside the medieval old town**, itself ringed by 111 carved bay windows
  (Erker); the whole thing is a five-minute walk from the main train station.

## 2. ACCESS

- **Zurich HB to St. Gallen: direct trains, journey time around 1 hour** (SBB timetable; both a 55-minute
  and a 59-minute figure are quoted by third-party timetable sites depending on the specific train, so no
  single minute figure is asserted here beyond "around an hour"). UNVERIFIED for an exact single minutes
  figure.
- **Zurich Airport to St. Gallen (Radisson Blu's own directions): IC1 train to St. Gallen, then bus 6 to
  Olma Messen, about 1 hour total**; by car around 80 km, about 1 hour via the A1 (radissonhotels.com).
- City transport: **VBSG (st.galler bus)** runs the city network, described by the tourist office as
  covering over 300 stops on 11 trolleybus and bus lines.
- **Mobility Ticket St.Gallen-Bodensee**: a guest card issued by participating accommodation providers
  that gives **free public transport in OSTWIND fare zones 210, 211 and 231** (covers the city and the
  region out to Lake Constance), valid from the day of arrival to the day of departure
  (st.gallen-bodensee.ch).
- No parking arrangement to describe: St. Gallen is not car-free.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. The Abbey District and Old Town**
- The cathedral, the Abbey Library and the 111 bay windows are all here. Einstein St.Gallen is at
  **Berneggstrasse 2**, about 3 minutes from the train station and next to the Abbey District. Hotel Dom
  is at **Webergasse 22**, described by the house as being "in the middle of the monastery quarter".

**B. The station quarter (Bahnhofplatz)**
- Hotel Metropol is at **Bahnhofplatz 3**, directly across from the main station, the only 3-star hotel in
  the pedestrian zone in the heart of town. Hotel Walhalla is "a few steps from the train station",
  first opened as a hotel in **1887**.

**C. OLMA / University / Drei Weieren side**
- Radisson Blu Hotel St. Gallen is at **St Jakob-Strasse 55**, directly across from the OLMA exhibition
  grounds, 0.25 mi (0.39 km) from the University of St. Gallen and 1.12 mi (1.80 km) from Drei Weieren
  (radissonhotels.com). St. Gallen Youth Hostel is further out at **Jüchstrasse 25**, a 10-minute walk from
  the centre, with a view as far as Lake Constance and Drei Weieren 18 minutes away on foot
  (youthhostel.ch).

## 4. SEASONS

**All year**
- Cathedral: **free entry**, open Monday to Wednesday 06:00-18:30 (until 19:00 in summer), Thursday to
  Saturday 07:00-18:30 (until 19:00 in summer), Sunday 07:30-20:30, closed to visitors during services and
  Sunday mornings.
- Abbey Library: open all year, **closed for about 3 weeks in November/December** (myswitzerland.com). No
  exact closure dates published for 2026; UNVERIFIED beyond "closed a few weeks in Nov/Dec".
- Textile Museum: open **Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00-17:00, all year**, closed 24/25 December
  (textilmuseum.ch).

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2, with published length or time, both from stadt.sg.ch)**
1. **Stadtspaziergang (self-guided city walk)** through the old town, **about 1.5 hours** (stadt.sg.ch,
   "Themenwege und Wanderwege").
2. **Weiherwegroute 1 to Drei Weieren**, the shortest of the five official Weiherweg routes past the
   three ponds above the city, **1 hour 40 minutes** (stadt.sg.ch).

**Rides and sights**
1. **Abbey Library (Stiftsbibliothek)** — built **1758-1767** by architect **Peter Thumb**, a Rococo hall
   holding **around 170,000 books**, including **over 400 volumes more than 1000 years old**, and a
   **2,700-year-old Egyptian mummy, Shepenese** (myswitzerland.com). **Adults CHF 18** for the combined
   Abbey ticket, **CHF 12** for students and apprentices, **free for children under 16** with a parent
   (myswitzerland.com, "Combined ticket for the Abbey of St. Gall"). Cameras are not allowed inside
   (widely reported by visitors; no official statement fetched, so treat as UNVERIFIED for the exact
   rule).
2. **St. Gallen Cathedral** — Baroque, **two towers**, mid-18th century, free entry (hours above); part of
   the Abbey District UNESCO listing since **1983**.

**Sights typical of the place (the bay windows and the abbey precinct)**
1. **The 111 carved bay windows (Erker)** of the old town, dating from the late Middle Ages to modern
   times, historically the mark of the town's textile merchants (myswitzerland.com, "Old Town of
   St. Gallen").
2. **Textile Museum**, Vadianstrasse 2 — **adults CHF 12**, students/apprentices up to 26 **CHF 5**,
   under-18 free, groups of 10+ **CHF 9 per person**, open Tuesday-Sunday 10:00-17:00 (textilmuseum.ch).

**Restaurants (2, from the St.Gallen-Bodensee Tourismus directory)**
1. **Wirtschaft zur alten Post** — next to the Abbey District, listed by the tourist office.
2. **Schützengarten** — brewery, restaurant and bar at the main train station, home of the Schützengarten
   brewery (st.gallen-bodensee.ch).

## 6. HOTELS (6 read and loaded on 16/09/2026)

1. **Einstein St.Gallen** — https://einstein.ch/en/ — Berneggstrasse 2, Abbey District edge
   - About 3 minutes' walk from the train station, next to the monastery district

   - Four dining venues on site: Einstein Gourmet, Bistro St.Gallen, Wine Cellar, Einstein Bar
2. **Hotel Dom** — https://hoteldom.ch/en/ — Webergasse 22, monastery quarter
   - Building modernised in stages, attic reconstruction completed in 2019
   - Around 55 people with increased support needs train or work at the hotel, run under Stiftung
     Förderraum
   - ibex fairstay silver sustainability certification; on-site Stadtbistro restaurant
3. **Hotel Metropol** — https://www.hotel-metropol.ch/ — Bahnhofplatz 3, opposite the station
   - The only 3-star hotel in the pedestrian zone in the heart of St. Gallen
   - 32 rooms; restaurant O'Premier with views over the station square
4. **Hotel Walhalla** — https://hotelwalhalla.ch/ — a few steps from the train station
   - First opened as a hotel in 1887; 100 rooms
   - Brasserie Walhalla and the Walhalla Bar; six conference rooms

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do I get from Zurich to St. Gallen?** Direct trains run about every 20 to 30 minutes and take
   around an hour. (SBB)
2. **Is the cathedral free to visit?** Yes, at any time outside services, roughly 06:00/07:00 to 18:30 or
   19:00 depending on the day and season, and 07:30-20:30 on Sunday.
3. **What does the Abbey Library cost?** The combined Abbey ticket (cathedral and library) is CHF 18 for
   adults, CHF 12 for students and apprentices, and free for children under 16 with a parent.
   (myswitzerland.com)
4. **Do I need a bus ticket if I'm staying the night?** No: participating hotels issue the Mobility Ticket
   St.Gallen-Bodensee, free public transport in the city and out to Lake Constance for the length of the
   stay. (st.gallen-bodensee.ch)
5. **What are the bay windows everywhere in the old town?** The 111 carved Erker, mostly built by the
   town's historic textile merchants, some with carved names like the Camel or the Pelican.
   (myswitzerland.com)
