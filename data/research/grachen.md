## 2. ACCESS

Nearest railheads: **Visp** (main line) and **St. Niklaus** (Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn towards Zermatt).
No railway reaches Grächen.

- **From St. Niklaus station**, postbus to the village centre of Grächen in **20 minutes**.
  Source: graechen.ch/en/Info-Service/A-Z/Bahnhof-St.Niklaus_p_598
- The tourist office states trains run to Visp or St. Niklaus with **hourly bus connections** through
  the day, and points visitors to the SBB route planner.
  Source: graechen.ch/en/Info-service/Getting-here-public-transport
- **By car.** Grächen is partly car-free. Car parks named by the tourist office: the **Millegga car park**
  at **CHF 15 a day** for a covered space, the **Autosilo Grächen** indoor car park, plus several private
  operators. Electric taxis deliver luggage from the car parks to the accommodation, bookable in advance,
  at **CHF 20 to 25** depending on the route.
  Source: graechen.ch/en/Info-service/Getting-here-public-transport
- **Airports.** The tourist office names Zurich, Basel, Geneva and Milan, all continuing by train or bus
  via Visp, with the Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn as the scenic option (same page).
- **Ski bus.** A ski pass gives free use of the ski bus linking **St. Niklaus, Gasenried, Niedergrächen
  and Grächen**.
  Source: graechen.ch/en/Activities/Skiing-and-Sledging

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

**A. Dorfplatz and the car-free old village.**
The historic core, closed to cars, with the village square at its centre. Boutique Hotel Walliserhof is
at Dorfplatz 1 (hotel-walliserhof.ch). Family Hotel Desirée, Dorfweg 462, states it is in the car-free
village centre with the mountain railways and the shops within walking distance (hoteldesiree.ch).
B&B Hotel Alpina, Hofe 454, gives "a few minutes' walk from the village square and the mountain lifts"
(hotelalpinagraechen.ch).
Points: no cars; lifts and shops on foot.

**B. By the Hannigalp cable car (Bärgjistrasse / Dorfstrasse).**
The Grächen to Hannigalp gondola is the main lift, summer and winter. Hotel Gädi, Bärgjistrasse 466,
states it stands **directly next to the Hannigalp cable car** (hotel-gaedi.ch). Aktiv Hotel & Spa
Hannigalp is at Dorfstrasse 246 and runs a free arrival and departure taxi to the village square
(hannigalp.ch).
Points: shortest walk to the gondola; the Hannigalp lift runs 08:30 to 12:15 and 13:15 to 17:30.

**C. Niedergrächen and Gasenried (the outlying hamlets).**
Gasenried, above Grächen, is the start of the Europaweg towards Zermatt and one of the stops on the ski
bus route, along with Niedergrächen and St. Niklaus; the ski bus is free with a ski pass.
Sources: graechen.ch/en/Activities/Skiing-and-Sledging and graechen.ch hiking guide (route 1,
Grächen to Gasenried)
Points: quieter and cheaper than the centre, but you depend on the bus.

## 4. SEASONS

**Winter** (graechen.ch/en/Activities/Skiing-and-Sledging):
- **44 km of ski runs**
- the cable car reaches **2114 m** (Hannigalp), and further lifts go up to **2864 m**
- a snowpark and a family park with children's attractions
- **two toboggan runs on Hannigalp**: an easy one at the family park and a **3 km forest run down to
  Z'Seew**
- the ski pass includes the ski bus St. Niklaus to Gasenried to Niedergrächen to Grächen
**Winter 2026/2027 opening and closing dates are not published** on the pages checked.

**Summer** (graechen.ch/en/time-for-hiking):
- **over 250 km of marked hiking trails**
- the tourist office writes of more than 40 four-thousand-metre peaks and almost 600 glaciers in the
  surrounding region
- a 3-for-2 cable car ticket package is advertised for summer visitors

**Hannigalp cable car hours**, published on the tourist office home page: **08:30 to 12:15 and
13:15 to 17:30**, with an early ride at **07:30** bookable in advance through the tourist office.
Source: graechen.ch/en/

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2), both with figures from the tourist office's own hiking guide.**
1. **Hannigalp to Taa to Grächen (route 11).** 4.97 km, **1 h 55**, graded easy. Comes down from the
   Hannigalp top station to the village.
   Source: graechen.ch/en/Activities/Hiking-outdoor-experiences/Hiking-Guide
2. **Grächen Forest and Nature Loop (route 43).** 5.76 km, **2 h 30**, graded medium.
   Source: same hiking guide
   (Other routes published with figures on the same guide: Grächen to Gasenried to the ridge, 5.81 km,
   3 h 15, medium; Grächen to Egga to Bärgji, 2.69 km, 50 min, easy; Hannigalp to Stafel to
   Chrüterubord to Grächen, 5.51 km, 2 h 30, medium; St. Niklaus to Sparren to Jungtal, 6.38 km, 4 h,
   medium.)

**Mountain rides / attractions (2).**
1. **Grächen to Hannigalp cable car, top station 2114 m.** Runs 08:30 to 12:15 and 13:15 to 17:30, with
   a 07:30 ride bookable through the tourist office. At the top: the Hannigalp family mountain restaurant
   with the SiSu Wolkenland indoor playground and a day nursery, the Hannighüsli restaurant, and two
   bars, Schnööbar and Stafelbar.
   Sources: graechen.ch/en/, graechen.ch/en/Activities/Skiing-and-Sledging,
   graechen.ch/en/Cultural-culinary-highlights/Culinary-joys-in-the-mountains
2. **Europaweg (route 8), Grächen to Zermatt.** 32.73 km, **13 h 55**, graded hard, a mountain hike.
   The long-distance route that leaves from this side of the Mattertal.
   Source: graechen.ch/en/Activities/Hiking-outdoor-experiences/Hiking-Guide

**Restaurants (2), both listed by the tourist office.**
1. **Le Chalet**, in the village. Listed by the tourist office for Valais flavours and traditional Swiss
   cooking, with "Les Trois Fondues", a multi-course fondue meal served with regional wines. It is the
   restaurant of the Family Hotel Desirée.
   Sources: graechen.ch/en/Cultural-culinary-highlights/Culinary-joys-in-the-valley and hoteldesiree.ch
2. **Hannighüsli**, mountain restaurant on Hannigalp. Listed by the tourist office for a mix of
   traditional Valais dishes and international cooking, with vegetarian options and a sun deck; it runs
   in winter.
   Source: graechen.ch/en/Cultural-culinary-highlights/Culinary-joys-in-the-mountains
   (Other tables the tourist office names in the village: Restaurant Walliserkanne, Grächerhof,
   Bärgji-Alp, Thomas-Platter-Stube, Restaurant Hotel Gädi, Restaurant Walliserhof Kuhstall.
   On the mountain: Hannigalp family restaurant, Schnööbar, Stafelbar, Jungeralp.)

**Typical to the place: the fairy-tale gondola and the children's area.**
The Grächen to Hannigalp gondola is a fairy-tale gondola: its cabins are decorated with Grimm tales and
the story lasts as long as the ride. The tourist office presents Grächen as a family mountain village,
with a snowpark, a family park and a nursery at Hannigalp taking children from age 2.
Sources: graechen.ch/en/Activities/Skiing-and-Sledging and
graechen.ch/en/Cultural-culinary-highlights/Culinary-joys-in-the-mountains
NOTE: the detail of ten fairy-tale cabins and a seven-minute ride is carried by skiresort.ch and other
aggregators, not by graechen.ch. It is left in UNVERIFIED.

4. **B&B Hotel Alpina*** Grächen** - https://hotelalpinagraechen.ch/  (verified, HTTP 200)
   Sector: car-free historic village. Facts from the hotel's own site: Hofe 454; a few minutes on foot

tourist office as restaurants attached to houses (Thomas-Platter-Stube, Bärgji-Alp) were not matched to
a verified hotel URL.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do you get to Grächen?**
   Train to Visp or St. Niklaus, then a postbus. From St. Niklaus station the postbus reaches the village
   centre in 20 minutes, with hourly connections through the day.
   Sources: graechen.ch/en/Info-Service/A-Z/Bahnhof-St.Niklaus_p_598 and
   graechen.ch/en/Info-service/Getting-here-public-transport
2. **Can I drive up and park?**
   Yes, but Grächen is partly car-free, so the tourist office tells you to check which zone your
   accommodation is in first. The Millegga car park costs CHF 15 a day for a covered space, and electric
   taxis bring luggage from the car parks to the door for CHF 20 to 25.
   Source: graechen.ch/en/Info-service/Getting-here-public-transport
3. **Is it a good base with children?**
   The tourist office runs a family park and a snowpark on Hannigalp with a nursery taking children from
   age 2, and the gondola up is a fairy-tale gondola. Two hotels in the car-free centre are built around
   families: Desirée has a 90 m2 playroom, and Hannigalp has the only indoor pool in the village.
   Sources: graechen.ch/en/Activities/Skiing-and-Sledging, hoteldesiree.ch, hannigalp.ch
4. **How big is the skiing?**
   44 km of runs, with the cable car reaching 2114 m and the higher lifts 2864 m, plus two toboggan runs
   on Hannigalp, one of them a 3 km forest run down to Z'Seew.
   Source: graechen.ch/en/Activities/Skiing-and-Sledging
5. **What is there to walk in summer?**
   Over 250 km of marked trails. Short options published by the tourist office include Hannigalp to Taa
   to Grächen, 4.97 km in 1 h 55, and the Forest and Nature Loop, 5.76 km in 2 h 30. The Europaweg to
   Zermatt, 32.73 km in 13 h 55, starts from this side of the valley.
   Sources: graechen.ch/en/time-for-hiking and graechen.ch hiking guide
