## 2. ACCESS

**From Geneva airport, by train** (Swiss timetable, sample 15/10/2026 10:00)
- IR 90, **direct, 1 h 14** (10:55 to 12:09)
- IR 95, **direct, 1 h 22** (10:14 to 11:36)
- IC 1 to Lausanne then RE 33, 1 h 26 with one change.

**From Lausanne, by train**
- RE 33, **direct, 20 min** (10:01 to 10:21)
- IR 95, direct, 22 min.

**Onward from Montreux**
- Vevey: RE 33 direct, **6 min**.
- Chateau-d'Oex: MOB PE 30, **direct, 1 h 11**.
- Gstaad: MOB PE 30, **direct, 1 h 38**.
- Rochers-de-Naye: cog railway R 37, **direct, 46 to 48 min**.

**By car and parking**
- Yes, Montreux is reached by road. No car ban and no permit is published.
- Chillon Castle is at Avenue de Chillon 21, 1820 Veytaux, on the lake road south of the town.
- The Grand Lac Excelsior gives its address as Rue de Bon-Port 27 and Hotel Bon Port as
  Rue du Theatre 4, both in the town centre, which is where the paid town parking sits.
  No official municipal parking tariff was fetched; see UNVERIFIED.

**Boats**
- CGN calls at Montreux and at Chillon. The Swiss Travel Pass is valid on all CGN cruises except
  events, as are half-fare and GA travelcards. Source: cgn.ch/en/faq.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. The lakefront promenade and the Casino district**
- The quai runs from Territet to Clarens past the Casino; the Fairmont Le Montreux Palace and the
  Hotel Helvetie (Avenue du Casino 32) both sit on it.
- The Montreux Jazz Festival stages are on and around this front in July.
- MONA Montreux is one street back, at Grand-Rue 81.

**B. The old town on the hill (Rue du Temple, Rue du Pont)**
- Montreux's old quarter above the station, reached on foot from the lake.
- Tralala Hotel is at Rue du Temple 2, and the Restaurant du Pont at Rue du Pont 12.
- Quieter than the quai and closer to the station than to the water.

**C. Territet and Veytaux, towards Chillon**
- The southern end of the commune, on the lake road towards Villeneuve.
- Chillon Castle is at Avenue de Chillon 21, 1820 Veytaux; CGN and the train both stop nearby.
- The Rochers-de-Naye cog railway climbs from Montreux station through Glion and Caux above here.

## 4. SEASONS

- **Montreux Jazz Festival 2027: 2 to 17 July 2027**, the 61st edition. Founded in 1967 by
  Claude Nobs. Source: montreuxjazzfestival.com.
- **Chillon Castle opening hours by season:** April to September 09:00 to 19:00 (last entry 18:00);
  March and October 09:30 to 18:00 (last entry 17:00); November to February 10:00 to 17:00
  (last entry 16:00). Closed 1 January and 25 December. Source: chillon.ch.
- **Rochers-de-Naye cog railway closures for works:** the line is closed
  **14 September to 17 October 2026** and again **1 April to 21 May 2027**.
  Source: montreuxriviera.com/en/P69819. This is the single most important seasonal warning.
- **CGN**: "La Suisse" and "Montreux" sail mid-April to mid-October; "Rhone", "Savoie", "Vevey"
  and "Italie" sail all year. Source: cgn.ch/en/faq.
- **Lutry Grape Harvest Festival, 25 to 27 September 2026**, listed by Montreux Riviera
  (Lutry is on the Lausanne side of Lavaux). Source: montreuxriviera.com agenda.
- **Fete des Vignerons**: the 2019 edition ran 18 July to 11 August 2019 and the festival has been
  on the UNESCO Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity since
  1 December 2016. **No date is published for the next edition.** Do not print one.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **Lavaux vineyard terraces, Lutry to Saint-Saphorin.** 11.1 km, published walking time **03:15**,
   +369 m / -393 m, graded "easy", station to station. Published by the Montreux Riviera tourist
   office itself. Source: montreuxriviera.com/en/V1433. Reached from Montreux by train via Vevey.
2. **Rochers-de-Naye trails and via ferrata.** The tourist office lists the hiking trails, the via
   ferrata, the Rambertia alpine botanical garden and two restaurants (Buvette de Jaman and the
   Plein Roc panoramic restaurant) as what is open at the top in summer.
   Source: montreuxriviera.com/en/P69819. No distance or time is published there; do not invent one.

**Two named rides or sights**
1. **Chillon Castle**, Avenue de Chillon 21, 1820 Veytaux. Adults **CHF 15**, children CHF 7.
   Described by the castle itself as "Switzerland's most visited historical monument".
   Hours as in section 4. Source: chillon.ch.
2. **Montreux to Rochers-de-Naye cog railway.** Reaches **2 042 m** in **55 minutes** from Montreux,
   published by the tourist office; the Swiss timetable shows 46 to 48 minutes on the sample date.
   At the top: La Rambertia alpine botanical garden, the via ferrata, two restaurants.
   Source: montreuxriviera.com/en/P815 and /en/P69819.

**Also worth a line, with a published fact**
- **GoldenPass Express**: launched 11 December 2022, Montreux to Interlaken in **3 h 15** with no
  change, thanks to a variable-gauge bogie that switches from metre gauge (1 m) between Montreux
  and Zweisimmen to standard gauge (1.435 m) from Zweisimmen to Interlaken. Two return services a
  day. Source: MOB press pages and the GoldenPass Express entry.

**Two named tables**
1. **Le Palais Oriental**, Montreux. Its own site describes a Moorish building on the shore of Lake
   Geneva serving the cooking of Iran, Lebanon and Morocco. Source: palaisoriental.ch.
2. **Brasserie J5**, Montreux, on the lake. Its own site publishes continuous service
   **12:00 to 22:00** and a seasonal market-based menu, with limited tables and booking advised.
   Source: brasseriej5.ch.
3. (Spare) **Restaurant du Pont**, Rue du Pont 12, 1820 Montreux, in the old town. Source: restaurantdupont.ch.

  Source: chillon.ch/en/free-entry-to-chillon-castle-with-swiss-travel-pass/.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How long is the train from Geneva airport to Montreux?**
   1 h 14 direct on the IR 90, 1 h 22 direct on the IR 95. No change is needed.
2. **When is the Montreux Jazz Festival?**
   2 to 17 July 2027, the 61st edition. The festival was founded in 1967 by Claude Nobs.
3. **Is Chillon Castle in Montreux?**
   It is at Avenue de Chillon 21, in Veytaux, at the southern end of the Montreux Riviera, reached
   by train, bus or CGN boat. Adults pay CHF 15, or nothing with a Swiss Travel Pass carrying the
   Swiss Museum Pass option.
4. **Can I go up the Rochers-de-Naye?**
   The cog railway reaches 2 042 m in 55 minutes from Montreux, but the line is closed for works
   from 14 September to 17 October 2026 and again from 1 April to 21 May 2027.
5. **Can I reach Interlaken without changing trains?**
   Yes. The GoldenPass Express runs Montreux to Interlaken in 3 h 15 with no change, using a
   variable-gauge bogie at Zweisimmen. It started on 11 December 2022 and runs twice each way a day.
