# Research: Engelberg

Checked 16/09/2026. Only official sources: Engelberg-Titlis Tourismus AG (engelberg.ch), Zentralbahn
(zentralbahn.ch), Kloster Engelberg, the hotels' own sites. Wikipedia used only to cross-check altitude
and coordinates. Anything not confirmed on such a page is in UNVERIFIED.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Altitude: the tourist office says Engelberg "stretches from 1,000 to 3,020 metres above sea level" and
  that the village lies "in a wide high valley at around 1,000 metres above sea level".
  VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/
  Cross-check: Wikipedia gives the monastery church at 1013 m and Titlis, the highest point of the
  municipality, at 3238 m.
- Coordinates (village centre, monastery / station area): 46.8210 N, 8.4090 E.
  Cross-check only, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engelberg (46°49'15.7"N 8°24'32.3"E).
- Canton: Obwalden. VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/ ("The municipality belongs to the canton
  of Obwalden"). Engelberg has been an enclave within Obwalden since the French Revolution
  (same page).
- Region key: `central`.
- Car-free: **false**. You drive in on the main road from Stans and park in the village.
  VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/location-arrival/
- Residents: "a population of around 4,400". VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/
  Cross-check: Wikipedia gives 4,194 residents at December 2020.
- Beds: 1,956 hotel beds, around 1,000 beds in tourist hostels and guesthouses, approximately 3,200
  beds in holiday apartments, plus a campsite open all year. Over 800,000 overnight stays a year.
  VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/

## 2. ACCESS

From Lucerne
- Luzern to Engelberg in **43 minutes** on the Luzern-Engelberg Express. VERIFIED, zentralbahn.ch
  (de/erleben/freizeit/luzern-engelberg-express): "Sie reisen in nur 43 Minuten von Luzern nach Engelberg."
- The tourist office words the same leg as "with the Zentralbahn in 45 minutes through varied landscape
  and gorges up to Engelberg". VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/location-arrival/. The page keeps both figures
  rather than picking one.
- Boat combination: Lucerne by boat to Stansstad or Buochs (the same page also names Beckenried), then
  public bus and train to Engelberg. VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/location-arrival/

From Zurich
- Zurich airport to Lucerne: train connections every half hour, about 1 hour. Then the Zentralbahn leg.
  VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/location-arrival/
- By car: about 1 hour 15 minutes from Zurich, the same from Basel, 30 minutes from Lucerne.
  VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/location-arrival/

By car and parking
- Motorway A2 (Basel to Gotthard), exit Stans-Süd, then the main road 20 km to Engelberg, open all year.
  VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/location-arrival/
- Park+Rail: half-price parking at one of the 550 P+Rail stations. VERIFIED, same page.
- The arrival map lists a village car park at Bierlialp and an electric-car charging station.
  VERIFIED, same page.

Up the mountain
- Engelberg to the Titlis summit station takes about 30 minutes. VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/location-arrival/
  ("In one hour's drive from Zurich to Engelberg and only 30 minutes later you are standing on the TITLIS").
- The Titlis valley station is a few minutes on foot from Engelberg railway station.
  VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/summer/hiking/4-lakes-trail/

Works on the line, autumn 2026 (important for any page published now)
- Rail replacement Stansstad/Dallenwil to Engelberg **from 7 September to 1 November 2026**, for the
  Staldifeld double track in Oberdorf, track rebuilding between Stans and Dallenwil, renewals between
  Wolfenschiessen and the Engelberg tunnel portal, and partial refurbishment of the Engelberg tunnel.
  VERIFIED, zentralbahn.ch/de/kennenlernen/einblicke/bahnersatz-stansstad-dallenwil-engelberg-vom-7-september-bis-1-november-2026
- Follow-up works in the Engelbergertal **2 to 20 November 2026**. VERIFIED, zentralbahn.ch
  (de/kennenlernen/einblicke/nacharbeiten-zur-streckensperre-engelbergertal-2-11-20-11-2026, listed title).
- A **half-hourly service Lucerne to Engelberg is to start in December 2026**, which is what the double
  track is for. VERIFIED, same Zentralbahn page.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

**A. Station square and Bahnhofstrasse**
- The Luzern-Engelberg Express arrives here; 43 minutes to Lucerne.
- The Titlis valley station is a few minutes on foot from the railway station
  (engelberg.ch 4-lakes page).
- Hotel Bellevue-Terminus stands on the station square at Bahnhofstrasse 10 (its own site).

**B. Dorfstrasse and the monastery quarter**
- The Benedictine monastery, founded 1120, and its church are here; Chäs im Kloster is at Klosterhof 1.
- Village shops and most of the 33 village restaurants (engelberg.ch counts 33 restaurants plus 21
  mountain restaurants).
- Alpenclub is at Dorfstrasse 5 (its own site).

**C. Bänklialp, the golf course and the quiet valley floor**
- The 18-hole golf course lies at about 1000 m in Engelberg (baenklialp.ch).
- Hotel Bänklialp is at Bänklialpweg 25, away from the station, with rooms facing the monastery village.
- The Eienwäldli campsite is on this side of the valley (engelberg.ch lodging list).

## 4. SEASONS

Winter (figures from engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/ unless stated)
- 24 ski lifts, **82 km of slopes**, 50% of them snow-guaranteed.
- Valley run 12 km with a vertical drop of 2,000 m.
- 43 km of cross-country trails, 7 km of toboggan runs, 75 km of winter hiking trails, 24 km of
  snowshoe trails, five fun parks and children's play areas.
- "Snow-sure winter sports fun from October to May", an average of over 220 skiing days, described by
  the tourist office as the longest continuous winter skiing season in Switzerland.
  VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/winter/ski-snowboard/
- Titlis glacier at 3,020 m; the glacier cave entrance is on the first level of the summit station and
  admission is free. VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/winter/ski-snowboard/

Summer (same facts page)
- 14 cable cars, over 500 km of hiking trails, 150 km of bike trails, six via ferrata routes, an 18-hole
  golf course, the TITLIS Adventure Park and an outdoor swimming pool.

Titlis and the Rotair
- TITLIS ROTAIR, the **world's first rotating cable car**, opened in **1992**; TITLIS Xpress followed in
  2015. VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/
- The Rotair runs from the intermediate station Stand to the summit station at **3,020 m in 5 minutes**,
  and it is included in the ski ticket. VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/winter/ski-snowboard/
- Titlis Cliff Walk at **3,041 m**, which engelberg.ch calls Europe's highest suspension bridge, a few
  steps from the summit station. VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/winter/ski-snowboard/
- Other named installations at the summit: Ice Flyer, Glacier Park, glacier cave.
  VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/

2026/2027 operating dates
- Not published on the engelberg.ch pages fetched. See UNVERIFIED.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two named walks
1. **4-Lakes Hike (Vier-Seen-Wanderung)**, Trübsee, Engstlensee, Tannensee and Melchsee. The tourist
   office publishes the whole route as a two-day variant of **25 km and 1,400 m of ascent**; starting
   from Trübsee with the Titlis Xpress it is **18.4 km and 600 m**; from Jochpass **16 km and 200 m**;
   the family version is **6.5 km and 200 m**. The starting point is Engelberg railway station.
   VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/summer/hiking/4-lakes-trail/
2. **Walenpfad**: "On the 11 km long route ... you will hardly noticeably cover the approximately 600
   metres in altitude". A combination ticket at **CHF 29.00** is valid on the Zentralbahn, PostBus, the
   Bannalp cableways and the Engelberg-Ristis cableway, sold at the Bannalp and Brunni cableways and at
   Wolfenschiessen and Engelberg stations. VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/summer/hiking/walenpfad-trail/

Two named mountain rides
1. **Titlis**: summit station 3,020 m, Rotair from Stand in 5 minutes, about 30 minutes from Engelberg,
   glacier cave with free admission, Cliff Walk at 3,041 m. VERIFIED, engelberg.ch (pages above).
2. **Brunni**: "At 2,040 metres above sea level lies this sunny destination for hikers".
   VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/. Third option: **Fürenalp at 1,850 m**, same page.

Two named tables
1. **Restaurant Alpenclub**, Dorfstrasse 5, in the Alpenclub house, which also runs Restaurant Ober.
   VERIFIED, alpenclub.ch
2. **Yucatan**, the restaurant on the ground floor of Hotel Bellevue-Terminus on the station square,
   with the BARavan by Yucatan for events. VERIFIED, bellevue-terminus.ch
   Context figure: Engelberg counts 33 restaurants and 21 mountain restaurants.
   VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/

Typical of the place
- **Benedictine monastery**, founded in **1120** by Konrad von Sellenbüren; until the French Revolution
  the abbot was both spiritual and secular lord of the valley, and in 1798 the valley people gained
  political independence. Public guided tours are led by brothers of the community. The "Cultural
  Summer" programme ran **27 June to 8 August 2026**.
  VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/all-year-around/culture-traditions/benediktine-monastery/ and
  engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/
- **Chäs im Kloster Engelberg**, Klosterhof 1: the only cheese dairy in Switzerland inside the walls of
  a monastery, where four vats of milk are turned into the "Engelberger Klosterglocke", a mild cheese
  pressed into the shape of a monastery bell; there is a bistro and a "Mitmach-Chäsi".
  VERIFIED, engelberg.ch listing for Chäs im Kloster Engelberg (Klosterhof 1, 6390 Engelberg) and
  chaes-im-kloster.ch
- First ascent of the Titlis: **1744**. VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/
- Alpine cheese trail: "43 km leisurely hike ... past the most beautiful cheese dairies in the valley".
  VERIFIED, engelberg.ch/en/ (home page) and engelberg.ch/en/summer/alpine-cheese-trail/

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. *How long does the train take from Lucerne to Engelberg?*
   43 minutes on the Luzern-Engelberg Express. Source: zentralbahn.ch, Luzern-Engelberg Express page.
   The Engelberg tourist office words the same leg as 45 minutes. Source: engelberg.ch/en/location-arrival/
2. *Is the railway to Engelberg running normally in autumn 2026?*
   No. Buses replaced trains between Stansstad/Dallenwil and Engelberg from 7 September to 1 November
   2026, with follow-up works in the Engelbergertal from 2 to 20 November 2026. A half-hourly service
   Lucerne to Engelberg is planned from December 2026. Source: zentralbahn.ch construction pages.
3. *How high is Titlis and what is the Rotair?*
   The summit station is at 3,020 m. The TITLIS ROTAIR, which opened in 1992, is the world's first
   rotating cable car and takes 5 minutes from the Stand intermediate station to the summit.
   Source: engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/ and engelberg.ch/en/winter/ski-snowboard/
4. *How long is the ski season?*
   Engelberg publishes snow-sure skiing from October to May, an average of over 220 skiing days, and
   82 km of slopes served by 24 lifts. Source: engelberg.ch/en/winter/ski-snowboard/ and
   engelberg.ch/en/facts-figures/
5. *Can you visit the monastery and the cheese dairy?*
   The Benedictine monastery, founded in 1120, offers public guided tours led by brothers of the
   community, and Chäs im Kloster at Klosterhof 1 is the only cheese dairy in Switzerland inside
   monastery walls, where the Engelberger Klosterglocke is made. Source: engelberg.ch monastery page
   and the engelberg.ch listing for Chäs im Kloster Engelberg.
