Champery is described by Region Dents du Midi as one of the oldest tourist destinations in
Switzerland. It groups with Troistorrents and Val-d'Illiez under the "Region Dents du Midi" tourism
entity and sits inside the Portes du Soleil ski area.
Source: valais.ch/en/destinations/region-dents-du-midi/champery

## 2. ACCESS

**By train.** The AOMC line (Aigle-Ollon-Monthey-Champery), operated by TPC (Transports Publics du
Chablais), runs from Aigle through Ollon, Monthey, Troistorrents and Val-d'Illiez to Champery, the
terminus, "at the foot of the cable car." The line is 23.138 km long. Exact minute-by-minute journey
time is not stated on the Region Dents du Midi transport page checked; it points to the TPC mobility
app and timetable for current times.
Source: regiondentsdumidi.ch/en/information/transportation/travel-on-site/trains-27033/ and
sbb.ch/fr/loisirs-vacances/idees/offre.html/tagesausflug/la-ligne-aomc (line length)

**By car.** Champery is reached by road up the Val d'Illiez from Monthey, on the Rhone valley side of
the canton, near the French border.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

**A. Rue du Village (centre, train terminus and cable car base).**
The main street through the historic core. Hotel Suisse is at Rue du Village 55 (hotelsuissechampery.ch);
Hotel National Resort & Spa is at Rue du Village 47, in operation since 1896
(lenational.ch/regiondentsdumidi.ch); Boutique Hotel Beau-Sejour & Spa is at Rue du Village 114,
open since 1914 (beausejour.ch). Hotel Le Palladium, at Route du Centre Sportif 1, states it is
100 m from the cable car departure and the shops (admin-champery.ch/palladiumdechampery.ch).

**B. Village entrance (Route de Chavalet, arrival side).**
Hotel Le White sits "at the entrance to the village," at Route de Chavalet 3, fully renovated in 2015
(lewhite.ch).

**C. On the mountain (Planachaux, reached only by cable car).**
Hotel Plein Ciel sits at 1800 m, at Planachaux, described by the Region Dents du Midi as "perched in
the mountains above Champery," in the former arrival area of the historic cable car.
Source: regiondentsdumidi.ch/en/396396-hotel-plein-ciel-2833/

## 4. SEASONS

**Winter.** Champery is one of the resort villages of the Portes du Soleil, described by the operator
as Europe's largest cross-border ski and bike domain: 600 km of pistes (307 runs: 39 green, 130 blue,
105 red, 33 black) on 208 lift installations across 12 resorts straddling Switzerland and France.
Source: Portes du Soleil press kit, winter 2025/2026 (portesdusoleil.com)
Locally, the village sits at 1050 m and the Planachaux sector tops out at 2050 m.
Source: en.portesdusoleil.com/les-portes-du-soleil/the-resorts/champery/
**Exact opening and closing dates for winter 2026/2027 are not published** on the pages checked.

**Summer.** The Champery-Croix de Culet cable car and the Planachaux lifts run for hikers and
mountain bikers; a summer shuttle links Champery station/cable car to Barme in 35 minutes on a
winding mountain road.
Source: regiondentsdumidi.ch (Barme e-bike route page, via search result)

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2).**
1. **TVI 4, Barme to Champery** (Tour de la Vallee d'Illiez, stage 4). 11.4 km, 4 hours, 520 m of
   ascent, 980 m of descent, graded moderate. Through alpine meadows and mountain refuges down into
   the village.
   Source: regiondentsdumidi.ch/en/5831750-tvi-4-barme-champery-42724/
2. **Lac Vert, from Croix-de-Culet.** 12 km, 3 hours 45, 620 m of ascent and descent, graded moderate.
   Loop via Chaupalin, Col Portes de l'Hiver, Lac Vert (1980 m) and Col Portes du Soleil back to
   Croix-de-Culet.
   Source: regiondentsdumidi.ch/en/882098-lac-vert-from-croix-de-culet-10705/

**Mountain rides / sights (2).**
1. **Champery - Croix de Culet cable car.** Built in 1987 (Garaventa), rising 899 vertical metres from
   1037 m to 1936 m. Current cabins seat up to 125 each (2 cabins).
   Source: valais.ch (hike access page, via search result) and en.portesdusoleil.com/in-winter/openings-slopes-and-lifts/champery-lifts/
2. **Planachaux.** The main Champery ski/hike sector at 2050 m, reached by gondola from the village;
   site of the former arrival station now occupied by Hotel Plein Ciel.
   Source: en.portesdusoleil.com/les-portes-du-soleil/the-resorts/champery/

**Restaurants (2), both listed by the Region Dents du Midi restaurant directory.**
1. **Le Centre.** In the heart of the village, in an 18th-century chalet; seasonal cuisine.
   Source: regiondentsdumidi.ch/en/5411944-le-centre-16926/
2. **Cafe-Restaurant Le Nord.** One of the oldest establishments in Champery, serving customers since
   the start of the 20th century.
   Source: regiondentsdumidi.ch/en/396365-cafe-restaurant-le-nord-433/

**Typical to the place.**
**Rencontres Musicales de Champery**, a classical music festival, 27th edition **31 July to
14 August 2026**, under the artistic direction of Beatrice Berrut.
Source: monthey.ch/N19134/rencontres-musicales-de-champery-27eme-edition.html and
rencontres-musicales.ch

## 6. HOTELS

1. **Boutique Hotel Beau-Sejour & Spa** - https://www.beausejour.ch/en/  Sector: centre. Facts from
   the hotel's own site: Rue du Village 114; open since 1914 (official opening 1916); 3 chalets;
   spa "Nuage"; run by the Kleinknecht Zurkirchen family.
2. **Hotel Suisse** - https://hotelsuissechampery.ch/  Sector: centre. Facts from the hotel's own
   site: Rue du Village 55; 40 rooms in 5 categories; coworking space.
3. **Hotel National Resort & Spa** - https://www.lenational.ch/en  Sector: centre. Facts from the
   operator's own site: Rue du Village 47; open since 1896; 16 rooms with terrace or balcony.
4. **Hotel Le Palladium** - https://palladiumdechampery.ch/en/our-hotel/  Sector: centre. Facts from
   the operator's own site: Route du Centre Sportif 1; 18 rooms; 100 m from the cable car; guests get
   access to the centre's indoor/outdoor pools, fitness room and ice rink.
5. **Hotel Le White** - https://www.lewhite.ch/en/  Sector: village entrance. Facts from the hotel's
   own site: Route de Chavalet 3; 23 rooms and 2 suites; fully renovated 2015; wellness area with
   sauna.
6. **Hotel Plein Ciel** - Region Dents du Midi listing: https://www.regiondentsdumidi.ch/en/396396-hotel-plein-ciel-2833/
   Sector: on the mountain (Planachaux, 1800 m). Facts from the tourist-office listing: 29 rooms
   including a dormitory and two shared-WC "Refuge" rooms; restaurant, bar, spa. The operator's own
   booking-engine site (swissalpshotels.net) was not treated as a primary source; see UNVERIFIED.

Hotel named on the tourist office's Champery hotel list but left out: Chateauform - Les Chalets de
Champery, a group/seminar accommodation product rather than a standard hotel stay.

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

**Not published for the Swiss Travel Pass specifically.** Portes du Soleil operates its own regional
"Multi Pass" (not the national Swiss Travel Pass) which gives holders free rides on the
Champery-Croix de Culet cable car and all Portes du Soleil lifts, plus a 30 percent discount on
one-way or return pedestrian tickets bought at the Champery cable car ticket office. Whether the
national Swiss Travel Pass itself is accepted was not confirmed on an official page in this pass.
Source: en.portesdusoleil.com/offers/champery-croix-de-culet-cable-car-champery-en-3831109/

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Can you reach Champery by train?**
   Yes, the AOMC line from Aigle runs all the way to Champery, terminating at the foot of the cable
   car; the transport page does not publish an exact journey time and points to the TPC timetable.
   Source: regiondentsdumidi.ch/en/information/transportation/travel-on-site/trains-27033/
2. **How high does the Champery cable car go?**
   From the village at 1037 m to Croix de Culet at 1936 m, a rise of 899 vertical metres, in cabins
   seating up to 125 people.
   Source: en.portesdusoleil.com/in-winter/openings-slopes-and-lifts/champery-lifts/
3. **How big is the Portes du Soleil ski area that Champery belongs to?**
   600 km of pistes across 12 resorts in Switzerland and France, 307 runs and 208 lift installations,
   per the operator's winter 2025/2026 press kit.
   Source: portesdusoleil.com press kit
4. **Is there a hotel actually on the mountain above Champery?**
   Yes, Hotel Plein Ciel at 1800 m in the Planachaux sector, on the site of the former cable car
   arrival station, reachable only by lift or on foot.
   Source: regiondentsdumidi.ch/en/396396-hotel-plein-ciel-2833/
5. **Is Champery only a winter destination?**
   No. The Champery-Croix de Culet cable car and the Planachaux lifts run in summer too, serving
   marked hiking routes such as TVI 4 (Barme-Champery) and the Lac Vert loop.
   Source: regiondentsdumidi.ch route pages, section 5
