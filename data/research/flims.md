Flims is in three parts: **Flims Dorf** (the village and the lift station), **Flims Waldhaus** (the park and hotel quarter towards Lake Cauma) and **Fidaz** (the hamlet on the slope above).

## 2. ACCESS

Times from the Swiss national timetable (SBB / PostBus data), queried 16/09/2026.

- **Zürich HB to Flims Dorf: 1 h 54, one change.** Zürich HB 09:07 IC 3 (SBB) to Chur 10:22, then PostBus line 81 from Chur, Postautostation 10:28 to Flims Dorf, Bergbahnen 11:01.
- **Chur to Flims Dorf: 30 min** by PostBus (line 80 or 81) from the post bus station on top of Chur station. The tourist office publishes "From there erreichst du Flims Laax with the Postauto in weniger als 40 Minuten" (under 40 minutes). Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/anreise
- **No railway station in Flims.** Chur is the railhead.
- **By car:** the tourist office publishes "30 Minuten von Chur", "1 Stunde 45 Minuten von Zürich" and "In 20 Minuten bei uns" from the Reichenau motorway exit. Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/anreise
- **Parking:** several multi-storey car parks in Flims and Laax with height limits; published rates "from free for 30 minutes to CHF 32.80 for 24 hours"; reserved "Inside Parking" spots bookable. Source: https://www.laax.com/en/transportation
- **Airport:** a winter shuttle from Zurich Airport is published at CHF 79, to be reserved 48 hours in advance. Same source.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. Flims Dorf (1081 m)
- The village proper, on the Via Nova, and the valley station of the lifts up to Foppa and Naraus. The PostBus stop is "Flims Dorf, Bergbahnen".
- Hotel Vorab, Via Nova 38, CH-7017 Flims Dorf, publishes a free shuttle bus and free outdoor parking. Source: https://www.hotelvorab.ch/en/
- The Trutg dil Flem gorge walk ends "at Flims Talstation in the village center". Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/aktiv-sein/wandern/trutg-dil-flem

### B. Flims Waldhaus
- The wooded hotel quarter south of the village, closest to **Lake Cauma**. From Flims Waldhaus the forest path to the lake takes about 30 minutes on foot; a free lift drops about 80 m to the shore in about 10 minutes from the car park. Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/seen-schweiz/caumasee
- Waldhaus Flims Wellness Resort & Spa (Via dil Parc 3), Hotel Adula (Via Sorts Sut 3) and Schweizerhof Flims (Rudi Dadens 1) are all in this quarter.
- The Connbächli family walk starts "in Flims Waldhaus beim Caumasee Parkplatz". Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/aktiv-sein/wandern/connbaechli/connbaechli-flims-laax-falera

### C. Fidaz
- A small hamlet on the slope above Flims, the valley station of the **Milchseilbahn "Crap da Flem"** up to the Flimserstein at 2,050 m. FidazerHof is at Via da Fidaz 34, 7019 Fidaz.
- Sources: https://www.flimslaax.com/milchseilbahn and https://www.fidazerhof.ch/

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **Trutg dil Flem.** Published as **8.6 km**, **3 h 30** downhill and **4:30 h** uphill, from the lower Segnesboden (near the source of the Flem) down to "Flims Talstation" in the village centre, crossing **seven bridges** designed by the Grisons engineer **Jürg Conzett**. Passable only in "snow- and ice-free time". It won the main **Prix Rando 2014** of the Swiss Hiking Association. Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/aktiv-sein/wandern/trutg-dil-flem
2. **Connbächli, Flims Waldhaus to Conn.** Published as **3.2 km** out, "etwa eine Stunde", with a **2.5 km** return past Lake Cauma taking "etwa 40 Minuten". Starts "in Flims Waldhaus beim Caumasee Parkplatz". Accessible "von Mai bis Oktober, abhängig von der Schneelage". At Conn is the **Il Spir** viewing platform over the Rhine Gorge, "eine Aussichtsplattform ... die sich über mehrere Etagen erstreckt". Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/aktiv-sein/wandern/connbaechli/connbaechli-flims-laax-falera

**Two named mountain rides / attractions**
1. **Milchseilbahn "Crap da Flem", Fidaz to the Flimserstein.** Top station at **2,050 m**; "The journey between Fidaz and Crap da Flem takes approximately 8 minutes"; "A maximum of 8 persons can be transported per trip"; runs "daily from Tuesday to Sunday". Built to bring milk down from the alp. Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/milchseilbahn
2. **Lake Cauma (Caumasee).** Reached from Flims Waldhaus by a forest path of about 30 minutes, or by a free lift that drops about 80 m and cuts it to about 10 minutes from the car park; the lift runs during restaurant hours, "typically 8 AM in summer and 10 AM in winter". Summer water temperature "zwischen 17 und 24 Grad Celsius". Admission free. Published rules: no dogs in the bathing area (on a lead at the restaurants, off lead on the far shore), no drones or model aircraft, no barbecues or open fires, no nudity, no foil boards, no speaker music. Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/seen-schweiz/caumasee

**Two named restaurants (own site or tourist office listing, no opinion)**
1. **Restaurant CONN**, 7017 Flims, in the forest clearing between Flims and Trin by the Rhine Gorge. Serves "regional Köstlichkeiten" and "Klassiker der schweizer Küche". Summer 2026 season to **18 October 2026**, daily 09:30 to 17:00, hot food 11:00 to 16:00, "keine Ruhetage". Reached on foot in about 45 minutes from Flims Waldhaus or from Trin Mulin, by bike, or on cross-country skis when there is enough snow. Source: https://www.conn.ch/
2. **Legna Bar**, listed by the lift company with "large sun terrace directly at the valley station in Flims". Source: https://www.laax.com/en/food-drinks

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **The Rhine Gorge (Ruinaulta)**, called the "Swiss Grand Canyon" by the tourist office and "unique in the Alps". Seven published viewpoints: **Il Spir** (a "180-Grad-Blick", open all year, 45 minutes on foot from Flims Waldhaus), Islabord (May to October), Zault (March to November), Crap Signina (30 minutes on foot from Laax), Spitg (270 degree view, May to October, needs sure footing), Alix and Wackenau (May to October). The Rhaetian Railway runs through the gorge near water level; a PostBus Cabriolet runs twice a day between Laax and Tamins with reservation; the Valendas to Versam walk takes about 1 h 30. The **Punt Ruinaulta** suspension bridge, finished in 2010, spans 105 m and is 1.8 m wide, "among the longest hanging bridges in Graubünden". Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/naturerlebnisse/rheinschlucht
- **UNESCO Tektonikarena Sardona** is part of the destination. Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/aktiv-sein/wandern

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Flims Dorf or Flims Waldhaus?** Flims Dorf is the village and the lift station (the PostBus stop is "Flims Dorf, Bergbahnen" and the Trutg dil Flem walk ends at the valley station). Flims Waldhaus is the wooded hotel quarter nearest Lake Cauma, about 30 minutes on foot from the lake or about 10 minutes using the free lift down to the shore. Sources: https://www.flimslaax.com/aktiv-sein/wandern/trutg-dil-flem and https://www.flimslaax.com/seen-schweiz/caumasee
2. **Is there a train to Flims?** No. Chur is the railhead; PostBus takes 30 minutes from Chur station, and Zürich HB to Flims Dorf is 1 h 54 with that one change. Source: Swiss national timetable, checked 16/09/2026.
3. **Can I swim in Lake Cauma?** Yes, admission is free, and the published summer water temperature is "zwischen 17 und 24 Grad Celsius". Dogs are not allowed in the bathing area, and there are published bans on drones, barbecues, nudity, foil boards and speaker music. Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/seen-schweiz/caumasee
4. **How big is the ski area?** Flims Laax Falera publishes "216 km Pistenkilometer", 30 lifts, 70% of the terrain above 2,000 m, 5 snowparks and the world's longest continuous halfpipe, plus a 13 km run from La Siala to Flims over 1,700 vertical metres. Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/ski-snowboard
5. **When does the season run?** Winter pre-season 28 November to 17 December 2026, main season 18 December 2026 to 29 March 2027, low season 30 March to 11 April 2027. Source: https://www.laax.com/opening-hours
