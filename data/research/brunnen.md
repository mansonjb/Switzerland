## 1. IDENTITY

- Municipality: Brunnen is one of the two localities of the municipality of **Ingenbohl**, canton
  Schwyz; the welcome page describes Ingenbohl as encompassing "the two locations Brunnen and
  Ingenbohl". VERIFIED, ingenbohl.ch (homepage/willkommen).
- Canton: Schwyz. VERIFIED, ingenbohl.ch and erlebnisregion-mythen.ch (both use the 6440 Brunnen
  postal code and canton Schwyz addresses).
- Region key: `central`.
- Car-free: **false**. Brunnen is on the Axenstrasse road and reachable by car; it is also a mainline
  stop reachable "per Zug, Schiff, Bus oder Auto" (by train, boat, bus or car).
  VERIFIED, erlebnisregion-mythen.ch/sevices/anreise-mobilitaet
- History: Brunnen is named in the oldest Einsiedeln income register (1217-1222), naming "Arnold de
  Brunnon". The Bundesbrief (Federal Charter) confirming the alliance of Uri, Schwyz and Unterwalden was
  "given ze Brunne" on 9 December 1315. In Schwyz's oldest account book (1554-1579) the name Brunnen is
  mentioned "rund 420 Mal" (around 420 times), against roughly 100 for Schwyz itself. On 22 June 1888
  Brunnen's village council asked to merge into the municipality of Ingenbohl; the merger took effect
  16 December 1888, after the village's income from portage declined with the arrival of steamships
  (1837) and new roads (1865). Tourism grew from the 19th century, with Belle Epoque hotels marking
  Brunnen's shift into a resort. VERIFIED, ingenbohl.ch/geschichte
- Tourist information: Schwyz Tourismus AG / Erlebnisregion Mythen, Waldstätterquai 1, 6440 Brunnen
  (boat station), and headquartered at Bahnhofstrasse 15, 6440 Brunnen.
  VERIFIED, erlebnisregion-mythen.ch/de/ and schwyz-tourismus.ch (address block).

## 2. ACCESS

- General statement: "Sie erreichen die Erlebnisregion Mythen bequem per Zug, Schiff, Bus oder Auto"
  (you can reach the region comfortably by train, boat, bus or car).
  VERIFIED, erlebnisregion-mythen.ch/sevices/anreise-mobilitaet
- By boat: Brunnen is a scheduled Lake Lucerne stop; Lucerne's Pier 1 lists "Departures to Weggis,
  Vitznau (Rigi), Brunnen, Flüelen". VERIFIED, lakelucerne.ch (route listing, via lakelucerne.ch/en).
  The Swiss Travel Pass, Swiss Travel Pass Flex and Swiss Transfer Ticket give free travel on these SGV
  boats. VERIFIED, lakelucerne.ch (FAQ page).
- Rütli meadow, the traditional founding site of the Swiss Confederacy, is reached by "one ferry landing
  from Brunnen"; the Swiss Path (Weg der Schweiz) begins there.
  VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/brunnen/
- Up the mountain: cable car **Brunnen-Urmiberg**, operated by Rigi Bahnen AG; its fare row explicitly
  states "GA, Halbtax, Lucerne Travel Pass ... are not accepted", confirming it is a separate line from
  the Swiss Travel Pass network. VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/inform/prices-discounts

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

**A. Waldstätterquai, the lake promenade**
- The SGV boat landing and tourist information sit here; Switzerland Tourism calls Brunnen's lakeside
  promenade "one of the most beautiful in Switzerland". VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/
  brunnen/
- Seehotel Waldstätterhof, Waldstätterquai 6, stands directly on this promenade.

**B. Bahnhofstrasse and the station**
- Schwyz Tourismus AG's own address, Bahnhofstrasse 15, sits in this sector near the railway station.
  VERIFIED, schwyz-tourismus.ch (address block).

**C. Axenstrasse side, toward Morschach and the Urmiberg cable car**
- The Axenstrasse, the historic north-south route along Lake Uri, runs through this side of Brunnen.
  VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/brunnen/
- The Brunnen-Urmiberg cable car valley station is on this side. VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/inform/
  prices-discounts (fare table listing "Brunnen-Urmiberg").

## 4. SEASONS

- Brunnen's tourism draws on lake activities (steamer trips, kayaking, canoeing) in summer and on
  connections to the Rigi cable cars and the Stoos funicular for mountain/winter sports.
  VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/brunnen/
- Exact 2026/2027 seasonal opening dates for the Brunnen-Urmiberg cable car are not published on the
  pages fetched. See UNVERIFIED.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two named walks
1. **Brunnen lakeside promenade**, along the Waldstätterquai, described by Switzerland Tourism as one
   of the most beautiful lake promenades in the country. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/
   destinations/brunnen/
2. **Weg der Schweiz (Swiss Path)**, a 35 km themed trail around Lake Uri created for the 700th
   anniversary of the Confederacy in 1991, starting at the Rütli meadow reached by boat from Brunnen.
   VERIFIED, wiegederschweiz.ch (homepage) and myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/brunnen/

Two named rides/sights
1. **Cable car Brunnen-Urmiberg**, operated by Rigi Bahnen AG. VERIFIED, rigi.ch/en/inform/
   prices-discounts (fare table entry).
2. **Rütli meadow**, one ferry landing from Brunnen, the traditional founding site of Switzerland and
   start of the Swiss Path. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/brunnen/

Typical of the place
- **Schillerstein**, a natural obelisk of rock standing in the lake, visible from Brunnen's promenade
  across the Lake Uri basin. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/brunnen/
- **Bundesbrief history**: the 1315 Federal Charter confirming the alliance of Uri, Schwyz and
  Unterwalden was issued "ze Brunne" (at Brunnen). VERIFIED, ingenbohl.ch/geschichte
- **Axenstrasse**, the historic lakeside road connecting Brunnen south toward Flüelen.
  VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/brunnen/

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. *How do you get to Brunnen?*
   By train, boat, bus or car; Brunnen is a scheduled Lake Lucerne boat stop from Lucerne's Pier 1.
   Source: erlebnisregion-mythen.ch/sevices/anreise-mobilitaet and lakelucerne.ch
2. *What is the historic significance of Brunnen?*
   The 1315 Bundesbrief (Federal Charter) confirming the alliance of Uri, Schwyz and Unterwalden was
   issued at Brunnen; the village merged into the municipality of Ingenbohl in 1888. Source:
   ingenbohl.ch/geschichte
3. *Can you reach the Rütli meadow from Brunnen?*
   Yes, by one ferry landing; the Swiss Path (35 km, created 1991) starts there. Source:
   myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/brunnen/ and wiegederschweiz.ch
4. *Is the Brunnen-Urmiberg cable car covered by the Swiss Travel Pass?*
   No. Its own fare table states that GA, Halbtax and the Lucerne Travel Pass are not accepted. Source:
   rigi.ch/en/inform/prices-discounts
5. *What is the Schillerstein?*
   A natural rock obelisk standing in the lake, visible from Brunnen's promenade. Source:
   myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/brunnen/
