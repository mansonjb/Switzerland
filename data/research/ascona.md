# Ascona research dossier

Checked 16/09/2026. Official sources only (tourist office Ascona-Locarno / Organizzazione turistica Lago
Maggiore e Valli, Monte Verità Foundation, canton of Ticino for the Brissago Islands, SBB timetable,
Federal Statistical Office, hotels' own sites). No ratings, no reviews, no invented numbers.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Altitude: 196 m. Cross-checked on Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascona, infobox elevation 196 m).
  The tourist office does not publish an altitude figure for Ascona.
- Coordinates of the centre: 46.155520, 8.771057, the bus stop "Ascona, Centro" (Swiss public transport
  stop register via transport.opendata.ch/v1/locations?query=Ascona). The old-town stop "Ascona, Borgo"
  is at 46.157367, 8.770105.
- Canton: Ticino.
- Region key: `ticino`.
- Car-free: false. Ascona has no railway station and is reached by road or by bus; the lakefront Piazza
  Giuseppe Motta is pedestrianised but the town itself is not car-free.
- Residents: 5 322 permanent resident population, 31.12.2024, commune 5091 Ascona
  (Federal Statistical Office, STAT-TAB table px-x-0102010000_101).
- Hotel beds: NOT PUBLISHED by the tourist office on any page reached. See UNVERIFIED.

## 2. ACCESS

Ascona has no station. Trains go to Locarno, then a bus.

From Zurich (SBB timetable, weekday sample 22/09/2026, via the Swiss public timetable API
transport.opendata.ch):
- Zurich HB 09:33 (EC 15) -> Bellinzona 11:12, S 20 -> Locarno 11:45, then bus 316 from Locarno, Stazione
  11:50 -> Ascona, Fiume Maggia 11:59. Total 2 h 26, two changes.
- Zurich HB 09:05 (IC 2) -> Bellinzona 10:42, S 20 -> Tenero 11:08, bus 1 -> Ascona, Fiume Maggia 11:36.
  Total 2 h 31.

From Milan:
- Milano Centrale 09:43 -> Locarno 11:38 direct (RE 80), then bus 1 from Locarno, Piazza Stazione 11:44 ->
  Ascona, Fiume Maggia 11:51. Total 2 h 08, one change.

From Locarno:
- Bus 1, Locarno Piazza Stazione to Ascona Fiume Maggia, 7 minutes of riding, about 11 minutes door to door
  including the walk from the station platform; departures roughly every 15 minutes in the sample
  (09:14, 09:29, 09:44).

Driving and parking:
- You can drive. Ascona is reached from the A2/A13 through Locarno. The Swiss motorway vignette costs
  CHF 40 (rheinfall.ch/en/inform).
- Parking figures for the town are NOT PUBLISHED on the tourist office site. See UNVERIFIED.

Boats:
- The Lake Maggiore boats call at Ascona. Ascona to the Brissago Islands takes 15 minutes
  (ascona-locarno.com, Brissago Islands page).

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP

1. Piazza Giuseppe Motta and the lakefront
   The long pedestrian promenade along Lake Maggiore, lined with hotel terraces and cafés, and the departure
   point for the boats. Hotel Piazza Ascona is at Piazza G. Motta 29, Hotel Tamaro at Piazza Giuseppe Motta
   35, and the Seven Boutique Hotel at Piazza Giuseppe Motta 21 and 25 (ascona-locarno.com hotel listings).
   - Ristorante Al Pontile is at Piazzale G. Motta 31 on the same front (tourist office listing).
   - The lakefront is the centre of JazzAscona (see SEASONS).

2. The Borgo, the old town behind the front
   The lanes running back from the lake, with the parish church and its tall bell tower, called by the
   tourist office "the emblem of Ascona" on its lakeside walking route
   (ascona-locarno.com, Ascona Lakeside Route). Bus stop "Ascona, Borgo" at 46.157367, 8.770105.
   - Grotto Baldoria, a stone-table grotto, is at Via Omobono 9 in this part of town (tourist office listing).

3. Monte Verità and the hill above the town
   The wooded hill with the Monte Verità museum complex, park and hotel at Via Collina 84
   (monteverita.org; ascona-locarno.com hotel listing). Quieter than the front, and the place the town is
   culturally known for.
   - The Monte Verità hotel complex includes the Bauhaus Hotel, Villa Semiramis, Casa Monescia, Casa Marta
     and Casa Gioia (monteverita.org).

## 4. SEASONS

- JazzAscona: 24 June to 3 July 2027 on the Lungolago, running 11:00 to 03:00, with around fifteen concerts
  a day; the festival has run since 1985 (ascona-locarno.com/en/events/details/jazzascona-2027/10042).
- Monte Verità museum (Casa Anatta): season 19 March to 8 November, Wednesday to Saturday 14:00 to 18:00,
  Sunday and public holidays 10:00 to 13:00 and 14:00 to 18:00 (monteverita.org, Opening Hours & Tickets).
- Brissago Islands botanical park: 2026 season 18 March to 8 November, boats from Ascona in 15 minutes
  (ascona-locarno.com, Brissago Islands; isoledibrissago.ti.ch).
- Hotel Eden Roc publishes its own split year: summer season 2 April to 24 October 2026, winter season
  25 October 2026 to 25 March 2027 (tschuggencollection.ch, Hotel Eden Roc).
- Romantik Hotel Castello Seeschloss heats its garden pool to 25 degrees from mid or late April to early or
  mid October (castello-seeschloss.ch).
- Locarno Film Festival (4 to 14 August 2027) is 7 minutes away by bus and fills rooms in Ascona too
  (ascona-locarno.com event listing).

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two walks
1. Ascona Lakeside Route, SwitzerlandMobility route 653. Round trip, 6.7 km, 1 h 35, 6 m of ascent and
   6 m of descent, highest point 207 m, lowest point 194 m, difficulty T1 easy. It starts in the alleys of
   the old town, follows the lakeside promenade past the bathing place to the golf course, circles it and
   returns the same way past the church with its tall bell tower
   (ascona-locarno.com/en/hike/details/Ascona-Lakeside-Route-SwitzerlandMobile-653-/801413650).
2. Ascona - Balladrüm - Ascona. Round trip, 5.4 km, 2 h 00, 365 m of ascent and 365 m of descent, highest
   point 481 m, lowest point 203 m, difficulty T2 moderate; listed as public-transport-friendly,
   family-friendly and with refreshment stops
   (ascona-locarno.com/en/hike/details/Ascona-Balladr%C3%BCm-Ascona/62885068).

Two sights or rides
1. Brissago Islands botanical park. Switzerland's only botanical park on an island, on Isola di San
   Pancrazio, the larger of the two islands and the only one open to the public. 25 000 m2, more than 2000
   plant species grouped in five areas representing five continents. Opened to the public on 2 April 1950.
   Villa Emden, the neoclassical villa at the centre, was built in the 1930s by Max Emden and now holds a
   restaurant and hotel; the Roman baths, orangery and dock also date from his time. Season 18 March to
   8 November 2026. Boat from Ascona 15 minutes, from Locarno 40 to 60 minutes
   (ascona-locarno.com, Brissago Islands page; isoledibrissago.ti.ch).
2. Monte Verità museum complex, Casa Anatta. Full price CHF 12.00; reduced CHF 8.00 for over-65s, AVS/AI,
   groups of ten or more and students aged 17 to 25; CHF 4.00 with an IOstudio card; free for under-16s and
   for holders of the Museumspass, the Swiss Travel Pass, the Raiffeisen membership card, ICOM, VMS and
   AG Culturel. Season 19 March to 8 November (monteverita.org, Opening Hours & Tickets).

Two tables
1. Grotto Baldoria, Via Omobono 9, 6612 Ascona. Listed by the tourist office as a Ticinese grotto with
   stone tables and wooden benches serving polenta, alpine cheese and red wine; open daily 12:00 to 14:00
   and 18:00 to 22:30 (ascona-locarno.com/en/restaurants/details/Grotto-Baldoria/74282).
2. Al Pontile, Piazzale G. Motta 31, 6612 Ascona. Listed by the tourist office as an international and
   Italian restaurant on the lakeside with a lake view and a balcony; open daily 09:00 to 23:00
   (ascona-locarno.com/en/restaurants/details/Al-Pontile-Ristorante-Ascona/120512).

Typical, with a published fact
- Monte Verità is the reason Ascona is in the culture books: the Foundation runs a museum complex of
  Casa Anatta, Casa Selma, Casa dei Russi and the Elisarion Pavilion, plus a park, a hotel complex and a
  conference centre used by ETH Zurich (monteverita.org).
- The Brissago Islands enjoy what the tourist office calls a subtropical climate unique in Switzerland,
  which is why species that grow nowhere else in the country grow there
  (ascona-locarno.com, Brissago Islands page).

## 6. HOTELS

1. Castello del Sole Beach Resort & SPA - https://www.castellodelsole.com/en/ - lakefront, Ascona delta.
   Own site: five-star resort with its own farms supplying the kitchens ("from our farms to your table");
   restaurants Locanda Barbarossa, Tre Stagioni and Al Parco plus a beach snack bar; its own winery,
   Cantina alla Maggia, with visits and tastings.
2. Hotel Eden Roc - https://tschuggencollection.ch/en/hotel/hotel-eden-roc - lakefront.
   Own site: part of the Tschuggen Collection; describes itself as "Switzerland's only true beach resort";
   publishes a summer season 2 April to 24 October 2026 and a winter season 25 October 2026 to
   25 March 2027; watersports and spa on site.
3. Romantik Hotel Castello Seeschloss - https://www.castello-seeschloss.ch/welcome/ - Piazza, lakefront.
   Own site: directly on the Piazza of Ascona; three separate buildings around a Mediterranean garden with
   a heated pool (25 degrees from mid or late April to early or mid October); the main house has a
   medieval tower, the others are Casa "Marionette" with a stone tower and Villa "Rosa".
4. Albergo Carcani - https://carcani.ch/en/ - lakefront.
   Own site: hotel and restaurant on the Ticino lakefront; electric car charging station with reservable
   parking spaces; its own spa.
5. Hotel Piazza Ascona - https://www.hotelpiazza.ch/en/ - Piazza G. Motta 29.
   Own site: on Lake Maggiore, with two restaurants of its own, Ristorante al Pontile and Ristorante
   al Piazza; address Piazza G. Motta 29, CH-6612 Ascona.
6. Hotel Ascona - https://hotel-ascona.ch/en/ - above the town.
   Own site: describes itself as a building packed with history; has its own wellness area, food and drink,
   an art collection and rooms from standard to deluxe plus apartments.
7. Hotel Fondazione Monte Verità - https://monteverita.org/en/ - Via Collina 84, Monte Verità.
   Own site: hotel complex made up of the Bauhaus Hotel, Villa Semiramis, Casa Monescia, Casa Marta and
   Casa Gioia, with a restaurant, a park and a conference centre.
8. Seven Boutique Hotel Ascona - https://www.seven.ch/en/ - Piazza Giuseppe Motta 21 and 25.
   Own site: part of the Seven Group, which runs venues in Ascona, Locarno, Lugano, Klosters and
   Porto Ronco. NOTE: the group site does not present hotel facts on its landing page, so only the address
   from the tourist office listing and the group membership are confirmed. See UNVERIFIED.

No prices here; prices come from data/prices/*.json only.

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

Officially stated for Ascona:
- Monte Verità museum complex: free admission with the Swiss Travel Pass (monteverita.org, Opening Hours &
  Tickets, "Free: ... Swiss Travel Pass").
- Boats on Lake Maggiore and the local buses are ordinary public transport, included under the general rule
  ("unlimited travel by train, bus and boat", myswitzerland.com Swiss Travel Pass page).
- Brissago Islands admission with the pass: not published on the tourist office or cantonal page reached.
- For the Cardada cable car and the Locarno funicular, see data/research/locarno.md (50 per cent and
  25 per cent respectively).

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. Does Ascona have a train station?
   No. Trains go to Locarno and buses run on from there; bus 1 from Locarno Piazza Stazione reaches
   Ascona Fiume Maggia in 7 minutes. Source: SBB timetable via transport.opendata.ch, sample 22/09/2026.

2. How long does Zurich to Ascona take?
   About 2 h 26 with two changes, via Bellinzona and Locarno.
   Source: SBB timetable via transport.opendata.ch.

3. When is JazzAscona?
   24 June to 3 July 2027, on the Lungolago, with music from 11:00 to 03:00 and around fifteen concerts
   a day. Source: ascona-locarno.com/en/events/details/jazzascona-2027/10042.

4. How do I reach the Brissago Islands, and when are they open?
   By boat from Ascona in 15 minutes, or 40 to 60 minutes from Locarno; the 2026 season runs 18 March to
   8 November. Source: ascona-locarno.com Brissago Islands page and isoledibrissago.ti.ch.

5. Is the Monte Verità museum free with the Swiss Travel Pass?
   Yes, the Foundation lists the Swiss Travel Pass among the free-admission categories; the full price is
   CHF 12.00. Source: monteverita.org, Opening Hours & Tickets.
