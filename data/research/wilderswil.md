## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors, small village)

Wilderswil is a small village (2,879 residents); the official tourist listings return a limited number of standalone hotels, concentrated around two streets.

### A. Oberdorfstrasse (upper village)
- Hotel Baren, Oberdorfstrasse 1, 3812 Wilderswil, operating under "Tavernenrecht seit 1706" (tavern rights since 1706), 41 rooms, 86 beds. Source: https://www.brienzersee.ch/hotel/hotel-baeren-wilderswil
- The Dorfrundgang Wilderswil walking route passes "entlang der Kirchgasse vorbei an historischen Holzhausern" (along Kirchgasse past historic wooden houses) near here (see WHAT TO DO).

### B. Alte Staatsstrasse (near the station)
- Gasthof Hirschen, Alte Staatsstrasse 6, 3812 Wilderswil, "in der Nahe von Bahnhof und Sportzentrum" (near the station and sports centre), 10 rooms, 20 beds. Source: https://www.brienzersee.ch/hotel/gasthof-hirschen-wilderswil

### C. Bahnhof Wilderswil (valley station for Schynige Platte)
- The railway station itself, valley terminus of the historic Schynige Platte rack railway (built 1893), with departures "alle 40 Minuten" and a car park for those driving in. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/schynige-platte-bahn

## 4. SEASONS

Schynige Platte-Bahn: operating season "Mitte Juli - Ende Oktober" (mid-July to end of October), "Tagliche Fahrten alle 40 Minuten ab Bahnhof Wilderswil" (daily departures every 40 minutes from Wilderswil station). Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/schynige-platte-bahn

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **Dorfrundgang Wilderswil.** A village walking loop: "4,16 km lang" (4.16 km), duration "1:20 h", elevation gain "89 m", starting at "Bahnhof Wilderswil" and running "entlang der Kirchgasse vorbei an historischen Holzhausern" (along Kirchgasse past historic wooden houses). Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/tour/dorfrundgang-wilderswil
2. **Erholsam Wandern an der Lutschine.** A riverside walk ending in Wilderswil: "9,50 km lang" (9.5 km), duration "2:20 h", elevation gain "300 m", starting at "Lauterbrunnen, Bahnhof". Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/tour/erholsam-wandern-an-der-luetschine

**Two named mountain rides / sights**
1. **Schynige Platte-Bahn**, valley station Wilderswil. "Die Schynige Platte-Bahn wurde im Jahr 1893 erbaut"; the "7.3 Kilometer langen Strecke" rack railway "uberwindet... 1420 Hohenmeter"; the ride takes "53 Minuten"; along the way you see "den Thuner- und den Brienzersee sowie das Bergpanorama" (both Lake Thun and Lake Brienz, plus the mountain panorama). Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/schynige-platte-bahn
2. **Burgenweg in Wilderswil.** A circular heritage walk, "5,08 km lang", "1:45 h", easy difficulty, elevation range "580-684 m" (136 m up and down), starting at the "Dorfmuseum 'Alte Muhle'" (village museum) and passing the "Burgruine Rothenfluh" and the "Ruine Unspunnen" castle ruins before returning through the village. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/tour/burgenweg-in-wilderswil

**Two named restaurants (listed by the tourist office, no opinion)**
1. **Gasthof Hirschen**, Alte Staatsstrasse 6, 3812 Wilderswil. "Gutburgerlicher Familienbetrieb" with a "Spezialitaten-Restaurant mit vielen typischen schweizer Gerichte"; event space for up to 80 people. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/gastro/gasthof-hirschen-wilderswil
2. **Hotel Baren restaurant**, Oberdorfstrasse 1, 3812 Wilderswil. Rustic chalet-style dining room serving "Typische Schweizer- und Alpenlandkuche"; garden terrace, bar and event hall; the inn itself has held "Tavernenrecht seit 1706". Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/gastro/restaurant-hotel-baeren-wilderswil

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **Gateway village**: the regional tourist office describes Wilderswil as the starting point ("Eingangstor") to the two Lutschinen valleys and the base of the Schynige Platte. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/reiseziele/interlaken/wilderswil-gsteigwiler-saxeten
- **Ruine Unspunnen**, reached on the Burgenweg, is one of the two named castle ruins on the municipality's own heritage trail. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/tour/burgenweg-in-wilderswil
- **Memorialpark Saxetbach**: the municipality's own website states, of the flood of 27 July 1999, "Am 27. Juli 1999 starben durch eine Flutwelle 21 junge Menschen" (21 young people died in a flash flood on 27 July 1999); a memorial park commemorates the event. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/reiseziele/interlaken/wilderswil-gsteigwiler-saxeten and https://www.wilderswil.ch (Memorialpark Saxetbach listed in the municipal navigation)
- **800 Jahre Wilderswil**: the village marked its 800th anniversary with a jubilee programme, including a monthly "Dorfzytig". Source: https://www.wilderswil.ch/800-jahre-wilderswil/dorfzytig

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do I reach the Schynige Platte from Wilderswil?**
   The rack railway leaves directly from Wilderswil station: a 7.3 km, 53-minute climb of 1,420 m, built in 1893, departing every 40 minutes, open mid-July to end of October. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/schynige-platte-bahn
2. **How far is Wilderswil from Interlaken?**
   5 minutes direct by train on the Berner Oberland Bahn from Interlaken Ost. Source: Swiss public transport timetable data, queried 16/09/2026.
3. **Is there anything historic to see in the village itself?**
   Yes. The Burgenweg, a 5.08 km circular walk from the Dorfmuseum "Alte Muhle", passes two castle ruins, the Burgruine Rothenfluh and the Ruine Unspunnen. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/tour/burgenweg-in-wilderswil
4. **What is the Memorialpark Saxetbach?**
   A memorial to the flash flood of 27 July 1999, in which, per the tourist office, 21 young people died; the park is listed in the municipality's own navigation. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/reiseziele/interlaken/wilderswil-gsteigwiler-saxeten
5. **How big is Wilderswil?**
   2,879 residents (31 December 2025) across about 13 km2, at 600 m altitude. Source: https://www.wilderswil.ch/informationen/unsere-gemeinde

## NOTE FOR THE PARENT AGENT

Wilderswil's official tourist-office hotel listing returned only two standalone hotels (Hotel Baren, Gasthof Hirschen), short of the usual 6-10. The village is genuinely small (2,879 residents) and functions largely as the valley-station/gateway village for the Schynige Platte railway, with Interlaken (5 minutes away) absorbing most overnight demand. Worth a page for the Schynige Platte-Bahn story and the Burgenweg ruins, but flag the thin hotel inventory before committing to the "6 to 10 hotels" format used elsewhere; consider whether it should be folded into an Interlaken-area page instead of standing alone.
