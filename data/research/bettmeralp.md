## 2. ACCESS

**No road reaches Bettmeralp.** Two lifts leave from the **Bettmeralp valley station** (postal address
3993 Grengiols): a large-cabin cable car straight up, and an aerial tramway via **Betten Dorf**.
Source: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/bettmeralp

**Ride times published by the operator** (aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility/timetable-season-times):
| Section | Ride time | Transfer time to the View Point lift |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bettmeralp valley station to Bettmeralp | 7 minutes | 20 minutes |
| Bettmeralp to Bettmerhorn | 8 minutes | |

**By train.** The feeder railway Bettmeralp valley station to Bettmeralp runs **all year** and is part of
the **SBB GA and half-fare travelcard network**. The SBB luggage service can be used, and there are
Snow'n'Rail discounts. Booking two nights in a participating hotel gives free return travel by public
transport.
Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility

**By car.** You drive to the Bettmeralp valley station and leave the car there. The tourist office names
the **car park Bettmeralp valley station** (Grengiols), with EV charging points, and says there are
large outdoor and indoor car parks at the valley stations.
Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility

**Luggage.** Because the car-free village roads are under snow in winter, the tourist office recommends
booking the **luggage transport (baggage cart)** from the mountain station to the accommodation for
arrival and departure; it is sold in the webshop **from CHF 10 per person**. Buying cable car and ski
passes online beforehand is advised to avoid the ticket queue.
Sources: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility and
aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility/timetable-season-times

**Between the two plateau villages.** The **Aletsch Express**, an electric shuttle, links Bettmeralp and
Riederalp, sold from CHF 6.00 per person.
Source: aletscharena.ch webshop listing (Aletsch Express, Bettmeralp)

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

**A. The village centre (Hauptstrasse).**
The tourist office describes "a vibrant village centre with shops, bars and local grocery stores".
In winter there are ski racks instead of parking spaces outside the grocery store. Restaurant
Gletschertisch is at Hauptstrasse 40 and Restaurant Alpina, in business since 1955, at Hauptstrasse 103.
Points: shops and the cable car arrival on foot; ski-in and ski-out from the village.
Sources: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/bettmeralp,
aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/service-amenities/food-drink, alpina-bettmeralp.ch

**B. Seestrasse, towards Lake Bettmersee.**
The eastern side of the village, along the road to the lake. Hotel Alpfrieden is at Seestrasse 3
(alpfrieden.ch), Restaurant Walliserstube at Seestrasse 24 (walliserstube-bettmeralp.ch). The tourist
office calls Bettmersee a place to swim in summer.
Points: the lake and the open plateau on the doorstep; a few minutes further from the valley cable car.

**C. Blausee and the slope above (towards the Bettmerhorn lift).**
Hotel Waldhaus states it stands a few minutes from the village centre, with skis put on in front of the
hotel and the **Blausee chairlift** taking you into the Aletsch Arena ski area (waldhaus-bettmeralp.ch).
The Bettmeralp to Bettmerhorn cable car, 8 minutes, leaves from this side.
Points: true ski-in and ski-out; quieter than the centre.

## 4. SEASONS

**Season dates published by the operator** (aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility/timetable-season-times):
- **Winter 2026/2027: 5 December 2026 to 3 April 2027**
- Winter 2027/2028: 8 December 2027 to 17 April 2028
- Winter 2028/2029: 8 December 2028 to 7 April 2029
- **Summer 2026: 6 June to 25 October 2026**
- Summer 2027: 5 June to 24 October 2027
- Summer 2028: 10 June to 22 October 2028
- **Bettmeralp to Bettmerhorn, 6 June to 25 October 2026, 08:30 to 16:30**
- Maintenance closures published for Bettmeralp: 26.10 to 15.11.2026, 16.11 to 22.11.2026 and
  23.11 to 10.12.2026

**Winter.** The Aletsch Arena ski area has **104 km of slopes**, running from Riederfurka to Eggishorn,
with slopes above 2000 m which the operator says gives guaranteed snow from December to Easter. There is
a black run from the Eggishorn and a Glacier Panorama Tour. Around **300 days of sunshine a year**.
Sources: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/bettmeralp and
aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/winter/skiing

**Summer.** **300 km of prepared and secured hiking trails** in the Aletsch Arena. The oldest Swiss
stone pines in the Aletsch forest are up to **1000 years old**, and there are **5 suspension bridges**
in the Aletsch Goms region.
Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/summer-autumn/hiking

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2), both with figures published by the tourist office.**
1. **Circular hike from Bettmerhorn via Märjelensee to Bettmeralp.** 14.58 km, **5 h 30**, 1142 m of
   climb and 486 m of descent, graded difficult.
   Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/summer-autumn/hiking
2. **Riederalp via the Moraine Trail to the Bettmerhorn.** 9.31 km, **3 h 30**, 87 m of climb and 742 m
   of descent, graded medium. Links the two plateau villages along the glacier moraine.
   Source: same hiking page
   (Also published, without a time: the "Culture Discovery" themed trail from Bettmeralp down to Betten
   village, 5 km. And as a running route, the Aletsch Half Marathon, 20.95 km, 4 h 13, 1040 m of descent,
   starting at 3992 Bettmeralp.)

**Mountain rides / attractions (2).**
1. **View Point Bettmerhorn.** Reached by the Bettmeralp to Bettmerhorn cable car, 8 minutes, running
   6 June to 25 October 2026, 08:30 to 16:30. One of the four Aletsch Arena view points, all between
   **2227 and 2869 m**, each with a platform over the **Great Aletsch Glacier, 20 km long, the longest
   glacier in the Alps** and the heart of the **Swiss Alps Jungfrau-Aletsch UNESCO World Heritage Site**,
   with **40 four-thousand-metre peaks** in view.
   Sources: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/view-points and the timetable page
2. **Lake Bettmersee.** On the plateau east of the village, named by the tourist office as one of the
   sights of Bettmeralp, with swimming in summer.
   Source: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/bettmeralp

**Restaurants (2), both listed by the tourist office.**
1. **Restaurant Walliserstube**, Seestrasse 24, 3992 Bettmeralp. Rösti, fondue, raclette, dried meat,
   Eringer beef and other meat dishes with regional wines. Open 09:00 to 22:30, kitchen 11:30 to 17:00
   and 18:00 to 21:00, closed Wednesday and Thursday, raclette every evening from 18:00.
   Sources: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/service-amenities/food-drink and
   walliserstube-bettmeralp.ch
2. **Restaurant Alpina**, Hauptstrasse 103, 3992 Bettmeralp. Traditional Swiss home cooking. Opened in
   **1955** as the Tea-Room Alpina; 140 seats in the main room plus a separate room with 35 seats, a
   large sun terrace and a Giltstein stove.
   Sources: aletscharena.ch food and drink listing and alpina-bettmeralp.ch
   (Other tables the tourist office lists on Bettmeralp: Gletschertisch, Hauptstrasse 40, vegan and
   vegetarian; Restaurant Sportzentrum Bachtla, Tanzbodenstrasse 4; Pizzeria PiccoBello; Stafel Bar,
   Donnerstafelstrasse 29; Panorama-Restaurant Bettmerhorn; Restaurant Bättmerhitta; Restaurant
   Bettmerhof; Restaurant Alpfrieden; cable car bar Bettmerhorn.)

**Typical to the place: the chapel and the car-free plateau.**
The **Maria zum Schnee chapel** is named by the tourist office as an absolute highlight of Bettmeralp
and is part of the **Grand Tour of Switzerland**; there is also a marked Grand Tour photo spot on
Bettmeralp. In winter, the village has ski stands instead of parking spaces outside the grocery store.
Source: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/bettmeralp

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Can you drive to Bettmeralp?**
   No. The village is car-free and is reached only by the large-cabin cable car from the Bettmeralp
   valley station or by the aerial tramway via Betten Dorf. The ride up takes 7 minutes; you leave the
   car in the valley station car park.
   Sources: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/bettmeralp and the timetable page
2. **What do I do with my luggage?**
   The tourist office recommends booking the baggage cart from the mountain station to your
   accommodation, sold in the webshop from CHF 10 per person, because the village roads are under snow
   in winter.
   Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility
3. **When is the season?**
   The lifts run 6 June to 25 October 2026 in summer and 5 December 2026 to 3 April 2027 in winter.
   The Bettmerhorn cable car runs 08:30 to 16:30 through the summer season.
   Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility/timetable-season-times
4. **Bettmeralp or Riederalp?**
   Both are car-free and share the same 104 km ski area and the same view points over the Aletsch
   Glacier. Bettmeralp sits at 1970 m with the Bettmerhorn lift and Lake Bettmersee; Riederalp at
   1925 m with the Hohfluh and Moosfluh lifts and the Pro Natura Centre at Villa Cassel. The Aletsch
   Express electric shuttle links the two.
   Sources: the two destination pages on aletscharena.ch and the Aletsch Express listing
5. **Where do I see the Aletsch Glacier from?**
   From the View Point Bettmerhorn, 8 minutes above the village by cable car. The four Aletsch Arena
   view points sit between 2227 and 2869 m and look over the 20 km long glacier, the longest in the Alps.
   Source: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/view-points
