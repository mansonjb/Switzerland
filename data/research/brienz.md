## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

### A. Hauptstrasse and the lakefront
- The main street runs along the shore; Hotel Weisses Kreuz (Hauptstrasse 143) is "Directly on Lake Brienz",
  with a **2 km lakeside promenade** starting at the door, and is "near the train station, Brienzer Rothorn steam
  train, and boat landing". Hotel Brienzerburli is at Hauptstrasse 11 and Seehotel Bären at Hauptstrasse 72,
  both on the water. VERIFIED, each hotel's site.
- The SBB station, the Rothorn Bahn valley station (Hauptstrasse 149) and the boat landing are all on this
  stretch. VERIFIED.

### B. Above the village, on the slope
- Hotel Lindenhof, Lindenhofweg 15, is set in its own park above the lake with a view towards the Brienzer
  Rothorn. VERIFIED, hotel-lindenhof.ch.
- The School for Woodcarving is at Schleegasse 1, in the village behind the main street. VERIFIED,
  holzbildhauerei.ch.

### C. The south shore: Giessbach
- Grandhotel Giessbach, opened 1875, stands above the Giessbach falls on the far shore, reached by BLS boat and
  then the 1891 funicular. It is a sector you reach by water, not by walking from the village.
  VERIFIED, giessbach.ch.

## 4. SEASONS

### Summer
- **Brienz Rothorn Bahn 2026: 6 June to 25 October.** First departure from Brienz 07:36, last departure from
  Brienz 16:36, journey to the summit about **one hour**. The 07:36 from Brienz and the 09:06 from Rothorn Kulm
  run every Saturday and Sunday in July, August and September. Adult full-fare return **CHF 98**; seat guarantee
  **CHF 8** extra; children under 6 free; dogs CHF 10 a journey. VERIFIED,
  brienz-rothorn-bahn.ch/en/fahrplan-preise/
- **Ballenberg 2026: 9 April to 1 November, 10:00 to 17:00 daily**, over 100 authentic historic buildings from
  all over Switzerland and over 200 farm animals, at Museumsstrasse 100, 3858 Hofstetten bei Brienz.
  VERIFIED, ballenberg.ch/en/
- **Giessbach funicular: April to October**, maximum 40 passengers a trip, with direct connections to the BLS
  boats. VERIFIED, giessbach.ch.

### Winter
- The Rothorn Bahn does not run; it is a summer-only steam railway.
- Ballenberg is closed from 2 November.
- **Grandhotel Giessbach winter operation: 21 October 2026 to 20 March 2027**, with limited hours, Wednesday to
  Sunday. VERIFIED, giessbach.ch.
- BLS stops sailing Lake Brienz for the winter from 7 December (already sourced in `data/SOURCES-doing.md`).
- There is no ski area at Brienz itself. The Axalp plateau above the village is the local winter terrain; no
  official figures were captured for it. Do not invent any.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

### Two named walks
1. **The lakeside promenade**, **2 km**, running along the shore from Hotel Weisses Kreuz.
   VERIFIED as the hotel's own wording, weisseskreuz-brienz.ch. Attribute it to the hotel, or get the figure
   from the tourist office before printing it as a village fact.
2. **The Woodcarving Trail (Schnitzlerweg)**, named by Interlaken Tourismus among the walking routes at Brienz,
   with the Schwander Lebensweg (a circular route through the village) and the Jakobsweg towards Interlaken.
   VERIFIED as named routes, interlaken.swiss/en/destinations/lake-brienz/brienz-surroundings
   **Times and distances: NOT PUBLISHED on the page fetched.** Fetch the individual tour pages before writing.

### Two named mountain rides or attractions
1. **Brienz Rothorn Bahn.** Concession granted 1889, "officially opened on 16 June 1892", built in about
   16 months by around 700 workers, mostly Italian, at a cost of CHF 2.2 million including four steam
   locomotives, four carriages and two goods wagons. It "has not been electrified to this day". Operations were
   suspended in the First World War and resumed in 1931 after the community stopped the line being scrapped;
   the federal concession was renewed in 2019 for the next 50 years, and storm damage in 2024 cost around
   CHF 5 million to repair and closed that season early. Season 6 June to 25 October 2026, about one hour to
   the summit, where the operator says you can see "over 693 mountain peaks" and where the Rothorn Kulm mountain
   hotel stands. Diesel locomotives may be used when there are too many passengers or a technical problem.
   VERIFIED, brienz-rothorn-bahn.ch (history page and timetable page).
2. **Ballenberg, the Swiss open-air museum**, at Hofstetten bei Brienz: over 100 historic buildings brought from
   all over the country, over 200 farm animals, open 9 April to 1 November 2026, 10:00 to 17:00, about 11
   minutes by bus 151 from Brienz station. VERIFIED, ballenberg.ch and the official timetable.
   (House 1361, the alpine cheese dairy built at Kandersteg in 1780 where cheese is made almost daily in a
   copper cauldron, is already sourced in `data/SOURCES-doing.md` from the Interlaken research.)

### Two named restaurants (from the houses' own sites, no opinion)
1. **Seerestaurant Löwen**, Hauptstrasse 11, part of Hotel Brienzerburli, on the lake; the house describes it as
   rustic and home-style, with lunch, dinner and set menus. VERIFIED, brienzerburli.ch.
2. **Goethestübli**, in Hotel Weisses Kreuz, Hauptstrasse 143; the house calls it historic and rich in
   tradition. VERIFIED, weisseskreuz-brienz.ch.
Third option if one is rejected: **Restaurant Lindenblüte** at Hotel Lindenhof, Lindenhofweg 15.
VERIFIED, hotel-lindenhof.ch.

### Typical of the place: the two craft schools
- **School for Woodcarving Brienz** (Schule für Holzbildhauerei), Schleegasse 1, 3855 Brienz. It trains
  woodcarvers and describes itself as "an innovative training workshop for woodcarving and vocational school",
  covering **six artisanal wood professions**. VERIFIED, holzbildhauerei.ch.
- **Swiss Violin Making School Brienz** (Geigenbauschule), which teaches "Traditional work techniques as well as
  the use of innovative technologies" and calls itself "a modern training location in the heart of Switzerland".
  The municipality describes it as "a special educational institution of world renown".
  VERIFIED, geigenbauschule.ch and brienz.ch/portrait.
- The municipality calls Brienz "a carving village" with "a long tradition in wood processing", and Interlaken
  Tourismus calls it "Switzerland's woodcarving village". VERIFIED, brienz.ch/portrait and interlaken.swiss.

### Typical of the place: Giessbach
- **Grandhotel Giessbach**, opened **1875**, architect Horace Edouard Davinet, above the Giessbach falls on the
  south shore. The **funicular, built in 1891**, is described by the hotel as "one of the oldest funiculars in
  the world", still using switch rails based on the original 1891 design, maximum 40 passengers a trip, running
  April to October with direct connections to the BLS boats. VERIFIED, giessbach.ch.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do I get to Brienz?**
   By the Zentralbahn between Interlaken and Lucerne. Interlaken Ost to Brienz is 17 minutes on the
   Luzern-Interlaken Express and 20 minutes on the regional train; Lucerne is 1 h 27, Bern 1 h 17 with a change
   at Interlaken Ost. In summer the BLS boat is the other way in.
   Source: official Swiss timetable, checked 16/09/2026.

2. **When does the Rothorn steam railway run, and what does it cost?**
   6 June to 25 October 2026, first departure from Brienz 07:36 and last at 16:36, about an hour to the summit.
   An adult full-fare return is CHF 98, with a seat guarantee CHF 8 extra; under-6s travel free and dogs cost
   CHF 10. Capacity is limited, so booking ahead is the operator's own advice.
   Source: brienz-rothorn-bahn.ch/en/fahrplan-preise/

3. **Is the Rothorn railway really steam?**
   It opened on 16 June 1892 and "has not been electrified to this day", though the operator says diesel
   locomotives may be used for passenger services when there are too many passengers or a technical problem.
   Source: brienz-rothorn-bahn.ch.

4. **What is there to do in Brienz apart from the railway?**
   Ballenberg, the Swiss open-air museum at Hofstetten bei Brienz, with over 100 historic buildings from all
   over Switzerland and over 200 farm animals, open 9 April to 1 November 2026, 10:00 to 17:00, about 11 minutes
   from the station on bus 151. In the village itself there is the School for Woodcarving and the Swiss Violin
   Making School. Sources: ballenberg.ch, holzbildhauerei.ch, geigenbauschule.ch.

5. **Why is Brienz called the woodcarving village?**
   The municipality describes itself as a carving village with a long tradition in wood processing, and the
   School for Woodcarving on Schleegasse trains carvers across six artisanal wood professions. The violin
   making school is in the same village. Sources: brienz.ch/portrait, holzbildhauerei.ch, geigenbauschule.ch.

- Ballenberg ticket price and site area in hectares.
- Room counts for Weisses Kreuz (beds only, 45), Brienzerburli, Seehotel Bären and Grandhotel Giessbach.
