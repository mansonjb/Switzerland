## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. Schachenstrasse / harbour (Spiez Bay)
- The marina and beach area right on the bay, next to the castle.
- Belvedere Strandhotel, Schachenstrasse 39, 3700 Spiez, 4-star superior (Romantik Hotel), "56 stilvoll eingerichtete Hotelzimmer" (56 rooms), 108 beds, with "das private Strandbad sowie der Infinitypool" (private beach and infinity pool). Source: https://www.brienzersee.ch/hotel/belvedere-strandhotel-spiez
- Hotel Seegarten Marina, Schachenstrasse 3, 3700 Spiez, 3-star, "direkt am See, zentrale Lage" (directly on the lake, central location), 46 rooms, 78 beds. Source: https://www.brienzersee.ch/hotel/hotel-seegarten-marina-spiez
- Restaurant Seaside, Schachenstrasse 43, 3700 Spiez, seating "90" indoors plus "50" on the terrace. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/gastro/hotel-seaside-spiez

### B. Schlossstrasse / castle and vineyard
- Around Schloss Spiez and the Spiezer Rebberg vineyard slope above the bay.
- Schloss Spiez, Schlossstrasse, "umgeben von Weinreben in der Spiezer Bucht" (surrounded by vineyards in Spiez Bay). Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/schloss-spiez
- Schloss-Café, Schlossstrasse 16, 3700 Spiez, next to the castle. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/gastro/schloss-cafe-spiez
- Spiezer Alpine Weinkultur, Schlossstrasse 8, 3700 Spiez, wine cooperative outlet directly above the lake. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/spiezer-alpine-weinkultur

### C. Seestrasse / Faulensee direction
- Along the lake toward Faulensee, one of the five Bäuerten of the municipality.
- Hotel Eden, Seestrasse 58, 3700 Spiez, 4-star superior, "45 individuell eingerichtete Zimmer und Juniorsuiten" (45 individually furnished rooms and junior suites). Source: https://www.brienzersee.ch/hotel/hotel-eden-spiez-ag
- The Strandweg Spiez-Faulensee lakeside walk starts at the "Frei- und Seebad Spiez" (Spiez open-air lake bath) on this stretch (see WHAT TO DO).

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **Erlebnispfad Spiezer Rebberg.** A circular educational trail through the Spiez vineyard: "958 m lang" (958 m), duration "1-2 Stunden", elevation gain "48 m", with "12 Informationstafeln" (12 information panels), starting at "Unteres Rabhusli im Spiezer Rebberg". Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/tour/erlebnispfad-spiezer-rebberg
2. **Strandweg Spiez-Faulensee.** A lakeshore walk: "2,33 km lang" (2.33 km), duration "0:40" (40 minutes), elevation gain "6 m", starting at the "Frei- und Seebad Spiez" (Spiez open-air lake bath). Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/tour/strandweg-spiez-faulensee

**Two named mountain rides / sights**
1. **Niesenbahn**, from the valley station at Mulenen (near Spiez) to the summit at "2362 m u. M.". "In 15 Minuten fahrst du mit der historischen Standseilbahn von 1910 von der Talstation Mulenen zur Mittelstation Schwandegg. Von der Mittelstation bist du in 15 Minuten auf dem Gipfel" (15 minutes to the mid-station Schwandegg, then 15 more minutes to the summit; total 30 minutes), on a funicular dating from "1910". Beside the line runs "die langste Treppe der Welt mit 11'674 Stufen" (the world's longest staircase, 11,674 steps). Season "25. April - 8. November 2026". Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/niesenbahn
2. **Schloss Spiez.** "1300 Jahre alte Geschichte" (1,300 years of history); former resident "der Ritter Adrian von Bubenberg"; the adjoining "1000-jahrige, freie, konfessionslose Schlosskirche im fruhromanischen Baustil" (1,000-year-old, non-denominational, early-Romanesque castle church) is popular for weddings; the tower gives "ein atemberaubendes 360-Grad-Panorama". Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/schloss-spiez

**Two named restaurants (listed by the tourist office, no opinion)**
1. **Schloss-Café**, Schlossstrasse 16, 3700 Spiez. Open "1. Mai - 31. Oktober", hours as above. Seating "Interior: 38 seats; Terrace: 48 seats". Bistro and café serving regional cuisine, with an on-site museum. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/gastro/schloss-cafe-spiez
2. **Restaurant Seaside**, Schachenstrasse 43, 3700 Spiez. Cuisine "vegetarisch, regionale Kuche, gut burgerlich, Grill/Fleisch-Spezialitaten"; seating "90" indoors plus "50" on the terrace; has its own butcher shop ("eigene Metzgerei"). Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/gastro/hotel-seaside-spiez

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **Spiez Bay**: described by the regional tourist office as "die schonste Bucht Europas" (reportedly Europe's most beautiful bay). Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/reiseziele/thunersee/spiez-umgebung and https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/schloss-spiez
- **Spiez wine**: the Rebbau-Genossenschaft Spiez, founded in "1942", today farms "11,5 Hektaren Reben" (11.5 ha), mainly "Riesling-Sylvaner und Blauburgunder", producing "rund 75'000 bis 85'000 Flaschen Wein" a year. In the 18th century the vineyard covered about 45 ha before being grubbed up by 1915; the modern cooperative revived it from 1942. Source: https://www.spiez.ch/de/gemeinde/spiez-in-kuerze/weinbau-in-spiez.php
- **World's longest staircase**: alongside the Niesenbahn, "11'674 Stufen". Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/niesenbahn

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How far is Spiez from Bern?**
   30 minutes direct by train. Source: Swiss public transport timetable data, queried 16/09/2026.
2. **How do I get up the Niesen from Spiez?**
   Take the train to Mulenen, then the historic 1910 funicular: 15 minutes to the mid-station Schwandegg, then another 15 minutes to the 2,362 m summit (30 minutes total). Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/niesenbahn
3. **Is there really wine grown in Spiez?**
   Yes. The Rebbau-Genossenschaft Spiez, founded in 1942, farms 11.5 hectares at around 600 m elevation, among the highest vineyards north of the Alps, producing 75,000 to 85,000 bottles a year, mainly Riesling-Sylvaner and Blauburgunder. Source: https://www.spiez.ch/de/gemeinde/spiez-in-kuerze/weinbau-in-spiez.php
4. **What is Schloss Spiez's castle church?**
   A 1,000-year-old, non-denominational, early-Romanesque church next to the castle, popular for weddings, open in the same 1 May to 31 October season as the castle. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/schloss-spiez
5. **How big is Spiez?**
   13,445 residents (31 December 2025), across five districts: Spiez, Spiezwiler, Einigen, Faulensee and Hondrich. Source: https://www.spiez.ch/de/gemeinde/gemeindedaten/zahlen-daten-fakten.php
