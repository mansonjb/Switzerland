## 2. ACCESS

Nearest big station: **Sierre / Siders** on the main Rhone valley line.

**Funicular SMC Sierre to Crans-Montana** (crans-montana.ch/en/funicular):
- just over 4.2 km long
- 927 m of height gain
- 13 minutes of ride time, at 8 m per second
- completely renewed in 2022
- the walk from Sierre railway station to the funicular takes about 5 minutes, following a red line
  painted on the pavement
The tourist office's public transport page words the same trip as "about 15 minutes, with departures
every 20 minutes" (crans-montana.ch/en/public_transport). Both figures are the tourist office's own;
the 13 minutes is the technical ride time, the 15 minutes the timetabled trip.

**Bus Sierre to Crans-Montana**: about 40 minutes, one bus an hour
(crans-montana.ch/en/public_transport).

**Train times to the resort** given by the tourist office (crans-montana.ch/en/public_transport):
- Geneva Airport about 2 h 30
- Zurich Airport about 3 h
- Basel EuroAirport about 3 h 30
- Sion Airport about 1 h
- Milan Malpensa T1 about 4 h

**By car.** The plateau is reached by road from Sierre. Once there, the tourist office runs a free
shuttle bus network inside the resort (crans-montana.ch/en/public_transport).
Parking figures: not published on the pages checked, see UNVERIFIED.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

**A. Crans (west end, the golf plateau).**
The Crans-sur-Sierre Golf Club sits here, with the 18-hole Severiano Ballesteros course and the 9-hole
Jack Nicklaus course (crans-montana.ch/en/golf). The Crans to Merbé to Cry d'Er cable car leaves from
this side (crans-montana.ch/en/hiking_update_and_cable_cars). Guarda Golf Hotel & Residences is at
Route des Zirès 14 (guardagolf.com), Hostellerie du Pas de l'Ours at 41 rue du Pas de l'Ours
(pasdelours.ch).
Points: the two golf courses and the Omega European Masters are on this side; nearest cable car is
Crans to Cry d'Er.

**B. Montana (east end, the funicular and the lakes).**
The SMC funicular from Sierre arrives at the Crans-Montana SMC station on this side. Lac Grenon is here;
Hôtel du Lac is at Promenade de Grenon 1, on the lake, in the centre of the resort
(hoteldulac-crans-montana.ch). Hôtel Olympic states it is in the centre of Montana, on the shopping
street, at the bottom of the ski slopes (hotelolympic.ch).
Points: no transfer from the funicular; shops on the same street.

**C. Les Barzettes / Violettes and the slope above (Vermala, Mont-Paisible).**
The Barzettes to Marolires to Violettes cable car and the Funitel Violettes to Plaine Morte leave from
the eastern edge of the resort (crans-montana.ch/en/hiking_update_and_cable_cars). Hôtel Le Mont-Paisible,
Chemin du Mont-Paisible 12, states it is at the foot of the ski slopes and a few steps from the Violettes
cable car (montpaisible.ch).
Points: the fastest way to the Plaine Morte glacier; further from the shops than Crans or Montana.

## 4. SEASONS

**Winter 2026/2027, published by the operator** (mycma.ch/en-US/winter-opening-hours/):
the ski area runs from **19 December 2026 to 4 April 2027**, with a partial opening possible from
**28 November 2026** depending on snow. All dates subject to change.
Ski area: **140 km of marked pistes, all south facing**, from **1500 m to 3000 m**
(crans-montana.ch/en/ski). Access to the **Plaine Morte glacier** by the Violettes Funitel
(crans-montana.ch/en/hiking_update_and_cable_cars).
**FIS Alpine World Ski Championships, 1 to 14 February 2027**, in Crans-Montana, with the ski area
staying open during the event (crans-montana.ch/en/ski).

**Summer 2026, published by the operator** (mycma.ch/en-US/summer-prices/):
season from **13 June to 25 October 2026**. Montana to Arnouva runs 4 July to 13 September and again
3 to 25 October; Plaine Morte access runs 4 July to 13 September.
Hiking network: **320 km of signposted paths** (crans-montana.ch/en/summer/hiking/).

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2), both with figures published by the tourist office.**
1. **Bisse du Ro.** 11.3 km, 3 h 35, 144 m of climb, along the historic water channel with a suspended
   footbridge. Source: crans-montana.ch news page "Three unmissable hikes to explore this summer"
   (idcmt=Partenaire_news_4d0c77aeaa0ca19c992353992d7fe68a).
   Valais Wallis Promotion publishes a longer version of the same bisse: Tseuzier dam (1778 m) to
   Crans/Montana (1498 m), 12 km, plus 160 m and minus 440 m, 3 h 15, medium.
   Source: valais.ch/en/explore/activities/hiking/bisses/bisse-du-ro
2. **Marmot Trail (Sentier des Marmottes).** About 2 hours, 6.2 km, 80 m of climb, between Cry d'Er and
   the Cabane des Violettes. Source: same crans-montana.ch summer hikes page.
   (Third option: Grand Bisse de Lens, 8.8 km, 2 h 31, 23 m of climb, from the tourist office's spring
   hikes page, idcmt=Partenaire_news_06279dd9721fdeebc8a1c63aaad0be9f.)

**Mountain rides / attractions (2).**
1. **Plaine Morte glacier**, reached by the Funitel Violettes to Plaine Morte. The operator places the
   restaurant at the top at 3000 m. Summer running dates: 4 July to 13 September 2026.
   Sources: crans-montana.ch/en/hiking_update_and_cable_cars, mycma.ch/en-US/restaurant/15/,
   mycma.ch/en-US/summer-prices/
2. **Crans to Merbé to Cry d'Er cable car**, the main lift out of Crans, and the start of the Marmot
   Trail towards the Cabane des Violettes. Season and hours vary by sector, see the operator's timetable.
   Sources: crans-montana.ch/en/hiking_update_and_cable_cars and mycma.ch/en-US/summer-prices/
   Altitudes of the Cry d'Er and Chetzeron stations are not published by the operator, except through
   the Chetzeron hotel, which states 2112 m for its own building (chetzeron.ch).

**Restaurants (2).**
1. **Chetzeron.** At 2112 m on the slopes above Crans, in the converted concrete shell of an old gondola
   station, made into a restaurant in 2009 and into a hotel in 2014. Lunch and dinner served.
   Listed by the tourist office among the mountain restaurants.
   Sources: chetzeron.ch and crans-montana.ch/en/restaurants
2. **Plaine Morte.** At 3000 m, at the top of the Violettes Funitel. Fondues and Valais dishes, including
   the "assiette valaisanne". Listed by the tourist office and run by the lift operator.
   Sources: mycma.ch/en-US/restaurant/15/ and crans-montana.ch/en/restaurants

**Typical to the place: golf.**
Crans-sur-Sierre Golf Club on the plateau, with the **18-hole Severiano Ballesteros** course and the
**9-hole Jack Nicklaus** course, plus an indoor centre open all year. The **Omega European Masters**
returns **2 to 5 September 2027**.
Source: crans-montana.ch/en/golf

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

**Partially published.** The tourist office's public transport page lists the Swiss Travel Pass among
the tickets for reaching Crans-Montana and describes it as unlimited travel by train, bus and boat for
3, 4, 6, 8 or 15 consecutive days, and states that Half-Fare and GA cards are valid on Swiss public
transport. It does **not** state what the pass covers on the SMC funicular itself or on the mountain
lifts, so no coverage claim is made here.
Source: crans-montana.ch/en/public_transport

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do you get up to Crans-Montana from the valley?**
   From Sierre, either the SMC funicular (about 15 minutes, departures every 20 minutes, 4.2 km and
   927 m of height gain, ride time 13 minutes) or the bus (about 40 minutes, one an hour).
   Sources: crans-montana.ch/en/funicular and crans-montana.ch/en/public_transport
2. **Crans or Montana, which side should I stay on?**
   Crans is the golf end, with the Severiano Ballesteros and Jack Nicklaus courses and the Cry d'Er
   cable car. Montana is where the funicular from Sierre arrives, on Lac Grenon, with the shopping
   street. Free shuttle buses link the resort.
   Sources: crans-montana.ch/en/golf, crans-montana.ch/en/hiking_update_and_cable_cars,
   crans-montana.ch/en/public_transport
3. **When does the ski season run in winter 2026/2027?**
   19 December 2026 to 4 April 2027, with a partial opening possible from 28 November 2026 depending on
   snow. The FIS Alpine World Ski Championships run 1 to 14 February 2027 and the ski area stays open.
   Sources: mycma.ch/en-US/winter-opening-hours/ and crans-montana.ch/en/ski
4. **Is there skiing high enough to be reliable?**
   The area runs from 1500 m to 3000 m, with 140 km of south-facing pistes and access to the Plaine
   Morte glacier by the Violettes Funitel.
   Sources: crans-montana.ch/en/ski and crans-montana.ch/en/hiking_update_and_cable_cars
5. **What is open in summer?**
   The lift season runs 13 June to 25 October 2026, with Montana to Arnouva from 4 July to 13 September
   and again 3 to 25 October, and Plaine Morte from 4 July to 13 September. The hiking network is
   320 km of signposted paths.
   Sources: mycma.ch/en-US/summer-prices/ and crans-montana.ch/en/summer/hiking/
