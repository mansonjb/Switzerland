## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. Old Town (Altstadt), around the Obere Hauptgasse and Schlossberg
- The historic centre below Schloss Thun, with the distinctive Läuben: "Beidseitig hervorspringende untere Geschosse und darüberliegende Trottoirs" (two-storey shop rows with elevated walkways above). Source: https://www.thun.ch/sehenswuerdigkeiten
- Hotel Krone, Obere Hauptgasse 2, 3600 Thun, "in the historic old town center, positioned adjacent to the Aare river and near Thun Castle", 4-star, 34 rooms, 76 beds. Source: https://www.brienzersee.ch/hotel/hotel-krone-thun
- Hotel Restaurant Rathaus, Gerberngasse 1, 3600 Thun, in the old town near the Rathausplatz. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/gastro/hotel-restaurant-rathaus-thun

### B. Station / city centre (Bahnhof, Freienhofgasse)
- Around the railway station, a short walk from the old town.
- Hotel Aare, Freienhofgasse 3, 3600 Thun, "Direkt im Zentrum, 5 Gehminuten vom Bahnhof Thun entfernt" (right in the centre, 5 minutes' walk from Thun station), 60 rooms, 120 beds. Source: https://www.brienzersee.ch/hotel/hotel-aare-thun
- Hotel Emmental, Bernstrasse 2, 3600 Thun, a "Gasthaus seit 1898" (guesthouse since 1898), 14 rooms, 25 beds. Source: https://www.brienzersee.ch/hotel/hotel-emmental

### C. Lakeside / Schadaupark (Seestrasse)
- Along the Aarequai/Seestrasse toward Schadau Park and the lake, about 15 minutes' walk from the old town.
- Hotel Seepark, Seestrasse 47, 3602 Thun, 4-star superior, "91 stilvolle Zimmer und Suiten" (91 stylish rooms and suites), 121 beds, "directly on Lake Thun within the historic Schadau Park". Source: https://www.brienzersee.ch/hotel/hotel-seepark
- Schloss Schadau, Seestrasse 45, 3600 Thun, built in 1846, now also a small boutique hotel with 9 rooms inside the Schadau Park. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/schloss-schadau
- Hotel Alpha, Gwattstrasse 4, 3604 Thun, directly by the lake, 33 rooms, 67 beds. Source: https://www.brienzersee.ch/hotel/alpha-thun

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **Aarequai / Brahmsquai promenade.** "Mit dem Aarequai und dem daran anschliessenden Brahmsquai erstreckt sich von der Thuner Altstadt bis nach Hünibach eine prächtige Promenade" (a splendid promenade runs from Thun's old town to Hünibach), approximately 2 km. Source: https://www.thun.ch/sehenswuerdigkeiten
2. **Lakeside Uferweg.** "Vom Stadtzentrum bis zum Gwattlischenmoos säumt ein malerischer Uferweg über rund sechs Kilometer das Thunerseeufer" (a picturesque lakeside path of about 6 km runs from the city centre to Gwattlischenmoos). Same source.

**Two named mountain rides / sights**
1. **Schloss Thun.** Built "seit dem 12. Jahrhundert" by the Dukes of Zähringen, with "vier weisse Türme" (four white towers) visible from afar; inside is "den hochmittelalterlichen Rittersaal" (the high-medieval knights' hall) and a castle museum; audio guides in "Deutsch, Englisch, Französisch, Italienisch oder Chinesisch". Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/schloss-thun
2. **BLS boat on Lake Thun**, departing from Thun, including the nostalgic paddle steamer "Blümlisalp"; year-round service with an on-board restaurant. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/bls-schifffahrt-thunersee

**Two named restaurants (listed by the tourist office, no opinion)**
1. **Hotel Restaurant Rathaus**, Gerberngasse 1, 3600 Thun. Closed Sundays ("Ruhetage: Sonntag"). Seating "120 indoors total; 80 in banquet hall; 80 on terrace"; Italian, Mediterranean, fish and grill dishes plus pizzeria. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/gastro/hotel-restaurant-rathaus-thun
2. **Schloss Schadau Restaurant**, Seestrasse 45, 3600 Thun, inside Schadau Park. Seating "Interior: 50 seats; main hall 70; two side rooms 24 each; terrace 80; veranda 45"; French, Mediterranean, regional Swiss and vegan dishes. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/schloss-schadau and https://www.thunersee.ch/gastro/schloss-schadau-restaurant-thun

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **Thun-Panorama**, Schadaupark, painted by "Marquard Wocher" in "1814", "38 m Lauflänge" (38 m in circumference), described by the tourist office as "das älteste noch erhaltene Rundbild der Welt" (the oldest still-preserved panorama painting in the world). Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/thun-panorama
- **Schloss Schadau**, built in 1846, "neugotische Stilelemente", set "Inmitten der englischen Parkanlage - dem Schadaupark" directly on the lake, ranked among the "architektonisch bedeutendsten und schönsten Schlössern der Schweiz". Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/schloss-schadau
- **Stadtkirche**: dates from "dem 14. Jahrhundert" (the 14th century). Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/reiseziele/thunersee/stadt-thun
- **Ausschiesset**: Thun's annual autumn folk tradition, "für viele Thunerinnen und Thuner ein Höhepunkt im Jahr" (a yearly highlight for many Thun residents), with parades, music and shooting competitions. Source: https://www.thun.ch/geschichte

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How far is Thun from Bern?**
   19 minutes direct by train. Source: Swiss public transport timetable data, queried 16/09/2026.
2. **Can I see Eiger, Monch and Jungfrau from Thun?**
   The regional tourist office lists views of "Eiger, Monch und Jungfrau" as part of the Thun area's outlook. Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/reiseziele/thunersee/stadt-thun
3. **What is the Thun-Panorama?**
   A cylindrical painting of the town and its Alpine surroundings, painted by Marquard Wocher in 1814, "38 m" around, described by the tourist office as "das älteste noch erhaltene Rundbild der Welt" (the oldest still-preserved panorama painting in the world). Source: https://www.thunersee.ch/erlebnisse/poi/thun-panorama
4. **How old is Thun as a city?**
   Countess Elisabeth of Kyburg granted city rights in 1264; the old town, castle and city church were already built earlier by the Dukes of Zähringen. Source: https://www.thun.ch/geschichte
5. **Can I get from Thun to Spiez and Interlaken by train easily?**
   Yes, Thun to Spiez is 9 minutes direct, and Thun to Interlaken Ost is 35 minutes direct, both on the same line. Source: Swiss public transport timetable data, queried 16/09/2026.
