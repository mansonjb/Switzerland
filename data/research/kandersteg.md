## 2. ACCESS

Kandersteg has its own railway station on the BLS Lötschberg line, and it is the northern loading point of
the Lötschberg car-carrying train to the Valais.

| Leg | Timetabled time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bern to Kandersteg | **1 h 01, direct** | BLS RE1, no change |
| Spiez to Kandersteg | **0 h 28, direct** | BLS RE1 |
| Interlaken Ost to Kandersteg | **1 h 11, one change** | change at Spiez |
| Kandersteg station to the Sunnbüel valley station | about **35 minutes on foot**, or **about 10 minutes** on AFA bus line 241 | VERIFIED, sunnbuel.ch |
| Kandersteg to Goppenstein (Valais) with your car on the train | **"in just 15 minutes"** | VERIFIED, bls.ch car transport service |

**Driving and parking.** You can drive to Kandersteg, and the Lötschberg car train makes it a through route to
the Valais rather than a dead end. But the tourist office states parking in the village is limited and
recommends public transport. There is a dedicated car park at the Oeschinen gondola valley station
(listed as "Parkplatz Kandersteg Gondelbahn Oeschinen" by the destination); its capacity and tariff are not
published: UNVERIFIED.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

### A. Around the station and Innere Dorfstrasse
- Kandersteg station is the arrival point for every train and the walking start of the Gasterntal hike
  (the signpost to Gasterntal is at the station). VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch tour page.
- Hotel des Alpes is at Innere Dorfstrasse 63 and offers "free pickup from train". VERIFIED.
- Restaurant Schweizerhof is at Bahnhofstrasse 1. VERIFIED.

### B. Aeussere Dorfstrasse, towards the Oeschinen gondola
- Chalet-Hotel Adler, Aeussere Dorfstrasse 19, states it is about **five minutes' walk from the train station**
  and that the cable car to Lake Oeschinen is **within 10 minutes**. VERIFIED, chalethoteladler.ch.
- Belle Epoque Hotel Victoria at Aeussere Dorfstrasse 2 and Hotel Bernerhof at Aeussere Dorfstrasse 37 are on
  the same street. VERIFIED, hotel-victoria.ch and bernerhof.ch.
- This is the side you want if the Oeschinensee is the reason you came.

### C. The southern end: Doldenhorn, Sunnbüel and the Gasterntal
- Waldhotel Doldenhorn, Doldenhornstrasse 26, and Landgasthof Ruedihus, Hinder de Büele 6, sit at the quiet
  south end of the village. VERIFIED, doldenhorn-ruedihus.ch.
- The Sunnbüel cable car valley station is about 35 minutes on foot from the railway station, or 10 minutes on
  bus 241. VERIFIED, sunnbuel.ch.
- The Gasterntal walk starts at the station and passes the Sunnbüel valley station, then follows the Kander
  falls through the Chluse. VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch tour page.

## 4. SEASONS

### Summer
- **Oeschinen gondola**: daily **08:30 to 18:00**, last descent to Kandersteg at 18:00. From **20 June to
  20 September 2026 a time-slot reservation is required for the ascent**, in addition to a valid ticket.
  VERIFIED, oeschinensee.ch and adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch POI page.
- **Sunnbüel cable car** runs year round, with hours varying by season and weather; the published pattern at the
  time of checking was "Daily from 9:10 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. (last ascent at 4:40 p.m.)". VERIFIED, sunnbuel.ch.
- **Blausee** is "Open 365 days a year", with boat trips, playground, restaurant, hotel and cafe open daily.
  VERIFIED, blausee.ch.

### Winter
- Kandersteg is not a big downhill resort; it belongs to the Adelboden-Lenk-Kandersteg destination but the ski
  region pages count Adelboden and Lenk sectors, not Kandersteg. The village's winter offer published by the
  destination is winter hiking, snowshoeing, cross-country skiing and tobogganing.
  VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch.
- Chalet-Hotel Adler states a cross-country ski trail runs directly opposite the hotel. VERIFIED.
- Snowshoe trail Kandersteg to Oeschinensee is published by the destination as a winter route; on the circular
  trail towards "Läger" the lake is reached **in around 45 minutes**. VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch.
- Winter opening dates for the Oeschinen gondola 2026/27: NOT PUBLISHED on the pages fetched.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

### Two named walks (published figures)
1. **Kandersteg to Oeschinensee**: **4.4 km, 1 h 45**, 423 m of climb and 15 m of descent, from Kandersteg
   (1,171 m) to the lake, maximum altitude 1,594 m, rated moderate, dog-friendly.
   VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/activities/tour/detail/kandersteg-oeschinen-lake
2. **Gasterntal**: **8.9 km, 3 h**, 432 m of climb and 5 m of descent, from Kandersteg railway station to
   Heimritz, maximum 1,631 m, minimum 1,203 m, rated easy, waymarked yellow or red and white, with an optional
   bus back from Selden; the route follows the Kander falls through the Chluse and crosses a UNESCO World
   Heritage area. VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/activities/tour/detail/gasterntal-xi9vkxlc

If a shorter option is wanted: from the top of the Oeschinen gondola the lake is reached in **about 30 minutes**
on foot. VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch POI page.

### Two named mountain rides
1. **Oeschinen gondola** (Gondelbahn Oeschinensee): 8-seat cabins climbing **480 metres**, ride about
   **10 minutes**, daily 08:30 to 18:00, time-slot reservation required for the ascent from 20 June to
   20 September 2026. From the top station the lake, in a UNESCO World Heritage area and a nature reserve,
   is about 30 minutes on foot. A **750-metre mountain coaster** runs from the top station.
   VERIFIED, oeschinensee.ch and adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch.
2. **Sunnbüel cable car**: mountain station at **1,936 m**, ride **about eight minutes**, valley station
   reached from Kandersteg station in about 35 minutes on foot or about 10 minutes on AFA bus 241.
   VERIFIED, sunnbuel.ch.

### Two named restaurants (destination listing and the house's own site, no opinion)
1. **Restaurant Schweizerhof**, Bahnhofstrasse 1, 3718 Kandersteg. Open **Friday to Tuesday 10:00 to 22:00**,
   kitchen 11:30 to 16:00 and 18:00 to 20:30, **closed Wednesdays and Thursdays**. The house states it chooses
   ingredients with a focus on "locality and seasonality", with a constantly changing evening menu.
   VERIFIED, schweizerhofkandersteg.ch and the destination POI listing.
2. **Landgasthof Ruedihus**, Hinder de Büele 6. A country inn about 250 years old, run as the annexe of the
   Waldhotel Doldenhorn, serving Swiss specialities; it also has 10 rooms and 3 junior suites.
   VERIFIED, doldenhorn-ruedihus.ch.

### Typical of the place
- **The Lötschberg car train.** Kandersteg is the northern loading station: your car goes on the train and is
  on the other side of the mountain, at Goppenstein in the Valais, in 15 minutes. VERIFIED, bls.ch.
- **Kandersteg International Scout Centre**, the World Scout Centre described by its own site as "the Permanent
  Mini Jamboree, the dream of Lord Baden-Powell", with four buildings and a camping area for over 1,200 guests,
  65 km from Bern. VERIFIED, kisc.ch. (Founding year not stated on the page fetched.)
- **Blausee**, in the Kandertal at 3717 Blausee-Mitholz on the road down towards Frutigen: a nature park with
  an organic trout farm, a hotel with spa, restaurant and shop, open 365 days a year. VERIFIED, blausee.ch.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do I get to Kandersteg?**
   By direct train. Bern to Kandersteg is about 1 h 01 with no change on the BLS RE1, Spiez about 28 minutes,
   Interlaken Ost about 1 h 11 with one change at Spiez.
   Source: official Swiss timetable, checked 16/09/2026.

2. **Do I need to book to go up to the Oeschinensee?**
   Yes, in high summer. From 20 June to 20 September 2026 the gondola requires a time-slot reservation for the
   ascent as well as a valid ticket. The gondola runs daily 08:30 to 18:00 and climbs 480 metres in about
   10 minutes; from the top the lake is about 30 minutes on foot.
   Sources: oeschinensee.ch, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch.

3. **Can I walk up to the Oeschinensee instead?**
   Yes. The tourist office publishes Kandersteg to Oeschinensee as 4.4 km and 1 h 45, with 423 m of climb, rated
   moderate. Source: adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/activities/tour/detail/kandersteg-oeschinen-lake

4. **Can I bring a car, and can I drive on to the Valais?**
   You can drive to Kandersteg, though the tourist office says parking is limited and recommends public
   transport. From Kandersteg the BLS Lötschberg car train carries your car to Goppenstein in the Valais in
   15 minutes. Sources: adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch, bls.ch.

5. **What is the Scout centre in the village?**
   Kandersteg International Scout Centre, the World Scout Centre, with four buildings and a camping area for
   over 1,200 guests, 65 km from Bern. Source: kisc.ch.
