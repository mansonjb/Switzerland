## 2. ACCESS

Lenk is the terminus of the railway branch from Zweisimmen. Two ways in: the BLS Lötschberger via Bern, Spiez
and Zweisimmen, or the MOB GoldenPass Panoramic from Montreux to Zweisimmen and on.

| Leg | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Bern to Lenk | "approximately 1 hour 45 minutes" (tourist office); **1 h 46 with one change at Zweisimmen** (timetable) | VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch arrival page and the official timetable, which agree |
| Spiez to Lenk | **1 h 13, one change** at Zweisimmen | timetable |
| Zweisimmen to Lenk | **19 minutes, direct** | timetable |
| Zurich to Lenk | "2h 50 minutes" | VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch |
| Basel to Lenk | "2h 50 minutes" | VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch |
| Geneva to Lenk | "3h 30 minutes", via the GoldenPass Panoramic Montreux to Zweisimmen | VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch |
| Bern to Lenk by car | "approximately 1 hour 20 minutes", A6 towards Thun, exit Wimmis/Zweisimmen, then through the Simmenfluh tunnel | VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch |

**Parking.** "Summer parking is available free at several locations including Betelberg and Metsch valley
stations"; in winter there are paid short-term and long-term options, payable through Parkingpay or Easypark.
VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch arrival page.

**Guest card.** Visitors who pay the tourist tax get the **Simmental Card**, which includes free bus travel in
the region plus discounted or free offers. VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch. Garni-Hotel Alpenruh adds that from the
first night the card covers summer cable car passes for Betelberg and Metsch as well as buses, trains and
postal buses; treat the cable car element as the hotel's wording until confirmed on the tourist office page.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

### A. The village centre, around Kronenplatz and the station
- Hotel Krone is on Kronenplatz, "in the village center of Lenk im Simmental"; Garni-Hotel Alpenruh at
  Gässli 2 is described by the house as centrally placed. VERIFIED, mirolis-erlebniswelten.ch and
  alpenruh-lenk.ch.
- The station is the terminus of the branch from Zweisimmen and the bus hub for the valley.

### B. Badstrasse and the spa end of the village
- The Lenkerhof is at Badstrasse 20 and the Betelberg valley station at Badstrasse 1, so the gondola and the
  village's biggest hotel share a street. VERIFIED, lenkerhof.ch and lenk-simmental.ch POI page.
- This is the sector for anyone who wants to be at the lift.

### C. Aegertenstrasse, the sunny slope, and the valley head at Oberried
- Lenk Lodge (Aegertenstrasse 22) and Hotel Waldrand (Aegertenstrasse 12) are both on the raised, south-facing
  Aegerten side. VERIFIED, lenklodge.ch and hotelwaldrand.ch.
- Further up the valley, Hotel-Restaurant Simmenfälle sits at Oberriedstrasse 76, at the foot of the falls.
  VERIFIED, simmenfaelle.ch.
- Walking distances between these sectors are not published: UNVERIFIED.

## 4. SEASONS

### Summer
- **Betelberg gondola summer season 2026: 4 June to 25 October 2026**, first ascent from Lenk 08:30, last
  ascent from Lenk 16:30, last descent from Leiterli 17:00. From **31 October 2026** it runs Saturdays and
  Sundays 09:30 to 16:00 until the Adelboden-Lenk winter season starts.
  VERIFIED, betelberg.ch/bergsommer/betriebszeiten-sommer/
- **Alpine flower trail Betelberg: open June to September**, up to 70 species in flower in July and August.
  VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch.
- **Sibe Brünne** on the Rezliberg above Lenk, the source of the Simme: in summer **up to 2,800 litres a second**
  come out of the seven springs. VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/places/lenk

### Winter
- Lenk's Betelberg and Metsch belong to the **Adelboden-Lenk ski region**: "almost 200 kilometres of slopes" and
  "63 lifts" over six sub-areas (Adelboden-Lenk, Engstligenalp, Betelberg, Elsigen-Metsch, Silleren-Chuenisbärgli,
  Tschentenalp). VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk.ch/en/Experience/Skiregion. Note that the same operator's site also
  prints "210 kilometers" elsewhere: attribute the figure or drop it.
- **Winter 2026/27: continuous daily operation 5 December 2026 to 4 April 2027**, limited weekend operation from
  **28 November 2026**. VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk.ch/en/Service/Lifts-and-pistes
- **18 kilometres of classic and skating cross-country trails** on the Lenk valley floor, mostly flat, past the
  Lenkerseeli and on towards the Simmenfälle. VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch cross-country page.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

### Two named walks (published figures)
1. **Alpine flower trail Betelberg** (Alpenblumenweg): a circular loop that **starts and ends at the Leiterli
   mountain station**, **1.6 km** long, at **2,000 m above sea level**, with around **90 signs** naming the
   flowers; open **June to September**, with up to 70 species out in July and August.
   VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch/en/dc/detail/Service/alpenblumenweg-betelberg-4
2. **Leiterli to Tungelpass to Iffigsee to Iffigenalp**: **13.3 km, 4 h 35**, 531 m of climb and 946 m of
   descent, maximum 2,383 m, minimum 1,584 m, rated moderate, starting at Leiterli (top of the Betelberg
   gondola) and ending at Iffigenalp, with a bus or a walk back to Lenk. One short section at the Hengstensprung
   is secured with a fixed rope and is for experienced hikers.
   VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch/en/experiences-tours/touren-detailseite/detail/leiterli-tungelpass-iffigsee-iffigenalp-1

Winter alternative, if the guide needs one: **Simmenfälle**, a winter walk of **4.2 km in 1 hour**, 2 m of climb
and 42 m of descent, from the Simmenfälle restaurant back along the valley floor to Lenk, rated easy, between
1,065 m and 1,107 m. VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch/en/experiences-tours/touren-detailseite/detail/simmenfalle-lenk

### Two named mountain rides
1. **Betelberg gondola**, valley station Badstrasse 1 in Lenk, up to the **Leiterli** station: 6-seat cabins,
   of which **12 are "experience gondolas"** that tell stories in Swiss dialect and French through the
   characters Moritz the marmot, Mona the cow and Flöckli the goat; route Lenk to Stoss to Leiterli; summer
   season **4 June to 25 October 2026**. VERIFIED, betelberg.ch.
   Leiterli altitude: the tourist office writes "just under 2000 m" and the alpine flower trail page gives
   **2,000 m**; a search snippet gave 1,946 m and 850 m of climb, which could not be confirmed on an operator
   page. Use "just under 2,000 m" or the 2,000 m trail figure, not 1,946 m.
2. **Metsch** (Elsigen-Metsch sector): a Lenk-side valley station with free summer parking, part of the
   Adelboden-Lenk ski region. VERIFIED, lenk-simmental.ch arrival page and adelboden-lenk.ch.
   Altitudes and ride times: NOT PUBLISHED on the pages fetched.

### Two named restaurants (destination listing and the house's own site, no opinion)
1. **Hotel-Restaurant Simmenfälle**, Oberriedstrasse 76, at the head of the valley by the falls. Kitchen
   **11:45 to 14:00 and 18:00 to 20:00**; the season runs to about **9 November 2026**, weather and operations
   permitting. The house runs a "digital detox" policy and asks guests to switch off the wireless functions on
   their devices. VERIFIED, simmenfaelle.ch.
2. **Trattoria Krone**, Kronenplatz, in Hotel Krone. Mediterranean cooking at lunch and dinner, with a breakfast
   buffet of local Lenk products; the outdoor **Kronengärtli** opens from Whitsun until the first snow.
   VERIFIED, mirolis-erlebniswelten.ch/hotel-krone/

### Typical of the place
- **The Simmenfälle and the Sibe Brünne.** The Simme, the river that names the valley, comes out of seven
  crevices on the Rezliberg above Lenk, up to 2,800 litres a second in summer, and falls to the valley floor in
  a series of waterfalls. VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/places/lenk
- **The Iffigfall**, described by the destination as one of the most beautiful waterfalls in the Bernese
  Oberland, with the water dropping **more than a hundred metres** over several cascades.
  VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/places/lenk and lenk-simmental.ch.
- **The Lenkerseeli** at **1,064 m**, a small lake in a nature protection area beside the village centre.
  VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/places/lenk

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do I get to Lenk by train?**
   Lenk is the end of the branch from Zweisimmen. The tourist office publishes about 1 h 45 from Bern via the
   Lötschberger through Spiez and Zweisimmen, 2 h 50 from Zurich, 2 h 50 from Basel and 3 h 30 from Geneva by
   the GoldenPass Panoramic to Zweisimmen. The official timetable gives 1 h 46 from Bern and 19 minutes from
   Zweisimmen. Sources: lenk-simmental.ch arrival page; official Swiss timetable checked 16/09/2026.

2. **Is parking free in Lenk?**
   In summer, yes at several places including the Betelberg and Metsch valley stations. In winter there are paid
   short-term and long-term car parks, payable by app. Source: lenk-simmental.ch arrival page.

3. **When does the Betelberg gondola run?**
   Summer 2026: 4 June to 25 October, first ascent from Lenk 08:30, last ascent 16:30, last descent from
   Leiterli 17:00. From 31 October 2026 it runs Saturdays and Sundays 09:30 to 16:00 until the winter season
   opens. Source: betelberg.ch/bergsommer/betriebszeiten-sommer/

4. **What are the Sibe Brünne and the Simmenfälle?**
   The seven springs on the Rezliberg above Lenk are the source of the Simme, the river that gives the Simmental
   its name; in summer up to 2,800 litres a second come out of them, and the water falls to the valley floor as
   the Simmenfälle. Source: adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/places/lenk

5. **Is there skiing at Lenk itself?**
   Lenk's Betelberg and Metsch are two of the six sub-areas of the Adelboden-Lenk ski region, which the operator
   gives as almost 200 kilometres of slopes and 63 lifts. The 2026/27 season runs daily from 5 December 2026 to
   4 April 2027, with limited weekends from 28 November. There are also 18 km of cross-country trails on the
   valley floor. Sources: adelboden-lenk.ch, lenk-simmental.ch.
