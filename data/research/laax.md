Laax is in two parts: **Laax Dorf** (the old village, postcode 7031) and **Laax Murschetg** (the lift base, postcode 7032), joined by the Senda dil Dragun treetop walkway.

## 2. ACCESS

Times from the Swiss national timetable (SBB / PostBus data), queried 16/09/2026.

- **Zürich HB to Laax: 2 h 04, one change.** Zürich HB 09:07 IC 3 (SBB) to Chur 10:22, then PostBus line 81 from Chur, Postautostation 10:28 to Laax GR, Bergbahnen 11:11.
- **Chur to Laax, Bergbahnen (Murschetg): 40 min** by PostBus. Chur to Laax GR, Posta (the village): 53 min.
- **No railway station in Laax.** Chur is the railhead. The tourist office publishes "From there erreichst du Flims Laax with the Postauto in weniger als 40 Minuten". Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/anreise
- **By car:** "30 Minuten von Chur", "1 Stunde 45 Minuten von Zürich", "In 20 Minuten bei uns" from the Reichenau motorway exit. Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/anreise
- **Parking:** the Laax Murschetg garage takes reserved Inside Parking bookings by QR code; published rates from free for 30 minutes to CHF 32.80 for 24 hours; coach parking must be reserved online. Source: https://www.laax.com/en/transportation
- **Airport:** a winter transfer from Zurich Airport is published at CHF 79, reserved 48 hours ahead. Same source.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. Laax Murschetg (the lift base)
- The purpose-built base at the LAAX valley station, on Via Murschetg. **rocksresort** is "Located directly at the LAAX valley station" with "The LAAX gondola is right on the doorstep", and holds "8 Restaurants" and "9 Stores". **signinahotel** (Via Murschetg 15) sits "directly at the LAAX valley station and within the rocksresort". **Riders Hotel** is at Via Murschetg 1.
- Sources: https://www.rocksresort.com/en/ , https://www.signinahotel.com/en/ , https://ridershotel.com , https://www.laax.com/en/sleep

### B. Laax Dorf (the old village, 7031)
- The original village around the Laaxersee, about 1.5 km from the lift base and joined to it by the **Senda dil Dragun** walkway ("The 1.56 kilometer long walkway" connecting "the two districts of Laax Murschetg and Laax Dorf"). Hotel Posta Veglia is on the Via Principala.
- Sources: https://www.laax.com/en/senda-dil-dragun and https://postaveglialaax.ch/

### C. Up the mountain
- Laax is one of the few Graubünden resorts where the lift company publishes places to sleep above the valley: **Segneshütte** at "2,100 m above sea level" in the UNESCO Tektonikarena Sardona, "One of the oldest mountain huts in the region", open all year; **GALAAXY mountainhostel** at "2252 m above sea level", "directly on the ski slope", winter only; **Berghaus Nagens**, winter only, "just a few steps until you're right on the slope"; and a **TCS pop-up glamping** site at "2,000 meters above sea level" from "end of June to mid-September".
- Source: https://www.laax.com/en/sleep

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks**
1. **Senda dil Dragun.** Published as **"The 1.56 kilometer long walkway"** through the treetops between Laax Murschetg and Laax Dorf, starting at rocksresort. It has "a slide, five adventure spots, four platforms with seating areas, as well as five marble run installations". Open all year: 09:00 to 18:00 from May to October, 10:00 to 15:30 from November to April. "Access is only permitted on foot as well as with a wheelchair or stroller"; "Dogs or animals of any kind are unfortunately not allowed on the trail" except assistance and guide dogs on a lead. Entry from CHF 22. Source: https://www.laax.com/en/senda-dil-dragun
2. **Trutg dil Flem** (shared with Flims, reachable from Laax by the destination lifts and buses). Published as **8.6 km**, **3 h 30** downhill and **4:30 h** uphill, from the lower Segnesboden to the Flims valley station, over **seven bridges** by the Grisons engineer **Jürg Conzett**. Passable only in the "snow- and ice-free time". Main prize, **Prix Rando 2014**. Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/aktiv-sein/wandern/trutg-dil-flem

**Two named mountain rides / attractions**
1. **LAAX gondola from Laax Murschetg to Crap Sogn Gion.** The valley station is right at rocksresort; the area it opens has "216 km Pistenkilometer" and "70%" of its terrain above 2,000 m, with the world's longest continuous halfpipe and 5 snowparks. Sources: https://www.rocksresort.com/en/ and https://www.flimslaax.com/ski-snowboard
2. **Vorab glacier and the Vorab Restaurant at 2,570 m**, the highest restaurant in the LAAX ski area, winter only. Sources: https://www.flimslaax.com/ski-snowboard and https://www.laax.com/en/food-drinks

**Two named restaurants (listed by the lift company, no opinion)**
1. **Ristorante Camino**, in the valley at Laax. Serves "Homemade pasta and risotto variations, meat and fish dishes, traditional pizza from the wood-fired oven and grill specialties". Source: https://www.laax.com/en/food-drinks
2. **Segneshütte**, at the lower Segnesboden, "2,100 m above sea level", in the UNESCO Tektonikarena Sardona, "One of the oldest mountain huts in the region", serving "Original Swiss delicacies", open "All year round". Own site segneshuette.ch. Sources: https://www.laax.com/en/food-drinks and https://www.laax.com/en/sleep

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **Freestyle.** LAAX publishes 5 snowparks with over 90 obstacles and the world's longest continuous halfpipe, plus a Freestyle Outdoor Park at rocksresort and a Kids Freestyle Academy with its own café. Sources: https://www.flimslaax.com/ski-snowboard , https://www.laax.com/en/sleep , https://www.laax.com/en/food-drinks
- **rocksresort as a village in itself:** "248 Apartments", "8 Restaurants", "9 Stores". Source: https://www.rocksresort.com/en/
- **Rhine Gorge (Ruinaulta).** The **Crap Signina** viewpoint is "30-minute walk from Laax" with a covered shelter; a PostBus Cabriolet runs twice daily between Laax and Tamins with reservation. Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/naturerlebnisse/rheinschlucht

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Laax Murschetg or Laax Dorf?** Murschetg is the lift base: rocksresort and the signinahotel are "directly at the LAAX valley station" and the gondola is "right on the doorstep". Laax Dorf is the old village, joined to the base by the Senda dil Dragun treetop walkway, "The 1.56 kilometer long walkway". Sources: https://www.rocksresort.com/en/ , https://www.signinahotel.com/en/ , https://www.laax.com/en/senda-dil-dragun
2. **Is there a train to Laax?** No. Chur is the railhead; the PostBus takes 40 minutes from Chur station to Laax GR, Bergbahnen, and Zürich HB to Laax is 2 h 04 with that one change. Source: Swiss national timetable, checked 16/09/2026.
3. **Can I sleep up on the mountain?** Yes, and unusually the lift company lists the options: Segneshütte at 2,100 m (all year), GALAAXY mountainhostel at 2,252 m and Berghaus Nagens (winter only), and a TCS pop-up glamping site at 2,000 m from the end of June to mid-September. Source: https://www.laax.com/en/sleep
4. **What is Laax known for?** The lift company publishes 5 snowparks with over 90 obstacles and the world's longest continuous halfpipe, on an area of "216 km Pistenkilometer" with "70%" of the terrain above 2,000 m. Source: https://www.flimslaax.com/ski-snowboard
5. **When does the season run?** Winter pre-season 28 November to 17 December 2026, main season 18 December 2026 to 29 March 2027, low season 30 March to 11 April 2027. Source: https://www.laax.com/opening-hours
