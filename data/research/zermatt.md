## 1. IDENTITY

- Slug on the sheet: `zermatt`. Region key `valais`. Canton: Valais (Wallis).
- Altitude on the sheet: **1608 m**. No official page contradicts it outright, but Zermatt Tourismus and
  Zermatt Bergbahnen usually round to "1600 m" in marketing copy. Wikipedia gives 1608 m for the village.
  KEEP 1608 on the sheet.
- Coordinates on the sheet: 46.0207 / 7.7491 (village centre, near the main station). Consistent with the
  village centre. No change.
- carFree: **true**, and this is a legal rule, not a habit. Zermatt Tourismus: "Zermatt is car-free.
  Private vehicles are only allowed as far as Täsch." Offenders face penalties.
- How cars are handled: you leave the car at the **Matterhorn Terminal Täsch**, 2100 covered spaces, plus
  over 1000 more spaces from private operators in Täsch. From Täsch a shuttle train runs **every 20 minutes**
  and takes **12 minutes** to the centre of Zermatt.
- Inside the village: electric taxis, horse-drawn carriages, bicycles, and a **free e-bus on 2 routes**.
- Residents: around **5800 to 6000**, depending on the source and the date. Not confirmed on
  gemeinde.zermatt.ch during this check (the page returned HTTP 429). Treat as UNVERIFIED until refetched.
- Tourist beds: no official figure found. UNVERIFIED.

## 2. ACCESS

Published by Zermatt Tourismus (zermatt.swiss/en/arrival):
- **Zurich Airport to Zermatt: about 3.5 hours** by train.
- **Geneva Airport to Zermatt: about 4 hours**. Basel Airport: about 4 hours.
- Trains to Zermatt run **every half hour**.
- Rail gateway is **Visp** (and Brig), from where the Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn runs up the valley.
  MGB half-hourly Visp to Zermatt. Published journey time Visp to Zermatt is NOT stated on an official
  page I could reach; timetable aggregators give around 1 h 05 to 1 h 10. UNVERIFIED, do not publish a figure.
- Alternative arrival: **Matterhorn Alpine Crossing**, cable car from Cervinia (Italy) to Zermatt, with
  luggage transport.

Parking (car-free arrangement):
- **Matterhorn Terminal Täsch**: 2100 covered spaces, plus 1000+ private spaces in Täsch.
- Shuttle train Täsch to Zermatt: every 20 minutes, 12 minutes.
- No published parking tariff found on the official arrival page. UNVERIFIED.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. Bahnhofstrasse and the station end**
- The main street runs from the station south through the village; the big houses sit on it
  (Zermatterhof at Bahnhofstrasse 55, Mont Cervin Palace at Bahnhofstrasse 31, Monte Rosa at
  Bahnhofstrasse 80).
- The **Gornergrat Bahn valley station is 50 metres from Zermatt railway station** (zermatt.swiss listing).
- Grand Hotel Zermatterhof states it is about 10 minutes on foot from the station.

**B. Hinterdorf and Kirchplatz (the old village)**
- The old part of the village holds **more than 30 buildings** in the traditional upper Valais style:
  barns, stores, stables and houses **built between the 16th and 18th centuries**, in larch, many raised on
  stone slabs on stilts.
- The **Matterhorn Museum Zermatlantis is on Kirchplatz**.

**C. Winkelmatten and the south end (towards the Matterhorn Glacier Paradise valley station)**
- The quiet residential end of the village, uphill and south of the centre, served by the **free e-bus**
  (2 routes, per Zermatt Tourismus).
- The valley station for the Matterhorn Glacier Paradise cable cars is at this end of the village.
- CAUTION: I did not find an official zermatt.swiss page for the Winkelmatten district itself (the URL I
  tried returned 404). The e-bus fact is official; the "quiet residential" wording is editorial, keep it
  free of numbers.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2)**
1. **5-Seenweg (Five Lakes Walk)** — Switzerland Tourism route page: **11 km, 3 h 15**, 380 m of ascent and
   660 m of descent, from Blauherd down to Sunnegga, past Stellisee, Grindjisee, Grünsee, Moosjisee and
   Leisee. (Signposted as Zermatt hike no. 11; SwitzerlandMobility route 186.)
   NOTE: a separate figure of 9.8 km / 2 h 30 circulates on blogs. Use the Switzerland Tourism figures only.
2. **Riffelseeweg (trail no. 21)** — short walk from the Gornergrat line: **2.9 km, about 1 hour**, from
   Rotenboden down to Riffelberg past the Riffelsee. Attribute to gornergrat.ch (their hiking listing).
   The gornergrat.ch/en/pages/hiking URL returned 404 on this check; refetch and confirm before publishing.

**Rides and sights (2 + the typical two)**
1. **Gornergrat Bahn** — cog railway from Zermatt to **Gornergrat at 3089 m**, **33 minutes**, valley
   station 50 m from Zermatt station, panorama over **28 to 29 four-thousand-metre peaks**. Intermediate
   stops: Findelbach, Riffelalp, Riffelberg, Rotenboden. Fare "from CHF 66.00"; **50% discount with
   Half-Fare Card, GA or Swiss Travel Pass** (gornergrat.ch ticket page).
2. **Matterhorn Glacier Paradise** — **3883 m**, described by the operator as "Europe's highest mountain
   station". About **40 minutes** from Zermatt including transfers. Adult return **from CHF 120.00**,
   **50% off with Swiss Travel Pass or Half Fare Card**. At the top: viewing platform (360 degrees, 38
   Alpine summits), the Glacier Palace, the Cinema Lounge, the Crystal Ride cabins.
3. **Matterhorn Museum Zermatlantis** — Kirchplatz, opened **2006**, built underground, shows the
   **original snapped rope from the first ascent of 1865**, reconstructed old houses, a multimedia room and
   a Matterhorn relief. Adults **CHF 12**, seniors CHF 10, students CHF 8, teenagers CHF 7, under 9 free
   with an adult. Hours: 1 June to 30 September 14:00-18:00 daily; 1 to 31 October 15:00-18:00 daily;
   closed 1 to 19 November; 20 November to 17 December Friday to Sunday 15:00-18:00; 18 to 31 December
   15:00-18:00 daily.

**Restaurants (2, from the Zermatt Tourismus listings)**
1. **Chez Vrony** — Findeln, **2100 m**. Listed as Italian, regional, Swiss and international; specialities
   fish, game, fondue and raclette; own organic products to traditional Alpine recipes; family run for over
   100 years. Open daily 11:30 to 16:00 (hot meals in that window). zermatt.swiss listing.
2. **Findlerhof** — Findeln, **2051 m**, above Zermatt in the middle of a hiking and skiing area.
   zermatt.swiss listing.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Can I drive to Zermatt?** No. Zermatt Tourismus states the village is car-free and private vehicles
   are only allowed as far as Täsch. You park at the Matterhorn Terminal Täsch (2100 covered spaces) and
   take the shuttle train, every 20 minutes, 12 minutes to the village. (zermatt.swiss/en/arrival)
2. **How long does it take from Zurich Airport?** About 3.5 hours by train, according to Zermatt Tourismus;
   about 4 hours from Geneva or Basel airports. Trains run every half hour. (zermatt.swiss/en/arrival)
3. **How high is the Gornergrat and how long is the ride?** The Gornergrat station is at 3089 m and the cog
   railway takes 33 minutes from Zermatt, from a valley station 50 m from the main railway station.
   (gornergrat.ch, zermatt.swiss)
4. **Does the Swiss Travel Pass cover the mountain railways in Zermatt?** No, not free. It gives 50% off the
   Gornergrat Bahn and 50% off Matterhorn Glacier Paradise. (gornergrat.ch, matterhornparadise.ch)
5. **When does the ski season run?** 1 November 2026 to 3 May 2027 overall; the Sunnegga/Rothorn sector runs
   28 November 2026 to 11 April 2027 and the Gornergrat sector 28 November 2026 to 18 April 2027.
   (matterhornparadise.ch)
