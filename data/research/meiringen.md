## 2. ACCESS

Meiringen is on the Zentralbahn line between Interlaken Ost and Lucerne over the Brünig. The Luzern-Interlaken
Express calls here.

| Leg | Timetabled time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Interlaken Ost to Meiringen | **31 minutes** on the Luzern-Interlaken Express, **33 minutes** on the regional R70, both direct | |
| Lucerne to Meiringen | **1 h 10** on the Luzern-Interlaken Express, **1 h 15** on the InterRegio, both direct | |
| Bern to Meiringen | **1 h 31, one change** at Interlaken Ost | |
| Meiringen to Brienz | **11 minutes**, direct | |
| Meiringen to the Aare gorge | two purpose-built halts on the Meiringen-Innertkirchen line, **Aareschlucht West** and **Aareschlucht Ost**, one at each entrance | VERIFIED, aareschlucht.ch |
| Reichenbachfall-Bahn valley station | at the private clinic in **Meiringen, Willigen**; PostBus stop "Willigen, Klinik Reichenbach", or a Meiringen-Innertkirchen-Bahn stop | VERIFIED, grimselwelt.ch |

**Driving and parking.** Free parking for cars and coaches at the Aare gorge (west entrance). At the
Reichenbachfall-Bahn there are only a few spaces at the station itself, and the operator directs drivers to the
"public parking lots Alpbach Meiringen". VERIFIED, aareschlucht.ch and grimselwelt.ch.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

### A. The village centre, around Bahnhofstrasse and Kirchgasse
- Parkhotel du Sauvage is at Bahnhofstrasse 30, "in a quiet location in the centre of Meiringen"; Hotel Alpbach
  is at Kirchgasse 17; Hotel Dakota at Amthausgasse 2. VERIFIED, each hotel's site.
- Haslital Tourismus is on Bahnhofplatz 12, at the station, and the Sherlock Holmes Museum is in the old English
  Church in the centre. VERIFIED.

### B. Willigen and the east end, towards the Aare gorge and the Reichenbach falls
- The Reichenbachfall-Bahn valley station is at Willigen, by the private clinic. VERIFIED, grimselwelt.ch.
- The Aare gorge west entrance is at Aareschluchtstrasse 3, with its own station halt and free parking.
  VERIFIED, aareschlucht.ch.
- This is where the two set-piece attractions are; no hotel with an official site was confirmed in this sector
  in this pass.

### C. Up the pass roads: Rosenlaui, Schwarzwaldalp and Hasliberg
- Hotel Rosenlaui sits at **1,330 m** in the Rosenlaui valley on the Grosse Scheidegg road, with a PostBus free
  ticket included in the room rate. VERIFIED, rosenlaui.ch.
- Chalet-Hotel Schwarzwaldalp is further up the same road, still in the municipality of Meiringen, postcode
  3860. VERIFIED, schwarzwaldalp.ch.
- The Hasliberg terraces (Reuti, Goldern, Hohfluh, Wasserwendi) carry most of the region's ski beds and are a
  separate place from Meiringen: do not mix them into the Meiringen hotel list.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

### Two named walks
1. **Through the Aare gorge.** The gorge is **1.4 km long and 200 metres deep**, walked on galleries and through
   tunnels and over bridges, with an entrance at each end (west, with the restaurant and the free car park, and
   east, the coach drop-off), each with its own railway halt. Open 3 April to 1 November 2026 at the west
   entrance and 9 May to 1 November at the east. Adults CHF 13.00.
   VERIFIED, aareschlucht.ch and haslital.swiss. **Walking time through the gorge: NOT PUBLISHED.**
2. **Reichenbach falls, the long way round.** Grimselwelt publishes a route that goes up by the funicular and
   then "via Zwirgi to the other side of the falls to Sherlock Holmes' crash site", **about 2 hours 30 minutes**
   for the descent. VERIFIED, grimselwelt.ch.

The Haslital publishes "over 300 kilometres of signposted hiking trails" as a regional total, but its route
database is JavaScript-rendered and no individual distances were captured. VERIFIED for the 300 km figure only.

### Two named mountain rides
1. **Reichenbachfall-Bahn**: a funicular running "since 1899" up to the **110-metre-high Reichenbach Falls**,
   two cars of 24 passengers, season 2 May to 4 October 2026, 09:00 to 17:30, last ascent 17:15, valley station
   at Willigen. VERIFIED, grimselwelt.ch.
   **DISCREPANCY:** the Sherlock Holmes Museum's own site calls the fall 120 metres. Attribute whichever figure
   you use; do not print both flat.
2. **Meiringen-Hasliberg gondolas to the Alpen tower**: Reuti to Bidmi to Mägisalp, then Mägisalp to the
   **Alpen tower** panorama restaurant, which Haslital Tourismus describes as having "a view of over 400
   mountain peaks". Summer 13 June to 25 October 2026. The lifts run between 600 and 2500 m and carry over half
   a million people a year. VERIFIED, meiringen-hasliberg.ch and haslital.swiss.
   Exact station altitudes: only "Bidmi family restaurant: self-service restaurant at 1423 metres above sea
   level" is published. VERIFIED, haslital.swiss.

### Two named restaurants (Haslital Tourismus listing, no opinion)
1. **Tea Room Frutiger**, Meiringen. The listing reads: "Fine Haslital specialities - from Tatzelwurm to
   meringues and Hasli cake." VERIFIED, haslital.swiss catering listing. This is the place to hang the
   meringue line on, because the tourist office itself names meringues there.
2. **Restaurant Aare Gorge**, Meiringen: "A self-service restaurant and halls for all kinds of events", at the
   west entrance of the gorge. VERIFIED, haslital.swiss catering listing.

Others named in the same listing, if a swap is needed: Sauvage Restaurant ("Varied, regional - and freshly
prepared"), Restaurant Pizzeria Bahnhöfli ("Classic cuisine and freshly prepared pizzas"), Ristorante Castello
("Italian cuisine"), Steakhouse Leopold, Restaurant Rössli.

### Typical of the place
- **The Aare gorge**: 1.4 km long, 200 m deep, cut by the Aare glacier, with glacial mills and limestone
  formations, and its own two railway halts. VERIFIED, aareschlucht.ch and haslital.swiss.
- **The Reichenbach falls and Sherlock Holmes.** Conan Doyle killed Holmes at the Reichenbachfall in "The Final
  Problem". The Sherlock Holmes Museum in Meiringen is housed in the old English Church and holds what it calls
  the only authentic reconstruction in the world of the sitting room at 221b Baker Street; outside, a life-size
  bronze of Conan Doyle sits on Conan Doyle Place with **60 puzzle symbols**, each pointing to one Holmes story.
  VERIFIED, sherlockholmes.ch and grimselwelt.ch.
- **The Rosenlaui glacier gorge**, described by Haslital Tourismus as having "Massive waterfalls, bizarre rock
  formations and romantic grottos", up the Grosse Scheidegg road. VERIFIED, haslital.swiss.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do I get to Meiringen?**
   By the Zentralbahn over the Brünig. Interlaken Ost to Meiringen is 31 minutes on the Luzern-Interlaken
   Express and 33 minutes on the regional train; Lucerne is 1 h 10, Bern 1 h 31 with a change at Interlaken Ost.
   Source: official Swiss timetable, checked 16/09/2026.

2. **When is the Aare gorge open and what does it cost?**
   In 2026 the west entrance is open 3 April to 1 November and the east entrance 9 May to 1 November, from 08:30
   with closing at 17:30 or, between 13 June and 20 September, 18:30. Adults from 16 pay CHF 13.00, children
   from 6 to 15.99 CHF 8.00, under 6 free. The gorge is 1.4 km long and 200 metres deep.
   Source: aareschlucht.ch/de/Info/informationen

3. **Can I get to the Aare gorge without a car?**
   Yes. The Meiringen-Innertkirchen line has a halt at each entrance, Aareschlucht West and Aareschlucht Ost.
   There is also free parking for cars and coaches at the west entrance. Source: aareschlucht.ch.

4. **What is the Sherlock Holmes connection?**
   Conan Doyle had Holmes die at the Reichenbach falls in "The Final Problem". The funicular has run up to the
   falls since 1899, the museum in the old English Church holds a reconstruction of the Baker Street sitting
   room, and a bronze of Conan Doyle sits outside on Conan Doyle Place with 60 puzzle symbols, one per story.
   Sources: grimselwelt.ch, sherlockholmes.ch.

5. **Is there skiing at Meiringen?**
   The skiing is on the Hasliberg terraces above the village, reached by the gondola from Hasliberg Reuti.
   The area has 60 kilometres of pistes and 25 km of winter walking trails, and the 2026/27 season runs
   19 December 2026 to 29 March 2027. Sources: meiringen-hasliberg.ch.
