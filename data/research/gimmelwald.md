# Research dossier: Gimmelwald

Checked 16/09/2026. Official sources: Schilthornbahn (schilthorn.ch), Switzerland Tourism, the village site
gimmelwald.ch, Lauterbrunnen and Mürren tourist offices, and each house's own site. No ratings, no reviews.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Slug on the sheet: `gimmelwald`. Region key `bernese-oberland`. Canton: **Bern**. Gimmelwald is a hamlet
  of the **municipality of Lauterbrunnen**.
- Altitude on the sheet: **1363 m**. **TWO OFFICIAL SOURCES DISAGREE.** Schilthornbahn publishes
  **1367 m.a.s.l.** for the Gimmelwald station, and Switzerland Tourism publishes **1367 m** for the
  village. Chalet Anneli's own page also uses 1367 m. Wikipedia gives 1363 to 1364 m.
  **RECOMMENDED CHANGE: altitude 1367.**
- Coordinates on the sheet: 46.5462 / 7.8914. Consistent with the hamlet. No change.
- carFree: **true**. Switzerland Tourism: Gimmelwald "can only be reached by cable car" and is car-free.
  The village townscape itself is protected.
- How you arrive: there is no road for visitors. You either take the **Schilthornbahn cable car from
  Stechelberg** (one section up), or come **down from Mürren** (cable car or on foot). Cars are left in
  **Stechelberg**, at the Schilthornbahn valley station car park.
- Residents: **109 at December 2025** (figure from a Wikipedia summary of the federal register, not
  confirmed on a cantonal or federal page during this check). Treat as UNVERIFIED until checked on the
  Federal Statistical Office.
- Tourist beds: no official figure. UNVERIFIED. In practice the village has 8 listed addresses (below).

## 2. ACCESS

- The Schilthornbahn runs **Stechelberg - Gimmelwald - Mürren - Birg - Schilthorn (Piz Gloria)**.
  Published station altitudes: Mürren **1638 m**, Birg **2677 m**, Schilthorn **2970 m**.
- The **Stechelberg to Mürren cable car is the steepest cableway in the world, gradient 159.4 per cent**
  (Schilthorn Cableway Ltd).
- Gateway legs: rail to **Interlaken Ost**, train to **Lauterbrunnen**, then **bus to Stechelberg**, then
  the cable car up to Gimmelwald. Published leg times for Interlaken Ost to Lauterbrunnen and the
  Lauterbrunnen to Stechelberg bus are already recorded in data/SOURCES.md for the other Oberland guides;
  reuse them rather than inventing new ones.
- **Parking for the car-free village: Stechelberg**, at the Schilthornbahn valley station. Chalet
  Husmättli states it plainly: park in Stechelberg and take the Schilthorn cable car. No official page
  giving the number of spaces or the tariff was found. UNVERIFIED.
- **Published maintenance closures (schilthorn.ch tariff and timetable page):**
  - Stechelberg - Gimmelwald - Mürren: **19 to 30 October 2026**, **26 April to 7 May 2027**,
    28 to 29 October 2027.
  - Mürren - Allmendhubel: 19 October to 11 December 2026, 5 April to 28 May 2027.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

Gimmelwald is one hamlet of a few dozen buildings, so "areas" means the three ways of staying there.

**A. By the cable car station**
- The Schilthornbahn station is the centre of the village; there is a **playground at the cable car
  station** (Switzerland Tourism).
- Chalet Anneli states it is **about 3 minutes on foot from the Gimmelwald station**.
- Esther's Guesthouse is at **Kirchstatt 751a**, close to the station.

**B. The quiet upper lanes**
- Olle & Maria's B&B, at **Reindli 783**, states it is **five minutes from the cable car station, in a very
  quiet location**.
- Hotel Mittaghorn is at **Bi Wyleren**, at the top of the village. It is **temporarily closed** and not
  taking bookings (see hotels).

**C. Stechelberg and Mürren, one cable car away**
- If nothing in Gimmelwald is free, the two neighbours are one section away: **Mürren at 1638 m** above,
  **Stechelberg** below. Mürren already has its own guide on this site, so cross-link it.

## 4. SEASONS

- **Cable car**: runs all year, with the maintenance windows listed above (19 to 30 October 2026 and
  26 April to 7 May 2027 for the Stechelberg - Gimmelwald - Mürren line). This is the single most useful
  seasonal fact for Gimmelwald: when that line stops, the village is only reachable on foot.
- **Mountain Hostel Gimmelwald**: hostel and restaurant **open daily 15 May to 18 October** (own site).
  Café and bar 07:30 to 22:30, food 12:00 to 21:00.
- Schilthorn / Piz Gloria expected winter season start given by the operator on the Gimmelwald page:
  **19 December 2025** (for that season; refresh for 2026/27 before publishing).
- No ski area in Gimmelwald itself. The skiing is the **Mürren-Schilthorn** area, covered in the Mürren guide.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2, published times from gimmelwald.ch)**
1. **Gimmelwald to Mürren** — **about 55 minutes**, **270 m of climb**. Passable with a pushchair if you
   stay on the paved road.
2. **Gimmelwald to the Sprutz waterfall and Spielbodenalp** — **1 h 30**, **270 m of climb**, intermediate.
   The path **goes behind the waterfall** before zigzagging up to the alp.
   (Other published times on the same page: Gimmelwald to Stechelberg 2 h 20, 550 m of descent;
   Gimmelwald to the Sefinental via Chilchbalm 2 h 45, 370 m; Tanzbödeli detour 45 minutes, 80 m up
   and down, on the difficult 6 to 9 hour Gimmelwald - Obersteinberg route.)

**Rides and sights (2)**
1. **Schilthornbahn to Piz Gloria** — Gimmelwald is a station on the line to **Birg 2677 m** and
   **Schilthorn / Piz Gloria 2970 m**. The **Stechelberg to Mürren section is the world's steepest
   cableway at 159.4 per cent**. Adult return fares published by the operator (valid to 12 December 2026):
   **Stechelberg - Mürren CHF 23.60**, **Stechelberg - Schilthorn CHF 115.00**, **Mürren - Schilthorn
   CHF 91.40**.
2. **The village itself** — Switzerland Tourism calls it one of the most unspoilt mountain villages in
   Switzerland: wooden chalets, old barns, farm animals, and **a protected townscape**. There is a
   **via ferrata** and a **climbing garden** in the village, and it is the **starting point for walks into
   the Sefinental**.

**Places to eat (2, from their own sites)**
1. **Pension Gimmelwald** — terrace with mountain views, a bar and a fireplace; **Mönchs Shack**, an
   outdoor kitchen, open **12:00 to 17:00 daily in summer**; three-course evening menus by reservation;
   a trust-based **Honesty Shop**. The house is **150 years old**.
2. **Mountain Hostel Gimmelwald** — hostel restaurant and bar, **food daily 12:00 to 21:00**, café and bar
   **07:30 to 22:30**, open **15 May to 18 October**.

**The typical local fact**: Gimmelwald has **no road access for visitors at all**; every arrival is by cable
car or on foot. That is stronger than Zermatt's or Saas-Fee's arrangement and is the whole story of the page.

## 6. HOTELS (the full list of the village, 8 addresses, from gimmelwald.ch)

Gimmelwald genuinely has no large hotels. The complete official accommodation list is below. Where a house

   - Kirchstatt 751a, 3826 Gimmelwald

   - The Eggimann family has taken travellers since 1994

5. **Chalet Husmättli** — https://www.husmaettli.ch/ — village
   - A whole house for 2 to 6 people, three bedrooms

**Hotel Mittaghorn** — https://mittaghorn.ch/en/ — Bi Wyleren. **Temporarily closed and not taking

people there.

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

- **Stechelberg to Gimmelwald and on to Mürren appears to be free with the Swiss Travel Pass**, as is the
  bus Lauterbrunnen to Stechelberg, with a **50% discount above Mürren**: the discounted adult return
  Mürren to Schilthorn is **CHF 45.70**, exactly half the published adult return of CHF 91.40.
  The CHF 45.70 figure is on the schilthorn.ch tariff table. The "free as far as Mürren" wording came from
  a search summary, not from a page I read directly. **VERIFY ON schilthorn.ch OR swisstravelsystem.com
  BEFORE PUBLISHING.**
- **Berner Oberland Pass**: adults travel free to the Schilthorn, per the same summaries. UNVERIFIED.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do you get to Gimmelwald?** Only by cable car or on foot. The Schilthornbahn runs Stechelberg to
   Gimmelwald to Mürren; cars stay in Stechelberg. (schilthorn.ch, myswitzerland.com)
2. **Can you walk to Mürren?** Yes, about 55 minutes and 270 m of climb, and the paved road version works
   with a pushchair. (gimmelwald.ch)
3. **When does the cable car stop?** For maintenance on the Stechelberg - Gimmelwald - Mürren line, 19 to
   30 October 2026 and 26 April to 7 May 2027. (schilthorn.ch)
4. **Is there a hotel in Gimmelwald?** Not in the usual sense. The village lists a pension, a hostel, a
   guesthouse, a B&B and a few chalets. Hotel Mittaghorn has been closed for renovation since 2020 and its
   owners say the reopening date is uncertain. (gimmelwald.ch, mittaghorn.ch)
5. **How high is Gimmelwald?** 1367 m according to the Schilthornbahn and Switzerland Tourism, on the
   cable car line between Stechelberg and Mürren (1638 m). (schilthorn.ch, myswitzerland.com)
