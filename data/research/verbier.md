## 2. ACCESS

Nearest railhead: **Le Châble** (line Martigny to Le Châble, RegionAlps).

Published train times to Le Châble (verbier.ch/en/practice/move-transport-and-mobility/getting-to-verbier/):
- Sion 1 h 10
- Lausanne 1 h 40
- Geneva 2 h 40
- Zurich 4 h
- Paris 6 h 15
- London 9 h 30

Verbier 4Vallées gives door-to-door figures by train of Geneva 2 h 30, Bern 3 h, Zurich 4 h, Basel 4 h
(verbier4vallees.ch/en/useful-information/getting-to-verbier).

**Last leg, Le Châble to Verbier** (verbier.ch, same page): two options at the same fare.
- Gondola Le Châble to Verbier, arriving at Médran, running continuously 05:10 to 23:55.
  The live-information page for summer 2026 shows the same line at 05:10 to 23:55
  (verbier4vallees.ch/en/useful-information/live-information-summer).
  The operator's own press release words it as 05:15 to 23:50 all year round except on maintenance days
  (verbier4vallees.ch press release "Operating hours extended for the Le Châble-Verbier line").
  On maintenance days the bus service between Le Châble and Verbier is increased to compensate (same release).
- Bus, which sets you down at Station Poste on the central square. Free the same day for ski pass holders
  while the gondola is running (verbier.ch).
- The tourist office describes this as "the final 20 minutes" of the trip (verbier.ch).

**By car.** Verbier is reached by road. From Martigny the operator gives 30 minutes by car
(verbier4vallees.ch/en/useful-information/getting-to-verbier). Car parks listed by the tourist office:
in Verbier, Catogne, Ermitage, Périn, Marais Vert; at Le Châble, Curala P1 to P3, Les Posettes,
Verger de la Cure, plus a P4 motorhome facility. The Le Châble foot-of-lifts car park is free for up to
10 days (verbier.ch, same page).

**Direct train.** The Verbier Express / VosAlpes Express runs from Geneva Airport and from Fribourg at
weekends and on public holidays all year, and carries a 30 percent reduction on a one-day or half-day
ski pass (verbier.ch and verbier4vallees.ch).

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

**A. Médran (bottom of the lifts).**
Rue de Médran runs down from the centre to the Médran lift station, where the Le Châble gondola arrives
and where the ski lifts leave for Les Ruinettes. Hotels sit on this street: W Verbier at Rue de Médran 70
(verbier.ch listing), Les Chamois at Rue de Médran 9 (hotel-chamois.ch). Hôtel Mirabeau states it is
"less than 1 minute flat walking" from the Médran lift departure and the town centre (mirabeauhotel.ch).
Points: gondola from Le Châble arrives here; no transfer to the ski lifts.

**B. Place Centrale / Carrefour Central (village centre).**
The shops, the bars and the tourist office. The Le Châble bus sets down at Station Poste on the central
square (verbier.ch). Hôtel Ermitage is at Carrefour Central 18 and states it is next to the tourist
office (ermitage-verbier.ch). Hotel Vanessa, Chemin des Vernes 1, gives 50 m to the central square and
350 m to the Médran lifts (hotelvanessa.ch).
Points: 50 m to the square and 350 m to the lifts, by the Vanessa's own count; the bus from Le Châble
stops here.

**C. Les Creux (north side, towards the Savoleyres lift).**
A quieter residential slope above and to the north of the centre. Chalet d'Adrien is on the Chemin des
Creux (chalet-adrien.com), Hôtel Bristol at Route des Creux 4 (bristol-verbier.ch). The Verbier to
Savoleyres cable car leaves from this side of the resort (verbier4vallees.ch lift list).
Points: further from Médran, so more walking or the hotel shuttle; La Cordée des Alpes, on the same side
at Rue du Centre Sportif 24, gives "less than 500 m" to the village centre and 15 minutes on foot or
5 minutes by its shuttle to the lifts (kvhotels.com).

## 4. SEASONS

**Winter.** The 4 Vallées is described by the operator as the largest ski area entirely located in
Switzerland, with 410 km of slopes and a high point of 3330 m at Mont-Fort
(verbier4vallees.ch/en/ and verbier.ch/en/). The operator's home page states the winter sports season
runs from November to late April.
**Opening dates for winter 2026/2027 are not published** on verbier4vallees.ch at the date of this check.

**Summer.** The Le Châble to Verbier gondola runs all year (verbier4vallees.ch press release).
The summer 2026 live-information page shows 19 installations in total, with the Le Châble to Verbier
cable car open and Mont-Fort and Jumbo listed with hours of 09:15 to 13:00 / 14:30 to 15:30 and
09:15 to 12:45 / 13:45 to 15:45 respectively (verbier4vallees.ch/en/useful-information/live-information-summer).
The tourist office publishes 500 km of hiking trails and 19 km of downhill mountain-bike runs
(verbier.ch/en/).
The Bisse du Levron carries water from early June to late October (verbier4vallees.ch bisse-du-levron page).

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2).**
1. **Sentier des Chamois.** 15.5 km, 5 hours, 676 m of climb, maximum altitude 2265 m, graded very
   difficult, one way, from Verbier (start reached by lift at Les Ruinettes or La Chaux) to Fionnay,
   with a bus back to Le Châble. A few exposed sections.
   Source: verbier.ch/en/offers/sentier-des-chamois-verbier-en-2794010/
2. **3-D Sculpture Park.** 2.2 km, 1 h 15, 21 m of climb, graded easy, there and back, between La Chaux
   and Les Ruinettes at about 2300 m. The tourist office calls it the most popular and easy walk in Verbier.
   Source: verbier.ch/en/offers/3-d-sculpture-park-verbier-en-2794014/
   (Third option if needed: Lac des Vaux loop from Les Ruinettes, 7.6 km, 2 h 50, 594 m of climb,
   high point 2607 m, graded quite difficult. Source: verbier.ch/en/offers/lac-des-vaux-by-verbier-verbier-en-3225110/)

**Mountain rides / attractions (2).**
1. **Mont-Fort, 3330 m**, the highest point of the 4 Vallées, reached from Verbier by the lifts through
   Les Ruinettes, La Chaux and Col des Gentianes. In summer 2026 the Mont-Fort aerial cableway is listed
   with hours 09:15 to 13:00 and 14:30 to 15:30. Ride times per section are not published.
   Sources: verbier4vallees.ch/en/ and verbier4vallees.ch/en/useful-information/live-information-summer
2. **Le Châble to Verbier gondola**, running 05:10 to 23:55 all year, the only lift in the resort that is
   part of the regional public transport timetable since 12 December 2021.
   Source: verbier4vallees.ch/en/useful-information/news/le-chable-verbier-now-considered-public-transport_news_183246

**Restaurants (2), both listed by the tourist office.**
1. **La Marlénaz.** On the heights above Verbier at 1895 m. Traditional dishes and local produce,
   with a view of the Combins massif.
   Source: verbier.ch/en/practice/taste-restaurants-and-bars/la-marlenaz-verbier-en-2923068/
2. **Le Caveau.** Open all year in the resort, more than 50 years in business. Local charcuterie, cheese
   from the mountain pastures of the region and from Verbier, salads and meat dishes.
   Source: verbier.ch/en/practice/taste-restaurants-and-bars/le-caveau-verbier-en-2923021/

**Typical to the place.**
**Verbier Festival**, classical music, held in Verbier. The 33rd edition ran in summer 2026; the 34th
runs **15 July to 1 August 2027**, with general tickets on sale from 23 November 2026.
Source: verbierfestival.com/en/

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Can you reach Verbier by train?**
   Not all the way. Trains run to Le Châble, then a gondola (arriving at Médran) or a bus (setting down
   at Station Poste on the central square) covers the last stretch, which the tourist office calls the
   final 20 minutes. Both cost the same fare.
   Source: verbier.ch/en/practice/move-transport-and-mobility/getting-to-verbier/
2. **How late does the gondola from Le Châble run?**
   Continuously from 05:10 to 23:55, all year except on maintenance days, when extra buses run.
   Sources: verbier.ch (same page) and verbier4vallees.ch press release on extended operating hours.
3. **Should I stay in Verbier or down in Le Châble?**
   Le Châble has the station and free parking for up to 10 days at the foot of the lifts; Verbier has the
   shops, the tourist office and the Médran lift station. The gondola links the two from 05:10 to 23:55.
   Source: verbier.ch/en/practice/move-transport-and-mobility/getting-to-verbier/
4. **Which part of Verbier is closest to the lifts?**
   Rue de Médran, which runs down to the Médran lift station. Hôtel Mirabeau states less than 1 minute
   on the flat from the Médran departure; Hotel Vanessa, by the central square, gives 350 m.
   Sources: mirabeauhotel.ch and hotelvanessa.ch
5. **Is there anything to do in Verbier in summer?**
   The tourist office publishes 500 km of hiking trails and 19 km of downhill mountain-bike runs, and the
   Verbier Festival of classical music runs 15 July to 1 August 2027.
   Sources: verbier.ch/en/ and verbierfestival.com/en/
