Fribourg straddles the Sarine (Saane) river, whose gorge separates the French-speaking and
German-speaking halves of the city; the tourist office markets it as the "Capital of Fondue," and
since 2023 the city has belonged to UNESCO's Creative Cities Network in the field of gastronomy.
Source: fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/ and fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/restaurants

## 2. ACCESS

Fribourg sits on the Lausanne-Bern railway line, roughly midway between the two. Exact minute journey
times were not read from an official page in this pass (no working access/transport page was found
under fribourg.ch's tourism section with WebFetch alone, and TPF's own site returned 404 for the pages

## 4. SEASONS

Fribourg is a year-round city destination, not a ski resort; no lift pass or snow report is published
by the tourist office. The funicular is stated as closed for works **27 August to 4 December 2026** on
the City Card page (see section 5); the fortifications walking tour is open 09:00-19:00 from
1 May to 1 November.
Source: fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/fribourg-city-card/ and fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/tour-of-the-fortifications

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2).**
1. **Tour of the fortifications.** The city's medieval walls: six towers, five gates, seven sections
   of wall and one Great Bulwark, built between the 13th and 15th centuries and described by the
   tourist office as "the most important ensemble of medieval military architecture in Switzerland."
   Full route takes about 3 hours. Open 09:00-19:00, 1 May to 1 November.
   Source: fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/tour-of-the-fortifications
2. **Guided public tour of the Old Town.** A scheduled walking tour of the old town run by the tourist
   office (fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/visits/guided-public-tours-of-the-old-town/). No fixed
   distance/duration figure was read from this page in this pass.

**Sights / rides (2).**
1. **Cathedral of St Nicholas.** In the Bourg district, called by the tourist office "undoubtedly
   Fribourg's most famous landmark," a Gothic building. The Fribourg City Card includes the cathedral
   tower ascent (March to November) and an audio guide. Opening hours, tower height and step count were
   not read from an official page in this pass.
   Source: fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/groups/st-nicholas-cathedral and fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/fribourg-city-card/
2. **Fribourg funicular.** A public funicular included in TPF zone 10 public transport and covered by
   the Fribourg City Card; scheduled to be unavailable 27 August to 4 December 2026. Technical details
   (power source, journey time, history) were not confirmed on an official page in this pass; see
   UNVERIFIED.
   Source: fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/fribourg-city-card/

**Restaurants.**
Only one place could be named from an official page in this pass: **Cafe Villars**, listed on the
Fribourg City Card as the venue for Swiss pastry and candy included with the card. The general
restaurant and "Top Chefs" directory pages on fribourg.ch render their listings with JavaScript and
returned no restaurant names to a direct fetch; a second named restaurant is not included rather than
invent one. See UNVERIFIED.
Source: fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/fribourg-city-card/

**Typical to the place.**
**"Capital of Fondue."** The tourist office's own positioning for the city, alongside membership since
2023 of UNESCO's Creative Cities Network in gastronomy, and the "Taste My Fribourg" initiative
promoting artisanal chocolate and brewery culture.
Source: fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/ and fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/restaurants

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **What does the Fribourg City Card include?**
   Access to seven museums (Art and History Museum, Espace Jean Tinguely-Niki de Saint Phalle,
   Espace 1606, Swiss Puppets Museum, Kaeserberg, Bible+Orient Museum, SwissBierMuseum), public
   transport in TPF zone 10 including the funicular, the cathedral tower climb (March-November) and
   audio guide, Motta swimming pool (May-September), urban golf and pastry at Cafe Villars.
   Source: fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/fribourg-city-card/
2. **How much does the Fribourg City Card cost?**
   CHF 20 (adult) / CHF 10 (child 6-16) for one day April-September, CHF 15/CHF 5 October-March;
   two-day prices CHF 30/CHF 12 (Apr-Sep) and CHF 25/CHF 10 (Oct-Mar).
   Source: fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/fribourg-city-card/
3. **What are the fortifications you can walk around Fribourg?**
   A medieval defensive circuit of six towers, five gates, seven wall sections and one Great Bulwark,
   built in the 13th-15th centuries, walkable in about 3 hours, open 09:00-19:00 from 1 May to
   1 November.
   Source: fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/tour-of-the-fortifications
4. **Why is Fribourg called the "Capital of Fondue"?**
   It is the tourist office's own positioning for the city's gastronomy, reinforced by Fribourg's 2023
   entry into UNESCO's Creative Cities Network in the gastronomy field.
   Source: fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/
5. **Is the Fribourg funicular running year-round?**
   No; the Fribourg City Card page states it is unavailable 27 August to 4 December 2026.
   Source: fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/fribourg-city-card/
