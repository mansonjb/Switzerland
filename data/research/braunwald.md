# Research: Braunwald

Checked 16/09/2026. Only official sources: Switzerland Tourism (myswitzerland.com), the hotels' own
sites. The municipal site (braunwald.ch) and the regional tourist board (glarnerland.ch) both returned
HTTP 403 to every fetch attempted in this session and could not be used; see UNVERIFIED. Wikipedia used
only to cross-check altitude and coordinates.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Altitude: 1,256 m. VERIFIED, panoramahotel-braunwald.ch (the hotel's own site states its own
  altitude as "1,256 meters above sea level"). Cross-check: Wikipedia gives the same figure, 1,256 m,
  and coordinates 46.933 N, 9.000 E, for Braunwald.
- Canton: Glarus. VERIFIED, hotel-cristal.ch ("Hotel Cristal ... Braunwald, Canton Glarus") and the
  postal code 8784 Braunwald used by all hotels cited below.
- Region key: `eastern`.
- Car-free: **true**. Switzerland Tourism describes Braunwald as a "car-free holiday resort" and a
  "car-free mountain village". VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/. Confirmed
  independently by Panoramahotel Braunwald's own site: "Car-free location accessible by cable car (7
  minutes from Linthal)"; Märchenhotel Braunwald likewise calls itself "a car-free mountain village";
  Hotel Tödiblick states it is "located in car-free Braunwald". VERIFIED, panoramahotel-braunwald.ch,
  maerchenhotel.ch and hotel-toediblick.ch

## 2. ACCESS: HOW BRAUNWALD IS REACHED (no car)

- **Braunwaldbahn funicular**, from Linthal: "the Braunwaldbahn will take you from here ... in just
  seven minutes." VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/. Panoramahotel Braunwald's
  own site gives the identical figure: "accessible by cable car (7 minutes from Linthal)".
  VERIFIED, panoramahotel-braunwald.ch
- Zurich to Linthal: "you can reach Linthal from Zurich in an hour and a half."
  VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/
- Once in the village, a mid-station gondola serves Hüttenberg, on the way to Grotzenbüel; Hotel
  Cristal describes its own location as "bei der Mittelstation Hüttenberg der Gondelbahn zum
  Grotzenbüel" (at the Hüttenberg middle station of the gondola to Grotzenbüel).
  VERIFIED, hotel-cristal.ch
- Overnight guests receive the GlarnerlandPass, giving "unlimited use of public transport throughout
  the canton Glarus" for the length of their stay; Panoramahotel Braunwald confirms the same benefit
  directly ("free public transport pass (GlarnerlandPass) during stays").
  VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/ and panoramahotel-braunwald.ch
- Horse-drawn carriages are offered within the resort as an alternative to any motorised transport.
  VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/
- The operator's own site (braunwald.ch) and the regional tourist board (glarnerland.ch) returned
  HTTP 403 to every fetch attempted in this session, so the funicular's exact ascent in metres, its
  track length, and its opening year could not be confirmed on an official page in this session; see
  UNVERIFIED for the Wikipedia-only cross-check of 1907 as an opening year.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

**A. Dorfstrasse, the village centre**
- Märchenhotel Braunwald, Dorfstrasse 24, and Panoramahotel Braunwald, Dorfstrasse 3, both stand on
  this street. VERIFIED, maerchenhotel.ch and panoramahotel-braunwald.ch

**B. Schwändibergstrasse**
- Hotel Tödiblick, Schwändibergstrasse 19, a family-run chalet hotel operating "for over 100 years",
  now in its third generation. VERIFIED, hotel-toediblick.ch

**C. Hüttenberg, at the gondola mid-station toward Grotzenbüel**
- Hotel Cristal stands "bei der Mittelstation Hüttenberg der Gondelbahn zum Grotzenbüel", 5-10 minutes'
  walk from the village, close to the ski facilities and the Swiss Schneesport School.
  VERIFIED, hotel-cristal.ch

## 4. SEASONS

- Winter: sledging and winter sports are named by Switzerland Tourism among Braunwald's activities.
  VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/
- Summer: hiking to the Oberblegisee, the four-peak via ferrata route, and the Bartli dwarf-themed
  forest trail for families. VERIFIED, same page.
- Exact 2026/2027 opening/closing dates for the Braunwaldbahn or the Grotzenbüel gondola are not
  published on the pages fetched. See UNVERIFIED.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two named walks
1. **Braunwald-Oberblegisee-Weg**, to the Oberblegisee lake in the Glärnisch massif, with views of the
   Tödi (3,613 m) and the Ortstock. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/
2. **Four-peak via ferrata route** (Vierländer- or four-summit via ferrata as named by the tourist
   office), one of Braunwald's named summer routes. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/
   braunwald/

Two named rides/sights
1. **Braunwaldbahn funicular**, from Linthal to Braunwald in 7 minutes. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/
   en-us/destinations/braunwald/ and panoramahotel-braunwald.ch
2. **Gondola to Grotzenbüel**, via the Hüttenberg mid-station. VERIFIED, hotel-cristal.ch

Typical of the place
- **Bartli, the dwarf of the magical forest**: a themed children's trail named directly by the tourist
  office. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/
- **Alpinmuseum Braunwald**, the resort's own alpine museum, named among its attractions.
  VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/
- **Horse-drawn carriages**, used within the resort in place of any motor traffic, since the village is
  entirely car-free. VERIFIED, myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/

## 6. HOTELS

   - Dorfstrasse 3, 1,256 m, 4-star, 32 rooms and 3 suites.
   - Sauna, steam bath, relaxation room, Summit Bar, official Swiss Esports House; free GlarnerlandPass
     for guests. VERIFIED, panoramahotel-braunwald.ch
2. **Märchenhotel Braunwald**, https://www.maerchenhotel.ch/ , sector: Dorfstrasse.
   - Dorfstrasse 24, 4-star superior, family suites in Standard/Exklusiv/Paradies categories.
   - "Märliland" children's fairy-tale area, adults-only rooftop "Wellness on the top", heated outdoor
     pool, member of Swiss Family Hotels. VERIFIED, maerchenhotel.ch
3. **Hotel Tödiblick**, https://www.hotel-toediblick.ch/ , sector: Schwändibergstrasse.
   - Schwändibergstrasse 19, 17 rooms, third-generation family-run chalet hotel over 100 years old.
   - Larch-wood beds and Riposa mattresses made in the Glarus region; regional-sourcing restaurant.
     VERIFIED, hotel-toediblick.ch
4. **Hotel Cristal**, sector: Hüttenberg gondola mid-station.
   - At the Hüttenberg mid-station of the Grotzenbüel gondola, 5-10 minutes from the village centre.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. *How do you get to Braunwald, since it has no road for cars?*
   By the Braunwaldbahn funicular from Linthal, a 7-minute ride; Linthal itself is about 1.5 hours from
   Zurich. Source: myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/ and panoramahotel-braunwald.ch
2. *Is Braunwald really car-free?*
   Yes, confirmed both by Switzerland Tourism and directly by three hotels in this dossier
   (Panoramahotel, Märchenhotel, Hotel Tödiblick), which each describe their own setting as car-free.
   Source: panoramahotel-braunwald.ch, maerchenhotel.ch, hotel-toediblick.ch
3. *What do overnight guests get for local transport?*
   The GlarnerlandPass, unlimited public transport across canton Glarus for the length of the stay.
   Source: myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/ and panoramahotel-braunwald.ch
4. *What is there to do with children?*
   The Bartli dwarf-themed magical forest trail and the Alpinmuseum Braunwald. Source:
   myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/
5. *What is the Oberblegisee hike?*
   The Braunwald-Oberblegisee-Weg, into the Glärnisch massif, with views of the Tödi (3,613 m) and the
   Ortstock. Source: myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/braunwald/
