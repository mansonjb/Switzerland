# Research dossier: Stein am Rhein

Checked 16/09/2026. Official sources: Stadt Stein am Rhein (steinamrhein.ch), Schaffhauserland
Tourismus (schaffhauserland.ch, the regional tourist office that also runs the Stein am Rhein tourism
page), Schifffahrtsgesellschaft Untersee und Rhein (urh.ch), Swiss Youth Hostels (youthhostel.ch), and the
hotels' own sites. No ratings, no reviews.

NOTE: the web-search tool ran out of budget for this session before this dossier was started, so all
research here was done with direct WebFetch calls only (no WebSearch). A number of hotel and sight domains
that were guessed directly did not resolve (DNS errors), so the hotel list is shorter than the other
dossiers in this batch and several detail pages (Kloster St. Georgen's own site, Burg Hohenklingen's own
site, the Lindwurm Museum's own site) could not be reached at all this session. Everything below marked
VERIFIED was read on the page named.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Slug on the sheet: `stein-am-rhein`. Region key `eastern`. Canton: **Schaffhausen**.
- Altitude: **401 m**, at the former Benedictine monastery St. Georgen (Wikipedia, cross-check only; no
  altitude figure was found on an official page this session).
- Coordinates (Wikipedia, cross-check only): 47.667 / 8.850.
- carFree: **false**.
- Resident population: **4,000**, a milestone reported by the municipality itself on **18 August 2026**
  (steinamrhein.ch, "Aktuelles").
- Official address: **Rathausplatz 1, 8260 Stein am Rhein** (steinamrhein.ch).
- The municipality holds the **UNICEF "Kinderfreundliche Gemeinde" (child-friendly municipality)** label
  (steinamrhein.ch).
- What defines the place practically: the **old town sits directly at the point where the Rhine leaves
  Lake Constance (Untersee)**, with **Burg Hohenklingen** on the wooded hill above it and the former
  **Kloster St. Georgen** monastery right at the water's edge.

## 2. ACCESS

- **From Zurich: about 1 h 10 by train** (S-Bahn), arriving a short walk across the Rhine bridge from the
  old town (schaffhauserland.ch, "Anreise"). No single exact minutes figure from Schaffhausen was
  confirmed on an official page this session; treat as UNVERIFIED beyond "under an hour by S-Bahn".
- **Boats (Schifffahrtsgesellschaft Untersee und Rhein, URh)**: Stein am Rhein is a stop on the
  Schaffhausen-Stein am Rhein-Kreuzlingen/Konstanz route. **Adult single fare to Schaffhausen: CHF 36.40**;
  **to Konstanz: CHF 57.40 (capped at CHF 49.50)**; **group fares (10+) CHF 25.60 to Schaffhausen, CHF
  40.20 to Konstanz** (urh.ch, "Preise"). **Half Fare Card and GA travelcard both accepted on all
  journeys, at half the listed fare** (urh.ch), capped at CHF 31 for the Konstanz run.
- As of this session, URh reported a **service interruption between Stein am Rhein and Diessenhofen due to
  low water**, with rail suggested as a substitute, and a **reduced Kreuzlingen/Konstanz-Stein am Rhein
  schedule running through 18 October 2026** (urh.ch). This is a live operating note, not a structural
  fact, and will be out of date by the time this is published; re-check before use.
- No parking arrangement to describe: Stein am Rhein is not car-free.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. Rhine-front old town**
- Hotel Rheinfels is at the **northern bridgehead** of the town, a former customs and warehouse building,
  hotel and restaurant for **over 60 years**. Hotel Rheingerbe is at **Schiffländi 5**, directly on the
  Rhine, with a waterfront terrace.

**B. Inside the painted old town**
- Hotel Adler is at **Adlergässli 4**, inside the historic core, three-star, family-run, recently
  renovated (2024). The Bäckerei-Konditorei am Schaubmarkt, run by the same family since **1993**, is also
  inside the old town.

**C. Riverside towards the lido, out of the old town**
- Stein am Rhein Youth Hostel is at **Hemishoferstrasse 87**, next to the Riipark lido, with a garden,
  barbecue area and playground, close to the Rhine Falls.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2)**
1. **Old town to Burg Hohenklingen** — the castle sits on the wooded hill directly above the old town,
   with views over the **Hegau, Lake Constance and the Rhine landscape** (schaffhauserland.ch). No
   official published distance or walking-time figure was found this session; UNVERIFIED for exact
   minutes.
2. **The Rhine bridge to the old town** — the train station sits across the river from the historic
   centre; the walk into town is described by the tourist office as short (schaffhauserland.ch,
   "Anreise"). No published exact metres/minutes figure; UNVERIFIED.

**Rides and sights**
1. **URh boats** — see fares and Half Fare/GA terms in section 2. The Rhine here is the point where it
   leaves the Untersee arm of Lake Constance.
2. **Burg Hohenklingen** — medieval castle above the town; described by Schaffhauserland Tourismus as
   giving views over the Hegau, Lake Constance and the Rhine. No official opening hours/admission price
   page could be reached this session (the castle's own site did not resolve); UNVERIFIED for hours and
   price.

**What is typical of the place**
1. **The painted facades of the old town** — Schaffhauserland Tourismus calls them **"weltberühmt"
   (world-renowned)**, without publishing an exact count in the pages read this session. No numeric total
   is asserted here.
2. **Kloster St. Georgen** — a former Benedictine abbey right on the water, described by the tourist
   office as **"one of the best-preserved medieval monastery complexes in Switzerland,"** today a museum.
   No official hours/price page could be reached this session (the museum's own site did not resolve);
   UNVERIFIED for hours and price.
3. **Lindwurm Museum** — a local-history house museum, named in passing on the Swiss Youth Hostels page for
   Stein am Rhein; its own site did not resolve this session, so hours/price are UNVERIFIED.

**Restaurants (2, from the Schaffhauserland Tourismus directory)**
1. **Hotel Restaurant Rheinfels** — known for fish specialities, GastroSuisse 3-star, member of the
   "Tafelgesellschaft zum Goldenen Fisch" (schaffhauserland.ch).
2. **Bäckerei-Konditorei am Schaubmarkt** — bakery and patisserie in the old town, run by Franz and Monika
   Marty since 1993 (schaffhauserland.ch).

## 6. HOTELS (4 read and loaded on 16/09/2026)

1. **Hotel Rheinfels** — https://www.rheinfels.ch — northern bridgehead, on the Rhine
   - Former customs, storage and warehouse building; hotel and restaurant for over 60 years
   - Banquet hall for up to 120 people; award-recognised fish-forward, regional/French cuisine
   - Restaurant open daily 08:00-21:00, closed Wednesdays in September, Wednesdays and Thursdays from
     October
2. **Hotel Adler** — https://www.adlersteinamrhein.ch — Adlergässli 4, old town
   - Three-star, family-run, renovated 2024, bike garage, self check-in
   - Seasonal opening: 20 March-9 November 2026 and 30 November 2026-17 January 2027
   - About 20 minutes from Frauenfeld or Schaffhausen, 35 minutes from Kreuzlingen or Winterthur
3. **Hotel Rheingerbe** — https://www.rheingerbe.ch — Schiffländi 5, on the Rhine
   - Single, double, Rhine-view double and Rhine-view suite room categories
   - Restaurant with Mediterranean/Spanish-inspired menu and waterside terrace
4. **Stein am Rhein Youth Hostel** — https://www.youthhostel.ch/en/hostels/stein-am-rhein-youth-hostel —
   Hemishoferstrasse 87
   - Multi-bed and double rooms; garden with barbecue area and playground next to the Riipark lido
   - Table tennis, football and volleyball equipment; close to the Rhine Falls
   - ibex fairstay gold and HI Quality & Sustainability certified

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do I get from Zurich to Stein am Rhein?** By train, about 1 h 10, arriving a short walk from the
   old town across the Rhine bridge. (schaffhauserland.ch)
2. **Can I arrive by boat?** Yes, URh runs Stein am Rhein on its Schaffhausen-Kreuzlingen/Konstanz route;
   an adult single to Schaffhausen is CHF 36.40, half price with a Half Fare Card or GA. (urh.ch)
3. **What is Stein am Rhein actually known for?** Its painted medieval facades, called "world-renowned" by
   the regional tourist office, plus Burg Hohenklingen on the hill above and the former Kloster St.
   Georgen monastery on the waterfront.
4. **Is the old town walkable from the station?** Yes, a short walk across the Rhine bridge.
   (schaffhauserland.ch)
5. **How many people live in Stein am Rhein?** The municipality passed 4,000 residents, reported in August
   2026. (steinamrhein.ch)
