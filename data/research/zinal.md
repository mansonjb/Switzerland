Zinal sits at the head of Val d'Anniviers, below the Couronne Imperiale (Imperial Crown), a chain
that includes the Weisshorn, Bishorn, Zinalrothorn, Obergabelhorn, Matterhorn and Dent Blanche.
Tourism has been part of village life for 150 years.
Source: valdanniviers.ch/en/Z15449/zinal-en

## 2. ACCESS

**Nearest railhead: Sierre/Siders**, on the Simplon line.

**Last leg, Sierre to Zinal.** PostBus (CarPostal) runs a year-round service from Sierre through
Vissoie to Zinal (lines in the 12.45x series: Vissoie-Zinal is line 453; a summer-only shuttle line
455 links Zinal-Grimentz-Moiry on weekends and daily from June to October 2026).
Source: valdanniviers.ch/en/Z15453/bus-and-shuttles and the PostAuto network map for Sierre et
Val d'Anniviers (postauto.ch)
**Exact journey time in minutes is not published on the valdanniviers.ch bus page checked**; see
UNVERIFIED.

**By car.** Val d'Anniviers is reached from the Rhone valley motorway (A9) via Sierre, then the valley
road through Vissoie to Zinal. The tourist office states the valley "is easy to reach. By motorway,
train or bus" without giving minute figures (valdanniviers.ch/en/Z15451/getting-here-and-transport).
More than 2800 parking spaces are spread across the villages and resorts of Anniviers
(same source, via search result). Free parking for the Sierre-Zinal race is at Mottec, downhill from
Zinal; once these fill, road access to the village centre is filtered to residents, accredited people
and shuttles only (sierre-zinal.com/fr/parking-acces-zinal-2884.html). A year-round motorhome pitch
with electricity, water and grey-water dump is at Parking Montferrier Nord, winter rate CHF 30 per
vehicle per day (valdanniviers.ch, camping-car pitch page).

**Alternative approach: over the mountain from Grimentz.** The Grimentz-Sorebois cable car, running
since January 2014, covers about 3 km in around 7 minutes and is described by Valais Wallis Promotion
as the third-longest cable car route in Switzerland; from Sorebois the Zinal-Sorebois-Espace Weisshorn
gondola continues down to Zinal. This links the two resort villages without a road.
Source: valais.ch/en/explore/activities/ski-snowboard/ski-areas/zinal

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

Zinal is a single, compact village at the road-end, so hotels cluster within a few hundred metres of
each other rather than in distinct districts; the areas below reflect the village's own street plan.

**A. Rue des Cinq 4000 (village spine, closest to the Sorebois cable car).**
The main street, named for the five 4000 m peaks visible from it. Hotel Le Besso at no. 27 states it
is 300 m from the Zinal-Sorebois cable car (le-besso.ch); Hotel Europe at no. 20 gives the same 300 m
(europezinal.ch); Hotel La Pointe de Zinal at no. 15 gives 200 m to the lifts (lapointedezinal.ch).
Points: shortest walk to the cable car, restaurants and shops on the same street.

**B. Montferrier (approach side, towards the valley road and the motorhome parking).**
Pension de la Poste, at Montferrier 29, sits on this side of the village, near the Montferrier Nord
car park (lapostezinal.ch).
Points: closer to the road-in and the free/paid car parks, a short walk to the centre.

**C. Sorebois mountainside (on-mountain, reached by the cable car, not by road).**
The Restaurant de Sorebois and the Cabane de Sorebois hut sit at 2440 m at the middle station of the
Zinal-Sorebois-Espace Weisshorn gondola.
Source: valdanniviers.ch/en/P116889/your-stay/food-and-drink/restaurant-de-sorebois-2440m and
valdanniviers.ch/en/P114661/your-stay/accomodation/sorebois-hut
Points: on-mountain sleeping in a staffed hut, not a hotel; reached only by lift or on foot, not by car.

## 4. SEASONS

**Winter.** The Grimentz-Zinal ski area totals 115 km of pistes (48 slopes: 11 black, 22 red, 10 blue,
5 yellow) between 1570 m and 2900 m (2880 m per valais.ch), served by 21 lift installations across two
linked sectors, Bendolla (Grimentz side) and Sorebois (Zinal side). Slopes face north/north-east.
Source: valais.ch/en/explore/activities/ski-snowboard/ski-areas/zinal
**Exact opening and closing dates for winter 2026/2027 are not published** on the pages checked.

**Summer.** The Zinal-Sorebois-Espace Weisshorn gondola and the Grimentz-Sorebois cable car both run
in summer for hikers and mountain bikers; Espace Weisshorn (top station area) sits at 2241 m
(bike-grimentz-zinal.ch). The Cabane de Petit Mountet, reached on foot from Zinal, is generally open
June to October (petitmountet.ch, via search result). The Sierre-Zinal mountain race runs each summer;
see section 5.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2).**
1. **Hiking at altitude in Zinal.** 12 km, 5 hours, 950 m of ascent and 950 m of descent, graded for a
   "good walker." Passes a mountain lake among alpine pastures and a small summit overlooking the
   village.
   Source: valdanniviers.ch/en/P177934/hiking-at-altitude-in-zinal
2. **Zinal - Sorebois, route no. 4.** Two options given by the tourist office: ride the cable car up
   and walk down to Zinal, "about 2 hours, easy," recommended for families; or climb up on foot through
   forest and pasture below the cable car line, "2 hours, medium difficulty."
   Source: valdanniviers.ch/en/V3744/things-to-see-and-do/summer/zinal-sorebois-no-4
   (Third option if needed: Zinal - Cabane du Petit Mountet, parcours no. 7, via the Plats de la Lee
   and Vichiesso, described by the tourist office without a published distance or time figure.
   Source: valdanniviers.ch/fr/V3768/a-faire/en-ete/zinal-parcours-n07-petit-mountet)

**Mountain rides / sights (2).**
1. **Zinal-Sorebois-Espace Weisshorn gondola.** Detachable 10-person cable car built in 2020, replacing
   a 1966 70-person reversible cableway; capacity raised to 1800 people per hour. Middle/top station
   area (Espace Weisshorn) at 2241 m.
   Source: bike-grimentz-zinal.ch and valais.ch/en/explore/activities/ski-snowboard/ski-areas/zinal
2. **Grimentz-Sorebois cable car.** Running since January 2014, about 3 km, roughly 7 minutes, linking
   Grimentz village to Corne de Sorebois; described by Valais Wallis Promotion as the third-longest
   cable car route in Switzerland.
   Source: valais.ch/en/explore/activities/ski-snowboard/ski-areas/zinal

**Restaurants (2).**
1. **Restaurant de Sorebois, 2440 m.** At the middle station of the Zinal gondola. Table-service
   Le Montagnard, open 12:00-14:30, local specialities, booking recommended; self-service Le Buffet,
   winter only, family dishes and children's menu.
   Source: valdanniviers.ch/en/P116889/your-stay/food-and-drink/restaurant-de-sorebois-2440m
2. **Pension de la Poste, Montferrier 29.** Family-run for three generations; seasonal menu changed
   twice a season.
   Source: lapostezinal.ch/en/

**Typical to the place.**
**Sierre-Zinal, "la course des cinq 4000."** Mountain race from Sierre to Zinal, 31 km with 2200 m of
elevation gain, run each year on the eve of the second Sunday of August. The 2027 edition (54th) is
scheduled for **Saturday 7 August 2027**.
Source: sierre-zinal.com/fr/course-sierre-zinal-date-parcours-341.html

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Can you reach Zinal by train?**
   Not directly. The nearest station is Sierre, on the Simplon line; PostBus line 453 continues via
   Vissoie to Zinal. Sion/Sierre area tourist pages do not publish an exact journey time.
   Source: valdanniviers.ch/en/Z15453/bus-and-shuttles
2. **Is Zinal car-free?**
   No. The village is reached by road and has public and motorhome parking; access is only filtered to
   residents and shuttles on the day of the Sierre-Zinal race, when arrival crowds fill the Mottec car
   parks.
   Source: sierre-zinal.com/fr/parking-acces-zinal-2884.html
3. **How big is the Grimentz-Zinal ski area?**
   115 km of pistes (48 slopes) between 1570 m and 2900 m, on 21 lift installations across two linked
   sectors, Bendolla and Sorebois.
   Source: valais.ch/en/explore/activities/ski-snowboard/ski-areas/zinal
4. **What is the Sierre-Zinal race?**
   A 31 km mountain race with 2200 m of ascent from Sierre to Zinal, known as "la course des cinq
   4000," run on the eve of the second Sunday of August; the 2027 (54th) edition is Saturday
   7 August 2027.
   Source: sierre-zinal.com/fr/course-sierre-zinal-date-parcours-341.html
5. **Can you reach Zinal from Grimentz without a car?**
   Yes, by the Grimentz-Sorebois cable car (about 7 minutes) connecting to the Zinal-Sorebois-Espace
   Weisshorn gondola down into Zinal, both part of the same lift network.
   Source: valais.ch/en/explore/activities/ski-snowboard/ski-areas/zinal
