Sion is described by the municipality as the political and administrative capital of Valais and
the economic centre of the "Agglo Valais central." Maximum altitude within municipal territory
2300 m (sion.ch/sion).

## 2. ACCESS

**By train.** Sion station sits on the Simplon line (Lausanne - Sion - Brig), used by InterCity and
InterRegio services linking it to the rest of the network. Sion Tourisme's own access page does not
publish journey times and instead points visitors to the national timetable at www.cff.ch
(siontourisme.ch/fr/comment-venir-a-sion). No specific minute-by-minute journey times are published
on an official Sion page checked in this pass; see UNVERIFIED.

**By car.** Sion is served by the A9 motorway. The tourist office also lists the historic alpine
passes and tunnels that reach the Rhone valley: Grand-St-Bernard, Simplon, and the St-Maurice and
Lotschberg tunnels (siontourisme.ch/fr/comment-venir-a-sion). Public transit and the municipal
underground car park are free from Friday 17:00 to Saturday midnight (same source).

**By air.** Sion has its own regional airport, Aeroport de Sion (sionaeroport.ch), with Geneva and
Milan airports also cited by the tourist office as nearby alternatives (siontourisme.ch). Air Mountain
operates scheduled flights from Sion to Alghero, Calvi, Figari and Saint-Tropez, plus a weekly charter
to Palma de Mallorca from 28 May to 8 October 2026 (sionaeroport.ch route information found via
Air Mountain schedules).

**By bus.** PostBus (CarPostal) timetables at www.postauto.ch, per the tourist office
(siontourisme.ch/fr/comment-venir-a-sion).

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

Sion Tourisme's own hotel page groups accommodation into three types: "near the train station, near
the historic centre, or on the outskirts" (siontourisme.ch/fr/hotels).

**A. Station / Grand-Champsec (business and arrival area).**
East of the centre, along Avenue de Grand-Champsec and Route de Chippis, close to the station and the
A9 access. ibis Sion is at Avenue de Grand-Champsec 21 (all.accor.com); Moxy Sion is at Route de
Chippis 64 (marriott.com); Holiday Inn Express & Suites Sion is at Avenue de Tourbillon 18, by the
station forecourt (ihg.com).

**B. Historic centre / old town.**
Between the Valere and Tourbillon hills, the medieval core with the Saturday market. Hotel Elite is at
Avenue du Midi 6, "less than 5 minutes" from the station and old town on foot (hotelelitesion.ch);
Hotel Castel is at Rue du Scex 38, described by the hotel as "just below the Chateau de Tourbillon"
(hotelcastel.ch).

**C. Outskirts (Conthey / St-Leonard, both listed on the Sion Tourisme hotel page).**
Le Relais du Simplon is in Pont-de-la-Morge, commune of Conthey, a few kilometres west of Sion
(relaisdusimplon.ch). Motel-Restaurant 13 Etoiles is on Route du Simplon in St-Leonard, between Sion
and Sierre (motel13etoiles.ch). Both are listed as Sion accommodation by siontourisme.ch/fr/hotels but
sit outside the municipal boundary; a car or bus link to central Sion is needed.

## 4. SEASONS

Sion sits in the Rhone valley at 490 m, in the driest part of Switzerland; the municipality states
this directly: "des precipitations plus faibles que dans le reste de la Suisse" (sion.ch/sion). It is
a year-round city destination rather than a ski resort: no lift pass or snow-report page is published
by siontourisme.ch. Regional ski areas (Anzere, Veysonnaz-Thyon, Nax) are reachable by car from Sion
but are separate resorts with their own operators; no official Sion page bundles their schedules.
The Chateau de Tourbillon itself is open 15 March to 15 November and closed the rest of the year
(tourbillon.ch, see section 6).

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2).**
1. **Chemin du Vignoble, stage 3, Sion to Venthone.** Part of the cross-canton vineyard trail that
   runs on the south-facing slopes of the Rhone valley between 450 m and 800 m. Sion is a stage town
   on the route.
   Source: valais.ch/en/explore/activities/hiking/hikes and cheminduvignoble.ch/le-chemin/sion/
2. **Bisse de Clavoz, Sion to Montorge and back.** A round trip along one of the historic irrigation
   channels above the city, about 2 hours.
   Source: siontourisme.ch/fr/randonnees

**Sights / rides (2).**
1. **Chateau de Tourbillon.** Fortified bishop's residence built at the turn of the 13th-14th century
   by Bishop Boniface de Challant, destroyed by fire in 1788. Free entry. Open 10:00-18:00 from 1 May
   to 30 September, 11:00-17:00 from 15 March to 30 April and 1 October to 15 November, closed
   16 November to 14 March. Reached on foot up the rock, no vehicle access.
   Source: tourbillon.ch
2. **Basilique de Valere.** 11th-13th century fortified church on the hill facing Tourbillon, home to
   what is described as the world's oldest playable organ (1435), with summer organ concerts.
   Source: myswitzerland.com/en-us/experiences/sion-valere-tourbillon-old-town/

**Restaurants (2).**
1. **Le Relais du Mont d'Orge.** North of Sion; listed in the Sion Tourisme dining guide.
   Source: siontourism.ch/index.php/fr/vins-et-gastronomie/guide-de-la-restauration/
2. **Les Iles, Route des Berges 21.** Listed in the Sion Tourisme dining guide, by the Domaine des
   Iles leisure park.
   Source: siontourism.ch/index.php/fr/vins-et-gastronomie/guide-de-la-restauration/

**Typical to the place.**
**Sion sous les etoiles**, open-air music festival on the Plaine de Tourbillon, next to the Tourbillon
stadium. Over 400,000 attendees across its first 10 editions. Next edition **14 to 17 July 2027**.
Source: sionsouslesetoiles.ch
**Sion Wine Tour**, a guided tour of the vineyards run by Sion Tourisme (CHF 59 per person).
Source: siontourisme.ch/en/

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Can you walk to the Chateau de Tourbillon?**
   Yes and it is the only way in: no vehicle access, a signed path climbs the rock from the old town.
   Entry is free. Open 10:00-18:00 in summer (1 May-30 September), shorter hours in shoulder season,
   closed 16 November to 14 March.
   Source: tourbillon.ch
2. **Is Sion a ski resort?**
   No. Sion is the cantonal capital in the valley floor at 490 m; it is a base for visiting the region
   rather than a ski station, and no lift pass or snow report is published by Sion Tourisme.
   Source: siontourisme.ch, sion.ch/sion
3. **How do you get to Sion?**
   By train on the Simplon line, by car via the A9 motorway, or by air via the regional Sion Airport,
   with Geneva and Milan airports as alternatives. Sion Tourisme's own access page does not publish
   train journey times and refers visitors to cff.ch.
   Source: siontourisme.ch/fr/comment-venir-a-sion
4. **Where do most hotels sit in Sion?**
   Sion Tourisme itself groups them into three areas: near the train station, near the historic
   centre, or on the outskirts.
   Source: siontourisme.ch/fr/hotels
5. **Is Sion a wine destination?**
   Sion is the second-largest wine commune in Valais with 420 ha of vineyard, on the Chemin du
   Vignoble trail, and the tourist office runs its own guided Sion Wine Tour.
   Source: sion.ch/sion, siontourisme.ch/en/
