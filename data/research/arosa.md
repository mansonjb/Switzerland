## 2. ACCESS

Times from the Swiss national timetable (SBB / Rhaetian Railway data), queried 16/09/2026.

- **Zürich HB to Arosa: 2 h 31, one change.** Zürich HB 09:38 IC 3 (SBB) to Chur 10:52, then Rhaetian Railway R 16 Chur 11:08 to Arosa 12:09.
- **Chur to Arosa: 1 h 01, direct**, Rhaetian Railway R 16 (the Chur to Arosa line, which starts in the street outside Chur station).
- **Zürich Airport to Chur: 1 h 33**, one change at Zürich HB.
- **By car from Chur:** "about 40 minutes on a winding road uphill". The Schanfiggerstrasse "winds through the valley, covering a distance of 30 km and 360 curves, overcoming a height difference of 1,320 meters". Coach limits: maximum width 2.3 m, maximum height 4.0 m, maximum weight 18 t. There are driving bans for motor vehicles and motorcycles "on alpine, goods, and forest roads", with permits "from the municipality or via SMS or the e24 app". Parking information is published at arosa-parking.com, and Park & Rail is available in Chur. Source: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Arosa/Up-to-date/Facts-and-Figures/Getting-there
- **Local transport for guests:** the Arosa Card, given from the first night's stay, includes the local Arosa bus and the Rhaetian Railway between Lüen-Castiel and Arosa (see section 7).

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. Around the station and the Obersee (village centre)
- Arosa station sits at the top of the Chur line, right by the lake. The **Arosa Weisshorn cable car** valley station is "located right next to Arosa station and is accessible via a passerelle". Source: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/poi/cable-car/weisshorn-cable-car/22646891/
- Arosa Vetter Hotel, Seeblickstrasse 9, publishes "20 m from the cable car station and 50 m from the Arosa train station". Source: https://www.arosa-vetter-hotel.ch/en/
- This is the sector for arriving by train with luggage and for the Weisshorn lift.

### B. Innerarosa
- The upper, older end of the village, around the **Hörnli-Express** gondola whose valley station is "near the Gada bus stop". Source: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/de/gastro/arosa-lenzerheide/hoernli-huette/22696417/
- Hotel Alpensonne, Innere Poststrasse 217, is "located in Inner-Arosa, only 150 meters away from the cable car, ski school, and bus stops"; the Hörnli station is "approximately five minutes away". Source: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/de/Arosa/Entdecken/Hotel-Alpensonne_isd_366330

### C. Maran and Prätschli (the sun terrace above the village)
- A plateau above Arosa reached by road and bus, with the golf course and the start of the forest walks. The Eichhörnliweg from the village ends here.
- Waldhotel Arosa, Prätschlistrasse 38, publishes its position as "1,795 metres above sea level" with "immediate proximity of two mountain railways". Source: https://www.waldhotel.ch/en/
- Golf & Sports Hotel Hof Maran is at Maranerstrasse 66, by the golf course: "The Panorama Golf Course Arosa is one of the highest ... 18-hole golf courses in Europe". Source: https://www.hofmaran.ch/en/
- Quieter than the centre, and a bus ride or a walk from the station.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks (official Arosa Lenzerheide route data)**
1. **Eichhörnliweg (Squirrel Trail), Arosa to Plateau Maran.** Published as **1.7 km**, **0:36 h**, 102 m of ascent and 6 m of descent, difficulty "easy", highest point 1,860 m, lowest 1,769 m. Runs from the village through the forest to Maran; there are information boards with puzzles for children, and a barbecue area and playground near the end. Usable in any season. Source: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/tour/theme-trail/eichhoernliweg-arosa/17633919/
2. **4 lakes hike to the Obersee, the two Prätschsees and the Scheideggseeli.** Published as **10.5 km**, **3:10 h**, 360 m of ascent and 360 m of descent, lowest point 1,738 m, highest 2,098 m, difficulty "moderate". Starts and ends at the Obersee in Arosa. Source: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/tour/theme-trail/4-lakes-hike-to-obersee-the-two-praetschsees-and-scheideggseeli/53672416/

**Two named mountain rides / attractions**
1. **Arosa Weisshorn cable car.** Valley station "located right next to Arosa station and is accessible via a passerelle". The summit restaurant is at **2653 m**, with a "360° Demoscope Panorama window" giving views of "over 400 mountains in Switzerland and nearby countries". Source: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/poi/cable-car/weisshorn-cable-car/22646891/
2. **Arosa Bear Sanctuary (Arosa Bärenland)**, at **2,000 metres above sea level**, "a unique animal conservation project established in cooperation with Arosa Tourism and FOUR PAWS". "The first section of the Arosa-Weisshorn cable car takes you directly to the visitor platform at the Arosa Bear Sanctuary", cable car 09:00 to 17:00, last descent 17:40. Three published walking routes reach it: from the Prätschli bus stop by the Ricola Adventure Trail, from the station by the Squirrel Trail, Maran and Prätschli, and from central Arosa past the Bergkirchli chapel. Sources: https://arosabaerenland.swiss/en and https://arosabaerenland.swiss/en/Your-visit/Getting-here

**Two named restaurants (listed by the tourist office, no opinion)**
1. **Restaurant Alpenblick**, Oberer Schwelliseeweg, 7050 Arosa. "Eine grosse Terrasse mit 180 Plätzen" (a large terrace with 180 seats) and about 120 seats indoors; it is also a mountain hotel. Website alpenblick-arosa.ch. Source: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/de/gastro/arosa-lenzerheide/restaurant-alpenblick/20539426/
2. **Hörnli-Hütte**, on the highest point of the Hörnligrat at **2509 m**, built by the Skiclub Arosa and inaugurated on 9 December 1923. Mountain inn serving food and drink in both seasons, with rooms. Reached by the Hörnli-Express from the valley station near the Gada bus stop. Website hoernliarosa.ch. Source: https://maps.arosalenzerheide.swiss/de/gastro/arosa-lenzerheide/hoernli-huette/22696417/

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **The Chur to Arosa railway.** Arosa is the terminus of the Rhaetian Railway line from Chur, 1 h 01 from Chur, and the line is the only one in the group that starts in the street outside Chur station. The Arosa Card covers this line between Lüen-Castiel and Arosa from 13 June to 25 October 2026. Sources: Swiss national timetable and https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Arosa/Summer/Arosa-Card
- **The Arosa Card** is unusually generous: it includes the lifts, the bus and the train, see section 7.
- **Panorama Golf Course Arosa**, at Maran, published by Hof Maran as "one of the highest ... 18-hole golf courses in Europe". Source: https://www.hofmaran.ch/en/

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

**Not published.** No official page states what the Swiss Travel Pass covers on the Arosa mountain railways.

The local card is the story here, and it is a strong one:
- **Arosa Card.** "As a holiday guest, you will receive the Arosa Card from your first night's stay in a hotel, holiday apartment or campsite", valid "from the pre-season weekend at the end of May until the end of October 2026".
- It includes **all open Arosa mountain railways** (Arosa Weisshorn cable car, Hörnli-Express, Urdenbahn), the **local Arosa bus** and the **Rhaetian Railway between Lüen-Castiel and Arosa** (the rail part valid 13 June to 25 October 2026), plus the rope park and Skill Center, pedalo and boat hire, the lido, the ice sports hall, ChippinGolf and the driving range, and entry to the local history museum and the viaduct museum. Reduced entry to the Bear Sanctuary and the archery park.
- Excluded: "The card is not valid for arrival and departure, for travel to school or work"; "Lenzerheide mountain railways are not included"; bike carriage is charged (30% off with the card); equipment hire is not included.
- Day guests can buy it: adults CHF 50.00, children 6 to 15 CHF 25.00.
Source: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Arosa/Summer/Arosa-Card

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Is it worth staying in Arosa itself rather than day-tripping from Chur?** Yes for the card alone: from the first night in a hotel, holiday apartment or campsite you get the Arosa Card, which in summer includes all open Arosa mountain railways, the local bus and the Rhaetian Railway between Lüen-Castiel and Arosa. Source: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Arosa/Summer/Arosa-Card
2. **Station, Innerarosa or Maran?** The station end puts you 20 m from the Weisshorn cable car (Arosa Vetter Hotel publishes that figure); Innerarosa is 150 m from the Hörnli-Express (Hotel Alpensonne); Maran and Prätschli are the quiet terrace above the village, with the golf course and the forest walks. Sources: the three hotel pages linked above.
3. **How long does the train take?** Chur to Arosa is 1 h 01 direct on the Rhaetian Railway. Zürich HB to Arosa is 2 h 31 with one change at Chur. Source: Swiss national timetable, checked 16/09/2026.
4. **Can I see the bears without hiking?** Yes. "The first section of the Arosa-Weisshorn cable car takes you directly to the visitor platform at the Arosa Bear Sanctuary", at 2,000 m; the cable car runs 09:00 to 17:00 with the last descent at 17:40. Source: https://arosabaerenland.swiss/en/Your-visit/Getting-here
5. **When does the ski area open?** Arosa Lenzerheide publishes 28 November 2026 for the winter pre-season, full operation from 19 December 2026 to 29 March 2027, then partial operation to 11 April 2027. These are "the earliest possible opening" dates and can move. Source: https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Ski-Area/Lift-Company/Operating-hours
