Further municipality figures (leukerbad.org/gemeindezahlen): 748 registered voters, 39 nationalities,
total area 67.2 km2 of which 51 km2 unproductive, population density 21 per km2, highest point the

restaurant establishments, 3 public thermal baths, 2 cable cars.

## 2. ACCESS

Nearest railhead: **Leuk** on the Rhone valley line. No railway reaches Leukerbad.

- Train to Leuk, then **bus 471** operated by Leuk-Leukerbad Busbetriebe (Thermenstrasse 8, 3954
  Leukerbad) up to the Leukerbad bus terminal. The Thermalhotel De France, in the village, gives the
  bus leg as **about 35 minutes** and its own door as a 5-minute walk from the terminal.
  Sources: leukerbad.ch/anreise (train then bus, operator named) and thermalhotels.ch
- The tourist office publishes door-to-door times of **3 hours from Geneva Airport** and
  **3.5 hours from Zurich Airport** by train (leukerbad.ch/anreise).
- Bus timetables published by the tourist office, valid from 14 December 2025: Leuk SBB to Leukerbad,
  Leukerbad to Albinen, Leuk SBB to Albinen, Sierre to Salgesch to Varen, Leuk SBB to Feschel and
  Guttet, Leuk SBB to Erschmatt, Varen to Leuk to Agarn (leukerbad.ch/anreise).
- **By car**: the tourist office states the village is reached by private car and that several car
  parks are available on site. It also lists a local bus, a shuttle bus and taxis (leukerbad.ch/anreise).
- **Mobility Ticket, summer 2026**: guests of the partner hotels Alex and Therme 51° get a free return
  journey on public transport from anywhere in Switzerland to Leukerbad, valid **1 May to 31 October 2026**
  (leukerbad.ch/anreise).
- Luggage forwarding by SBB is advertised by the tourist office on the same page.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

**A. Dorfplatz, the village square.**
The Leukerbad Therme and the Walliser Alpentherme both sit here, and the bus terminal is a few minutes
away. The Thermalhotel De France checks in at Dorfplatz 1 and reaches the Walliser Alpentherme through
its own internal passage (thermalhotels.ch).
Points: the two big public baths are on the doorstep; 5 minutes on foot from the bus terminal.

**B. Thermenstrasse, towards the Torrent cable car.**
The street that runs south-east out of the centre towards the Torrent lift. Le Bristol, Thermenstrasse 95,
states it is **250 m from the Torrent cable car** and has its own thermal spring (lebristol.ch). Hotel
Alpenblick is at Thermenstrasse 68 (alpenblick-leukerbad.ch). Hotel Astoria, Untere Maressenstrasse 38,
gives 5 minutes on foot to the Torrent cable car and to the village centre, 5 minutes to the baths and
20 minutes to the Gemmi cable car (astoria-leukerbad.ch).
Points: shortest walk to the ski and hiking lift; still within 5 minutes of the baths.

**C. Tuftstrasse and the Gemmi side (north end of the valley).**
The Gemmi cable car leaves from Gemmistrasse 12 (Gemmi-Bahnen AG). Hotel Résidence & Spa Sources des
Alpes is at Tuftstrasse 37, with its own 36 °C thermal pool (sourcesdesalpes.ch); the Hotel de la Croix
Fédérale is at Tuftstrasse 7 (croix-federale.ch).
Points: quieter end under the Gemmi cliffs; the Gemmi cable car climbs nearly 1000 m from here.

## 4. SEASONS

**The thermal water runs all year.** The springs deliver **over four million litres of warm water a day**,
at **51 °C**, which the tourist office says is the largest thermal water occurrence in Europe. It takes
about 40 years for mountain water to seep down, take up minerals and heat up before it comes back out.
Three public baths are fed by it: **Leukerbad Therme, Walliser Alpentherme & Spa, Therme 51°**, plus
three sauna areas, three children's pools and 18 wellness providers.
Sources: leukerbad.ch/thermalwasser and leukerbad.ch/en/thermal-baths

**Winter figures published by the tourist office** (leukerbad.ch/en/winter):
- 50 km of ski pistes
- 50 km of winter walking trails
- 4 km of toboggan runs
- 18 km of cross-country trails
**Winter 2026/2027 opening and closing dates are not published** on the pages checked.

**Lift hours currently published** (leukerbad.ch/en/torrent, read 16/09/2026):
- Leukerbad to Rinderhütte: 08:40 to 12:00 and 12:40 to 16:40, a departure every 20 minutes
  (on the hour, x:20, x:40), midday break 12:00 to 12:40, last ride up 16:20, last ride down 16:40
- Flaschen to Torrentalp: 09:00 to 12:15 and 13:15 to 16:15
- Torrentalp to Rinderhütte: 09:00 to 12:15 and 13:15 to 16:15

**Cards.** The Summer Card gives daily unlimited use of the Gemmi and Torrent lifts plus one entry a day
to one of the three public baths, and stays usable up to the winter season. The Winter Card gives daily
unlimited use of the Gemmi and Torrent lifts and of the public baths.
Sources: leukerbad.ch/en/thermal-baths and leukerbad.ch/en/winter

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2), both with figures published by the tourist office.**
1. **Ascension du Torrenthorn (climb to the Torrenthorn).** 6.8 km, **3 h 00**, 690 m of climb and 690 m
   of descent, graded medium, a loop. Starts at **Rinderhütte**, the top station of the Torrent lift, and
   climbs over the Torrent alpine pastures to the summit, which the page calls the "Rigi of the Valais",
   with a 360-degree view. Above about 2700 m the grass gives way to rock.
   Source: leukerbad.ch/fr/tour/ascension-du-torrenthorn-29148418 (page updated 7 July 2026)
2. **Parcours panoramique sur Torrent (Torrent panorama trail).** 4.1 km, **1 h 30**, no climb and 384 m
   of descent, graded easy, one way. From **Rinderhütte at 2350 m** down through the alp to the
   **Torrentalp mid-station at 1920 m**. Marked as pushchair accessible, dogs allowed, family outing.
   Source: leukerbad.ch/fr/tour/parcours-panoramique-sur-torrent-57757309 (updated 10 August 2026)
   (Winter options on the same site: Jägerboden snowshoe loop from the Gemmi Pass, 5.7 km, 2 h 30,
   278 m of climb, medium; Daubensee cross-country loop, 5.1 km, 1 h, high point 2221 m and low point
   2204 m, with a 7 km and a 5 km version.)

**Mountain rides / attractions (2).**
1. **Gemmi cable car**, from Leukerbad up to the **Gemmi Pass at 2350 m**, a climb of nearly 1000 m,
   "in just a few minutes". At the top: the Panorama Restaurant, the hotel and the Panorama Spa.
   A second cable car runs from the pass down to **Lake Daubensee** and back. The Gemmi has been a
   crossing between Valais and Bern since the Middle Ages, used by pack animals carrying wine, salt and
   cheese, and is the gateway to the Pfyn-Finges Nature Park, a Swiss park of national importance.
   Source: gemmi.ch/en/
2. **Torrent lifts**, from the village to **Rinderhütte at 2350 m** via the Torrentalp mid-station at
   **1920 m**, with a second line from Flaschen to Torrentalp. Every 20 minutes, midday break.
   Sources: leukerbad.ch/en/torrent and leukerbad.ch/fr/tour/parcours-panoramique-sur-torrent-57757309

**Restaurants (2), both listed by the tourist office.**
1. **Alpina Restaurant**, in Leukerbad. Listed by the tourist office as a warm dining room and a sunny
   terrace, with a daily menu, cheese and meat fondues, roast chicken served in a basket and Valais
   specialities. Its house dish is tripe the chef's way. Telephone +41 27 472 27 27.
   Source: leukerbad.ch/en/gastronomy
2. **Rinderhütte Panoramarestaurant**, at the Rinderhütte top station of the Torrent lift, reached by the
   Torrent cable car or by the gondola from Flaschen. Traditional mountain dishes, and the starting point
   for summer and winter outings.
   Source: leukerbad.ch/en/winter
   (The tourist office lists more than 40 restaurants in and around Leukerbad on the same gastronomy page,
   including 51° Restaurant & Bar, Alpenblick, Birchen, Bodmenstübli and Briand.)

**Typical to the place: the thermal baths.**
Three public baths fed by springs at 51 °C, in what the tourist office calls the largest thermal bathing
resort in the Alps. The Leukerbad Therme is described by the tourist office as the largest thermal baths
in Europe, with several outdoor pools and whirlpools, an outdoor sports pool, two slides and a children's
water area. Modern bathing tourism here dates from **1501**, when cardinal Matthäus Schiner bought the
rights to the warm springs, invested in the baths and had an inn built.
Sources: leukerbad.ch/en/thermal-baths and leukerbad.ch/thermalwasser

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Can you get to Leukerbad by train?**
   Not all the way. Trains run to Leuk, then bus 471 climbs to the Leukerbad bus terminal, about 35
   minutes. The tourist office gives 3 hours from Geneva Airport and 3.5 hours from Zurich Airport
   door to door.
   Sources: leukerbad.ch/anreise and thermalhotels.ch
2. **What is actually in the thermal water?**
   The tourist office says it takes about 40 years for mountain water to seep down, take up minerals and
   heat to 51 °C before it comes back out. Over four million litres come out of the ground every day,
   feeding three public baths.
   Sources: leukerbad.ch/thermalwasser and leukerbad.ch/en/thermal-baths
3. **Which hotels have their own thermal water?**
   Le Bristol has its own thermal spring with indoor and outdoor baths; Sources des Alpes has a private
   thermal pool at 36 °C; Therme 51° has four thermal pools from 36 to 44 °C; Regina Terme has a natural
   thermal bath with unfiltered spring water.
   Sources: lebristol.ch, sourcesdesalpes.ch, therme51.ch, reginaterme.ch
4. **Is Leukerbad worth it if I do not ski?**
   The tourist office publishes 50 km of winter walking trails alongside 50 km of pistes, and the Gemmi
   cable car runs up to the 2350 m plateau all year for walking, snowshoeing and the Panorama Restaurant.
   Sources: leukerbad.ch/en/winter and gemmi.ch/en/
5. **How do I get up to the Gemmi Pass and to the Torrent slopes?**
   Two separate cable cars leave the village: the Gemmi cable car climbs nearly 1000 m to the pass at
   2350 m, and the Torrent line runs to Rinderhütte at 2350 m via the Torrentalp mid-station at 1920 m,
   every 20 minutes with a midday break.
   Sources: gemmi.ch/en/ and leukerbad.ch/en/torrent

## 10. UNVERIFIED

  checked. The ticket shop sells 2025/26 passes at the date of this check.
- Ride times in minutes for the Gemmi cable car and for the Torrent line: not published. gemmi.ch only
  says "in just a few minutes".
- Tourist beds or overnight stays: the municipality publishes about 60 hotel and restaurant
  establishments but no bed count.

  main lines (Gemmi and Torrent), not to the ski area's lift count.

  official page checked.

  hotel list to cite; the nine hotels above were each verified on their own site instead.

  resolve; both are presented through leukerbad.ch and thermalhotels.ch.
