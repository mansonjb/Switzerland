The municipality of Riederalp also takes in the villages of **Greich, Goppisberg and Ried-Mörel**,
halfway up between the valley and the alp (same page). The tourist office puts the temperature
difference with the valley at **roughly 10 degrees** (same page).

## 2. ACCESS

**No road reaches Riederalp.** It is reached from **Mörel**, on the Rhone valley line, by cable car.
Two routes, both published by the operator with ride times
(aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility/timetable-season-times):

| Section | Ride time | Transfer time to the View Point lift |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mörel to Greich to Riederalp Mitte | 9 minutes | 10 minutes |
| Mörel to Ried-Mörel | 5 minutes | |
| Ried-Mörel to Riederalp West | 8 minutes | 5 minutes to Hohfluh, 20 minutes to Moosfluh |
| Riederalp to Hohfluh | 4 minutes | |
| Riederalp to Moosfluh | 7 minutes | |

The tourist office's arrival note: from Mörel-Filet there is a gondola to **Riederalp West**, with the
option of getting off halfway at **Ried-Mörel**, and parking in Mörel right next to the valley station.
Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility

**By train.** The feeder railway Mörel to Riederalp runs **all year** and is part of the **SBB GA and
half-fare travelcard network**. SBB luggage service, Snow'n'Rail discounts and saver tickets are
advertised. Booking two nights in a participating hotel gives free return travel by public transport.
Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility

**By car.** You drive to Mörel-Filet and leave the car. Car parks named by the tourist office:
**Car Park Mörel-Riederalp AG**, the **Stone barn park** and **Parking Aletsch**, all at Mörel-Filet,
with EV charging points.
Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility

**Luggage.** The car-free village roads are under snow in winter, so the tourist office recommends
booking the **baggage cart** from the mountain station to the accommodation, sold in the webshop
**from CHF 10 per person**.
Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility

**Between the plateau villages.** The **Aletsch Express** electric shuttle links Riederalp and
Bettmeralp, sold from CHF 6.00 per person (aletscharena.ch webshop listing).

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

**A. Riederalp Mitte.**
Where the Mörel to Greich to Riederalp Mitte gondola arrives, 9 minutes from the valley. The transfer
from here to a View Point lift is given as 10 minutes.
Points: shortest arrival from Mörel; 10 minutes to the next lift.
Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility/timetable-season-times

**B. Riederalp West.**
Where the gondola from Ried-Mörel arrives, 8 minutes above Ried-Mörel and 5 minutes above Mörel.
From here the transfer is 5 minutes to the **Hohfluh** lift and 20 minutes to **Moosfluh**.
Points: closest to the Hohfluh view point; the split route lets you stop at Ried-Mörel on the way up.
Source: same timetable page

**C. The Aletschpromenade and the east side (golf course, Moosfluh).**
The Aletschpromenade runs east through the village past the **9-hole golf course**. Golfhotel Riederhof
is at Aletschpromenade 25, immediately at the course (golfhotel-riederhof.ch); the Tea-Room Zurschmitten
is at Aletschpromenade 70 (aletscharena.ch food and drink listing). The Valaisia apartments, Furrerstrasse
11, state they are next to the slopes and a few minutes' walk from the Moosfluh cable car station
(valaisia-riederalp.ch).
Points: the golf course and the Moosfluh lift on the doorstep; the Riederalp to Moosfluh ride is
7 minutes.

Above the village, on the ridge at **Riederfurka**, stands **Villa Cassel**, now the Pro Natura Centre
Aletsch (see below).

aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/riederalp

(golfclub-riederalp.ch).

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2), both with figures published by the tourist office.**
1. **Moosfluh Geology Trail.** 706 m, **30 minutes**, 30 m of climb, themed trail, graded easy, at
   3987 Riederalp. The short one, straight off the Moosfluh lift.
   Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/summer-autumn/hiking
2. **Circular hike Riederalp to Riederfurka to Riederhorn and back.** 7.44 km, **2 h 45**, 375 m of
   climb and 375 m of descent, graded medium. Passes Villa Cassel on the Riederfurka ridge.
   Source: same hiking page
   (Also published: Aletsch Glacier Viewing Trail, 6.84 km, 2 h 50, 399 m, medium; Riederalp via the
   Moraine Trail to the Bettmerhorn, 9.31 km, 3 h 30, medium; circular hike from Moosfluh via
   Märjelensee to Riederalp, 23.18 km, 6 h 15, difficult; Belalp over the suspension bridge to Riederalp
   via Silbersand and Bischofsitz, 12.49 km, 4 h 30, difficult; Fiescheralp to Riederalp via the
   Herrenweg and Aletschpromenade, 7.91 km, 2 h 30, easy.)

**Mountain rides / attractions (2).**
1. **View Points Hohfluh and Moosfluh.** Riederalp to Hohfluh is a **4-minute** ride, Riederalp to
   Moosfluh **7 minutes**. Both are among the four Aletsch Arena view points, which sit between
   **2227 and 2869 m**, each with a platform over the **Great Aletsch Glacier, 20 km long, the longest
   glacier in the Alps** and the heart of the **Swiss Alps Jungfrau-Aletsch UNESCO World Heritage Site**,
   with **40 four-thousand-metre peaks** in view. Moosfluh runs 6 June to 25 October 2026, 08:30 to 16:30.
   Sources: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/view-points and the timetable page
2. **Pro Natura Centre Aletsch, Villa Cassel, Riederfurka.** The first nature conservation centre in
   Switzerland when it opened in **1976**, in the timber-framed summer residence built at the beginning
   of the 20th century by the English banker **Sir Ernest Cassel**. After Cassel's death the villa ran
   as a hotel for decades. Exhibitions, events and guided excursions; **open in summer only**. Since a
   refurbishment in **2019** the centre runs carbon neutral on an air-water heat pump and photovoltaics.
   Source: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/pro-natura-center-aletsch

**Restaurants (2), both listed by the tourist office.**
1. **Restaurant Alpenrose**, Bahnhofstrasse 24, 3987 Riederalp. Listed by the tourist office as Italian,
   with vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options; the house runs a restaurant, a pizzeria and an
   umbrella bar.
   Sources: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/service-amenities/food-drink and alpenrose-riederalp.ch
2. **Tea-Room Zurschmitten**, Aletschpromenade 70, 3987 Riederalp. Listed by the tourist office as a
   coffee house.
   Source: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/service-amenities/food-drink
   (Other tables the tourist office lists on Riederalp: Chüestall mountain restaurant, Restaurant
   Matterhorn, Bar-Buvette Moosfluh, Restaurant Danys, Restaurant Silbersand, Restaurant Riederfurka,
   VIVA Bar, the bar of the Golfhotel Riederhof, and a tea salon.)

**Typical to the place: the highest golf course in Switzerland.**
The **Golfclub Riederalp** course has **9 holes at 2000 m** and is described by the club as the highest
golf course in Switzerland, reached by cable car, with the 4000 m peaks and the Aletsch Glacier in view.
Season 2026: **4 June to 18 October 2026**.
Source: golfclub-riederalp.ch

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

**Partially published.** The tourist office states that the feeder railways **Mörel to Riederalp,
Bettmeralp valley station to Bettmeralp and Fiesch to Fiescheralp run all year round and are part of the

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do you get to Riederalp?**
   By cable car from Mörel. Mörel to Greich to Riederalp Mitte takes 9 minutes; the other route is Mörel
   to Ried-Mörel, 5 minutes, then Ried-Mörel to Riederalp West, 8 minutes. There is no road.
   Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility/timetable-season-times
2. **Where do I leave the car?**
   At Mörel-Filet, in the Car Park Mörel-Riederalp AG, the Stone barn park or Parking Aletsch, all with
   EV charging points and all next to the valley station.
   Source: aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility
3. **What is there to do apart from skiing?**
   The Pro Natura Centre at Villa Cassel on the Riederfurka, open in summer, the 9-hole golf course at
   2000 m (4 June to 18 October 2026), 300 km of hiking trails, the suspension bridge over the Massa
   Gorge, and the Hohfluh and Moosfluh view points over the Aletsch Glacier.
   Sources: aletscharena.ch Pro Natura page, golfclub-riederalp.ch,
   aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/summer-autumn/hiking,
   aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/riederalp
4. **Which side of Riederalp should I stay on?**
   Riederalp Mitte is where the 9-minute gondola from Mörel arrives; Riederalp West is 5 minutes from
   the Hohfluh lift; the Aletschpromenade side puts you at the golf course and a few minutes from the
   Moosfluh station.
   Sources: the timetable page, golfhotel-riederhof.ch, valaisia-riederalp.ch
5. **Is it cooler up there in summer?**
   The tourist office gives a temperature difference of roughly 10 degrees between Riederalp at 1925 m
   and the Rhone valley below.
   Source: aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/riederalp

## 10. UNVERIFIED

- **Hotels. This is the big gap.** Only the Golfhotel Riederhof could be verified on its own site.
  The Aletsch Arena tourist office keeps accommodation behind a booking widget (shop.aletscharena.ch)
  whose cookie banner cannot be dismissed without giving consent, so no plain hotel list could be read.
  Two more names appear in the tourist office's restaurant listing and are very likely hotels
  (Restaurant Matterhorn, Restaurant Riederfurka), and the Art Furrer group is known to run houses here,
  but **artfurrer.ch is flagged by Cloudflare as a suspected phishing site** and was not opened. A manual
  pass is needed before a Riederalp page can be built.
- Resident population and tourist beds of Riederalp: no official figure found.
- Altitudes of the Hohfluh and Moosfluh stations individually: only the range 2227 to 2869 m for the
  four view points is published.
- Number of lifts in the Aletsch Arena ski area: not published.
- Length and par of the golf course: the club page references a score card and handicap table but does
  not state them in the text read.
- Swiss Travel Pass coverage: not stated. Only the GA and half-fare statement is published.
- Chalet Arolle (zurschmitten.ch) is a holiday rental on Riederalp at 1870 m with a beam carved 1698,
  within 5 minutes of the cable car; it is a rental, not a hotel, so it is not in the hotel list.
