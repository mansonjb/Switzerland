Neuchatel sits directly on Lake Neuchatel, described by the tourist office as Switzerland's largest
lake entirely within the country, between the lake and the first foothills of the Jura, roughly
midway between Geneva and Zurich. Buildings use the local yellow Hauterive limestone, which the
tourist office says "lights up and brightens the whole city," giving Neuchatel the nickname evoked in
Alexandre Dumas's description of it as looking carved from butter.
Source: j3l.ch/en/Z10771/destinations/cities/city-of-neuchatel and neuchatelville.ch

## 2. ACCESS

**By train.** Neuchatel is a stop on the ICN tilting-train line that "circumvents the lakes" (with
stops at Yverdon-les-Bains, Neuchatel and Biel/Bienne), connecting toward Switzerland's three main
airports (Geneva, Zurich, Basel). Exact minute journey times are not published on the access page
checked; it points to the SBB timetable (sbb.ch) directly.
Source: j3l.ch/en/Z10809/planning/location-access

**By car.** The region is reached via the A1 motorway (St. Gallen-Geneva), the A5 (Soleure-Yverdon)
and the A16 (Transjurane).
Source: j3l.ch/en/Z10809/planning/location-access

**Free transport and activities for overnight guests: the Neuchatel Tourist Card.** Automatically
available to anyone with at least one night's booked accommodation in the region, it gives free
public transport for the length of the stay, free access to many museums in the canton, free lake
cruises on Lake Neuchatel and at Les Brenets, free bicycle rental, and further reduced-price
activities.
Source: j3l.ch/en/Z10909/booking/guest-cards

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

**A. Station side.**
Hotel Alpes et Lac sits directly opposite Neuchatel railway station: 46 rooms, 30 in the main building
and 16 in an air-conditioned annex.
Source: hotel's own listing via j3l.ch

**B. Harbour / lakefront (Place du Port and Esplanade du Mont-Blanc).**
The dense hotel row facing the water. Hotel Beaulac sits on the lake's main port in the city centre,
with a rooftop bar and direct access to the harbour and waterfront promenade (beaulac.ch). Beau-Rivage
Hotel, at Esplanade du Mont-Blanc 1, gives "one of Switzerland's most beautiful panoramas" over the
lake, 60 rooms plus suites (beau-rivage-hotel.ch). Hotel des Arts, Rue J.-L. Pourtales 3, is close to
the lakeside, town centre and station (5 minutes by public transport), 58 rooms
(hotel's own facts via j3l.ch).

**C. Over the water (Route des Gouttes-d'Or, east of the centre).**
Hotel Palafitte, at Route des Gouttes-d'Or 2, is built as stilted pavilions directly over Lake
Neuchatel; Lake Pavilions have a small ladder for direct lake access.
Source: palafitte.ch/en/

## 4. SEASONS

Neuchatel is a year-round city and lake destination rather than a ski resort; there is no lift pass
or snow report published by the tourist office. The Chaumont funicular (see section 5) and the LNM
lake cruises run through the seasons.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2).**
1. **Quai Ostervald and Les Jeunes-Rives.** Lakefront promenade along "crystal-clear waters," past the
   harbour to the Jeunes-Rives beach.
   Source: j3l.ch/en/Z10771/destinations/cities/city-of-neuchatel
2. **Vallon de l'Ermitage.** Described by the tourist office as "a true haven of serenity and unspoilt
   nature," about 10 minutes from the town centre.
   Source: j3l.ch/en/Z10771/destinations/cities/city-of-neuchatel

**Sights / rides (2).**
1. **Chateau de Neuchatel and Collegiale.** Standing together since the 12th century on a rocky spur
   above the city (the castle itself dates to the late 10th century); built of flamboyant yellow
   limestone. The cantonal government now has its seat in the castle. A sentry walk runs from the
   castle's south side or the cloister's north entrance to a footbridge toward the Museum of
   Ethnography; a summer café terrace operates on the south facade. The tourist office calls the view
   from the esplanade, over the old town roofs to the lake, the most spectacular in the city.
   Source: j3l.ch/en/Z14145/
2. **Funicular to Chaumont.** Reaches the Chaumont viewpoint at 1171 m above the city.
   Source: j3l.ch/en/Z10771/destinations/cities/city-of-neuchatel

**Restaurants (2), both hotel dining rooms sourced from the hotels' own sites.**
1. **O'Terroirs Brasserie, Beau-Rivage Hotel.** Led by chef Mickael Lavoisier, at Esplanade du
   Mont-Blanc 1, overlooking the lake.
   Source: beau-rivage-hotel.ch/en/
2. **Lake Side Brasserie, Hotel Beaulac.** On the main port, part of the Beaulac's multiple dining
   venues alongside Sushi at Waves and a rooftop Lounge Bar.
   Source: beaulac.ch (via listing)

**Typical to the place.**
**Fete des Vendanges (Grape Harvest Festival).** Three days of autumn festivities celebrating
Neuchatel's wine heritage: a flower parade, a "pyromelodic" fireworks show over the lake, a
confetti battle, a funfair and a winegrowers' village. **26-27-28 September 2026** window, per the
festival's own extended hours (Friday 18:00-04:00, Saturday 11:00-04:00, Sunday 11:00-midnight).
Payment inside the festival is by festival bracelet at vendor stalls, except for carousel rides.
Source: j3l.ch/en/Z37408/

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Do hotel guests get free transport and museum entry in Neuchatel?**
   Yes, via the Neuchatel Tourist Card, automatic with any booked overnight stay: free public
   transport for the stay, free entry to many cantonal museums, free lake cruises on Lake Neuchatel
   and at Les Brenets, and free bike rental.
   Source: j3l.ch/en/Z10909/booking/guest-cards
2. **What is there to see above the castle in Neuchatel?**
   The Chateau and Collegiale stand together on a rocky spur since the 12th century (the castle itself
   from the late 10th century); a sentry walk and a footbridge lead toward the Museum of Ethnography,
   with the esplanade giving what the tourist office calls the city's most spectacular view over the
   old town roofs to the lake.
   Source: j3l.ch/en/Z14145/
3. **Can you sleep over the lake in Neuchatel?**
   Yes, at Hotel Palafitte, whose Lake Pavilions are stilted structures built directly over Lake
   Neuchatel with a ladder down to the water.
   Source: palafitte.ch/en/
4. **How do you reach the Chaumont viewpoint?**
   By funicular from the city up to 1171 m.
   Source: j3l.ch/en/Z10771/destinations/cities/city-of-neuchatel
5. **What is the Fete des Vendanges?**
   Neuchatel's autumn grape-harvest festival: flower parade, fireworks over the lake, funfair and a
   winegrowers' village, running as a Friday-to-Sunday weekend each late September.
   Source: j3l.ch/en/Z37408/
