## 1. IDENTITY

- Slug on the sheet: `schaffhausen`. Region key `eastern`. Canton: **Schaffhausen** (cantonal capital).
- Altitude: **403 m**, at Bahnhofstrasse (Wikipedia, cross-check only; no altitude figure found on an
  official page this session, but this is the standard figure and nothing contradicts it).
- Coordinates (Wikipedia, cross-check only): 47.700 / 8.633.
- carFree: **false**.
- Resident population: **38,990** (stadt-schaffhausen.ch, live statistics counter, read 16/09/2026).
- Official address: **Stadthausgasse 12, 8200 Schaffhausen**.
- What defines the place practically: the **old town, with 171 carved oriel windows (Erker)**, sits
  between the **Rhine** and the **Munot**, the circular 16th-century fortress on the hill directly above
  it; the **Rhine Falls**, Europe's largest waterfall by the tourist office's own description, are a few
  minutes away by train or boat at Neuhausen am Rheinfall.

## 2. ACCESS

- **Zurich to Schaffhausen: about 40 minutes by train** (versus 45 minutes by car for the 50 km distance).
  **Zurich Airport to Schaffhausen: about 49 minutes by train** (versus 38 minutes by car)
  (schaffhauserland.ch, "Anreise").
- No parking arrangement to describe: Schaffhausen is not car-free.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. Inside the old town**
- Kronenhof Schaffhausen is at **Kirchhofplatz 7**, "in the heart of the old town", 42 rooms and suites,
  3-star, with a city wellness area. Hotel Rüden is described by the tourist office as "at the edge of
  the historic old town" (schaffhauserland.ch listing; the hotel's own domain did not resolve this
  session).

**B. Station side / Zur Bleiche**
- Vienna House by Wyndham Zur Bleiche Schaffhausen is described by the tourist office as "central to the
  main station and the idyllic old town" (schaffhauserland.ch listing; the hotel's own domain did not
  resolve this session). Hotel & Restaurant Promenade is a "city hotel in greenery with its own hotel
  park" (schaffhauserland.ch).

**C. Edge of town, towards the Rhine and the Randen hills**
- Hotel Park Villa is at **Parkstrasse 18**, a historic castle-style building on the edge of the old town,
  25 rooms, with its own tennis court. Schaffhausen Youth Hostel is at **Randenstrasse 65**, in a former
  castle with red shutters, close to the Rhine.

## 4. SEASONS

**All year**
- **Munot fortress: free entry**, accessible areas open **May-September 08:00-20:00, October-April
  09:00-17:00** (munot.ch). The **Munotglöggli bell is struck by hand every evening at 21:00 for five
  minutes**, a tradition going back to a bell cast in **1589** at Hans Frei's workshop in Kempten,
  originally used to warn of fire or attack and to signal the closing of the city gates and taverns
  (munot.ch).
- **Museum zu Allerheiligen**: **Tuesday-Sunday 11:00-17:00**, **open until 20:00 on the last Thursday of
  the month**, closed Mondays; the separate Museum Stemmler site is open **Sundays 11:00-17:00 and the
  first Saturday of the month**, free entry (allerheiligen.ch).
- **Rhine Falls: accessible around the clock, 365 days a year**; **Schloss Laufen side, CHF 5 adults / CHF
  3 children 6-15**, includes the Historama and viewing platforms; **Schlössli Wörth side: no entrance fee**
  (rheinfall.ch).

**Autumn**
- **Museumsnacht at Museum zu Allerheiligen: 19 September 2026, free entry** (allerheiligen.ch).

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2, both official published distances, rheinfall.ch)**
1. **Small Rhine Falls circular trail** — **3.5 km, about 1 hour**, 51 m ascent / 53 m descent.
2. **Grand Rhine Falls circular trail** — **7 km, about 1 h 50**, 120 m ascent / 120 m descent.

**Rides and sights**
1. **Munot fortress** — a circular 16th-century fortress; the Munotverein, with about **3,500 members**,
   has maintained it for **over 180 years**. The dry moat has been a **fallow deer enclosure since 1905**,
   at the request of the city's beautification society; by tradition the lead buck takes the name of the
   sitting city president (munot.ch).
2. **Rhine Falls boat trips (Ernst Mändli AG)** — the falls are **about 150 m wide**; boats run basin
   tours from Schloss Laufen and Schlössli Wörth, including the "Schiff & Wein" evening cruise, **CHF
   55.00 per adult**, departing 16:45/17:00 and returning by 19:00 (rheinfall.ch).

**What is typical of the place**
1. **The 171 carved oriel windows (Erker)** of the old town (schaffhauserland.ch).
2. **Museum zu Allerheiligen**, Klosterstrasse 16 — six permanent collections (archaeology, history, art
   and natural history) inside a former Romanesque abbey; hours above.

**Restaurants (2, from the Schaffhauserland Tourismus directory)**
1. **Güterhof - Gastronomie am Rhein** — a former warehouse on the Rhine, now a restaurant with bar,
   lounge and terrace, close to the Munot (schaffhauserland.ch).
2. **Brauerei Falken AG** — brewery restaurant, the brewery itself founded in **1799**, described by the
   tourist office as among Switzerland's five largest independent breweries (schaffhauserland.ch).

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **How do I get from Zurich to Schaffhausen?** About 40 minutes by train, or 49 minutes by train from
   Zurich Airport. (schaffhauserland.ch)
2. **Is the Munot free to visit?** Yes, the publicly accessible part of the fortress is free of charge,
   every day, within its seasonal hours (8am-8pm May-September, 9am-5pm October-April). (munot.ch)
3. **Why does a bell ring every evening?** The Munotglöggli, cast in 1589, is struck by hand at 9pm for
   five minutes, continuing a centuries-old fire/attack warning and curfew signal. (munot.ch)
4. **What lives in the Munot's moat?** Fallow deer, there since 1905; by tradition the lead buck is named
   after the sitting city president. (munot.ch)
5. **How do I see the Rhine Falls?** They are accessible 24/7, year-round; the Schloss Laufen side charges
   CHF 5 for adults (Historama and viewing platforms included), the Schlössli Wörth side is free.
   (rheinfall.ch)
