# Research: Einsiedeln

Checked 16/09/2026. Only official sources: Kloster Einsiedeln (kloster-einsiedeln.ch), Bezirk
Einsiedeln (einsiedeln.ch), Einsiedeln-Ybrig-Zürichsee Tourismus (eyz.swiss), SOB Südostbahn (sob.ch),
the hotels' own sites. Wikipedia not needed for altitude/coordinates in this session (the municipal

## 1. IDENTITY

- Altitude: 882 m ü. M. VERIFIED, einsiedeln.ch/?page=1276 (Bezirk Einsiedeln facts page).
- Canton: Schwyz. VERIFIED, same page.
- Region key: `central`.
- Car-free: **false**. Einsiedeln is reached via the A3 motorway exits at Richterswil or Schindellegi,
  "in less than 15 minutes", and has public parking including the Parkhaus Brüel.
  VERIFIED, eyz.swiss/de/anreise-nach-einsiedeln
- Residents: 16'621 (as of 20 January 2026). VERIFIED, einsiedeln.ch/?page=1276
- Area: 110.4 km2 (11'036 ha). VERIFIED, same page.
- The Abbey (Kloster Einsiedeln), a Benedictine community, is home to about 40 monks and is called
  "die bedeutendste Wallfahrtsort der Schweiz" (Switzerland's most important pilgrimage site).
  VERIFIED, kloster-einsiedeln.ch
- 2026 marks "500 Jahre Wiederaufblühen" (500 years of revival) since the abbey, "beinahe ausgestorben"
  (almost extinct) in 1526, was revived. VERIFIED, kloster-einsiedeln.ch

## 2. ACCESS

- Train: SBB/Südostbahn (SOB) runs "a half-hourly schedule from Zurich to Einsiedeln"; the
  Voralpen-Express (Romanshorn-Lucerne line) calls hourly from eastern Switzerland and the greater
  Lucerne area. VERIFIED, eyz.swiss/de/anreise-nach-einsiedeln
- Car: reachable "from both highway exits [Richterswil and Schindellegi, on the A3] in less than 15
  minutes". VERIFIED, same page.
- Local buses run at approximately 20-minute intervals between the centre and outlying parking areas
  such as the Schanzen. VERIFIED, same page.
- Once in the village, "Vom Bahnhof führt die Hauptstrasse durch das Dorf in Richtung Klosterplatz"
  (the main street leads from the station through the village to the monastery square).
  VERIFIED, eyz.swiss/de/einsiedeln
- Hotel guests receive the ÖV-Karte for free local public transport.
  VERIFIED, eyz.swiss/de/einsiedeln

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

**A. Klosterplatz, directly by the Abbey**
- Boutique Hotel St. Georg: "tastefully furnished rooms less than 50 m from the famous Kloster
  Einsiedeln". VERIFIED, eyz.swiss/de/hotels-in-der-region-einsiedeln
- Hotel Drei Könige, a family-run hotel "an bester Lage direkt auf dem Klosterplatz" (in the best
  location, directly on the monastery square), with cloister views. VERIFIED, same page.

**B. Hauptstrasse and the village centre**
- Hotel Linde, Schmiedenstrasse 28, centrally located, styled as a "KunstHotel" with a restaurant, and
  rooms ranging from simple pilgrim rooms to a junior suite. VERIFIED, linde-einsiedeln.ch
- Gasthaus Meinradsberg, Ilgenweidstrasse 3, a restaurant with rooms offered in cooperation with Hotel
  Drei Könige. VERIFIED, meinradsberg.ch

**C. Toward Sihlsee**
- Sihlseebadi Roblosen, the free lakeside lido at Egg near Einsiedeln, lies on this side of the village,
  30.6 km from Zurich's main station. VERIFIED, eyz.swiss/de/besuchen/natur/sihlseebadi-roblosen
- Hotel Brunni, positioned by the tourist office as a starting point for hiking and tours in this
  direction. VERIFIED, eyz.swiss/de/hotels-in-der-region-einsiedeln

## 4. SEASONS

- Public guided tours of the abbey run "täglich um 14 Uhr (ausser Sonn- und Feiertage)" (daily at 2pm,
  except Sundays and public holidays). VERIFIED, eyz.swiss/de/einsiedeln
- Sihlseebadi Roblosen opens seasonally: May-June and from 16 August, from 10am; July to mid-August,
  from 9am; September depends on the weather, closing in poor or cold conditions.
  VERIFIED, eyz.swiss/de/besuchen/natur/sihlseebadi-roblosen
- Winter: cross-country skiing and snowshoeing around the region. VERIFIED, eyz.swiss/de/einsiedeln

## 5. WHAT TO DO

Two named walks
1. **Panoramaweg Sihlsee**, 9.4 km, about 2 h 34 of walking time, +146 m ascent, easy grade. It starts
   at the Adlermättli information board in front of Kloster Einsiedeln, passes the old Marstall and the
   St. Benedict statue, reaches Birchli with views of the monastery and the Sihlsee, descends to the
   shore, follows the lake past the sailing area and Roblosen beach, and returns via forest trails and
   the Galgenchappeli chapel. VERIFIED, outdoor-schwyz.ch/de/tour/themenweg/panoramaweg-sihlsee/32532687/
2. **Sihlsee shore path to Roblosen**, the lakeside section of the same official route, linking central
   Einsiedeln to the free Sihlseebadi Roblosen lido. VERIFIED, eyz.swiss/de/besuchen/natur/
   sihlseebadi-roblosen and outdoor-schwyz.ch (route description above).

Two named rides/sights
1. **Kloster Einsiedeln (Klosterkirche and Gnadenkapelle)**, home of the Black Madonna ("Schwarze
   Madonna"), with public guided tours daily at 2pm except Sundays and holidays.
   VERIFIED, kloster-einsiedeln.ch and eyz.swiss/de/einsiedeln
2. **Marienbrunnen**, on the Klosterplatz: the current fountain dates from 1752 and carries a statue of
   the Immaculata by the Italian sculptor Domenico Pozzi; it has 14 spouts. During the French
   revolutionary invasion of 1798, a local resident, Helene Gyr, bought the fountain to save it from
   destruction and later returned it to the monastery. VERIFIED, kloster-einsiedeln.ch/kloster/
   gesamtuebersicht/gelaende-raeume/marienbrunnen-und-klosterplatz

Two named tables
1. **Gasthaus Meinradsberg**, Ilgenweidstrasse 3: seasonal, mostly regional produce, a summer garden
   under the trees and a fireside indoor room in winter; open Wednesday to Sunday.
   VERIFIED, meinradsberg.ch
2. **Restaurant Allegrino**, at Hotel Allegro, Lincolnweg 23: regional cuisine, plus a rooftop bar with
   live music at the same address. VERIFIED, hotel-allegro.ch

Typical of the place
- **Founding history**: around 835 the hermit Meinrad, a monk of Reichenau abbey, settled on the site
  of today's Gnadenkapelle; he was killed by two robbers on 21 January 861; other hermits continued at
  his martyrdom site; in 934 Eberhard, provost of Strasbourg cathedral (d. 958), gathered the hermits
  into a Benedictine community under the Rule of St Benedict. VERIFIED, kloster-einsiedeln.ch/kloster/
  geschichte
- **Museums on the Klosterplatz**: the Panorama painting, Museum Fram and a gingerbread museum are
  named among Einsiedeln's sights by the tourist office. VERIFIED, eyz.swiss/de/einsiedeln

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. *How do you get from Zurich to Einsiedeln?*
   By SOB train on a half-hourly schedule, or the hourly Voralpen-Express from eastern Switzerland and
   Lucerne; by car via the A3 at Richterswil or Schindellegi, under 15 minutes to the village. Source:
   eyz.swiss/de/anreise-nach-einsiedeln
2. *Can you visit the abbey and see the Black Madonna?*
   Yes, public guided tours run daily at 2pm except Sundays and public holidays; the Black Madonna is
   in the Gnadenkapelle. Source: kloster-einsiedeln.ch and eyz.swiss/de/einsiedeln
3. *What is the origin of Einsiedeln Abbey?*
   The hermit Meinrad settled the site around 835 and was killed there in 861; the community became a
   formal Benedictine abbey in 934 under Eberhard of Strasbourg. Source: kloster-einsiedeln.ch/kloster/
   geschichte
4. *What is there to do at the Sihlsee?*
   The 9.4 km Panoramaweg Sihlsee loop from the monastery, and the free Sihlseebadi Roblosen lido with
   swimming, beach volleyball and a summer open-air cinema. Source: outdoor-schwyz.ch and eyz.swiss/de/
   besuchen/natur/sihlseebadi-roblosen
5. *What is 2026's anniversary at the abbey?*
   500 years since the abbey's revival after nearly being extinguished in 1526. Source:
   kloster-einsiedeln.ch
