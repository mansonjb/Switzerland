# Research dossier: St. Moritz

Checked 16/09/2026. Official sources: St. Moritz Tourismus (stmoritz.com), Engadin St. Moritz Tourismus
(engadin.ch and maps.engadin.ch), Engadin St. Moritz Mountains AG (mountains.ch), Corvatsch Diavolezza

## 1. IDENTITY

- Slug on the sheet: `st-moritz`. Region key `graubunden`. Canton: **Graubünden (Grisons)**, Maloja region,
  Upper Engadine.
- Altitude on the sheet: **1822 m**. **DISCREPANCY WORTH KNOWING:** the tourist office's own strapline is
  "St. Moritz - Lifestyle at **1,856 m** above sea level" (stmoritz.com/en). 1856 is also the year the Kulm
  opened, so the figure is a brand device as much as a measurement. The municipality is usually given as
  1822 m, and the village parts differ: **St. Moritz Dorf about 1830 m, St. Moritz Bad about 1775 m**.
  **RECOMMENDATION: keep 1822 on the sheet**, and if the page mentions 1856 at all, attribute it to the
  tourist office rather than stating it flatly.

## 2. ACCESS

- Rail gateway is **Chur**, then the **Rhaetian Railway Albula line** to St. Moritz, or **Tirano (Italy)**
  via the Bernina line.
- **Bernina Express**: Chur to Tirano **about 4 hours**, **St. Moritz to Tirano about 2 h 15**, over
  **196 bridges and through 55 tunnels** (rhb.ch). Diverted via the Vereina tunnel from Thursday
  **29 October to Friday 13 November 2026** for engineering works. **Seat reservation compulsory** in the
  panoramic cars, on top of the ticket.
- **Albula and Bernina lines are a UNESCO World Heritage Site, listed in 2008**; the Bernina line is the
  highest transalpine railway route and one of the steepest adhesion railways in the world (rhb.ch).
- Zurich Airport to St. Moritz: aggregators give roughly 3 h 30 to 4 h with changes at Zurich HB and Chur.
  **No official figure obtained.** UNVERIFIED, do not publish a number.
- No parking arrangement to describe: St. Moritz is not car-free.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. St. Moritz Dorf (about 1830 m)**
- The upper village on the slope above the lake, and the pedestrian centre. Via Traunter Plazzas is the
  hotel street: Steffani at no. 6, Hauser at no. 7, Crystal Hotel at no. 1.
- The **Corviglia funicular** starts here at St. Moritz Dorf and climbs via Chantarella to Corviglia. Note
  the 2026 closure under SEASONS.
- The walk **around Lake St. Moritz starts and ends at St. Moritz station**: **4.3 km, 1 h 05**, almost flat.

**B. St. Moritz Bad (about 1775 m)**
- The lower, quieter half at the far end of the lake, historically the spa quarter (the mineral springs are
  what first brought visitors).
- The **Signalbahn cable car runs from St. Moritz Bad up to Signal**, and in summer 2026 it is the way up
  to Corviglia while the funicular is closed.
- The youth hostel is here, at Via Surpunt 60, and Hotel Laudinella too.

**C. Suvretta, Champfèr and Silvaplana-Surlej, out of town**
- Suvretta House sits apart from the centre in its own grounds; Champfèr is a village section of the
  municipality at about 1825 m.
- **Surlej (Silvaplana)** is the foot of the **Corvatsch** cable car, about 6 km from St. Moritz.
  Nira Alpina is at Via dal Corvatsch 76 there.

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2, official figures from maps.engadin.ch, the destination's own trail database)**
1. **Around Lake St. Moritz** — **4.3 km, 1 h 05**, 2 m of ascent and 2 m of descent, circular from and back
   to **St. Moritz station**, easy, about 73% asphalt.
2. **Heidi's Flower Path** — **1.1 km, 20 minutes**, 18 m up and 18 m down, from **Chantarella** to the
   Heidi hut, with **around 200 different plant species**, mostly protected mountain flowers.
   (Reached from St. Moritz Dorf by the Chantarella funicular section; check the 2026 closure first.)

**Rides and sights (2, plus the two the brief asks for)**
1. **The Bernina line and the Bernina Express** — the UNESCO route (listed 2008) from **Chur to Tirano in
   about 4 hours**, or **St. Moritz to Tirano in about 2 h 15**, over 196 bridges and through 55 tunnels,
   past the Landwasser Viaduct, the helical tunnels, the Brusio circular viaduct and the Palü glacier.
   Seat reservation compulsory in the panoramic cars.
2. **Corvatsch, 3303 m** — the highest cable car station in Graubünden, up from **Surlej (Silvaplana)**,
   summer season **20 June to 16 October 2026**, departures every 20 minutes.
   (Second option if a nearer mountain is wanted: **Muottas Muragl**, funicular running **6 June to
   25 October 2026**, with late departures to 23:00.)
3. **The lake** — frozen hard enough in February to carry horse racing; 765,000 m2, ice 40 to 80 cm.
4. **Corviglia and Piz Nair** — Corviglia has 44 km of pistes; the Piz Nair cable car runs 27 June to
   18 October 2026. The funicular from St. Moritz Dorf is shut for overhaul in summer 2026.

**Restaurants (2, from the St. Moritz Tourismus directory)**
1. **Chesa Veglia, Pizzeria Heuboden** — Via Veglia 2, in a **farmhouse built in 1685**, the pizzeria set in
   the former hayloft. The same house holds the Patrizier Stuben, Grill Chadafö, the Carigiet fondue stübli
   and the Polo Bar. (The stmoritz.com listing page truncated on fetch; refetch for the exact wording.)
2. **Hatecke** — Via Maistra 16, a bistro next to the butcher's shop of the same name, known for dried meat.
   (Also only from the directory listing summary; refetch before publishing.)
   SAFER ALTERNATIVE, already fully sourced: the **Hauser confiserie and restaurant**, Via Traunter Plazzas 7,
   open daily 07:00 to 22:00 with the kitchen closing at 21:00, serving Grisons specialities, and selling
   Engadine nut cake and Graubünden pear bread from its own confiserie.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Should I stay in St. Moritz Dorf or St. Moritz Bad?** Dorf is the upper village and the pedestrian
   centre, at about 1830 m, with the hotel street Via Traunter Plazzas and the Corviglia funicular. Bad is
   the lower, quieter half at about 1775 m, with the Signalbahn up to Signal. (stmoritz.com, engadin.ch)
2. **Is the Corviglia funicular running?** Not in summer 2026: Engadin St. Moritz Tourismus states it is
   closed for a major overhaul, and you go up instead by the Signalbahn from St. Moritz Bad plus a shuttle
   bus, or by the Celerina to Marguns gondola. (engadin.ch)
3. **What is the Bernina Express and is it covered by my pass?** It runs Chur to Tirano in about 4 hours and
   St. Moritz to Tirano in about 2 h 15, over 196 bridges and through 55 tunnels, on lines listed by UNESCO
   in 2008. Swiss Travel Pass and GA holders still need the compulsory seat reservation. (rhb.ch)
4. **When are the races on the frozen lake?** White Turf runs on three Sundays in February; for 2027 the
   racing days are 7, 14 and 21 February. The lake covers 765,000 m2 and carries 40 to 80 cm of ice.
   (whiteturf.ch, graubuenden.ch)
5. **Which is the highest mountain station?** Corvatsch at 3303 m, the highest in Graubünden, reached from
   Surlej; the summer season in 2026 runs 20 June to 16 October. (corvatsch-diavolezza.ch, engadin.ch)
