# Research dossier: Basel

Checked 16/09/2026. Official sources: Basel Tourismus (basel.com), Kunstmuseum Basel
(kunstmuseumbasel.ch), Basler Münster / Basel Cathedral (baslermuenster.ch), Fähri-Verein Basel
(faehri.ch, leu-faehri.ch), the hotels' own sites. No ratings, no reviews.

the browser tool navigating official sites directly (WebFetch returned 403 on basel.com and bs.ch, so the
browser was used instead, which worked). Several hotel and canton-statistics domains guessed directly did

## 1. IDENTITY

- Slug on the sheet: `basel`. Region key `mittelland`. Canton: **Basel-Stadt**.
- Altitude: **261 m** at the Barfüsserkirche (Wikipedia, cross-check only; the nearby Basel SBB station
  sits at 276 m). No altitude figure was read on an official Basel-Stadt page this session, so this is a
  cross-check figure, not a first-party one.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia, cross-check only): 47.555 / 7.591.
- carFree: **false**.
- Resident population: reported by Wikipedia as **177,595** (31 December 2024) for the municipality, with
  the wider metropolitan area around 830,000. No official bs.ch or stadt-page confirmation could be read
  this session (the canton's statistics pages returned 404 on every URL tried); treat the population
  figure as UNVERIFIED pending a direct read of an official page.
- What defines the place practically: the **Rhine cuts the city into Grossbasel (left bank, the old town
  and the Münster) and Kleinbasel (right bank)**, crossed on foot by the **Mittlere Brücke** and by **four
  historic passenger ferries** that use only the current, no motor.

## 2. ACCESS

- No exact minutes figure for Zurich-Basel could be confirmed on an official page this session (only
  Wikipedia's operational description of "three trains per hour to Zürich Hauptbahnhof" was found, which
  is not a first-party timetable source under this dossier's sourcing rule). UNVERIFIED, do not publish an
  exact minutes figure without a direct SBB timetable read.
- **EuroAirport Basel-Mulhouse-Freiburg**: bus line 50 runs "Bahnhof SBB/SNCF - Brausebad - Kannenfeldplatz
  - EuroAirport" (Wikipedia, cross-check only for the route; not used as a primary fact here beyond noting
  the connection exists).
- **BaselCard**: given free with every booking at a Basel hotel, hostel, B&B or apartment; check-in gives a
  personal card (or a web-app version). It grants **free travel on public transport for the length of the
  stay (up to 30 days)** in TNW zones 10, 11, 13/EuroAirport, 14 and 15, **including the transfer from the
  airport or station to the hotel** on the strength of the booking confirmation alone, plus **25% off
  museums, Basel Zoo, Theater Basel (evening box office only), the sightseeing bus, the public Old Town
  walking tour, the Jakob's Leckerly workshop tour, Basler Personenschifffahrt day boat trips, the
  Reigoldswil-Wasserfallen cable car and Nordstern**, and a **CHF 25/day e-bike rental (Guest Bike Basel)
  at the SBB station** (basel.com, "BaselCard"). It is **not valid** for the trip to the Vitra Design
  Museum, to France, or to the Reigoldswil-Wasserfallen cable car itself, as these lie outside the card's
  travel zone.
- No parking arrangement to describe: Basel is not car-free.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 areas)

**A. Grossbasel old town, on the Rhine**
- Grand Hotel Les Trois Rois is at **Blumenrain 8**, "perched right on the Rhine, in the heart of Basel's
  Old Town" (lestroisrois.com). Der Teufelhof Basel occupies **two combined 18th-century town houses in
  the old town**, with its own theatre and two restaurants (teufelhof.com).

**B. Kleinbasel, the Rhine's right bank**
- Hotel Krafft is at **Rheingasse 12**, "directly on the beautiful Rhine promenade and in the lively
  Rheingasse", a house with guest history going back to **1873** (krafftbasel.ch). Volkshaus Basel, in
  **Kleinbasel**, was designed by **Herzog & de Meuron**, a few minutes' walk from the Rhine and the Messe
  Basel exhibition grounds (volkshaus-basel.ch).

**C. St. Alban quarter, "Basel's Little Venice"**
- Basel Youth Hostel is at **Maja Sacher-Platz 10**, in the St. Alban district, 2 minutes' walk from
  Kunstmuseum Basel | Gegenwart and 2 minutes from the St. Alban-Rhine ferry (youthhostel.ch). The
  district itself is described by the tourist office as a place of "narrow alleys past ancient houses and
  mills, and never far from the water" (basel.com, "Old Town").

## 4. SEASONS

**All year**
- **Basel Münster (Cathedral)**: summer hours **Mon-Fri 10:00-17:00, Sat 10:00-16:30, Sun/holidays about
  11:30-17:00**; winter hours **Mon-Fri 11:00-16:00, Sat 11:00-16:30, Sun/holidays about 11:00-16:00**;
  Advent hours **Mon-Fri 11:00-18:00, Sat 11:00-16:30, Sun about 11:30-16:00**. Closed **1 January, Good
  Friday and 24 December**. During **Basel Fasnacht**, open 11:00-14:00 only, cloister closed
  (baslermuenster.ch).
- **Tower climb (Georgsturm and Martinsturm)**: about **250 steps** each; **CHF 6 single admission, CHF 4
  reduced (AHV/students/apprentices/groups of 6+), free under 14**, free for holders of a disability pass,
  and free for refugees and asylum seekers; last entry 30 minutes before the Münster closes, no entry
  12:00-12:20 during midday prayers (baslermuenster.ch).
- **Kunstmuseum Basel | Hauptbau & Neubau**: **Tue 10:00-18:00, Wed 10:00-20:00, Thu-Sun 10:00-18:00,
  closed Monday**; **Gegenwart site: Tue-Sun 11:00-18:00, closed Monday**. **Collection free Tue+Thu+Fri
  17:00-18:00, Wed 17:00-20:00, and every first Sunday of the month** (kunstmuseumbasel.ch).
- **Rhine ferries (Fähri)**: summer timetable daily **09:00-20:00** (extended to 22:00 during the Basler
  Herbstmesse), winter timetable daily **11:00-17:00**; suspended entirely **above 7.90 m water level**
  (leu-faehri.ch, for the Münster-Fähre "Leu"; the other three ferries are run as separate small
  businesses and may vary).

**Winter/specific dates**
- **Basel Museums Night: Friday, 22 January 2027**, with a single ticket covering **over 40 museums and
  cultural sites** (youthhostel.ch, citing the "26th Basel Museums Night").

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Walks (2)**
1. **Signed Old Town audio walking tour** — **60 to 120 minutes**, self-guided, past the main sights of
   the Old Town (basel.com, "Old Town").
2. **"Stories of Basel's Old Town" guided tour** — covers the Münster, the Barfüsserkirche and the Town
   Hall; **from CHF 25 per person**, **25% off with the BaselCard** (basel.com).

**Rides and sights**
1. **The four Basel ferries (Fähri)** — cable-guided, current-powered, no motor: **Wild Maa** (St. Alban),
   **Leu** (Münster), **Vogel Gryff** (Klingental) and **Ueli** (St. Johann). On the Leu: **adults CHF
   2.00 per crossing, children/dogs/bikes CHF 1.00**; a 20-trip pass costs **CHF 36 on board or CHF 38** at
   sales points. **TNW and SBB tickets, and the BaselCard, are explicitly not valid on the ferries**
   (faehri.ch, leu-faehri.ch).
2. **Basel Münster towers** — climb via a narrow stair of about 250 steps up the Georgsturm or Martinsturm
   for views over the city; prices and hours in section 4.

**What is typical of the place (the Basel museums)**
1. **Kunstmuseum Basel**, St. Alban-Graben 8 — **adults over 20: CHF 30** (special exhibition + collection)
   or **CHF 25** (collection and exhibitions only); **students 20-25: CHF 20/CHF 12; teenagers 13-19: CHF
   12** both tiers; **BaselCard: CHF 22.50/CHF 18.75**; **first Sunday of the month: CHF 16** for the
   special exhibition. Free collection viewing at the times listed in section 4. The museum is a
   **Swiss Museum Pass** partner (a separate annual pass, not the Swiss Travel Pass) giving free entry to
   over 500 Swiss/French/German museums (kunstmuseumbasel.ch).
2. **The Old Town's over 200 fountains**, the Mittlere Brücke (completed **1905**, with a predecessor
   dating to around **1220**), the Pfalz terrace behind the Münster, and the Spalentor, one of the
   **original eight city gates** (basel.com, "Old Town").

**Restaurants (2)**
1. **Restaurant/Brasserie at Hotel Krafft**, Rheingasse 12, on the Rhine promenade, "Schöne Momente am
   Rhein seit 1873" (krafftbasel.ch).
2. **Brasserie and IMI Bar at Volkshaus Basel**, Kleinbasel, built around a tree-covered inner courtyard
   (volkshaus-basel.ch).

## 6. HOTELS (5 read and loaded on 16/09/2026)

1. **Grand Hotel Les Trois Rois** — https://lestroisrois.com — Blumenrain 8, Grossbasel, on the Rhine
   - First documented as the inn "Drei Könige" in **1681**; three carved king figures added to the facade
     in **1754**
   - Restaurant Cheval Blanc by Peter Knogl holds **3 Michelin stars**; the hotel itself holds 3 Keys in
     the Michelin Guide
   - Guests have included Empress Michiko of Japan, James Joyce and Pablo Picasso
2. **Hotel Krafft** — https://krafftbasel.ch — Rheingasse 12, Kleinbasel, on the Rhine promenade
   - 4-star boutique hotel across five floors, hosting guests "seit 1873"
   - River Rooms and City Rooms; summer terrace on the Rhine
   - Part of the Krafft Gruppe, which also runs Nomad Hotel & Eatery and Silo Hostel & Living Room in
     Basel
3. **Der Teufelhof Basel** — https://teufelhof.com — old town, Grossbasel
   - Two combined 18th-century town houses; classified by HotellerieSuisse as a Design & Lifestyle,
     Business and Unique hotel
   - Its own in-house theatre plus two restaurants, Atelier and Bel Etage
4. **Volkshaus Basel** — https://volkshaus-basel.ch — Kleinbasel
   - 4-star boutique and design hotel designed by Herzog & de Meuron
   - A few minutes' walk from the Rhine and the Messe Basel exhibition grounds
   - Brasserie, IMI Bar and a tree-covered inner courtyard
5. **Basel Youth Hostel** — https://www.youthhostel.ch/en/hostels/basel — Maja Sacher-Platz 10, St. Alban
   quarter
   - 66 rooms, 234 beds; 4-6 bed dorms, family and double rooms
   - Award-winning design hostel almost directly on the Rhine; BaselCard included
   - 2 minutes' walk to Kunstmuseum Basel | Gegenwart, 2 minutes to the St. Alban-Rhine ferry

## 7. SWISS TRAVEL PASS

- **Kunstmuseum Basel: NOT the Swiss Travel Pass.** The museum's own page names the separate **Swiss
  Museum Pass** (an annual pass covering over 500 museums in Switzerland, France and Germany) and the
  **BaselCard** (25% off) as its relevant partner schemes; it does not mention the Swiss Travel Pass.
  Do not conflate the Swiss Museum Pass with the Swiss Travel Pass.
- **Basel Münster and tower climb: not applicable/NOT PUBLISHED.** The Münster itself is free to enter;
  no Swiss Travel Pass position was found for the tower climb fee.
- **Rhine ferries: NOT PUBLISHED here**, and note that TNW/SBB tickets and even the local BaselCard are
  explicitly rejected on board (leu-faehri.ch), so a Swiss Travel Pass should not be assumed valid either.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **What does the BaselCard actually cover?** Free public transport for your whole stay (up to 30 days,
   including the transfer from the airport or station), plus 25% off museums, the zoo, boat trips, the
   Old Town walking tour and more. It comes free with any Basel hotel, hostel or B&B booking.
   (basel.com)
2. **Can I use the BaselCard on the Rhine ferries?** No — the ferries explicitly do not accept TNW/SBB
   tickets or the BaselCard; a crossing is CHF 2 for adults, paid on board. (leu-faehri.ch)
3. **Is the Münster free to visit?** Yes, within its opening hours; climbing the towers (about 250 steps)
   costs CHF 6 for adults, CHF 4 reduced, free under 14. (baslermuenster.ch)
4. **When is Kunstmuseum Basel's collection free?** Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 5-6pm, Wednesday 5-8pm,
   and every first Sunday of the month. (kunstmuseumbasel.ch)
5. **How do the four Basel ferries work?** They are guided across the Rhine by an overhead cable, using
   only the current for power, no motor: Wild Maa, Leu, Vogel Gryff and Ueli, one per historic crossing
   point. (faehri.ch)
