## 2. ACCESS

**There is no railway station in Adelboden.** The railhead is Frutigen on the BLS Lötschberg line; PostBus
route 230 runs Frutigen station to "Adelboden, Post".

| Leg | Timetabled time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bern to Adelboden, Post | 1 h 23, one change | BLS RE1 Bern to Frutigen, then bus 230 |
| Spiez to Adelboden, Post | 0 h 50, one change | RE1 Spiez to Frutigen, then bus 230 |
| Interlaken Ost to Adelboden, Post | 1 h 33, two changes | via Spiez and Frutigen |
| Frutigen to Adelboden, Post | bus 230 | the only public link |
| Adelboden village to the Engstligenalp valley station (Unter dem Birg) | about **4 km** up the valley, "at the entrance to the village of Adelboden, turn left at the traffic circle and drive around 4 kilometers into the valley to the valley station" | VERIFIED, engstligenalp.ch/en/arrival |
| By bus to Unter dem Birg | from Frutigen take the bus to "Adelboden, Oey", change, and continue to the last stop "Adelboden, Unter dem Birg", next to the valley station | VERIFIED, engstligenalp.ch/en/arrival |

**Driving and parking.** You can drive to Adelboden. At the Engstligenalp valley station: "There are plenty of
free parking spaces available where you can leave your car during your stay." VERIFIED, engstligenalp.ch.
Parking in the village itself: not published, UNVERIFIED.

**Guest card.** An overnight stay gives a guest card that includes public transport in the destination.
VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/booking/guest-card. Exact perimeter not captured: check before use.

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (3 sectors)

### A. Dorfstrasse, the village centre
- The main street. Hotel Adler at Dorfstrasse 19 is "positioned centrally near mountain railways"; Hotel Bären
  at Dorfstrasse 22; The Cambrian at Dorfstrasse 7; The Brecon at Dorfstrasse 88. VERIFIED, each hotel's page
  and the destination's accommodation listing.
- The "Adelboden, Post" bus stop, the arrival point from Frutigen, is on this street.

### B. Above the village, towards Tschentenalp and Bellevue
- Bellevue Parkhotel & Spa on Bellevuestrasse; Hotel Alpina at Flecklistrasse 40; Hotel Steinmattli at
  Risetensträssli 10. VERIFIED, hotel sites.
- The Tschentenalp cable car leaves from the upper edge of the village.
- Exact walking distances from these hotels to the centre are not published: UNVERIFIED.

### C. Up the valley, towards Unter dem Birg and the Engstligen falls
- About 4 km from the village, the end of the road and of bus route 230, where the Engstligenalp cable car
  starts and where the lower Engstligen fall walk begins. VERIFIED, engstligenalp.ch.
- Free parking at the valley station. VERIFIED.
- Accommodation here is mountain accommodation (Berghotel Engstligenalp on the plateau); its own domain did not
  resolve on 16/09/2026, so no hotel is proposed for this sector.

  Adelboden-Lenk, Engstligenalp, Betelberg, Elsigen-Metsch, Silleren-Chuenisbärgli, Tschentenalp.
  VERIFIED, https://www.adelboden-lenk.ch/en/Experience/Skiregion

- Tschentenalp published opening blocks: **19 October to 30 November 2026, Friday to Tuesday, 10:00 to 16:20**;

- **Audi FIS Ski World Cup Adelboden, 71st edition, 9 and 10 January 2027**, on the Chuenisbärgli, giant slalom
  and slalom, with the programme running Friday 8 to Sunday 10 January 2027.

### Summer

## 5. WHAT TO DO

### Two named walks (published figures)
1. **Rundwanderweg Engstligenalp** (the plateau loop): **4.4 km, 1 h 05**, 26 m of climb and 26 m of descent,
   high point 1963 m, low point 1937 m, starting and ending at the Engstligenalp mountain station (2000 m),
   rated easy and published as wheelchair-accessible and pushchair-friendly.
   VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/aktivitaeten/tour/detail/rundwanderweg-engstligenalp
2. **Wasserfallrunde** (the lower Engstligen fall loop): **2.3 km, 35 minutes**, 69 m of climb and 63 m of
   descent, from the bus stop "Chäli" by the bridge at Unter dem Birg round to the lower Engstligen fall and
   back through the forest past Restaurant Bergbach to the cable car station.
   VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/aktivitaeten/tour/detail/unterer-wasserfallrundwanderweg-engstligenalp

### Two named mountain rides
1. **Engstligenalp cable car** (Bergbahnen Engstligenalp AG): valley station Engstligenstrasse 75, about 4 km
   up the valley from the village; **"The cable car ride up to Engstligenalp takes 7 minutes"**; the plateau is
   at **2000 m** and is called Switzerland's largest; summer season **13 June to 18 October 2026**; cabins are
   wheelchair accessible. VERIFIED, engstligenalp.ch.
2. **Tschentenalp cable car** (Tschentenbahn), from the upper edge of Adelboden to **1950 m**, ride about
   **5 minutes**; published autumn/early-winter blocks 19 October to 30 November 2026 (Fri to Tue) and
   1 to 11 December 2026 (daily), 10:00 to 16:20. VERIFIED, tschentenalp.ch and adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch.
   **DISCREPANCY:** one page gives 1940 m instead of 1950 m. Either attribute the figure or drop it.

### Two named restaurants (destination listing, no opinion)
1. **Restaurant Hohliebe-Stübli**, Fuhrenstrasse 27, 3715 Adelboden. The listing states "an imaginative menu and
   selected Swiss wines", a parlour setting with outdoor fire baskets.
   VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/places/poi/detail/restaurant-hohliebe-stuebli-1
2. **Bergrestaurant Sillerenbühl**, Sillerenbühl, 3715 Adelboden, on the Silleren-Chuenisbärgli ski sector.
   The listing states it is newly renovated and has a children's playground and a large sun terrace.
   VERIFIED, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch/en/places/poi/detail/sillerenbuehl-restaurant-1

### Typical of the place
- **The Engstligen falls**, 600 m over two cascades, reached either from the valley floor at Unter dem Birg
  (Wasserfallrunde, 2.3 km) or from the top of the cable car. VERIFIED, engstligenalp.ch.
- **The World Cup on the Chuenisbärgli**, 9 and 10 January 2027, 71st edition.
  VERIFIED, weltcup-adelboden.ch.

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Is there a train to Adelboden?**
   No. The nearest station is Frutigen on the BLS Lötschberg line, and PostBus route 230 runs from there to
   "Adelboden, Post". Bern to Adelboden is about 1 h 23 with that one change, Spiez about 50 minutes.
   Sources: official Swiss timetable, checked 16/09/2026; engstligenalp.ch for the bus routing.

2. **How high is Adelboden and how many people live there?**
   1,350 m, with 3,534 residents over a municipal area of 88 km2. About 58% of the housing is second homes.
   Source: https://www.3715.ch/

3. **How do I see the Engstligen falls?**
   The falls drop 600 metres over two cascades. From the valley, the Wasserfallrunde is 2.3 km and about
   35 minutes from the "Chäli" bus stop at Unter dem Birg. From above, the Engstligenalp cable car takes
   7 minutes to the 2000 m plateau, where the loop walk is 4.4 km and about 1 h 05.
   Sources: engstligenalp.ch, adelboden-lenk-kandersteg.ch tour pages.

4. **When is the ski season in Adelboden?**
   Bergbahnen Adelboden-Lenk publish continuous daily operation from 5 December 2026 to 4 April 2027, with
   limited weekend operation from 28 November 2026.
   Source: https://www.adelboden-lenk.ch/en/Service/Lifts-and-pistes

5. **When is the World Cup race, and should I avoid that weekend?**
   The 71st Audi FIS Ski World Cup Adelboden runs on the Chuenisbärgli on 9 and 10 January 2027, with the
   programme from Friday 8 to Sunday 10 January. Source: https://weltcup-adelboden.ch/en/
