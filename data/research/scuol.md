## 2. ACCESS

Times from the Swiss national timetable (SBB / Rhaetian Railway data), queried 16/09/2026.

- **Zürich HB to Scuol-Tarasp: 2 h 37, one change.** Zürich HB 09:38 IC 3 (SBB) to Landquart 10:41, then Rhaetian Railway RE 24 Landquart 10:49 to Scuol-Tarasp 12:15.
- **Chur to Scuol-Tarasp: 1 h 44, one change** at Landquart.
- **Scuol-Tarasp to Zernez** (for the Swiss National Park): 33 min, direct, Rhaetian Railway R 15.
- **Scuol-Tarasp is the terminus** of the Rhaetian Railway's Engadine line. The tourist office puts it plainly: "The adventure begins at the railway station: the mountain railway takes you directly to the Motta Naluns hiking and biking area". Source: https://www.engadin.com/en/the-lower-engadin/holiday-resorts/scuol
- Note for booking: "Sagliains is a transfer station and you must buy a valid ticket to Lavin (direction Scuol-Tarasp) or Susch (direction Zernez)". Source: https://www.engadin.com/en/guestcard
- **By car:** yes. The Vereina car-carrying train from Klosters Selfranga loads to Sagliains, 18 minutes through the tunnel, every 30 minutes. Source: https://www.rhb.ch/en/vereina-car-transporter/
- **Evening on-call bus (line 922, Bus-Taxi Engiadina Bassa):** "must be booked free of charge up to one hour before departure via the SBB Mobile app or at www.sbb.ch", for evening runs from Scuol and Scuol-Tarasp Staziun to Ftan from 19:30, to Tarasp from 20:25, to Sent from 21:25 and to Valsot (Friday and Saturday only) from 21:25. Source: https://www.engadin.com/en/guestcard
- **Free local transport with a ski pass:** "During the winter season, public transport within the closed area of Scuol is free of charge. This applies to local buses, ski buses and PostBus routes. With a valid ski pass, travel on the Scuol – Vulpera – Tarasp and Scuol – Sent – Sur En routes is also free of charge until 8.30 p.m." Source: https://www.engadin.com/en/activities-experiences/scuol-ski-region

## 3. WHERE TO SLEEP (three sectors)

### A. The Stradun and the baths
- The lively main street of Scuol: "Boutiques and restaurants invite you to linger on the lively Stradun." The **Bogn Engiadina** mineral bath and the Motta Naluns gondola valley station are here, and the tourist office itself is at Stradun 403a.
- Four hotels have **direct indoor access to the Bogn Engiadina**: Hotel Belvedere, Boutique-Hotel GuardaVal, Badehotel Belvair and Hotel Astras. Source: https://www.bognengiadina.ch/en
- Sources: https://www.engadin.com/en/the-lower-engadin/holiday-resorts/scuol

### B. The old village quarters (Scuol Sot and Scuol Sura)
- The lower and upper old quarters below the Stradun, where "Mineral water bubbles from the fountains in the alleys of the old village quarters". Sgraffito houses, and the Engadin Fountain Bath event is held "in the historic village centre of Scuol" (next dates published: 19 September and 18 October 2026).
- Boutique Hotel ÜJA is "mitten im Herzen von Scuol" with 11 rooms.
- Sources: https://www.engadin.com/en/the-lower-engadin/holiday-resorts/scuol , https://www.bognengiadina.ch/en , https://www.uja-scuol.ch/

### C. Tarasp and Vulpera, across the Inn
- The other side of the valley: Tarasp castle, Vulpera and the golf course. The Belvedere hotels publish "The hotels are only 3 km away from the golf course" (Vulpera) with a 25% green fee reduction for their guests. The ski pass covers the bus "Scuol – Vulpera – Tarasp" until 20:30 in winter.
- Sources: https://www.guardaval-scuol.ch/en/ and https://www.engadin.com/en/activities-experiences/scuol-ski-region

## 5. WHAT TO DO

**Two named walks (official Engadin Scuol route data)**
1. **Flurina Trail**, from the Motta Naluns mountain station. Published as **2.2 km**, **1:30 h**, 99 m of ascent and 18 m of descent, difficulty "Simple". Source: https://www.engadin.com/en/hikes-from-mountain-station-motta-naluns
2. **Motta Naluns to Alp Clünas to Alp Laret to Ftan to Scuol.** Published as **15.4 km**, **4:45 h**, 362 m of ascent and 1,224 m of descent, difficulty "Medium". Source: same page.

**Two named mountain rides / attractions**
1. **Gondola Scuol to Motta Naluns.** The mountain station is at about **2,100 m**: "Start your day 2100 metres above sea level above the Engadin valley". In summer there is a **10 km scooter (Trotinett) descent** on the panorama track from Motta Naluns to Scuol. Summer season 23 May to 1 November 2026, 08:30 to 17:00. Sources: https://www.engadin.com/en/experiences/summer/mountain-railways-scuol and https://www.guardaval-scuol.ch/en/
2. **Bogn Engiadina Scuol**, the mineral bath. "Six indoor and outdoor pools with massage jets, bubbles, waterfalls and a swim-current channel, as well as a brine pool". Published pool data: panorama pool about 34 degrees, 161.5 m2, 177.5 m3, 130 cm deep; active pool about 33 degrees, 152.5 m2; brine pool at 2% salt, about 33 degrees, 71 m2; whirlpool about 37 degrees, 23 m2; cold water grotto about 16 degrees, 7.5 m2; warm water grotto about 37 degrees, 19.5 m2; steam room about 46 degrees. Open all year. A Roman-Irish bath takes "up to 3.5 hours". A free behind-the-scenes tour runs "every Tuesday, 4.00 p.m.", about 1 hour, from reception. Sources: https://www.bognengiadina.ch/en and https://www.engadin.com/en/wellness-health/wellness/mineral-bath-bogn-engiadina-scuol

**Two named restaurants (listed by the tourist office, no opinion)**
1. **Saloon San Jon**, Scuol. The tourist office listing says it offers "Graubünden specialities" and "cowboy specialities matching the saloon, such as grill and smoker fun to the coveted San Jon Burger". Source: https://www.engadin.com/en/restaurants-scuol
2. **Bar La Palma**, "Restaurant / Bar La Palma in the ski area at 2400 m above sea level". Source: same page.
(Other Scuol restaurants listed by the tourist office: Gasthaus Mayor, Nam Thai, Hotel Restaurant Astras, Plazzin, Restaurant Alpetta, Restaurant La Terrassa, Restaurant Conrad, Restaurant Hotel Arnica, Pizzaria Restorant Allegra.)

**Typical of the place (published facts)**
- **Mineral water everywhere.** "Mineral water bubbles from the fountains in the alleys of the old village quarters – and at Bogn Engiadina, Scuol becomes the spa queen of the Alps." The tourist office also publishes signposted **mineral water trails** with circular routes and a folding map, and the municipality has approved a credit for mineral water positioning. Sources: https://www.engadin.com/en/the-lower-engadin/holiday-resorts/scuol and the mineral water news pages on engadin.com
- **Romansh.** The municipal website is written in Romansh; the tourist office states "Quia a Scuol es il rumantsch üna cultura vivüda" is the local reality. Source: https://www.scuol.net/
- **S-charl and the Clemgia gorge.** "Beyond the Clemgia Gorge lies S-charl. A mining and bear museum recall the silver era, while Val Mingèr and the God da Tamangur pine forest offer pure nature." The municipality published a closure of the Clemgia gorge "a partir da subit fin a nouv avis" (from now until further notice) in 2026. Sources: https://www.engadin.com/en/the-lower-engadin/holiday-resorts/scuol and https://www.scuol.net/
- **Swiss National Park.** Scuol-Tarasp to Zernez, the park's main gate, is 33 minutes by train, and the guest card gives "CHF 1.00 reduction for adults" at the National Park Centre in Zernez. Sources: Swiss national timetable and https://www.engadin.com/en/guestcard

## 8. FAQ CANDIDATES

1. **Do I need a car in Scuol?** No. Scuol-Tarasp is the terminus of the Rhaetian Railway, and from your first night you get a Guestcard that includes free 2nd class rail between Zernez and Scuol-Tarasp and all PostBus lines between Brail and Martina, with your dog travelling free. Source: https://www.engadin.com/en/guestcard
2. **What is the difference between the Guestcard and the Guestcard PLUS?** PLUS comes with the participating hotels and adds all Lower Engadine, Val Müstair and Samnaun bus lines, unlimited summer mountain railway rides with bike carriage, one winter ascent and descent with toboggan hire, and 20% off the Bogn Engiadina bathing and sauna area. Source: https://www.engadin.com/en/guestcard
3. **Which hotels let me reach the mineral baths without going outside?** The bath itself lists four: Hotel Belvedere, Boutique-Hotel GuardaVal, Badehotel Belvair and Hotel Astras. Source: https://www.bognengiadina.ch/en
4. **How big is the ski area?** Bergbahnen Scuol publishes "25 Pisten mit 70 Pistenkilometern", three valley runs (to Scuol, to Ftan and the Traumpiste to Sent), a 3.5 km toboggan run from Prui to Ftan and winter walking trails "zwischen 1.9 und 13.7 km". Winter 2026/27 runs 12 December 2026 to 4 April 2027. Sources: https://www.bergbahnen-scuol.ch/de/winter/skigebiet-scuol-motta-naluns and https://www.bergbahnen-scuol.ch/de/informationen/betriebszeiten-winter
5. **Is Scuol a good base for the Swiss National Park?** Zernez, the park's main gate, is 33 minutes by direct Rhaetian Railway train, and the Guestcard covers that leg and gives CHF 1.00 off adult entry to the National Park Centre. Sources: Swiss national timetable, checked 16/09/2026, and https://www.engadin.com/en/guestcard
