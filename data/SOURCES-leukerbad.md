# Sources: data/guides/leukerbad.ts

Checked 16/09/2026. Every fact below comes from the research dossier `data/research/leukerbad.md`,
itself sourced to My Leukerbad AG (leukerbad.ch), the municipality (leukerbad.org), Gemmi-Bahnen
(gemmi.ch), or the hotels' own sites. No ratings, no reviews, no invented numbers.

## Identity and access

- Altitude 1411 m | VERIFIED | https://www.leukerbad.org/gemeindezahlen
- Residents 1556 | VERIFIED | https://www.leukerbad.org/gemeindezahlen
- Car-free: false, private car access and several car parks | VERIFIED | https://leukerbad.ch/anreise
- No railway to Leukerbad; train to Leuk then bus 471, about 35 minutes, operator Leuk-Leukerbad Busbetriebe | VERIFIED | https://www.thermalhotels.ch/en/ and https://leukerbad.ch/anreise
- About 3 hours from Geneva Airport, about 3.5 hours from Zurich Airport, door to door | VERIFIED | https://leukerbad.ch/anreise
- Mobility Ticket, guests of the Alex and Therme 51° hotels, free return public transport, valid 1 May to 31 October 2026 | VERIFIED | https://leukerbad.ch/anreise

## Thermal water and baths

- Over four million litres of warm water a day, at 51 °C, largest thermal water occurrence in Europe, about 40 years for the water to seep down and return | VERIFIED | https://leukerbad.ch/thermalwasser
- Three public baths: Leukerbad Therme, Walliser Alpentherme & Spa, Therme 51°, plus 3 sauna areas, 3 children's pools, 18 wellness providers | VERIFIED | https://leukerbad.ch/en/thermal-baths
- Leukerbad Therme called the largest thermal baths in Europe, outdoor pools, outdoor sports pool, two slides, children's water area | VERIFIED | https://leukerbad.ch/en/thermal-baths

## Seasons

- Winter: 50 km of ski pistes, 50 km of winter walking trails, 4 km of toboggan runs, 18 km of cross-country trails | VERIFIED | https://leukerbad.ch/en/winter
- Torrent lift hours: Leukerbad-Rinderhütte 08:40-12:00 and 12:40-16:40 every 20 min; Flaschen-Torrentalp and Torrentalp-Rinderhütte 09:00-12:15 and 13:15-16:15 | VERIFIED | https://leukerbad.ch/en/torrent

## What to do

- Torrenthorn ascent: 6.8 km, 3 h, 690 m of climb, medium, loop from Rinderhütte | VERIFIED | https://leukerbad.ch/fr/tour/ascension-du-torrenthorn-29148418
- Torrent panorama trail: 4.1 km, 1 h 30, 384 m of descent, easy, Rinderhütte 2350 m to Torrentalp mid-station 1920 m, pushchair accessible, dogs allowed | VERIFIED | https://leukerbad.ch/fr/tour/parcours-panoramique-sur-torrent-57757309
- Gemmi cable car: climb of nearly 1000 m to the Gemmi Pass at 2350 m, Panorama Restaurant, hotel, Panorama Spa, second cable car to Lake Daubensee, crossing used since the Middle Ages | VERIFIED | https://www.gemmi.ch/en/
- Alpina Restaurant: daily menu, cheese and meat fondues, roast chicken in a basket, Valais specialities, house dish tripe the chef's way | VERIFIED | https://leukerbad.ch/en/gastronomy
- Rinderhütte Panoramarestaurant, at the Torrent top station | VERIFIED | https://leukerbad.ch/en/winter

## UNVERIFIED (not published in the guide)

- Winter 2026/2027 opening and closing dates for the Torrent and Gemmi lifts: not published on the pages checked, so no `calendar` block is included.
- Ride times in minutes for the Gemmi cable car and the Torrent line: gemmi.ch only says "in just a few minutes", not used as a figure.
- Tourist beds or overnight stays: the municipality publishes about 60 hotel and restaurant establishments but no bed count.
- Swiss Travel Pass coverage on the Gemmi or Torrent lifts and on the bus from Leuk: not stated on any official page checked, so no `pass` block is included.
- Hotel Alex Leukerbad: named as a partner hotel by the tourist office, but the matching hotelalex.ch domain is a different hotel in Naters/Brig; not matched to a verified URL, so not in the hotel list.
