# Sources: data/guides/klosters.ts

Checked 16/09/2026. Every fact below comes from the research dossier `data/research/klosters.md`,
itself sourced to Destination Davos Klosters (davos.ch), the official panorama hiking map, the RhB
(rhb.ch), the Swiss national timetable, or the hotels' own sites. No ratings, no reviews, no invented
numbers.

## Identity and access

- Klosters Platz 1179 m, Klosters Dorf 1124 m, 2 km apart, each with its own station | VERIFIED | https://resources.davos.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/dokumente/broschueren_karten/Panorama-Wanderkarte_web.pdf
- Residents approx. 4,431 (2016), 13,074 guest beds (5,240 commercial / 7,820 private), 22,000 ha | VERIFIED | https://www.davos.ch/en/information/portrait-image/klosters
- Not car-free: main Landquart-Davos road runs through the village | VERIFIED | https://www.rhb.ch/en/vereina-car-transporter/ and hotel listings on davos.ch
- Zürich HB to Klosters Platz 1 h 51, one change at Landquart; Chur to Klosters Platz 57 min | VERIFIED | Swiss national timetable (SBB / Rhaetian Railway / PostBus), queried 16/09/2026
- Klosters Dorf to Klosters Platz about 7 min by PostBus line 235 | VERIFIED | Swiss national timetable, queried 16/09/2026
- Vereina car transporter, Klosters Selfranga to Sagliains, 19 km tunnel in 18 minutes, every 30 minutes | VERIFIED | https://www.rhb.ch/en/vereina-car-transporter/
- Davos Klosters Premium Card: free 2nd class train and bus travel within zone 200/210 of the Klosters tariff association, given from the first night | VERIFIED | https://www.davos.ch/en/information/guest-information/guest-card/premium-card
