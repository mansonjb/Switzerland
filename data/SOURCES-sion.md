## UNVERIFIED (not used on the page)

- Swiss Travel Pass or Swiss Museum Pass coverage of the Basilique de Valere or cantonal museums: not found on an official page, so no `pass` block is published for this guide.
- Sion Airport passenger/scheduled-route figures beyond the Air Mountain routes: not itemised on an official page.
- Ninda Boutique Hotel: listed by some aggregators as a Sion-area stay, but its own site gives Haute-Nendaz as its address, a separate resort; not used as a Sion hotel.
