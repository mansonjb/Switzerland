## Seasons, calendar and what to do

- Glacier 3000 guarantees skiing November to April, weather permitting, from 2971 m at Scex Rouge | VERIFIED | https://www.alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/Z41
- Village toboggan run: 7.2 km, 1719 m to 1167 m (568 m of vertical), open daily 09:00-16:30 snow-dependent, night sessions every Thursday | VERIFIED | https://www.alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/V1331
- Lac Retaud: 1685 m, swimming, paddling, fishing, crystal-clear water, conifer forest, views of Scex Rouge and Glacier 3000, ideal for a family walk | VERIFIED | https://www.alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/P8781
- Glacier 3000 cable car: 15 minutes from Col du Pillon to Scex Rouge at 2971 m, views of 24 peaks above 4000 m including the Eiger, Monch, Jungfrau, Matterhorn, Grand Combin and Mont Blanc | VERIFIED | https://www.alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/Z41
- Peak Walk by Tissot: 107 m suspension bridge at 3000 m, open year-round, operator describes it as unique in the world for linking two peaks | VERIFIED | https://www.alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/P5496
- Via ferrata des Dames Anglaises: 0.35 km, 1.5 h, +275 m/-3 m; via ferrata de la cascade du Dar: 0.4 km, 1.5 h, +185 m/-256 m; via ferrata du Rocher Jaune: 0.83 km, 2 h, +283 m/-321 m | VERIFIED | https://www.alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/G4041
- Meilleret sector and Col de la Croix via the Diablerets Express, routes toward the Creux de Champs cirque and the Pierredar refuge; Tour du Massif des Diablerets, 4-5 day itinerary | VERIFIED | https://www.alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/Z218
- La Belle Descente: local restaurant, terroir and seasonal cuisine | VERIFIED | https://www.alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/G4042
