# Sources: data/guides/scuol.ts

Checked 16/09/2026. Every fact below comes from the research dossier `data/research/scuol.md`, itself
sourced to Engadin Scuol Samnaun Val Müstair (engadin.com), Bergbahnen Scuol (bergbahnen-scuol.ch), the
Cumün da Scuol (scuol.net), Bogn Engiadina (bognengiadina.ch), the Swiss national timetable, or the
hotels' own sites. No ratings, no reviews, no invented numbers.

## Identity and access

- About 4,600 residents, largest municipality in Switzerland by area, villages Ardez, Ftan, Guarda, Scuol, Sent, Tarasp, Romansh as a living culture | VERIFIED | https://www.scuol.net/
- Altitude 1250 m, coordinates 46.7988 / 10.3030 | VERIFIED (cross-check only) | https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuol
- Zürich HB to Scuol-Tarasp 2 h 37, one change at Landquart; Chur to Scuol-Tarasp 1 h 44; Scuol-Tarasp to Zernez 33 min | VERIFIED | Swiss national timetable (SBB / Rhaetian Railway), queried 16/09/2026
- Sagliains is a transfer station, ticket must be bought to Lavin (for Scuol-Tarasp) or Susch (for Zernez) | VERIFIED | https://www.engadin.com/en/guestcard
- Vereina car-carrying train, Klosters Selfranga to Sagliains, 18 minutes, every 30 minutes | VERIFIED | https://www.rhb.ch/en/vereina-car-transporter/
- Evening on-call bus line 922: book free up to one hour before departure via SBB Mobile app or sbb.ch, routes to Ftan, Tarasp, Sent, and (Friday/Saturday only) Valsot | VERIFIED | https://www.engadin.com/en/guestcard
- Guestcard: free from first overnight stay, 2nd class rail Zernez to Scuol-Tarasp, all PostBus/Engadin Bus lines Brail to Martina, dog travels free, valid on arrival and departure days | VERIFIED | https://www.engadin.com/en/guestcard
- Guestcard PLUS (participating hotels): adds all Lower Engadine/Val Müstair/Samnaun bus lines, unlimited summer mountain-railway rides with bike carriage, one winter ascent/descent with toboggan hire, 20% off Bogn Engiadina bathing and sauna | VERIFIED | https://www.engadin.com/en/guestcard
- In winter, a valid ski pass gives free travel on the Scuol-Vulpera-Tarasp and Scuol-Sent-Sur En bus routes until 8.30 pm | VERIFIED | https://www.engadin.com/en/activities-experiences/scuol-ski-region

## Seasons and ski area

- Winter 2026/27: 12 December 2026 to 4 April 2027, with three daily-hours blocks | VERIFIED | https://www.bergbahnen-scuol.ch/de/informationen/betriebszeiten-winter
- 25 pistes, 70 km of slopes, 13 lift installations (1 gondola, 6 chairlifts, 3 drag lifts, 3 magic carpets) | VERIFIED | https://www.bergbahnen-scuol.ch/de/winter/skigebiet-scuol-motta-naluns
- Toboggan run Prui to Ftan, 3.5 km, with night tobogganing; winter walking trails 1.9 to 13.7 km; Panoramaloipe, Snowpark, Kinderland Nalunsin with a crèche; barrier-free skiing (#ChairInABox) | VERIFIED | same page
- Motta Naluns mountain station about 2,100 m, 10 km scooter descent in summer | VERIFIED | https://www.engadin.com/en/experiences/summer/mountain-railways-scuol
- Bogn Engiadina open all year; sauna area closed until early October 2026 | VERIFIED | https://www.bognengiadina.ch/en

## What to do

- Flurina Trail: 2.2 km, 1 h 30, 99 m ascent, 18 m descent, difficulty simple, from the Motta Naluns mountain station | VERIFIED | https://www.engadin.com/en/hikes-from-mountain-station-motta-naluns
- Motta Naluns to Alp Clünas, Alp Laret, Ftan, Scuol: 15.4 km, 4 h 45, 362 m ascent, 1,224 m descent, difficulty medium | VERIFIED | same page
- Bogn Engiadina pools: six indoor/outdoor pools plus a brine pool at 2% salt, sizes and temperatures published, free behind-the-scenes tour every Tuesday at 4 pm, 20% discount for RhB/RailAway arrivals | VERIFIED | https://www.bognengiadina.ch/en

- Saloon San Jon and Bar La Palma at 2,400 m, listed by the tourist office | VERIFIED | https://www.engadin.com/en/restaurants-scuol

## UNVERIFIED (dropped, not written into the guide)

- Altitude of Scuol is not published by an official source; 1250 m is a Wikipedia cross-check only, used because it matches the figure already on the destination sheet.
- Lift count discrepancy on the operator's own page (12 vs 13 lifts): used the detailed breakdown (13: one gondola, six chairlifts, three drag lifts, three magic carpets).
- Traumpiste length discrepancy (piste table says 10.0 km, page text says 11 km): not resolved, so no specific length was printed for this run; described only as "the valley run to Sent" in the dossier, and not named at all in the published guide to avoid printing an unresolved figure.
- Summer 2026 lift operating dates (gondola 23 May to 1 November, Ftan-Prui 13 June to 18 October, Naluns 16 July to 18 October): sourced only from a hotel's guest-card page, not confirmed directly on bergbahnen-scuol.ch; not printed in the guide.
- Bogn Engiadina total area, "13,000 m2": quoted by the Belvedere hotels, not by the bath itself; not printed as an unattributed figure.
- Chasa Alpina (lift-company accommodation on Motta Naluns): its page was not read in this session, so it is not included among the hotels.
- Tourist beds and overnight stays for Scuol: not published, not written.
- Whether Scuol Sot and Scuol Sura are the correct local names for the old quarters: not confirmed on the pages read, so the guide calls them "the old village quarters" without those specific names.
- Whether the Swiss Travel Pass gives any local reduction: not published; the guide carries no `pass` block, even though the Guestcard coverage table is detailed, because no single published fare comparison (without pass vs with pass) exists to complete that field.
- "The other end of the canton" framing for Scuol versus Laax and Flims (quickAnswer, intro context): a general geographic orientation drawn from the project brief and the destinations' published coordinates, not a specific figure from either dossier, and not printed as a numbered distance.
