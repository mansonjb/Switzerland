# Sources: data/guides/arosa.ts

Checked 16/09/2026. Every fact below comes from the research dossier `data/research/arosa.md`, itself
sourced to the municipality of Arosa (gemeindearosa.ch), Arosa Lenzerheide (arosalenzerheide.swiss),
the Arosa Bear Sanctuary (arosabaerenland.swiss), the Swiss national timetable, or the hotels' own
sites. No ratings, no reviews, no invented numbers.
