Checked 16/09/2026, from data/research/bettmeralp.md. Official sources only (Aletsch Arena AG / aletscharena.ch, and the hotels' own sites).

- Altitude 1970 m, canton Valais, car-free, reached only by the large-cabin cable car from Bettmeralp valley station or the aerial tramway via Betten Dorf; village centre with shops, bars and grocery stores; ski racks instead of parking spaces in winter; Maria zum Schnee chapel, absolute highlight, part of the Grand Tour of Switzerland with a marked photo spot; Lake Bettmersee named as a sight, swimming in summer; around 300 days of sunshine a year; 104 km of slopes | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/bettmeralp
- Ride times: valley station to Bettmeralp 7 min, transfer to View Point lift 20 min; Bettmeralp to Bettmerhorn 8 min; season dates winter 2026/27 5 December 2026 to 3 April 2027, summer 2026 6 June to 25 October 2026; Bettmerhorn cable car 6.6-25.10.2026 08:30-16:30; maintenance closures 26.10-15.11.2026, 16.11-22.11.2026, 23.11-10.12.2026; baggage cart from CHF 10 per person | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility/timetable-season-times
- Feeder railway (valley station to Bettmeralp) runs all year, part of the SBB GA and half-fare travelcard network, SBB luggage service, Snow'n'Rail discounts, 2 free nights of public transport with a 2-night hotel booking; car park at the valley station (Grengiols) with EV charging points, large outdoor and indoor car parks; baggage cart recommended because the car-free roads are under snow in winter; buy passes online in advance to avoid the queue | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/travel-mobility
- Aletsch Express electric shuttle links Bettmeralp and Riederalp, from CHF 6.00 per person | VERIFIED | aletscharena.ch webshop listing (Aletsch Express, Bettmeralp)
- Four Aletsch Arena view points between 2227 and 2869 m; Great Aletsch Glacier 20 km long, longest in the Alps; 40 four-thousand-metre peaks in view; Swiss Alps Jungfrau-Aletsch UNESCO World Heritage Site | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/about-the-region/destination/view-points
- 104 km of slopes from Riederfurka to Eggishorn, slopes above 2000 m give guaranteed snow December to Easter, black run from the Eggishorn, Glacier Panorama Tour, around 300 days of sunshine | VERIFIED | https://www.aletscharena.ch/en/planning-booking/winter/skiing
