Checked 16/09/2026. Every fact below comes from the research dossier `data/research/pontresina.md`,
itself sourced to Pontresina Tourism (pontresina.ch), the Gemeinde Pontresina Jahresbericht 2024, Swiss
public transport open data (transport.opendata.ch), Engadin St. Moritz Tourismus (engadin.ch), and the
hotels' own sites. No ratings, no reviews, no invented numbers.

## Seasons and what to do

- Diavolezza summit "2,978 metres above sea level"; Piz Palü and Piz Bernina "4049 metres above sea level"; heated outdoor jacuzzi at the top | VERIFIED | https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/mountains-and-valleys/the-diavolezza-and-the-lagalb
- Lagalb: "the steepest serviced downhill run in the Grisons with a gradient of 86%" | VERIFIED | https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/mountains-and-valleys/the-diavolezza-and-the-lagalb
- Muottas Muragl summit "2,453 metres above sea level", gradient "up to 55 per cent", operates "up until 23:00h" | VERIFIED | https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/mountains-and-valleys/alp-languard-muottas-muragl

- Berghaus Diavolezza, Rest. Bellavista: "Located at 3,000 meters elevation", hot meals "11:00am - 3:30pm", signature dish Spaghetti Diavolezza, telescope on the terrace | VERIFIED | https://www.pontresina.ch/en/guide/restaurants-bars/eatdrink-berghaus-diavolezza
