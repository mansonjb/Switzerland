- Population 38,990 (also written 38'990 / 38 990), official address Stadthausgasse 12, 8200 Schaffhausen | VERIFIED | https://www.stadt-schaffhausen.ch

- Old town: 171 carved oriel windows (Erker), sitting between the Rhine and the Munot | VERIFIED | https://www.schaffhauserland.ch/region/schaffhausen.html

- Munot moat (Munotgraben): fallow deer enclosure since 1905, at the request of the city's beautification society, lead buck traditionally named after the sitting city president | VERIFIED | https://munot.ch/leben-im-munotgraben/
- Access: Zurich to Schaffhausen about 40 minutes by train, versus about 45 minutes by car for the 50 km distance; Zurich Airport to Schaffhausen about 49 minutes by train, versus about 38 minutes by car | VERIFIED | https://www.schaffhauserland.ch/region/schaffhausen/anreise.html
- Restaurants Guterhof - Gastronomie am Rhein (former warehouse on the Rhine, bar, lounge, terrace, close to the Munot) and Brauerei Falken AG (brewery restaurant, brewery founded 1799, among Switzerland's five largest independent breweries) | VERIFIED | https://www.schaffhauserland.ch/erleben/essen-trinken/gesamte-gastronomie.html
- Hotel directory listing: Kronenhof, Ruden, Vienna House Zur Bleiche, Promenade, Park Villa | VERIFIED | https://www.schaffhauserland.ch/erleben/uebernachten/alle-unterkuenfte.html
- Museum zu Allerheiligen: Klosterstrasse 16, six permanent collections (archaeology, history, art, natural history) in a former Romanesque abbey, Tuesday-Sunday 11:00-17:00, until 20:00 on the last Thursday of the month, closed Mondays | VERIFIED | https://www.allerheiligen.ch
- Rhine Falls: accessible around the clock, 365 days a year; Schloss Laufen side CHF 5 adult / CHF 3 child (6-15), includes Historama and viewing platforms; Schlössli Wörth side free; falls about 150 m wide | VERIFIED | https://www.rheinfall.ch/en/inform and https://www.rheinfall.ch/en/
- Rhine Falls boat trips: Ernst Mändli AG, "Schiff & Wein" evening cruise CHF 55 per adult, departs 16:45 or 17:00, returns by 19:00 | VERIFIED | https://www.rheinfall.ch/en/experience
