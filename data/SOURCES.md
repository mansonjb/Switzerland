## B. Wengen transport

1. **Car-free / parking at Lauterbrunnen.** VERIFIED Wengen is car-free, road ends at Lauterbrunnen. Parkhaus Lauterbrunnen is a real, covered multi-storey car park (940 spaces, next to the station, run by Parkhaus Lauterbrunnen AG since 1976) — [jungfrau.ch/parkhaus-lauterbrunnen-ag](https://www.jungfrau.ch/en-gb/corporate/jungfrau-railways/jungfraubahn-holding-ag/parkhaus-lauterbrunnen-ag/). **Daily/weekly price: UNVERIFIABLE from an official price page in this session** — the jungfrau.ch corporate page gives no tariff, and secondary sources disagree (CHF 14–18/day cited variously). **Safe wording:** "covered parking at Lauterbrunnen station (Parkhaus Lauterbrunnen, 940 spaces); check parkhaus-lauterbrunnen.ch or jungfrau.ch for current daily/weekly rates" — don't print a specific CHF figure without a live official quote.

2. **Lauterbrunnen → Wengen (WAB).** Official jungfrau.ch page states **12 minutes**, not 14–15. **WRONG**, correct to 12 min. Source: [jungfrau.ch — Wengernalpbahn AG](https://www.jungfrau.ch/en-gb/corporate/jungfrau-railways/jungfraubahn-holding-ag/wengernalpbahn-ag/). First/last trains: **UNVERIFIABLE precise clock times** in this session (aggregator data was inconsistent/unreliable, not from an official timetable). Safe wording: "frequent service (roughly every 30 min) from early morning to around midnight; exact first/last trains vary by day and season — check the SBB/jungfrau.ch timetable for your travel date," rather than asserting "6h to midnight."

3. **Wengen → Kleine Scheidegg.** Claim of 18 min is **WRONG** and the mockup's own suspicion (~25–30 min) is closer to correct. Official jungfrau.ch page gives Lauterbrunnen→Kleine Scheidegg as **42 minutes** total; subtracting the confirmed Lauterbrunnen→Wengen leg (12 min) implies **Wengen→Kleine Scheidegg ≈ 28–30 minutes**. Secondary aggregators (Rome2Rio) show 22–24 min, which is lower but still nowhere near 18. **Correct to "about 25–30 minutes."** Source: [jungfrau.ch — Wengernalpbahn AG](https://www.jungfrau.ch/en-gb/corporate/jungfrau-railways/jungfraubahn-holding-ag/wengernalpbahn-ag/) (for the 42 min figure).

4. **Kleine Scheidegg → Jungfraujoch.** Claim 35 min. Sources found range 35–45 min (Jungfrau Railway climb is steep and single-track with passing loops, so timing varies by service). Treat 35 min as **VERIFIED as a plausible minimum but UNVERIFIABLE as the fixed figure** — no single official minute count was retrievable in this session. Safe wording: "about 35–40 minutes."

5. **Door-to-door Wengen → Jungfraujoch.** Adding items 3+4 (≈28–30 + ≈35–40 min) plus a Kleine Scheidegg connection wait gives roughly **1 h 10–1 h 20**, not "~1 h." **WRONG/optimistic** — reword to "about 1 h 10–1 h 20, including the change at Kleine Scheidegg."

6. **Interlaken Ost → Lauterbrunnen.** Multiple secondary sources (Trainline, Omio, Rome2Rio) converge on **~19–22 minutes**; no single official SBB figure was retrievable via fetch in this session (SBB's timetable is JS-rendered, not fetchable as static HTML). **Safe to publish as "about 20 minutes," flagged as sourced from aggregated schedules rather than a single SBB page.**

7. **Luggage.** VERIFIED in substance: Wengen station has free luggage trolleys and baggage lockers; the village allows only service/taxi vehicles, which are electric; many hotels send an electric cart/luggage sledge to the station if given a train time. Sources: [Wengen Tourism arrival page](https://wengen.swiss/en/destination/arrival.html), [Wikivoyage Wengen](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Wengen), hotel pages below. Good to publish as general village-wide practice; avoid naming it as guaranteed at every single hotel without asking (see Section E per-hotel notes).

## C. Swiss Travel Pass (STP) coverage, Jungfrau region, 2026

- **Free with STP:** Interlaken–Lauterbrunnen–**Wengen**–**Kleine Scheidegg** on the Wengernalpbahn (and the equivalent Interlaken–Grindelwald leg) are covered as ordinary scheduled rail and are **free**, not merely discounted. So **Wengen → Kleine Scheidegg is FREE with STP**, not discounted.
- **Discounted with STP:** only the **Kleine Scheidegg → Jungfraujoch** (and Grindelwald Terminal/Eigergletscher → Jungfraujoch) leg on the Jungfrau Railway gets **25% off** the published fare — this part of the mockup claim is correct.
- Sources (consistent across several, non-official-only, secondary summaries — no single canonical jungfrau.ch coverage map page was fetchable): [myswissalps.com — STP & Jungfraujoch](https://www.myswissalps.com/activity/jungfraujoch/), [jungfrau.ch — Jungfraujoch connecting ticket to STP](https://www.jungfrau.ch/en-gb/jungfrau-travel-pass/connecting-ticket-to-jungfrau-travel-pass/). **Recommend confirming on the official swisstravelpass.ch "included services" map before publishing as an absolute claim**, since I could not fetch that map page directly (certificate error on swiss-travel-system.com).

## D. Männlichen & Eiger Express

- **Location in village:** the Wengen–Männlichen cable car valley station sits centrally in Wengen, a short walk up the main street (Dorfstrasse) from the train station, next to the tourist office — VERIFIED via [Jungfrau Region Tourismus map listing](https://jungfrauregion.swiss/en/map/detail/valley-station-cable-car-wengen-mannlichen-88ea34fd-5c45-44dc-bbac-84b8f135efef.html) and general village-layout sources (Wikivoyage). Not "8 min uphill" like Hotel Falken — it's central, not at the east end (see Section F correction).
- **2026 operating season (Wengen–Männlichen cableway):** summer season **23 May – 25 Oct 2026**; closed for spring maintenance **7 April – 22 May 2026**. Source: [maennlichen.ch/en/summer/information/timetable.html](https://www.maennlichen.ch/en/summer/information/timetable.html) and secondary corroboration. Autumn/winter gap after Oct 25 was not fully pinned down for the Wengen side specifically (the Grindelwald–Männlichen gondola's winter season, a *different* lift, runs 21 Nov 2026–25 Apr 2027 per the same source family) — **flag the exact autumn reopening date for the Wengen side as needing a direct maennlichen.ch check before publishing**, don't conflate the two lifts.
- **Eiger Express (Grindelwald Terminal → Eigergletscher):** VERIFIED **~15 minutes** by tricable gondola. **Grindelwald Terminal → Jungfraujoch total ≈ 45 minutes** per jungfrau.ch's own winter page ("From Grindelwald Terminal to the Jungfraujoch in just 45 minutes"), i.e. **faster than the Wengen route** (Wengen→Jungfraujoch ≈ 1h10–1h20 per Section B.5). Source: [jungfrau.ch winter Jungfraujoch page](https://www.jungfrau.ch/en-gb/winter/jungfraujoch-top-of-europe/).

## F. Village layout

Mockup's three-zone description is **roughly right but the east-end/Männlichen claim is off.**

- **Around the station:** VERIFIED — shops (incl. one of the two grocery stores), tourist shops, restaurants cluster right at the station, which is "in the heart of the village." Source: [Wikivoyage Wengen](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Wengen).
- **Above the village, north, near the church:** VERIFIED-ish — the Reformed church (built 1953) sits "perched on an outcrop at the north end of town" with a landmark clock tower; north/uphill from the centre fits "valley views, steep climb." Source: same Wikivoyage summary, corroborated by general descriptions.
- **Männlichen cable car at the "east end":** **WRONG as stated.** The Wengen–Männlichen valley station is **central**, just up the main street (Dorfstrasse) from the train station, next to the tourist office — not at a separate east end of the village. **Correct wording:** "the Männlichen cable car station sits centrally, a short walk up the main street from the train station" rather than describing it as a distinct third zone at the east end.

## G. Oberland region comparison claims

- **Mürren access "in two steps":** VERIFIED — Route 1: Lauterbrunnen → Grütschalp by cable car, then BLM train Grütschalp → Mürren (~20 min total). Route 2: Lauterbrunnen → Stechelberg by bus/postauto, then Stechelberg → Mürren cable car via Gimmelwald (~20–30 min total, direct Stechelberg–Mürren leg is ~4 min, via Gimmelwald ~12 min). Source: aggregated from [myswissalps.com Lauterbrunnen–Mürren guide](https://www.myswissalps.com/travel-guide/lauterbrunnen-murren/) and similar; no single official Mürren Tourism page was fetched directly, but the two-route structure is consistent and well-documented, including on [muerren.swiss/en/destination/arrival.html](https://muerren.swiss/en/destination/arrival.html) (listed but not fetched — recommend one direct check before publishing precise minute counts).
- **Mürren car-free / Gimmelwald car-free:** VERIFIED — both reachable only by train/cable car, no road access for private cars.
- **Grindelwald accessible by car:** VERIFIED (Grindelwald, unlike Wengen/Mürren/Gimmelwald, has a normal road and is not car-free).
- **Interlaken as arrival hub:** VERIFIED — Interlaken Ost/West are the standard rail gateway into the Jungfrau region from the rest of Switzerland.
- **Low-season closures "end April to mid-May and November":** **VERIFIED as a pattern, not as exact fixed dates.** Multiple independent travel-forum and blog sources for 2026 describe Wengen/Mürren/Lauterbrunnen-valley hotels and restaurants routinely closing for a break roughly **early/mid-April through late May** (ski season end to summer season start) and again in **November** (summer season end to ski season start, with snow itself often absent until December). No single official Jungfrau Region tourism page gives one fixed regional date — individual businesses set their own dates. **Safe wording:** "many hotels and restaurants close for a low-season break roughly mid-April to late May, and again in November — check individual opening dates before booking," rather than printing exact fixed dates as fact.
