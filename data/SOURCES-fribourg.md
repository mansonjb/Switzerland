# Sources: data/guides/fribourg.ts

Checked 16/09/2026. Every fact below comes from the research dossier `data/research/fribourg.md`, itself
sourced to fribourg.ch (official Fribourg Tourisme et Region pages) and the two hotels' own sites. This
pass had no web-search budget and several fribourg.ch listing pages (hotel directory, restaurant
directory, funicular technical page) render with JavaScript and returned no static content to WebFetch,
so hotel and restaurant coverage is thinner than usual. No ratings, no reviews, no invented numbers.

## What to do

- Tour of the fortifications: six towers, five gates, seven sections of wall, one Great Bulwark, built
  13th-15th century, described by the tourist office as the most important ensemble of medieval military
  architecture in Switzerland, full route about 3 hours, open 09:00-19:00, 1 May to 1 November | VERIFIED |
  https://fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/tour-of-the-fortifications
- Guided public tour of the Old Town, run by the tourist office | VERIFIED |
  https://fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/visits/guided-public-tours-of-the-old-town/
- Cathedral of St Nicholas: Bourg district, described by the tourist office as undoubtedly Fribourg's most
  famous landmark, Gothic building | VERIFIED | https://fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/groups/st-nicholas-cathedral
- Fribourg City Card includes the cathedral tower ascent, March to November, with an audio guide |
  VERIFIED | https://fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/fribourg-city-card/
- Fribourg funicular: public funicular, TPF zone 10, covered by the Fribourg City Card, scheduled
  unavailable 27 August to 4 December 2026 | VERIFIED | https://fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/fribourg-city-card/
- Cafe Villars: venue named on the Fribourg City Card for Swiss pastry and candy | VERIFIED |
  https://fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/fribourg-city-card/
- Fribourg City Card: seven museums (Art and History Museum, Espace Jean Tinguely-Niki de Saint Phalle,
  Espace 1606, Swiss Puppets Museum, Kaeserberg, Bible+Orient Museum, SwissBierMuseum), TPF zone 10
  transport including the funicular, cathedral tower climb (March-November) and audio guide, Motta
  swimming pool (May-September), urban golf, pastry at Cafe Villars | VERIFIED |
  https://fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/fribourg-city-card/
- Fribourg City Card price: CHF 20 adult / CHF 10 child (6-16), one day, April-September; CHF 15/CHF 5
  October-March; two-day CHF 30/CHF 12 (Apr-Sep) and CHF 25/CHF 10 (Oct-Mar) | VERIFIED |
  https://fribourg.ch/en/fribourg/fribourg-city-card/
