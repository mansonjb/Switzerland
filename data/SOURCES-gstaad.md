# Sources: data/guides/gstaad.ts

Checked 16/09/2026. Every fact below comes from the research dossier `data/research/gstaad.md`,
itself sourced to Gstaad Saanenland Tourismus, Bergbahnen Destination Gstaad, MOB / GoldenPass, BLS
or the hotels' own sites. No ratings, no reviews, no invented numbers.

## Identity and access

- Altitude 1050 m | VERIFIED (Wikipedia cross-check, no official Gstaad source prints a village altitude) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gstaad
- Gstaad is a village of the municipality of Saanen, canton Bern | VERIFIED | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gstaad
- Gstaad reached by road, not car-free | VERIFIED | https://www.gstaad.ch/en (arrival and parking information)
- MOB links Château-d'Oex, Rougemont, Saanen, Gstaad, Schönried, Saanenmöser, Zweisimmen, roughly every half hour | VERIFIED | https://www.gstaad.ch/en/summer/plan-book/getting-there-around/getting-around-the-region
- GoldenPass Express: four trains a day Montreux to Gstaad to Interlaken, no change of train, variable-gauge bogies, three classes | VERIFIED | https://www.gpx.swiss/en and https://www.gstaad.ch/en/summer/plan-book/getting-there-around/getting-around-the-region
- GoldenPass Express: 3 h 15 Montreux to Interlaken, 115.34 km (62.43 km metre gauge + 52.91 km standard gauge) | VERIFIED | https://www.bls.ch/en/freizeit-und-ferien/ausfluege/mob-goldenpass-express
- Bern to Gstaad about 1 h 53 (one change) or 1 h 56; Spiez to Gstaad 1 h 19 direct; Interlaken Ost to Gstaad 1 h 50 direct; Montreux to Gstaad 1 h 25 direct | VERIFIED against the official Swiss timetable, queried 16/09/2026 | re-check on https://www.sbb.ch
- GstaadCard: free 2nd class public transport during the stay, Lenk to Rougemont including PostBus to Turbach, Lauenen and Gsteig; NOT valid on the GoldenPass Express | VERIFIED | https://www.gstaad.ch/en/summer/plan-book/getting-there-around/getting-around-the-region
